先认清要保护的东西Identify what must survive

一次 0 或 1 的读数不足以描述一个量子比特；换一种测量方式，隐藏的相位就会影响答案。A single 0-or-1 readout does not describe a qubit. A different measurement basis can reveal the effect of its relative phase.

把一个普通比特读出来，只会得到 0 或 1。量子比特的状态也能用坐标描述，但坐标描述的是测量前的状态，不是同时藏着的两个经典答案。我们用 |0〉和 |1〉表示两个基向量，用 α、β 表示它们的振幅。|ψ〉读作“ket psi”，表示整个状态；测量 Z 时，得到 0、1 的概率分别是 |α|²、|β|²，二者之和必须为 1。A classical bit reads as 0 or 1. A qubit also has a coordinate description, but its coordinates specify the state before measurement, not two hidden classical answers. The basis vectors are |0〉 and |1〉, and their amplitudes are α and β. Read |ψ〉 as “ket psi”: it denotes the whole state. A Z measurement returns 0 or 1 with probabilities |α|² and |β|², which sum to one.

|ψ〉 = α|0〉 + β|1〉,　|α|² + |β|² = 1|ψ〉 = α|0〉 + β|1〉,　|α|² + |β|² = 1

振幅可以是复数。把 a+bi 画成平面向量 (a,b)：长度的平方是 a²+b²；i 满足 i²=−1。相位就是这个向量的方向，两个振幅之间的方向差叫相对相位。Amplitudes may be complex. Draw a+bi as the plane vector (a,b): its squared length is a²+b², and i satisfies i²=−1. Phase is the direction of this vector; the difference between two such directions is their relative phase.

为什么还要保留方向？比较 |+〉=(|0〉+|1〉)/√2 与 |−〉=(|0〉−|1〉)/√2。两者的 Z 读数都是一半 0、一半 1，可是测量 X 时，它们分别必然给出 +1 和 −1。X 测量在这两个叠加态组成的基底中提问；它的 +1 概率是 |α+β|²/2。振幅会相加或抵消，所以只记住 Z 的概率会丢失信息。Why retain direction? Compare |+〉=(|0〉+|1〉)/√2 with |−〉=(|0〉−|1〉)/√2. Both give 0 and 1 equally often in a Z measurement, but an X measurement gives +1 with certainty for the first and −1 for the second. X measures in this superposition basis: its +1 probability is |α+β|²/2. Amplitudes can reinforce or cancel, so the Z probabilities alone lose information.

下面先准备 |+〉，再施加 Z。Z 把 β 变成 −β，因此只改相位；X 则交换 α、β。它们也叫 Pauli 操作。H（Hadamard 门）把 |0〉变为 |+〉、|1〉变为 |−〉，让我们把两种测量基联系起来。操作 X 与“测量 X”是两件事：前者可逆地改变振幅，后者输出一个随机符号，并将状态投影到相应基态。Prepare |+〉 below, then apply Z. Z changes β to −β, affecting phase; X swaps α and β. These are Pauli operations. H, the Hadamard gate, maps |0〉 to |+〉 and |1〉 to |−〉, connecting the two measurement bases. Applying X and measuring X are distinct: the operation reversibly changes amplitudes, while the measurement produces a random sign and projects the state onto its corresponding basis state.

从 |+〉施加 Z 后，Z 的概率条完全不动，X 的 +1 概率却从 1 变成 0。这就是纠错必须保护的相位信息。给两个振幅同时乘同一个单位长度复数，只会整体转动两根箭头，所有测量概率都不变；这种整体相位可以忽略。下面把同一对 α、β 编码进许多物理比特，它们共同保存一个逻辑比特，而不是各自保存一份可随意读取的副本。After Z acts on |+〉, the Z probability bars stay fixed while the X +1 probability falls from one to zero. Error correction must protect this phase information. Multiplying both amplitudes by the same unit-length complex number rotates both arrows together and leaves every measurement probability unchanged; this global phase can be ignored. We will now encode the same α and β in many physical qubits. Together they store one logical qubit, not separately readable copies.

让局部检查留下一个自由度Let local checks leave one qubit

既要保护相位，又不能直接读出 α、β，就需要只检查编码约束的测量。To protect phase without reading α and β, we need measurements of the encoding constraints alone.

图中九个圆点 q₀ 到 q₈ 是物理数据比特，按行编号。一个带 X 的面同时询问它所接触比特的 X 结果之积；带 Z 的面询问 Z 结果之积。比如第一项检查 Sˣ₁=X₀X₁X₃X₄，单个 Pauli 的下标指数据比特。图中用 X1 作为这项检查的简写；完整检查表使用 Sˣ₁ 等名称，把检查和单点操作区分开。检查输出 +1 或 −1，不会列出每个数据比特的答案。The nine circles q₀ through q₈ are physical data qubits, numbered by row. An X face asks for the product of X outcomes on its support; a Z face asks for the corresponding Z product. For example, the first X check is X₀X₁X₃X₄, where each subscript on a Pauli names a data qubit. The diagram labels checks X1, X2, and so on; the full table uses Sˣ₁ and similar names to distinguish checks from single-qubit operators. A check returns +1 or −1, without reporting each data qubit’s answer.

同一个比特上的 X 和 Z 交换次序会多出一个负号，即 XZ=−ZX。如果两个多比特检查重叠在两个位置，就出现两个负号，相乘恢复正号。这里每一对 X、Z 检查都重叠零次或两次，所以交换测量次序不会互相破坏答案。这叫对易（commutation）。我们把所有八项独立检查都为 +1 的状态作为编码空间，检查本身称为稳定子（stabilizer）。On a single qubit, exchanging X and Z introduces a minus sign: XZ=−ZX. If two multi-qubit checks overlap at two positions, the two minus signs cancel. Every X–Z pair here overlaps at zero or two positions, so the measurements do not disrupt one another’s answers. This is commutation. The code space consists of states with +1 for all eight independent checks; the checks are stabilizers.

编码空间维数 = 2⁹ / 2⁸ = 2　→　|ψ̄〉 = α|0̄〉 + β|1̄〉dim(code) = 2⁹ / 2⁸ = 2　→　|ψ̄〉 = α|0̄〉 + β|1̄〉

每项独立二值约束把允许空间的维数减半。九个物理比特留下二维空间，恰好容纳第一章的一对振幅。上横线读作“bar”，表示逻辑态或逻辑操作；它不表示九份相同的状态。Each independent binary constraint halves the allowed dimension. Nine physical qubits leave a two-dimensional space, exactly enough for the amplitudes from Chapter 1. Read an overbar as “bar”: it marks a logical state or operation, not nine copies of the same state.

这两种逻辑基态怎样建立？先把九个数据比特都准备为 |0〉，所有 Z 检查和 Z̄ 都为 +1；再测 X 检查，并用已知 Z 型修正或记录处理它们的随机符号，就得到 |0̄〉。把 X、Z 互换，同理可准备 |+̄〉。终端读出 Z̄ 时可以逐点测量 Z，再结合检查关系译码某条代表行的奇偶；这会读出逻辑信息，通常也结束该逻辑态的保存，与平时只读稳定子不同。To prepare |0̄〉, initialize all nine data qubits in |0〉: every Z check and Z̄ then has sign +1. Measure the X checks and handle their random signs with known Z corrections or a corresponding record. Exchanging X and Z prepares |+̄〉 in the same way. For terminal Z̄ readout, individual Z measurements can be combined with check constraints to decode a representative row’s parity. This reads logical information and generally ends storage of that logical state, unlike ordinary stabilizer measurements.

先点 q₀ 加一个 X，观察哪些 Z 检查变号，再选择“整列 X”。单个错误只要与某项检查奇数次反对易，就会让该检查从 +1 变成 −1。把八个符号排成表，就是综合征（syndrome）。但整列 X 可以与每项检查对易；它保留编码约束，同时改变被编码的信息。First add X at q₀ and inspect the Z checks that change sign; then select “full X column.” An error flips a check whenever it anticommutes with that check at an odd number of positions. The ordered list of eight signs is the syndrome. A full X column commutes with every check: it preserves the encoding constraints while acting on the encoded information.

无声操作，何时算错误？When is a silent operation an error?

检查只认证编码约束。要区分修复与逻辑失败，还必须知道操作相对于计算意图的作用。Checks certify the encoding constraints. Recovery and logical failure depend on the operation relative to the intended computation.

上一章的 X̄ 可以把 |0̄〉换成 |1̄〉；如果程序要求这一步，它就是逻辑 X 门。如果环境悄悄施加同一条串，它就是逻辑错误。检查不会告诉我们“这一步是否被授权”，只会报告约束是否被破坏。因此有意操作必须进入控制记录，未知错误则需要根据综合征推断。这里的有意 Pauli 串作为整体作用来理解；若把单点操作拆到不同纠错轮之间，途中产生的检查变化也必须计入时序与标架。The X̄ from Chapter 2 can swap |0̄〉 and |1̄〉. If the program requests this action, it is a logical X gate. If the environment applies the same string unnoticed, it is a logical error. Checks do not report whether the operation was intended; they report broken constraints. Intended actions must therefore enter the control record, while unknown errors must be inferred from the syndrome. An intended Pauli string here is understood as a complete operation; if its single-qubit factors span separate correction rounds, intermediate check changes must also enter the schedule and frame.

看一个足够小、能算到底的反例。X₀ 和 X₃X₆ 只相差 X̄，所以给出完全相同的综合征。译码器（decoder）只看到这个综合征，必须选择修复 R。若真错误是 E=X₃X₆，而选择 R=X₀，所有警报会消失，却留下 RE=X̄。下面在你作出选择前隐藏真实错误，选择后才揭示残留。Consider a counterexample small enough to calculate completely. X₀ and X₃X₆ differ by X̄ and therefore have identical syndromes. A decoder sees only that syndrome and must choose a recovery R. If the actual error is E=X₃X₆ but it chooses R=X₀, every alarm disappears, yet RE=X̄ remains. The exercise hides the true error until you choose, then reveals the residual.

两种确定案例轮流出现，分别展示成功和可能的逻辑失败；这不是按某个噪声分布抽样。Two deterministic cases alternate to demonstrate recovery and possible logical failure; they are not samples from a noise distribution.

sⱼ(E) = x·zⱼ + z·xⱼ (mod 2),　修复成功 ⇔ RE ∈ Ssⱼ(E) = x·zⱼ + z·xⱼ (mod 2),　recovery succeeds ⇔ RE ∈ S

把每个 Pauli 写成两串 0/1：x 标记 X 分量，z 标记 Z 分量；两者都是 1 时是 Y，忽略整体相位。xⱼ、zⱼ 是第 j 项检查对应的两串 0/1。点积计算重叠数，mod 2 只保留奇偶；综合征位 1 表示检查符号 −1。S 是全部稳定子乘积的集合。在本模型里，修复成功要求残留属于 S，而不仅是 s(RE)=0。Write a Pauli as two binary strings: x marks its X components and z its Z components; both one means Y, up to global phase. xⱼ and zⱼ are the two binary strings for check j. A dot product counts overlaps, and mod 2 keeps only parity. Syndrome bit one means check sign −1. S is the set of stabilizer products. Recovery in this model requires the residual to belong to S, not merely s(RE)=0.

若各数据比特独立以小概率发生 X 错误，在这两个具体候选中，单点错误比指定的双点错误更可能，因此最小重量规则会选 X₀。这仍然不是必然正确的证明；真正按逻辑类做最大似然译码，要汇总同一类里所有候选的概率。这里的两个回合是反例展示，不是采样得出的失败率。If each data qubit independently suffers X with a small probability, the single error is more likely than the specified pair, so a minimum-weight rule selects X₀ among these two candidates. This is not a guarantee. Maximum-likelihood decoding by logical class sums the probabilities of every candidate in each class. The two rounds here demonstrate a counterexample; they do not estimate a failure rate.

有意的 Pauli 操作还可以延后执行。软件保存一个 Pauli frame（Pauli 标架），说明实际态与约定的参考态相差哪个 Pauli。比如标架含 X̄，未来的 Z̄ 读数就要翻号，因为 X̄ 与 Z̄ 反对易；X̄ 读数则不变。软件没有神奇地消除噪声，它是在正确推断或已知操作的前提下，持续修改后续门和测量的解释。这个记录方式很快会用来处理测量操作的随机分支。An intended Pauli action can also be deferred. Software stores a Pauli frame specifying the Pauli difference between the physical state and an agreed reference state. If the frame includes X̄, a future Z̄ readout changes sign because X̄ anticommutes with Z̄; an X̄ readout does not. Software does not remove noise by itself: given a correct inference or a known action, it updates the interpretation of later gates and measurements. We will use this record to handle random branches of a measurement-based operation.

警报清零只说明回到了编码空间；残留逻辑作用才决定是否修复成功。A cleared syndrome means a return to the code space; the residual logical action determines recovery success.

只问两个码块是否一致Ask only for joint parity

记录 Pauli 串能实现单比特逻辑操作。耦合两个逻辑比特，还需要一种不会分别读出它们的测量。Pauli strings act on individual logical qubits. Coupling two logical qubits requires a measurement that does not read each one separately.

把两个已经编码的逻辑比特叫 A、B。|00〉、|01〉、|10〉、|11〉是它们的四个逻辑基态。|ab〉=|a〉⊗|b〉中的 ⊗ 读作 tensor product（张量积），表示组合两个系统；两个单比特态相乘时，各基态的振幅也相乘。此后状态实验中的横线省略，但所有操作都发生在逻辑层。Z_AZ_B 的读数只区分“两位相同”与“两位不同”：00、11 的符号为 +1，01、10 的符号为 −1。这叫联合奇偶测量（joint parity measurement）。它没有透露哪一位是 0。Call two encoded logical qubits A and B. Their four logical basis states are |00〉, |01〉, |10〉 and |11〉. In |ab〉=|a〉⊗|b〉, ⊗ is the tensor product, combining two systems; the amplitudes of a product state multiply. We omit overbars in state experiments from here onward, but every operation is logical. Measuring Z_AZ_B distinguishes equal bits from different bits: 00 and 11 have sign +1; 01 and 10 have sign −1. This is a joint parity measurement. It does not reveal which individual bit is zero.

先各自准备 |+〉。两比特的联合态是四个基态各占振幅 1/2。如果 ZZ 返回 +1，只去掉 01、10 两项，保留 00 和 11 之间的相干叠加。这个结果不能写成两个单比特态的乘积，因此是纠缠态。若分别测量 Z_A、Z_B 再把结果相乘，最终会知道单个基态，并破坏这份相干性；两种测量的信息量不同。Prepare each qubit in |+〉. The joint state has amplitude 1/2 on each of the four basis states. A +1 result for ZZ removes 01 and 10 while preserving the coherent superposition of 00 and 11. The result cannot be written as a product of two single-qubit states: it is entangled. To suppress that risk, seam checks are repeated and decoded together with neighboring check histories. A sign change between rounds may come from a data error or a faulty readout; the last frame alone cannot distinguish them. Time therefore becomes part of the decoding graph. A majority vote on seam outcomes is not a substitute for full spacetime decoding.

距离 d 也不只约束静止码块。合并、分裂、移动边界期间，最短的危险故障路径也必须受到控制，包括测量辅助比特把一个故障传播到多个数据比特的情形。常见 surface-code surgery 设计让联合测量持续约 d 轮纠错；具体协议和噪声模型决定怎样排门、怎样译码，以及是否采用不同轮数。页面的十八点对易检查没有证明这些时序条件。Distance d is not only a property of a stationary patch. During merges, splits and boundary motion, dangerous fault paths must remain controlled, including faults on a measurement ancilla propagating to several data qubits. Common surface-code surgery designs use roughly d rounds of error correction for a joint measurement. The protocol and noise model determine gate order, decoding and any alternative round count. The page’s eighteen-qubit commutation check does not prove these scheduling conditions.

读到一个错误率或阈值数字，先问它属于哪一层：只在数据上加 Pauli 且检查完美，是 data-only 模型；再给抽象检查结果加噪声，是 phenomenological 模型；把故障放进准备、门、空闲和读出过程，是 circuit-level 模型。相关噪声、偏置噪声、泄漏到计算空间之外以及硬件漂移，还需要相应的额外假设。某层模型下的抑制结果，不能直接用作另一层的性能承诺。When reading an error rate or threshold, first identify the modeling layer. Pauli errors on data with perfect checks define a data-only model. Noise added to abstract check outcomes gives a phenomenological model. Faults in preparation, gates, idling and readout define a circuit-level model. Correlations, bias, leakage outside the computational space and hardware drift require additional assumptions. Suppression demonstrated in one layer does not promise performance in another.
