一句话总结In one sentence

qLDPC 码回答一个问题：在「每条检查只碰几个比特」的约束下，逻辑比特数 k 和码距 d 能不能都随 n 线性增长？表面码不能（第 1 章）；两条经典码的超图积做到 k ∝ n、d ∝ √n（第 2、3 章）；把 0/1 换成多项式的提升积给出 BB 那样的高码率有限块（第 4 章）；换成非阿贝尔群与扩展图，量子 Tanner 码把 k 与 d 都推到 ∝ n（第 5 章）。译码它们一般不能直接套匹配（一个错误会点亮不止两条检查），常用 BP-OSD，扩展码这类构造还可用局部翻转（第 6 章）。

qLDPC codes answer one question: under the constraint “each check touches only a few qubits”, can the number of logical qubits k and the distance d both grow linearly with n? The surface code cannot (Ch. 1); the hypergraph product of two classical codes reaches k ∝ n, d ∝ √n (Ch. 2–3); the lifted product, with polynomials in place of 0/1, gives high-rate finite blocks such as BB (Ch. 4); with non-abelian groups and expander graphs, quantum Tanner codes push both k and d to ∝ n (Ch. 5). Decoding them cannot in general be reduced to matching (one error lights more than two checks); the usual tools are BP-OSD and, for constructions such as expander codes, local flips (Ch. 6).

第 1 章Chapter 1

什么叫 LDPC，什么叫「好」What “LDPC” means, and what “good” means

两个形容词定义了这个领域。「低密度」说的是检查矩阵稀疏——每条检查、每个比特只牵涉常数个对象；「好」说的是编码率和相对距离都不随 n 衰减。经典世界六十年前就两者兼得；量子世界的同一个问题成了二十年的猜想，也是常数开销容错的钥匙。 Two adjectives define the field. “Low-density” means the check matrix is sparse — each check and each qubit involves a constant number of objects; “good” means rate and relative distance both survive as n grows. The classical world had both sixty years ago; the same question for quantum codes became a twenty-year conjecture, and the key to constant-overhead fault tolerance.

1.1经典 LDPC：稀疏矩阵，稠密信息Classical LDPC: sparse matrix, dense information

一条经典线性码由一个 m × n 的 0/1 矩阵 H 定义：合法的码字 c 是所有满足 Hc = 0（mod 2）的比特串——每一行是一条奇偶检查（《从高考数学到 Toric Code》2.3 节的两盏灯就是 H 的两行）。它的参数写成单方括号 [n,k,d]：k = n − rank H 个信息比特，d = 最轻的非零码字的重量。例：三位重复码的 H 有两行 (1, 1, 0) 与 (0, 1, 1)，秩 2，所以 k = 3 − 2 = 1；满足 Hc = 0 的非零串只有 111，重量 3，于是它是 [3,1,3]。「低密度LDPC = low-density parity-check：H 的每行每列的 1 都不超过一个与 n 无关的常数。Gallager 1962 年引入。」指 H 的每行每列 1 的个数有界。Gallager 在 1962 年就证明：随机的稀疏 H 几乎总是给出好码good code：一族码 k ≥ R·n 且 d ≥ δ·n，R、δ 为正常数。编码率 k/n 与相对距离 d/n 都不随 n 趋于零。——k 与 d 都与 n 成比例——而且稀疏性让它可以用局域的消息传递（BP）译码。

A classical linear code is defined by an m × n 0/1 matrix H: the valid codewords c are all bit strings with Hc = 0 (mod 2) — every row a parity check (the two lamps of “From High-School Math to the Toric Code”, §2.3 are two rows of H). Its parameters are written with single brackets [n,k,d]: k = n − rank H information bits, d = the weight of the lightest nonzero codeword. Example: the three-bit repetition code has H with rows (1, 1, 0) and (0, 1, 1), rank 2, so k = 3 − 2 = 1; the only nonzero string with Hc = 0 is 111, of weight 3, so it is a [3,1,3] code. “Low-densityLDPC = low-density parity-check: every row and column of H has at most a constant number of 1s, independent of n. 1.2量子版：两条稀疏矩阵，加一个对易条件The quantum version: two sparse matrices plus one commutation condition

量子 CSS 码需要两个矩阵 HX、HZ，都要稀疏，还要满足 HXHZᵀ = 0。就是这个额外条件让问题变难：随便取两个随机稀疏矩阵，它们几乎肯定不对易。1996 年 Calderbank 与 Shor 证明随机 CSS 码可以是好码，但那些检查是稠密的（每条碰约一半比特），无法测量。稀疏且好——「好的 qLDPC 码是否存在」——成了公开猜想，从 Tillich 与 Zémor 2009 年的 d ∝ √n（第 2 章）开始步步逼近，直到 2021 年 Panteleev 与 Kalachev、2022 年 Leverrier 与 Zémor 分别给出渐近好的构造（第 5 章）。

A quantum CSS code needs two matrices HX, HZ, both sparse, and also HXHZᵀ = 0. That extra condition is what makes the problem hard: two random sparse matrices almost never commute. In 1996 Calderbank and Shor showed that random CSS codes can be good, but their checks are dense (each touching about half the qubits) and cannot be measured. Sparse and good — “do good qLDPC codes exist?” — became an open conjecture, approached step by step from Tillich and Zémor's d ∝ √n in 2009 (Ch. 2) until Panteleev and Kalachev in 2021 and Leverrier and Zémor in 2022 gave asymptotically good constructions (Ch. 5).

1.3为什么值得：常数开销Why it matters: constant overhead

用表面码保护 K 个逻辑比特到目标错误率，需要约 K·d² 个物理比特，而 d 要随电路规模的对数增长：一个有 N 步的电路，要让每个逻辑比特每步的失败率 A(p/pth)⌈d/2⌉ 小于约 1/N，d 就得正比于 ln N。于是物理比特数是逻辑比特数的「多项式对数」倍（ln N 的某个幂次），规模越大越亏。Gottesman 在 2013 年证明：如果有一族合适的码率恒定的 qLDPC 码，其带噪症状纠错满足阈值与残余错误条件，那么在他的计算与连接假设下，容错量子计算的开销overhead：物理比特数与逻辑比特数之比。表面码约为 d² 并随规模的对数增长；好的 qLDPC 码让它趋于常数。可以是一个常数——每个逻辑比特只需固定个数的物理比特，与电路多大无关。Fawzi、Grospellier 与 Leverrier 2018 年用量子扩展码把这条路走通到线性时间译码。这就是「好」的全部意义：它把「一百万个物理比特能做多大的计算」从对数级差距变成常数级。

Protecting K logical qubits to a target error rate with surface codes needs about K·d² physical qubits, and d must grow with the logarithm of the circuit size: for a circuit of N steps, keeping each logical qubit’s per-step failure A(p/pth)⌈d/2⌉ below about 1/N needs d proportional to ln N. The physical count is then a “polylogarithmic” multiple of the logical count (some power of ln N), worse the larger the computation. That is the whole point of “good”: it turns “how large a computation can a million physical qubits run” from a logarithmic gap into a constant one.

1.4代价：非局域是必然的The price: nonlocality is unavoidable

《BB 码 · 高中数学版》1.1 节的 BPT 界（k·d² ≤ c·n）已经排除了二维局域的好码：好码要求 k·d² ∝ n³，远超 c·n。

The BPT bound of “BB Codes · High-School Edition”, §1.1 (k·d² ≤ c·n) already rules out good 2D local codes: good codes need k·d² ∝ n³, far above c·n.

Delfosse、Beverland 与 Tremblay 2021 年进一步问：码本身非局域，但只用二维局域电路去测量它的检查，要付出什么？他们的深度下界只管一类码，叫局部扩展码把「共用一条检查的两个比特」连一条边，得到比特之间的图（收缩 Tanner 图）。若这张图里任何不超过总数固定比例的比特集合，向外连出的边数都至少是集合大小的固定倍数，这族码就是局部扩展的。：把共用一条检查的两个比特连一条边，要求这张图里任何「不太大」（不超过全体的某个固定比例）的比特集合向外连出的边数，至少是集合大小的固定倍数。他们的结论（论文推论 1）对电路也有限定，只针对二维局域 Clifford 症状提取电路。比特排在一块共 N 个比特（数据比特与辅助比特都算）的二维方形网格上；每一步只能做单比特或两比特的 Clifford 门（CNOT、Hadamard 这类把 Pauli 算符变成 Pauli 算符的门）和单比特或两比特的 Pauli 测量，而且只作用在相距不超过固定距离的比特上；允许按先前测量结果的奇偶性做 Pauli 修正（经典通信不受距离限制）。对局部扩展码的这类电路，深度至少是 n/√N 的常数倍（n 是码长）。含非 Clifford 门的电路不在这个结论里。

Delfosse, Beverland and Tremblay asked in 2021 what it costs to measure the checks of a nonlocal code with a 2D local circuit only. Their depth bound covers one class of codes, local-expander codesJoin two qubits by an edge when they share a check; this gives a graph on the qubits (the contracted Tanner graph). A code family is local-expander if in this graph every set of qubits up to a fixed fraction of all of them has at least a fixed multiple of its own size in outgoing edges.: join two qubits by an edge when they share a check, and demand that every “not too large” set of qubits (up to a fixed fraction of all of them) has outgoing edges at least a fixed multiple of its size. Their result (Corollary 1 of the paper) also restricts the circuits, to 2D local Clifford syndrome-extraction circuits. The qubits sit on a 2D square grid of N qubits in total (data and ancilla qubits both counted); each step may apply only one- and two-qubit Clifford gates (gates such as CNOT and Hadamard that map Pauli operators to Pauli operators) and one- and two-qubit Pauli measurements, and only on qubits within a fixed distance of each other; Pauli corrections conditioned on the parity of earlier outcomes are allowed (classical communication has no range limit). For such circuits on local-expander codes the depth is at least a constant times n/√N (n the code length). Circuits with non-Clifford gates are outside this result.

表面码不在此列：一块 L × L 的比特区域向外的边只与周长成正比（∝ L），与区域大小 L² 之比 ∝ 1/L，趋于零；它的症状电路深度本来就是常数，论文正是拿它说明「局部扩展」这个假设去不掉。深度要不要增长，还取决于 N 怎么随 n 变：辅助比特与码长同量级（N ∝ n）时，深度至少 ∝ n/√n = √n；他们也给出了用 N ∝ n² 个比特、深度有界的电路，与下界不矛盾（此时 n/√N 是常数）。他们的电路级模拟用一族码率 1/25 的超图积码，码与译码器都不变：全连接时阈值约 10⁻³，二维局域电路在错误率低到 10⁻⁶ 时仍看不到阈值。所以想用扩展型的 qLDPC 码又不付出深度或比特数的代价，就需要长程连接：两层耦合（BB）、可移动原子、光子互联，或者分层地用表面码模拟（第 5 章的 hierarchical 码）。下图把「性价比」k·d²/n 放在一起比：

为什么 k 是 k₁k₂ + k₁ᵀk₂ᵀ？（用秩算一遍）Why is k = k₁k₂ + k₁ᵀk₂ᵀ? (a rank computation)

记 r₁ = rank H₁、r₂ = rank H₂。克罗内克积的秩是秩的乘积，而 [H₁ ⊗ I | I ⊗ H₂ᵀ] 的秩是 r₁n₂ + m₁r₂ − r₁r₂。理由：一个矩阵 [P | Q] 的秩 = rank P + rank Q − （P 的列空间与 Q 的列空间交集的维数）。这里两块的列向量都住在 m₁ × n₂ 的「表格」空间里：H₁ ⊗ I 的列空间是「每一列都属于 H₁ 的列空间 U」的表格（维数 r₁n₂），I ⊗ H₂ᵀ 的列空间是「每一行都属于 H₂ᵀ 的列空间 V」的表格（维数 m₁r₂）。选一组坐标，使 U 恰好是前 r₁ 行、V 恰好是前 r₂ 列，两者的交集就是「只在左上角 r₁ × r₂ 那块里非零」的表格，维数 r₁r₂，要扣掉一次。同理 rank HZ = n₁r₂ + r₁m₂ − r₁r₂。于是 k = n₁n₂ + m₁m₂ − (r₁n₂ + m₁r₂ − r₁r₂) − (n₁r₂ + r₁m₂ − r₁r₂) = (n₁ − r₁)(n₂ − r₂) + (m₁ − r₁)(m₂ − r₂) = k₁k₂ + k₁ᵀk₂ᵀ。最后一步是把四个乘积展开再合并——纯粹的多项式恒等式。「Künneth」是拓扑学里同一条公式的名字：积空间的同调等于因子同调的乘积之和。

Write r₁ = rank H₁, r₂ = rank H₂. The rank of a Kronecker product is the product of ranks, and the rank of [H₁ ⊗ I | I ⊗ H₂ᵀ] is r₁n₂ + m₁r₂ − r₁r₂. Reason: the rank of a matrix [P | Q] is rank P + rank Q − (the dimension of the intersection of their column spaces). Here both blocks’ column vectors live in the space of m₁ × n₂ “tables”: the column space of H₁ ⊗ I consists of tables whose every column lies in the column space U of H₁ (dimension r₁n₂), that of I ⊗ H₂ᵀ of tables whose every row lies in the column space V of H₂ᵀ (dimension m₁r₂). Choose coordinates in which U is exactly the first r₁ rows and V exactly the first r₂ columns; the intersection is then the tables that are nonzero only in the top-left r₁ × r₂ corner, of dimension r₁r₂, which must be subtracted once. Likewise rank HZ = n₁r₂ + r₁m₂ − r₁r₂. Hence k = n₁n₂ + m₁m₂ − (r₁n₂ + m₁r₂ − r₁r₂) − (n₁r₂ + r₁m₂ − r₁r₂) = (n₁ − r₁)(n₂ − r₂) + (m₁ − r₁)(m₂ − r₂) = k₁k₂ + k₁ᵀk₂ᵀ. The last step expands four products and regroups — a pure polynomial identity. “Künneth” is the name of the same formula in topology: the homology of a product space is a sum of products of the factors' homologies.

2.4d：因子距离的最小值，于是 d ∝ √nd: the minimum of the factor distances, hence d ∝ √n

先补一个定义。转置码（2.3 节）也有距离：dᵀ 是满足 Hᵀv = 0 的非零向量 v 里最轻的重量；kᵀ = 0 时这样的 v 不存在，dᵀ 就不参与比较。例：K₅ 的关联矩阵（第 3 章）里，Hᵀv = 0 要求每条边的两个端点取值相同，只有「五个顶点全选」一个非零解，所以 dᵀ = 5。然后是距离公式。Tillich 与 Zémor 证明：k₁k₂ 那个扇区里最轻的逻辑算符重 min(d₁, d₂)，k₁ᵀk₂ᵀ 那个扇区里最轻的重 min(d₁ᵀ, d₂ᵀ)，d 是非空扇区里的最小值——两个扇区都非空时 d = min(d₁, d₂, d₁ᵀ, d₂ᵀ)；某个扇区为空（比如 kᵀ = 0）时，它的两个距离不参与。

One definition first. A transpose code (§2.3) has a distance too: dᵀ is the smallest weight of a nonzero vector v with Hᵀv = 0; when kᵀ = 0 there is no such v and dᵀ takes no part in the comparison. Example: for the incidence matrix of K₅ (Chapter 3), Hᵀv = 0 demands that the two ends of every edge carry the same value, so the only nonzero solution is “all five vertices”, and dᵀ = 5. Now the distance formula. Tillich and Zémor proved that the lightest logical operator in the k₁k₂ sector has weight min(d₁, d₂) and the lightest in the k₁ᵀk₂ᵀ sector has weight min(d₁ᵀ, d₂ᵀ); d is the minimum over the non-empty sectors — d = min(d₁, d₂, d₁ᵀ, d₂ᵀ) when both are non-empty, while an empty sector (e.g. kᵀ = 0) contributes nothing.

上界一半可以直接造出来。把 H₁ 的一个最轻码字 c（重 d₁）铺在「比特 × 比特」块的第 j 列上：第 i 行第 j 列的格放 Z 当且仅当 cᵢ = 1，记作 Z列。它与每条 X 检查都对易：X 检查在这一列上碰到的正是 H₁ 的一行，与 c 重叠偶数次。它还不是检查的乘积，理由是能找到一个与所有检查对易、却与它反对易的算符。取 H₂ 的一个码字 u（这要求 k₂ ≥ 1），把列 j 选在 uⱼ = 1 的位置，再挑一个 cᵢ = 1 的行 i，把 u 铺在第 i 行上放 X，记作 X行。X行 与每条 Z 检查对易（Z 检查在这一行上碰到的是 H₂ 的一行，与 u 重叠偶数次）；X行 与 Z列 只在格 (i, j) 上重叠，一次，所以反对易。任何检查的乘积都与 X行 对易，Z列 却不，所以 Z列 不是检查的乘积，是一个重 d₁ 的逻辑算符。把两个因子的角色交换，同样把 H₂ 的一个最轻码字沿一行铺开放 X，得到重 d₂ 的逻辑算符（这次要求 k₁ ≥ 1）。例：两个因子都取三位重复码 [[1,1,0],[0,1,1]]，得到表面码 [[13,1,3]]；c = u = 111，Z列 是第 1 列的三个 Z，X行 是第 1 行的三个 X，两者只在左上角的格相交。所以 d 不会超过这几个数。反过来「任何逻辑算符至少这么重」是 Tillich 与 Zémor 定理的另一半，证明要细致地拆分一个逻辑算符在两块上的部分，本页不展开（见延伸阅读里他们的原文）。

The upper-bound half can be built directly. Lay a lightest codeword c of H₁ (weight d₁) along column j of the bit × bit block: the cell in row i, column j carries Z exactly when cᵢ = 1; call this Zcol. It commutes with every X check: on that column an X check meets exactly one row of H₁, which overlaps c an even number of times. It is also not a product of checks, because there is an operator that commutes with every check but anticommutes with it. Take a codeword u of H₂ (this needs k₂ ≥ 1), choose the column j where uⱼ = 1, pick a row i with cᵢ = 1, and lay u along row i carrying X; call this Xrow. Xrow commutes with every Z check (on that row a Z check meets one row of H₂, which overlaps u an even number of times), and Xrow and Zcol overlap only in cell (i, j), once, so they anticommute. Every product of checks commutes with Xrow and Zcol does not, so Zcol is not a product of checks: it is a logical operator of weight d₁. Swapping the roles of the two factors, a lightest codeword of H₂ laid along a row with X is a logical of weight d₂ (this time k₁ ≥ 1 is needed). Example: with both factors the three-bit repetition code [[1,1,0],[0,1,1]] you get the surface code [[13,1,3]]; c = u = 111, Zcol is the three Z's of column 1, Xrow the three X's of row 1, and they meet only in the top-left cell. So d cannot exceed these numbers. The converse, “every logical is at least this heavy”, is the other half of the Tillich–Zémor theorem; its proof has to split a logical carefully into its parts on the two blocks, and this page does not reproduce it (see their paper in the reading list).

4.4渐近：阿贝尔的坏，非阿贝尔的好Asymptotics: abelian bad, non-abelian good

《BB 码 · 高中数学版》6.6 节说过，固定项数、无限放大环面的 BB 码渐近是坏的（Postema 与 Kokkelmans 2025）：可交换的 Zl × Zm 太「规则」，低重量的逻辑算符总能沿某个方向铺开。突破来自换环：Panteleev 与 Kalachev 2021 年用非阿贝尔群的群代数做提升——让一个因子从左乘、另一个从右乘，左乘与右乘总是对易，所以 HXHZᵀ = 0 不靠交换律也成立——底矩阵取自扩展图上的经典 Tanner 码，证明所得的量子码族是渐近好的——k ∝ n 且 d ∝ n——从而解决了 qLDPC 猜想；同一篇论文顺带解决了经典编码论里「局部可测试码」的一个多年猜想。非阿贝尔性让平移不再两两对易，逻辑算符找不到可以「铺开」的方向；扩展性则保证：一个错误去掉稳定子成分之后若仍然较轻，它惊动的检查数与这个「约化重量」成比例（稳定子本身不亮任何检查，所以必须先去掉，见 3.4 节）。这两个成分下一章会以另一种形式再出现。

“BB Codes · High-School Edition”, §6.6 noted that BB codes with a fixed number of terms on an ever-larger torus are asymptotically bad (Postema and Kokkelmans 2025): the commutative Zl × Zm is too “regular”, and low-weight logicals can always spread along some direction. The breakthrough came from changing the ring: in 2021 Panteleev and Kalachev lifted over the group algebra of a non-abelian group — one factor acting by left multiplication, the other by right multiplication, which always commute, so HXHZᵀ = 0 survives without commutativity — with base matrices taken from classical Tanner codes on expander graphs, and proved the resulting quantum family asymptotically good — k ∝ n and d ∝ n — settling the qLDPC conjecture; the same paper also settled a long-standing conjecture about locally testable codes in classical coding theory. Non-commutativity leaves logicals no direction to “spread” along; expansion guarantees that an error which is still light after its stabilizer part is removed lights a number of checks proportional to this “reduced weight” (a stabilizer itself lights no check, which is why it must be removed first; see §3.4). Both ingredients return in another guise in the next chapter.

补课 · 群、阿贝尔与非阿贝尔，以及为什么「左乘」和「右乘」互不干扰Catch-up · Groups, abelian and non-abelian, and why “left” and “right” multiplication never interfere

群是一组「动作」：任意两个动作接连做，结果仍是这组里的一个动作；每个动作都能撤销；还有一个「什么都不做」。BB 码里的平移 xiyj 就是一个群：先向右走 1 步再向上走 1 步，和先向上再向右，落点一样，x·y = y·x。先后顺序无关的群叫阿贝尔群（交换群）。

A group is a set of “moves”: doing two moves in a row gives another move of the set; every move can be undone; and there is a “do nothing”. The shifts xiyj of the BB code form a group: one step right then one step up lands where one step up then one step right does, x·y = y·x. A group in which order never matters is abelian (commutative).

非阿贝尔的最小例子：三个杯子排成一行，动作是重新排列它们（共 6 种）。设 s =「交换第 1、2 个位置的杯子」，r =「每个杯子往右挪一格，最右边的挪到最左」。从 ABC 出发：先 s 后 r：ABC → BAC → CBA；先 r 后 s：ABC → CAB → ACB。两个结果不同，所以顺序要紧，这个群不是阿贝尔的。

The smallest non-abelian example: three cups in a row, the moves being their rearrangements (6 of them). Let s = “swap the cups in positions 1 and 2” and r = “every cup moves one place right, the rightmost to the far left”. Starting from ABC: s then r gives ABC → BAC → CBA; r then s gives ABC → CAB → ACB. Different results, so order matters and this group is not abelian.

群代数 GF(2)[G] 把 BB 教程里的多项式推广到任意群：一个元素就是「若干个群元素的和」，系数只有 0 和 1，乘法把两个和逐项相乘（群元素按群的规则合成）。BB 的环 R 就是 G = 环面平移群时的特例。

The group algebra GF(2)[G] extends the BB tutorial’s polynomials to any group: an element is “a sum of some group elements” with coefficients 0 or 1, and multiplication multiplies two sums term by term (group elements composing by the group’s rule). The BB ring R is the special case where G is the group of torus shifts.

左乘与右乘为什么互不干扰。非阿贝尔提升积让一个因子的元素 a 从左边乘上去（g ↦ a·g），另一个因子的元素 b 从右边乘（g ↦ g·b）。先左乘再右乘得到 (a·g)·b，先右乘再左乘得到 a·(g·b)。动作的合成满足结合律：三个动作按固定的顺序做下去，先把哪两个「合成一个」都一样，所以两者都是 a·g·b。于是 2.2 节那两个混合项仍然相同、仍然相加为零，HXHZᵀ = 0 照样成立，哪怕 a·b ≠ b·a。要紧的是 a 永远在左、b 永远在右，两者从不需要交换位置；交换律只在「a、b 要换位」时才用得上。

Why left and right multiplication never interfere. The non-abelian lifted product lets an element a of one factor multiply from the left (g ↦ a·g) and an element b of the other from the right (g ↦ g·b). Left first, then right gives (a·g)·b; right first, then left gives a·(g·b). Composition of moves is associative: doing three moves in a fixed order, it does not matter which two you merge first, so both are a·g·b. The two mixed terms of §2.2 are therefore still equal and still add to zero, and HXHZᵀ = 0 holds even though a·b ≠ b·a. What matters is that a always stays on the left and b on the right; they never have to trade places, and commutativity would only be needed if they did.

5.1量子 Tanner 码：[[392,48,?]]Quantum Tanner codes: [[392,48,?]]

经典的 Tanner 码Tanner code：在一张图的每个顶点上放一条小的局部码，要求与该顶点相连的比特满足这条局部码。图是扩展图时，得到好的经典 LDPC 码（Sipser–Spielman）。把一条小码「贴」在一张大图的每个顶点上：比特住在边上，每个顶点要求它周围的比特构成局部码的一个码字。Leverrier 与 Zémor 2022 年的量子版把舞台换成 Dinur 等人的左右 Cayley 复形。它由一个群 G（群见 4.4 节的补课）和两小组群元素 A、B 造出，分三步。① 顶点有两类，各是 G 的一份拷贝：V₀ 里的顶点记作 (h, 0)，V₁ 里的记作 (h, 1)，h 取遍 G，共 2|G| 个顶点。② 边只连不同类的顶点：(h, 0) 与 (ah, 1) 之间连一条 A 边（a ∈ A，从左边乘 a），(h, 0) 与 (hb, 1) 之间连一条 B 边（b ∈ B，从右边乘 b）。③ 正方形是四个顶点 (g, 0)、(ag, 1)、(gb, 1)、(agb, 0)，两条 A 边、两条 B 边围成一圈（下图）。

A classical Tanner codePlace a small local code at every vertex of a graph and require the bits attached to that vertex to satisfy it. When the graph is an expander this gives good classical LDPC codes (Sipser–Spielman). “glues” a small code onto every vertex of a large graph: bits live on edges, and each vertex demands that its surrounding bits form a codeword of the local code. Leverrier and Zémor's 2022 quantum version changes the stage to the left-right Cayley complex of Dinur et al. It is built from a group G (groups: see the catch-up in §4.4) and two small sets A and B of group elements, in three steps. ① There are two classes of vertices, each a copy of G: the vertices of V₀ are written (h, 0), those of V₁ are written (h, 1), with h ranging over G, 2|G| vertices in all. ② Edges join only vertices of different classes: (h, 0) and (ah, 1) are joined by an A-edge (a ∈ A, multiplying by a on the left), (h, 0) and (hb, 1) by a B-edge (b ∈ B, multiplying by b on the right). ③ A square is the four vertices (g, 0), (ag, 1), (gb, 1), (agb, 0), bounded by two A-edges and two B-edges (figure below).

对 A、B 的要求：各自对取逆封闭（a 在 A 里，a⁻¹ 也在），大小同为 Δ；两张 Cayley 图（h 连 ah 的图、h 连 hb 的图）都是扩展图，而且是谱意义下接近最优的那种（这也保证了 A、B 各自反复相乘能走遍整个群，即它们是生成元）；再加一个「完全不共轭」条件：对所有 g、a、b，ag ≠ gb。最后这条保证一个正方形的四个顶点互不相同：两个 V₁ 顶点 ag 与 gb 不同就是这条本身；两个 V₀ 顶点若相同，agb = g 即 ag = gb⁻¹，而 b⁻¹ 也在 B 里，同样被排除。反例：G 可交换且 a = b⁻¹ 时（比如整数 mod 7 的加法，a = 1、b = −1，这时 agb 就是 g + 1 − 1 = g），「正方形」塌掉。

The conditions on A and B: each is closed under inverses (if a is in A, so is a⁻¹), both have the same size Δ; the two Cayley graphs (h joined to ah, and h joined to hb) are expanders, of the spectrally near-optimal kind (which also makes A and B each generators: repeated products of either reach the whole group); and a “total no-conjugacy” condition: ag ≠ gb for all g, a, b. The last one makes the four vertices of a square distinct: the two V₁ vertices ag and gb differ by the condition itself; the two V₀ vertices coincide only if agb = g, i.e. ag = gb⁻¹, and b⁻¹ is also in B, so that is excluded too. Counterexample: in a commutative G with a = b⁻¹ (say the integers mod 7 under addition, a = 1, b = −1, where agb is g + 1 − 1 = g) the “square” collapses.

量子比特住在正方形上，每个正方形一个。从一个 V₀ 顶点出发，(a, b) 的 Δ² 种选法各给一个正方形，所以每个顶点周围恰好 Δ² 个比特，可以排成一张 Δ × Δ 的表：行标 a、列标 b。每个正方形有两个 V₀ 顶点，被数了两次，所以 n = Δ²|G|/2。检查放在顶点上，由两条小的经典码决定：CA 是长为 Δ 的二元线性码，它的 Δ 个比特位用 A 的元素编号（每个 a ∈ A 一位）；CB 同样长为 Δ，比特位用 B 的元素编号。V₀ 顶点周围那张表上，张量码 CA ⊗ CB（每一列是 CA 的码字、每一行是 CB 的码字）的每个码字给一条 X 检查，作用在码字取 1 的那些比特上；V₁ 顶点上，两个对偶码上标 ⊥ 读「对偶码」：与 C 的每个码字都正交（逐位相乘再求和为 0，mod 2）的全部向量组成的码。的张量积 CA⊥ ⊗ CB⊥（不是张量码本身的对偶）的每个码字给一条 Z 检查。

Qubits live on squares, one per square. From a V₀ vertex, each of the Δ² choices of (a, b) gives one square, so every vertex has exactly Δ² qubits around it, which can be arranged in a Δ × Δ table: rows labelled by a, columns by b. Each square has two V₀ vertices and is counted twice, so n = Δ²|G|/2. Checks sit on vertices and are fixed by two small classical codes: CA is a binary linear code of length Δ whose Δ bit positions are labelled by the elements of A (one position per a ∈ A); CB likewise has length Δ, with positions labelled by B. On the table around a V₀ vertex, every codeword of the tensor code CA ⊗ CB (every column a codeword of CA, every row a codeword of CB) gives an X check acting on the qubits where the codeword is 1; on a V₁ vertex, every codeword of the tensor product of the dual codesSuperscript ⊥, read “dual code”: all vectors orthogonal to every codeword of C (bitwise product summed to 0 mod 2). 5.2同调积：[[25,1,4]]Homological products: [[25,1,4]]

超图积是「两条码相乘」；Bravyi 与 Hastings 2013 年的同调积homological product：把两个 CSS 码各看作一个链复形（《BB 码 · 高中数学版》4.4 节折叠盒里的 R → R² → R），取两个复形的张量积得到新的复形，再读出量子码。因子是随机码时，得到常数码率、线性距离、检查重量约 √n 的码。是「两个量子码相乘」：把两个 CSS 码各写成链复形，取张量积。用两个随机的量子码做因子，他们得到了第一族常数码率、线性距离的量子码，检查重量约 √n——不再是 LDPC，但比此前「检查重量 ∝ n」的随机码前进了一大步，也是通往好 qLDPC 码路上的重要中继。Atlas 有三个小见证：homological_product [[25,1,4]]、square_homological_product [[25,1,4]]、double_homological_product [[33,1,4]]，d 均穷举验证。

A hypergraph product multiplies “two codes”; Bravyi and Hastings' 2013 homological productView two CSS codes as chain complexes (the fold in “BB Codes · High-School Edition”, §4.4: R → R² → R), take the tensor product of the complexes, and read off a quantum code. With random factor codes this gives constant rate, linear distance and check weight about √n. multiplies “two quantum codes”: write each CSS code as a chain complex and take their tensor product. With two random quantum codes as factors they obtained the first family with constant rate and linear distance at check weight about √n — no longer LDPC, but a big step past random codes with check weight ∝ n, and an important waypoint toward good qLDPC codes. 5.3超球积：[[128,2,8]]The hypersphere product: [[128,2,8]]

Hastings 2016 年把环面码搬到高维流形上。在高维环面 Tn 上，若一个关于最小体积曲面的几何猜想成立，就得到检查重量只有对数级、距离达到 n1−ε（ε 任意小）的 CSS 码。在高维球面的积 Sn × Sn 上——这正是 Atlas 条目名字里的「超球积」——则无条件地得到另一族码：检查重量对数级，距离 ∝ √n，并且作为「量子局部可测试码」有逆多对数级的健全性（离码字越远的态，随机抽一条检查抓到它的概率越大）。前者把「用几何造码」推到了接近线性距离的边缘，后者的价值在可测试性而不在距离。Atlas 的 hypersphere_product 见证是 [[128,2,8]]（k = 2 来自两个球面因子各贡献一个逻辑比特；d 为文献声称）。

Hastings in 2016 moved toric codes onto high-dimensional manifolds. On high-dimensional tori Tn, assuming a geometric conjecture about minimal-volume surfaces, this gives CSS codes with only logarithmic check weight and distance n1−ε for arbitrarily small ε. On products of hyperspheres Sn × Sn — the “hypersphere product” in the Atlas entry’s name — it gives, unconditionally, a different family: logarithmic check weight, distance ∝ √n, and an inverse-polylogarithmic soundness as a “quantum locally testable code” (the farther a state is from the code, the likelier a random check catches it). The first took “codes from geometry” to the brink of linear distance; the second’s value lies in testability rather than distance. The Atlas’s hypersphere_product witness is [[128,2,8]] (k = 2 because each sphere factor contributes one logical qubit; d claimed from the literature).

5.4为硬件而生的家族：lacross、SC-qLDPC、分层码Families born for hardware: lacross, SC-qLDPC, hierarchical codes

三个 T1 条目代表三种「让 qLDPC 上机」的思路。lacross（Pecorari 等 2024，[[34,4,?]]）为长程连接的中性原子阵列设计高码率码，把检查的非局域性匹配到原子阵列真实能做的耦合模式上。SC-qLDPC（Hagiwara 与 Imai 2011，[[42,4,?]]）把经典编码论里的「空间耦合」搬到准循环量子码上，让检查沿一条带状结构逐段耦合。hierarchical（Pattison 等 2023，[[35,2,?]]）走另一条路：把一个 qLDPC 码与表面码级联，用局域门模拟非局域检查，以时间换连接——正是第 1 章 Delfosse–Beverland–Tremblay 界所允许的那种代价。三者的 d 在 Atlas 里都是问号：构造可执行、n 与 k 可复算，距离尚无证书。

Three T1 entries stand for three ways of “getting qLDPC onto hardware”. lacross (Pecorari et al. 2024, [[34,4,?]]) designs high-rate codes for long-range-connected neutral-atom registers, matching the checks' nonlocality to the coupling patterns atom arrays can actually realise. SC-qLDPC (Hagiwara and Imai 2011, [[42,4,?]]) transplants classical coding theory's “spatial coupling” onto quasi-cyclic quantum codes, coupling checks segment by segment along a band. hierarchical codes (Pattison et al. 2023, [[35,2,?]]) take another road: concatenate a qLDPC code with surface codes and simulate the nonlocal checks with local gates, trading time for connectivity — exactly the price Chapter 1's Delfosse–Beverland–Tremblay bound permits. All three have d as a question mark in the Atlas: constructions executable, n and k recomputable, distance without a certificate.

关键想法Key idea

通往好码的路线图：超图积（k ∝ n，d ∝ √n）→ 同调积（k ∝ n，d ∝ n，但检查重量 √n）→ 高维流形上的环面码（检查对数重量，d 接近线性但依赖几何猜想；超球积本身 d ∝ √n）→ 非阿贝尔提升积与量子 Tanner 码（k ∝ n，d ∝ n，检查重量常数）。每一步都在同一个三角形里挪动：码率、距离、检查重量。而在有限尺寸上，为特定硬件裁剪的家族与 BB 一样重要。

The roadmap to good codes: hypergraph product (k ∝ n, d ∝ √n) → homological product (k ∝ n, d ∝ n, but check weight √n) → toric codes on high-dimensional manifolds (logarithmic check weight; nearly linear d only under a geometric conjecture, the hypersphere product itself has d ∝ √n) → non-abelian lifted products and quantum Tanner codes (k ∝ n, d ∝ n, constant check weight). Every step moves within the same triangle: rate, distance, check weight. And at finite size, families tailored to specific hardware matter as much as BB.

6.1消息传递：每个比特问邻居「我错了吗」Message passing: every qubit asks its neighbours “am I wrong?”

本章只讲 BP 与 OSD 的要点。从概率推断讲起的完整讲解、逐步演示的 BP 与 OSD 实验台、各阶 OSD 与实际参数、电路级的探测器错误模型，见《BP-OSD 译码器 · 高中数学版》。

This chapter gives only the gist of BP and OSD. The full treatment, from probabilistic inference to step-by-step BP and OSD benches, the OSD orders, the parameters used in practice and circuit-level detector error models, is in “The BP-OSD Decoder · High-School Edition”.

在 Tanner 图上，每个比特带一个先验：以概率 p 出错，用对数似然比 ln((1−p)/p) 表示（《译码器 · 高中数学版》1.3 节的权重）。一轮 BP 分两步。检查到比特：一条亮着的检查告诉它的每个比特「除你之外，其他人看起来都没错，所以大概是你」；一条没亮的检查告诉大家「你们里出错的个数是偶数」。比特到检查：每个比特把自己收到的所有意见（除了问话的那条检查）加起来回传。几轮后，每个比特把全部意见加上先验做硬判决；若判决出的错误恰好解释所有警报，停止。「意见」具体怎么算有两种常用规则。和积（sum-product）规则按概率精确合并：检查发给比特 i 的消息 = (−1)症状 × 2 artanh(∏ tanh(mj/2))，乘积取遍其余比特发来的消息 mj。在没有圈的图上，用和积规则跑足轮数（让每条消息传遍整棵树）之后，算出的正是每个比特单独出错的精确概率（逐比特最可能的取值），这还不等于押整类错误的最大似然译码。最小和（min-sum）是它的省算版，下面马上用到；它即使在树上也只是近似。qLDPC 的 Tanner 图有很多圈，这时两种规则都只是近似。

手算一个。三位重复码，检查 C1 = b1 ⊕ b2、C2 = b2 ⊕ b3；症状是 C1 亮、C2 不亮；p = 0.1，每个比特的先验 ln 9 ≈ +2.2（正 = 相信没错）。消息用不带缩放因子的最小和规则来算（下面的实验台用的是另一种变体，见本节末）：检查 C 发给比特 i 的消息 = (−1)C 的症状 × （C 的其余比特发来的消息的符号之积）× （这些消息里绝对值最小的那个）。症状取 1（亮）或 0（不亮）。读法：绝对值是其余比特里最不可靠的那个的可靠度，一条推理链只和最弱的一环一样可靠；符号在数「检查亮着」与「其余比特里有几个相信自己错了」，合起来是奇数，就告诉 i「你大概错了」（负）。

例：一条没亮的检查连着 b1、b2、b3，b2 发来 −3（它相信自己错了），b3 发来 +1.5。它对 b1 说 (+1) × (−1)(+1) × min(3, 1.5) = −1.5：出错的个数得是偶数，b2 已经错了，所以 b1 大概也错了，但把握只有 b3 那么强。只看「亮不亮」定符号会给出 +1.5，方向正好反了。精确的和积规则给出 −2 artanh(tanh(1.5) × tanh(0.75)) ≈ −1.31：符号相同，最小和把把握估高了一点（最小和一贯如此，因为它只看最弱的一环、忽略其余环节也不完全可靠）。

回到重复码：下面用到的传入消息都是正的，符号只由亮不亮决定。第一轮：C1 对 b1 说 −2.2（它另一个比特 b2 的可靠度是 2.2），b1 的总意见 2.2 − 2.2 = 0，打平。b2 从 C2 听到 +2.2，于是第二轮它告诉 C1 的可靠度是 2.2 + 2.2 = 4.4，C1 再对 b1 说 −4.4：b1 的总意见 2.2 − 4.4 = −2.2，判为出错。b2 的总意见是 2.2（先验）− 2.2（C1）+ 2.2（C2）= +2.2，没错；b3 也是没错。判决「只有 b1 错」正好解释症状，停止。这张图没有圈，每条检查又只连两个比特（「其余比特」只有一个，取最小值不丢信息），所以这里最小和与和积给出同样的数：b1 的 −2.2 就是精确值 ln(0.1/0.9)，因为与症状相容的只有「b1 错」和「b2、b3 都错」两种，后验概率之比 0.9 : 0.1。

下面的实验台用归一化最小和：把上面的最小和消息再乘一个固定因子 α = 0.8，抵消最小和的过度自信；比如上一段例子里的 −1.5 变成 −1.2。它是近似规则，给出的数字不是精确概率。

On the Tanner graph every qubit carries a prior: in error with probability p, expressed as the log-likelihood ratio ln((1−p)/p) (the weight of “Decoders · High-School Edition”, §1.3). One BP round has two steps. Check to qubit: a lit check tells each of its qubits “everyone but you looks fine, so it is probably you”; an unlit check tells everyone “an even number of you are in error”. Qubit to check: each qubit sums all opinions it received (except from the check asking) and sends that back. After a few rounds each qubit adds all opinions to its prior and takes a hard decision; if the decided error explains every alarm, stop. There are two common rules for computing an opinion. The sum-product rule combines probabilities exactly: the message a check sends to qubit i = (−1)syndrome × 2 artanh(∏ tanh(mj/2)), the product running over the messages mj from its other qubits. On a cycle-free graph, sum-product run for enough rounds (until every message has crossed the whole tree) gives exactly the probability that each qubit individually is in error (the most likely value bit by bit), which is still not the class-level maximum-likelihood decoding. Min-sum is its cheaper approximation, used right below; it is approximate even on a tree. On qLDPC Tanner graphs, full of cycles, both rules are only approximate.

Work one by hand. The three-bit repetition code with checks C1 = b1 ⊕ b2 and C2 = b2 ⊕ b3; the syndrome has C1 lit and C2 unlit; p = 0.1, so each bit’s prior is ln 9 ≈ +2.2 (positive = believed fine). Messages use the min-sum rule without a scaling factor (the bench below uses a variant, see the end of this section): the message check C sends to bit i = (−1)syndrome of C × (product of the signs of the messages from C’s other bits) × (the smallest absolute value among those messages). The syndrome is 1 (lit) or 0 (unlit). How to read it: the size is the reliability of the least reliable other bit, since a chain of reasoning is only as reliable as its weakest link; the sign counts “the check is lit” plus “how many other bits believe they are wrong”, and if the total is odd it tells i “you are probably wrong” (negative).

Example: an unlit check on b1, b2, b3 receives −3 from b2 (b2 believes it is wrong) and +1.5 from b3. It tells b1 (+1) × (−1)(+1) × min(3, 1.5) = −1.5: the number of errors must be even, b2 is already wrong, so b1 is probably wrong too, but only as surely as b3 is right. Setting the sign from “lit or not” alone would give +1.5, exactly the wrong direction. The exact sum-product rule gives −2 artanh(tanh(1.5) × tanh(0.75)) ≈ −1.31: the same sign, with min-sum a little overconfident (it always is, because it looks only at the weakest link and ignores that the other links are not fully reliable either).

Back to the repetition code: every incoming message used below is positive, so the sign depends only on lit or unlit. Round one: C1 tells b1 −2.2 (its other bit b2 has reliability 2.2), and b1’s total is 2.2 − 2.2 = 0, a tie. b2 hears +2.2 from C2, so in round two it reports reliability 2.2 + 2.2 = 4.4 to C1, which now tells b1 −4.4: b1’s total is 2.2 − 4.4 = −2.2, decided wrong. b2’s total is 2.2 (prior) − 2.2 (C1) + 2.2 (C2) = +2.2, fine; b3 is fine too. The decision “only b1 is wrong” explains the syndrome; stop. This graph has no cycles and every check has only two bits (a single “other bit”, so taking the minimum loses nothing), so here min-sum and sum-product give the same numbers: b1’s −2.2 is the exact value ln(0.1/0.9), because only two errors fit the syndrome, “b1 wrong” and “b2 and b3 both wrong”, with posterior odds 0.9 : 0.1.

The bench below uses normalized min-sum: every min-sum message above is multiplied by a fixed factor α = 0.8 to offset min-sum’s overconfidence; the −1.5 of the example above becomes −1.2. It is an approximate rule, and its numbers are not exact probabilities.

6.2两个敌人：短圈与退化Two enemies: short cycles and degeneracy

第一个敌人是短圈：四步的圈（比特–检查–比特–检查回到起点）就是两条检查共用两个比特。消息沿圈绕一圈又回到自己手里，同一条证据被重复计算，信心被自我放大。要分清是哪张图。把 X 检查与 Z 检查画进同一张图，任何 CSS 码都满是四步圈：一条 X 检查与一条 Z 检查只要有重叠，就必须重叠偶数个（至少 2 个）比特才能对易；在超图积里每对有重叠的 X、Z 检查恰好重叠 2 个。但本实验台的 BP 只用一种检查：它读 Z 检查的症状、找 X 错误，在只有 Z 检查的 Tanner 图上传消息。这张图有没有四步圈，取决于因子。用 2.1 节的记号，两条 Z 检查共用两个比特只有两种可能：它们在同一行，且 H₂ 里有两行共用两列；或者它们在「检查 × 检查」块的同一列，且 H₁ 里有两列共用两行。所以 HGP(rep₃, rep₃)（表面码）的 Z 图没有四步圈：重复码的两行最多共用一列，两列最多共用一行。Hamming [7,4,3] 的 3 × 7 矩阵把 7 个非零三位向量各用一次作列，列 110 与 111 共用前两行；于是 HGP(Hamming, Hamming) 的 Z 图有 30 对共用两个比特的检查（7 × 3 + 3 × 3，本页的核对脚本逐对数过）。

The first enemy is short cycles: a four-step cycle (qubit–check–qubit–check back to the start) is two checks sharing two qubits. A message travels round and returns to its sender, the same evidence is counted twice, and confidence amplifies itself. Be clear about which graph. Draw X checks and Z checks in one graph and every CSS code is full of four-step cycles: an X check and a Z check that overlap at all must overlap in an even number (at least 2) of qubits to commute, and in a hypergraph product every overlapping X/Z pair overlaps in exactly 2. But the BP on this bench uses one check type only: it reads the Z-check syndrome to find X errors and passes messages on the Tanner graph of the Z checks alone. Whether that graph has four-step cycles depends on the factors. In the notation of §2.1, two Z checks can share two qubits in only two ways: they lie in the same row and two rows of H₂ share two columns, or they lie in the same column of the check × check block and two columns of H₁ share two rows. So the Z graph of HGP(rep₃, rep₃) (the surface code) has no four-step cycle: two rows of a repetition code share at most one column, and two columns share at most one row. The 3 × 7 Hamming [7,4,3] matrix uses each of the 7 nonzero three-bit vectors once as a column, so columns 110 and 111 share the first two rows; HGP(Hamming, Hamming) therefore has 30 pairs of Z checks sharing two qubits (7 × 3 + 3 × 3, counted pair by pair by this page's check script).

第二个敌人是量子特有的退化：两个不同的错误若相差一个稳定子，效果完全相同，译码器选哪个都对——但 BP 不知道这一点，它把置信在两个等价错误之间对半分，硬判决时两边都不选，或者各选一半，得到一个既不是 A 也不是 B 的混合，症状清不掉。到实验台上选 HGP(Hamming, Hamming) [[58,16,3]] 多试几次「BP 跑完」，看这两种失败长什么样：

6.3OSD：卡住时解方程OSD: solve a linear system when stuck

Panteleev 与 Kalachev 2019 年的解法：BP 卡住时不丢掉它的输出，而是把它当作排序。按 BP 给出的「出错可能性」从高到低排列比特，取前 rank(H) 个线性独立的列作为「可能出错的位置」，在这些位置上解线性方程 He = s（高斯消元），其余位置设为 0。这总能给出一个消掉症状的修正；译码成功当且仅当残差是稳定子——BP 的排序越准，这件事越常发生，但没有保证。这就是 OSDordered-statistics decoding：按可靠度排序后在基集上解 He = s。0 阶只解一次，没被选中的 n − rank(H) 个位置（非主元位置）全设为 0。更高阶让非主元位置也可以取 1：把非主元位置按同一个 BP 排序排好（最可能出错的在前）。组合扫描 OSD（OSD-CS）试遍「只翻其中一个」的所有情形，以及「在前 λ 个里翻两个」的所有情形（Roffe 等取 λ = 60），每种情形都重新解出主元位置，最后留下总重量最小的解。——本页只实现最简单的 0 阶。按实验台的「OSD 收尾」，再用「蒙特卡洛」比较 BP 单独与 BP+OSD 的失败次数：OSD 把「无答案」变成「有答案」，答案的对错取决于码的退化程度与 OSD 的阶数。Roffe、White、Burton 与 Campbell 2020 年把 BP+OSD 跑遍整个 qLDPC 版图，用组合扫描阶的 OSD 让环面码达到约 9.9% 的码容量阈值——与《译码器 · 高中数学版》里匹配译码的 10.3% 只差一点。

Panteleev and Kalachev's 2019 remedy: when BP stalls, keep its output as an ordering. Sort qubits by BP's “probability of error” from high to low, take the first rank(H) linearly independent columns as the “likely error positions”, solve the linear system He = s on them (Gaussian elimination) and set the rest to 0. This always yields a repair that clears the syndrome; decoding succeeds exactly when the residual error is a stabilizer — the better BP's ordering, the more often that happens, but there is no guarantee. That is OSDOrdered-statistics decoding: solve He = s on a basis set chosen in reliability order. Order 0 solves once and sets the n − rank(H) positions that were not chosen (the non-pivot positions) all to 0. Higher orders let non-pivot positions be 1 as well: sort the non-pivot positions by the same BP ordering (most likely in error first). Combination-sweep OSD (OSD-CS) tries every case “flip exactly one of them” and every case “flip two among the first λ” (Roffe et al. use λ = 60), re-solves the pivot positions for each case, and keeps the solution of smallest total weight. — this page implements only the simplest order 0. Press “Finish with OSD” on the bench, then “Monte-Carlo” to compare failures of BP alone and BP+OSD: OSD turns “no answer” into “an answer”, and whether the answer is right depends on the code's degeneracy and the OSD order. Roffe, White, Burton and Campbell ran BP+OSD across the whole qLDPC landscape in 2020, reaching about 9.9% code-capacity threshold on the toric code with combination-sweep OSD — just below the 10.3% for matching in “Decoders · High-School Edition”.

6.4扩展码的局部译码器：small-set-flipThe expander codes' local decoder: small-set-flip

量子扩展码（第 3 章）有一个不需要消息传递的译码器：反复寻找一小组比特（在一条检查的支撑范围内），翻转它们能让亮起的检查数严格减少，就翻；直到没有这样的小组。Leverrier、Tillich 与 Zémor 证明，只要因子图的度数固定、两侧都足够扩展，这个贪心过程在线性时间内纠正每一个重量不超过 c√n 的对抗性错误（c 是由度数与扩展参数决定的常数）。原因是 3.4 节的警报下限：约化重量低于一个 ∝ √n 的门槛时，亮起的检查至少是约化重量的 1/3，所以只要还有警报，就总能找到能减少警报的翻转；剩下的稳定子成分不亮检查，也不需要纠正。Fawzi、Grospellier 与 Leverrier 进一步证明它对随机错误也有阈值，并由此得到常数开销的容错计算。它是《译码器 · 高中数学版》里并查集译码器精神的远亲：局部、几乎不算术、靠图的结构保证正确。

Quantum expander codes (Ch. 3) have a decoder that needs no message passing: repeatedly look for a small set of qubits (within one check's support) whose flip strictly reduces the number of lit checks, and flip it; stop when no such set remains. Leverrier, Tillich and Zémor proved that when the factor graph has fixed degrees and enough expansion on both sides, this greedy procedure corrects every adversarial error of weight at most c√n in linear time (c a constant set by the degrees and expansion parameters). The reason is the alarm floor of §3.4: when the reduced weight is below a threshold ∝ √n, the lit checks number at least one third of the reduced weight, so as long as any alarm is on, a flip that reduces alarms exists; the stabilizer part left over lights no check and needs no correction. Fawzi, Grospellier and Leverrier further showed it has a threshold against random errors, hence constant-overhead fault tolerance. It is a distant cousin of the union-find decoder of “Decoders · High-School Edition” in spirit: local, almost arithmetic-free, correct by virtue of the graph's structure.

6.5阈值账本，第三次The threshold ledger, third time

《译码器 · 高中数学版》的五个限定项在这里同样适用，且更重要，因为 qLDPC 的数字常常跨模型混用。可核对的几条：环面码 + BP-OSD + 完美测量 ≈ 9.9%（Roffe 等 2020）；BB 家族 + 深度 7 电路 + 电路级噪声 + BP-OSD ≈ 0.8%（Bravyi 等 2024，译码器教程账本的语法）；量子扩展码 + small-set-flip 在随机噪声下存在阈值（Fawzi–Grospellier–Leverrier 2018，是存在性证明而非具体数字）。任何「qLDPC 码的阈值是 X%」若不带模型，都是不完整的句子。此外，qLDPC 译码还有一个匹配没有的实时性难题：BP 的迭代与 OSD 的高斯消元都比匹配重，几百比特的码在每微秒一轮的节拍下，译码器的速度本身就是研究前沿（《译码器 · 高中数学版》4.1 节的吞吐问题）。

The five qualifiers of “Decoders · High-School Edition” apply here too, and matter more, because qLDPC numbers are often mixed across models. The checkable ones: toric code + BP-OSD + perfect measurement ≈ 9.9% (Roffe et al. 2020); the BB family + depth-7 circuit + circuit-level noise + BP-OSD ≈ 0.8% (Bravyi et al. 2024, in the grammar of the decoder tutorial's ledger); quantum expander codes + small-set-flip have a threshold under random noise (Fawzi–Grospellier–Leverrier 2018, an existence proof rather than a number). Any “the qLDPC threshold is X%” without its model is an incomplete sentence. qLDPC decoding also carries a real-time problem matching does not: BP's iterations and OSD's Gaussian elimination are heavier than matching, and for codes of a few hundred qubits at one round per microsecond, decoder speed is itself a research frontier (the throughput problem of “Decoders · High-School Edition”, §4.1).

关键想法Key idea

qLDPC 的译码器是经典 BP 加上量子补丁：短圈与退化让 BP 卡住，OSD 用一次线性方程收尾，扩展码则有更简单的局部翻转。没有一种译码器对所有 qLDPC 码最优，阈值数字永远属于「码 + 噪声 + 电路 + 译码器」这一整套。

qLDPC decoders are classical BP plus quantum patches: short cycles and degeneracy stall BP, OSD finishes with one linear system, and expander codes admit simpler local flips. No decoder is optimal for every qLDPC code, and a threshold number always belongs to the whole package “code + noise + circuit + decoder”.

诚实总账The honesty ledger

全篇简化五项，一次结清：① 超图积实验台只用小因子（重复码、Hamming、K₅、随机 LDPC），好码所需的大扩展图与量子 Tanner 码本页没有实现；② d 一列：[[13,1,3]]、[[25,1,3]]、[[125,37,3]]、bb5 为穷举验证，[[656,170,5]] 由 Tillich–Zémor 公式与两个经典距离（6 与 5，逐个试遍确认）得出，[[128,2,8]] 为文献声称，[[392,48,?]] 等为未知；③ BP 演示是码容量模型（完美测量、只有 X 错误），OSD 只用 0 阶；④ 扩展图演示是对「扩展」的示意，不是原论文的具体图；⑤ 量子 Tanner 码的构造只讲了思想，没有推导鲁棒性条件。以上不影响任何定性结论。

Five simplifications, settled in one bill: ① the hypergraph bench uses only small factors (repetition, Hamming, K₅, random LDPC); the large expanders and quantum Tanner codes that good codes require are not implemented here; ② the d column: [[13,1,3]], [[25,1,3]], [[125,37,3]] and bb5 are exhaustively verified, [[656,170,5]] follows from the Tillich–Zémor formula and two classical distances (6 and 5, confirmed by trying every vector), [[128,2,8]] is a literature claim, [[392,48,?]] and others unknown; ③ the BP demo is a code-capacity model (perfect measurement, X errors only) with order-0 OSD; ④ the expansion demo is a cartoon of expansion, not the papers' specific graphs; ⑤ the quantum Tanner construction is explained in spirit without deriving the robustness condition. None of this touches any qualitative conclusion.
