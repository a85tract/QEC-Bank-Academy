拓扑替我们算出门 Topology computes the gates

环面码上，一条竖直的 X 圈和一条水平的 Z 圈恰好交叉一次；把任何一条圈怎样揉捏变形，交叉的次数可以变成 3、5、7，却永远是奇数。这个「变形也改不了的奇偶」正是逻辑 X 与逻辑 Z 反对易的原因。数学家把这一类「从圈的形状里读出来的数」叫拓扑不变量，而其中一族更精细的运算——把两个圈的标签乘成第三个标签——叫上积。Wanted 板上的这道题问的是：能不能让机器在 Atlas 登记的每一个「乘积码」的链复形上，把这些运算真正算出来，从而证明某个码天生带有一个非 Clifford 逻辑门。今天，表面码与 BB 码条目「逻辑操作」侧面里的全部 14 条记录都写着 claimed（从论文抄来）；机器算过的只有少数小码上 Pauli / Clifford 层面的 verified 记录（例如环面码的逻辑 X / Z 圈与两块对齐的 CNOT、[[4,2,2]] 的逻辑 CZ），没有任何一条非 Clifford 门是机器算出来的。 On the toric code, a vertical X loop and a horizontal Z loop cross exactly once; knead and deform either loop as you like and the crossing count can become 3, 5 or 7, but it is always odd. This “parity that no deformation can change” is exactly why the logical X and logical Z anticommute. Mathematicians call such numbers read off from the shape of loops topological invariants, and a finer family of operations among them, multiplying the labels of two loops into a third label, is called the cup product. The question on the Wanted board is whether a machine can actually compute these operations on the chain complexes of every “product code” registered in the Atlas, and thereby prove that a code natively carries a non-Clifford logical gate. Today all 14 records in the “Logical operations” facets of the surface and BB entries read claimed (copied from papers); the machine-computed ones are a few Pauli- or Clifford-level verified records on small entries (the toric code's logical X / Z loops and aligned-block CNOT, the [[4,2,2]] code's logical CZ), and no non-Clifford gate has ever been machine-computed.

前置：只需要《从高考数学到 Toric Code》——泡利串、碰撞定理、稳定子、环面码的缠绕圈。别的一切（交叉数、同调类、上积、Clifford 层级、链复形、乘积码、证据词）都从一个能在 3 × 3 环面上手数的玩具出发再命名。这道题的真正数学远超高中范围：本篇描述那个对象是什么、输入什么、输出什么、Atlas 为什么想要输出，并明确标出描述停在哪里。状态：open（Wanted 板原文）。 Prerequisite: only “From Exam Maths to the Toric Code”: Pauli strings, the collision theorem, stabilizers, the winding loops of the toric code. Everything else (crossing number, homology class, cup product, Clifford hierarchy, chain complex, product code, evidence words) is built from a toy you can count by hand on a 3 × 3 torus before it gets a name. The real mathematics of this item lies far beyond high school: this guide describes what the object is, what goes in, what comes out and why the Atlas wants the output, and marks explicitly where the description stops. Status: open (the word on the Wanted board).

第 1 章Chapter 1

这个问题用人话说是什么：两条圈交叉几次The problem in plain words: how many times do two loops cross

先在 3 × 3 环面码上数一个数：一条 Z 圈和一条 X 圈共用几条边。再把 Z 圈变形，重数一次。你会发现这个数会变，但它的奇偶不会。这一章从这个能手数的现象出发，一步步命名到「上积」，然后说清楚它和「门」有什么关系，最后逐字读 Wanted 板的原文。 First count one number on the 3 × 3 toric code: how many edges a Z loop and an X loop share. Then deform the Z loop and count again. The number changes; its parity does not. This chapter starts from that hand-countable phenomenon, names it step by step until we reach the “cup product”, explains what it has to do with “gates”, and finally reads the Wanted board word by word.

1.1玩具：3 × 3 环面上的两条圈The toy: two loops on a 3 × 3 torus

取 L = 3 的环面码，[[18, 2, 3]]（Atlas 标签 toric_L3）。第 0 章事实 4 给了两条逻辑算符。Z̄₁：沿第二行的 3 条横边各放一个 Z（蓝色）。X̄：沿第二列的 3 条横边各放一个 X——这 3 条横边被一条竖直的对偶路径dual path：不沿着边走、而是穿过边、从一块面走到相邻面的路径。X 型逻辑算符住在这种路径穿过的边上；它与每个星检查的字母都是 X，所以自然对易，不需要穿过偶数次。（橙色虚线）依次穿过，这条路径绕环面竖直一圈。两条算符各自与所有检查对易（Toric Code 教程 5.6 节已验）。现在只问一件事：它们共用几条边？

Take the toric code with L = 3, [[18, 2, 3]] (Atlas label toric_L3). Fact 4 of Chapter 0 gave two logical operators. Z̄₁: a Z on each of the 3 horizontal edges of the second row (blue). X̄: an X on each of the 3 horizontal edges of the second column; these 3 edges are crossed in turn by a vertical dual pathA path that does not run along edges but crosses them, stepping from one plaquette to a neighbouring one. X-type logical operators live on the edges such a path crosses; it shares only X letters with every star check, hence commutes without any even-crossing condition. (amber, dashed) that winds once vertically around the torus. Each operator commutes with every check (verified in section 5.6 of the toric-code guide). Now ask one thing only: how many edges do they share?

答案是 1：第二行、第二列那条横边。在那一格里 Z 遇上 X，c = 1，奇数——按碰撞定理，Z̄₁ 与 X̄ 反对易。这就是第 0 章事实 5 的「恰好交叉一次」，只不过我们把「交叉」落实成了「共用一条边」。人话：两条圈相遇的次数，决定两个逻辑算符是对易还是反对易。下面的沙盘就是这个码。先自己做一遍：切到 Z，点亮第二行的 3 条横边；切到 X，点亮第二列的 3 条横边；数一数两种颜色重叠的边。

The answer is 1: the horizontal edge in the second row and second column. In that cell a Z meets an X, c = 1, odd; by the collision theorem Z̄₁ and X̄ anticommute. This is the “cross exactly once” of fact 5 in Chapter 0, with “crossing” made concrete as “sharing one edge”. In plain words: the number of times two loops meet decides whether two logical operators commute or anticommute. The sandbox below is this code. Do it yourself first: switch to Z and light the 3 horizontal edges of the second row; switch to X and light the 3 horizontal edges of the second column; count the edges carrying both colours.

现在把 Z̄₁ 变形。「变形」在稳定子码里只有一种合法方式：乘上一条检查。乘上一块面检查（4 条边各放 Z），已经有 Z 的边上 Z·Z = I 消掉，没有 Z 的边上多出一个 Z——圈的形状变了，但它仍与所有检查对易，仍不是面检查的乘积，仍是同一个逻辑算符 Z̄₁（Toric Code 教程 5.6 节说的「相差一个可收缩部分即视为相同」）。下图把 Z̄₁ 乘上标 ★ 的两块面检查。重数一次它和 X̄ 共用的边：

Now deform Z̄₁. In a stabilizer code “deforming” has only one legal form: multiply by a check. Multiplying by a plaquette (a Z on each of its 4 edges) cancels Z·Z = I on edges that already carried a Z and adds a Z on those that did not; the loop changes shape, yet still commutes with every check, is still not a product of plaquettes, and is still the same logical operator Z̄₁ (“loops differing by a contractible part count as the same”, section 5.6 of the toric-code guide). 3. The number changed; its parity did not. That is no coincidence but a fact provable in three lines: X̄ commutes with every plaquette (facts 2 and 4), which is to say X̄ shares an even number of edges with any plaquette (0 or 2); multiplying by a plaquette therefore changes the number of shared edges by an even amount; odd plus even is odd. Notation: c(A, B) = the number of cells where A and B carry two different non-identity letters (an X meeting a Z, for a pure-X and a pure-Z string) (fact 1 of Chapter 0), here the number of edges the two loops share; “mod 2” means keep only the remainder on division by 2, the parity.

1.3给圈贴标签：四个类，一张 4 × 4 表Labelling loops: four classes, one 4 × 4 table

既然只有奇偶要紧，就给每条闭合圈贴一个两位的标签 (h, v)：h = 它绕环面水平方向的圈数的奇偶，v = 绕竖直方向的圈数的奇偶。可收缩的圈是 (0,0)，Z̄₁（水平圈）是 (1,0)，Z̄₂（竖直圈）是 (0,1)，两者相乘是 (1,1)。乘检查不改变标签（1.2 节）。Toric Code 教程 5.6 节末尾说过：把「相差检查乘积即视为相同」的圈归类，恰好四类，学名同调类homology class：把「相差一个可收缩部分」的圈视为同一个类。环面上的一阶 ℤ₂ 同调恰有 4 个类，对应 2 个逻辑比特的 2² = 4 维编码空间。。同样给 X̄ 圈（对偶路径）贴标签 (h′, v′)。那么两条圈的交叉数只由标签决定：

Since only parity matters, give every closed loop a two-digit label (h, v): h = the parity of how many times it winds around the torus horizontally, v = the parity of how many times it winds vertically. A contractible loop is (0,0), Z̄₁ (horizontal) is (1,0), Z̄₂ (vertical) is (0,1), their product is (1,1). Multiplying by checks does not change the label (section 1.2). Then the crossing parity of two loops depends only on the labels:

c mod 2　=　h · v′ + v · h′　(mod 2)

记号：(h, v) = Z 圈的标签，(h′, v′) = X 对偶圈的标签，每个都是 0 或 1；「·」是普通乘法，「+ (mod 2)」是只看奇偶的加法（1 + 1 = 0）。

Notation: (h, v) = label of the Z loop, (h′, v′) = label of the X dual loop, each digit 0 or 1; “·” is ordinary multiplication, “+ (mod 2)” is addition keeping only parity (1 + 1 = 0).

This is already a first instance of “topology computing for us”, except that what it computes is commutation, not gates.

1.4把两个标签乘成第三个：上积（只描述）Multiplying two labels into a third: the cup product (described only)

1.3 节的表把两个标签变成一个数（0 或 1）。数学里还有一种更精细的运算，把两个标签变成另一个标签，叫上积cup product，记作 ⌣：把两个 j 维、l 维的「标签」（上同调类）乘成一个 (j + l) 维的标签。它是「圈相交」的代数版本：两条圈的上积是它们相交处所代表的类——是类，所以不记住代表元具体在哪里相交。，记作 α ⌣ β。粗略地说：圈的标签是「1 维」的对象（圈是一条线）；两条圈的上积是一个「2 维」的对象，对应它们相交的那些点所构成的东西。在环面上，2 维的标签只有一种非平凡的可能——「整个曲面」——所以 (1,0) ⌣ (0,1) 就是「整个曲面」这个标签，而把它「数一数」（在整个曲面上求值）恰好得到 1.3 节表里的那个 1。人话：1.3 节的数是上积的影子；上积本身高一个层次：它是一个 2 维的类，还能再和第三条圈配对；它不保留的恰恰是代表元具体在哪里相交。

The table of section 1.3 turns two labels into a number (0 or 1). Mathematics also has a finer operation that turns two labels into another label, the cup productWritten ⌣: it multiplies a j-dimensional and an l-dimensional “label” (cohomology class) into a (j + l)-dimensional label. It is the algebraic version of “loops intersecting”: the cup product of two loop classes is the class of their intersection — a class, so it forgets exactly where representatives meet., written α ⌣ β. Roughly: a loop's label is a “1-dimensional” object (a loop is a line); the cup product of two loops is a “2-dimensional” object, corresponding to the thing made of the points where they meet. On the torus there is only one nontrivial 2-dimensional label, “the whole surface”, so (1,0) ⌣ (0,1) is that label, and “counting it” (evaluating on the whole surface) gives exactly the 1 in the table of 1.3. In plain words: the number of section 1.3 is the shadow of the cup product; the cup product itself lives one level up: it is a 2-dimensional class that can be paired again with a third loop; what it does not keep is the exact place where representatives meet.

描述在这里停下Where the description stops

上积的严格定义需要「上同调」——给每个顶点、每条边、每块面各分配一个 0/1 并规定边界规则——以及一套把两个分配「相乘」的公式。这些都在高中数学之外，本篇不写出公式，也不推导任何性质。你需要带走的只有三句：(1) 它的输入是两个标签（两个同调类或上同调类）；(2) 它的输出是一个高一维的标签；(3) 在环面上，它的「数值」就是 1.3 节那张表。Wanted 板用的更大的词上同调运算cohomology operation：所有「把标签变成标签」的自然运算的总称，上积是其中最基本的一种；更高阶的还有 Steenrod 平方等，本篇不涉及。，指的是上积以及它的一族推广。

最后一步是「门」。存住逻辑比特不等于计算；要计算就要对逻辑比特做门，而且最想要的是便宜的门——对每个物理比特各做一个小操作、深度为常数、不需要长时间的测量协议（表面码逻辑操作教程 1.1 节：需要哪些门）。这样一个操作能不能保持码空间、保持之后对逻辑比特做的是什么门，取决于逻辑算符之间怎样重叠：两两重叠决定它是不是一个 Clifford 门（H、S、CNOT 一类，把泡利串变成泡利串），三条逻辑算符同时相遇的地方决定它能不能是一个非 Clifford 门（T 一类）。而「三条圈同时相遇的地方」在拓扑语言里正是三个标签的上积 α ⌣ β ⌣ γ。这就是标题那句话的意思：如果机器能在码的拓扑结构上算出上积，它就能为一部分天生的门出具证书——理论条件覆盖到的那些——而不是判定这个码所有的门，也不能取代 Bravyi–König 那样的禁止定理。Atlas 设计文档 3.2 节把这一点写进了数据模型：“cohomology operations (cup products) — these are operations-facet inputs, since they certify native non-Clifford logicals on product codes (Zhu, arXiv:2501.19375).” 本篇没有读这篇论文，也不复述它的任何结果；上一段关于「两两重叠 / 三重重叠」的说法是对这条设计决定的人话转述，不是推导。

The last step is “gates”. Storing logical qubits is not computing; computing means applying gates to them, and the gates one wants most are the cheap ones, a small operation on each physical qubit, constant depth, no long measurement protocol (surface-code operations guide 1.1: which gates are needed). Whether such an operation preserves the code space, and which logical gate it then performs, depends on how the logical operators overlap: pairwise overlaps decide whether it is a Clifford gate (the H, S, CNOT kind, mapping Pauli strings to Pauli strings); the places where three logical operators meet at once decide whether it can be a non-Clifford gate (the T kind). And “where three loops meet at once” is, in topological language, precisely the cup product of three labels α ⌣ β ⌣ γ. That is what the title means: if a machine can compute cup products on a code's topological structure, it can certify particular native gates — the ones the theory constructs under its conditions — not classify every gate a code might have, and not replace obstructions such as Bravyi–König. The Atlas design document, section 3.2, wrote this into the data model: “cohomology operations (cup products) — these are operations-facet inputs, since they certify native non-Clifford logicals on product codes (Zhu, arXiv:2501.19375).” This guide has not read that paper and restates none of its results; the previous sentences about “pairwise / triple overlaps” are a plain-language paraphrase of that design decision, not a derivation.

The LogicalOperation facet is the facet holding the two tables of Chapter 2. Connects … directly: today there is no arrow between them; R2 stops at “which logical operators exist”, and “which gates act on them” is a separate line filled in by a person. A cup-product calculator is the missing arrow. In plain words: the Atlas already stores the structure of the loops; nobody has yet taught the machine to read gates off that structure.

3.3今天的用户做不到什么What a user cannot do today

Three things. First, ask “does gross have a native T gate” and get an answer from the machine. Today the card offers silence, nothing beyond five Clifford records, and silence is neither “yes” nor “no”. Second, compare the “computational power” of two product codes fairly. The Atlas ranks codes by storage figures of merit such as k·d²/n (BB guide 1.1); the why_it_matters of transversal-gate-cert says it plainly: “feeds fair comparison of computation, not just memory”. Without machine-computed gate tables the column “which code computes better” does not exist. Third, have the gate table of a newly registered product code filled in automatically. Today every new code needs a person to read papers and copy its operations; with a cup-product calculator the gate table would be computed the moment the chain complex is registered, like k. In plain words: today the Atlas can tell you how well a code stores, not how well it computes.

第 4 章Chapter 4

合格的答案长什么样，为什么难What a qualifying answer looks like, and why it is hard

一个合格的答案是一台程序：输入 Atlas 已经存着的链复形，输出一张上积表和从表里读出的、带证书的门清单。难度写的是 open-research——不是「知道怎么做但要做很多」，而是「连该怎么定义都还在研究」。这一章先说输入输出，再说三个诚实的难点，最后用玩具尺度对照一下：难的不是大小。 A qualifying answer is a program: input the chain complexes the Atlas already stores, output a cup-product table and, read off it, a certified list of gates. The difficulty reads open-research: not “known how, much to do” but “even the right definition is still being researched”. This chapter gives input and output first, then three honest difficulties, then a toy-scale comparison: size is not the hard part.

4.1输入与输出Input and output

输入。一个乘积码的链复形。Atlas 基础页 foundations/css-chain-complexes/intuition.md 的一句话定义：“Two classical codes with H_X H_Z^T = 0 are one chain complex; homology counts logicals.” 对环面码，这就是「顶点—边—面」三层和两张矩阵：哪些边碰哪个顶点（星检查），哪些边围哪块面（面检查）。此外还要输入这个码是怎样乘出来的——两个零件各是什么、怎样配对——因为上积的定义要用到乘积结构。

Input. The chain complex of a product code. The one-line definition on the Atlas foundations page foundations/css-chain-complexes/intuition.md: “Two classical codes with H_X H_Z^T = 0 are one chain complex; homology counts logicals.” For the toric code this is the three layers vertices, edges, faces and two matrices: which edges touch which vertex (star checks), which edges bound which face (plaquette checks). One must also input how the code was multiplied together, what the two parts are and how they pair, because the definition of the cup product uses the product structure.

HX HZT = 0

记号：HX = X 型检查的 0/1 矩阵（每行一条星检查，每列一个比特），HZ = Z 型检查的矩阵；上标 T = 转置（行列互换）；乘积为 0 是「每条星检查与每条面检查共用偶数个比特」的矩阵写法，即第 0 章事实 1 的对易条件（qLDPC 教程 1.2 节）。

Notation: HX = the 0/1 matrix of X-type checks (one row per star check, one column per qubit), HZ = the matrix of Z-type checks; superscript T = transpose (swap rows and columns); the product being 0 is the matrix form of “every star and every plaquette share an even number of qubits”, the commutation condition of fact 1 in Chapter 0 (qLDPC guide 1.2).

输出。两样东西。(1) 一张上积表：对逻辑算符类的每一对（以及每一个三元组），它们的上积是什么——环面码上就是 1.3 节那张 4 × 4 表（再加上三元组一栏，在环面上全为零，因为环面只有二维，没有「三条圈同时相遇」的地方）。(2) 从表里读出的门证书：对理论条件覆盖到的那些常深度物理操作，证明它保持码空间、实现哪个逻辑门、层级是 Clifford 还是非 Clifford，每条附一份机器能核对的证书——是「证明有这些门」，不是「判定全部门」。第 (2) 项就是能直接写进 operations 字段、origin 标 derived 或 verified 的东西。人话：进去的是 Atlas 已经有的矩阵和乘法配方，出来的是门的清单和证书。

Output. Two things. (1) A cup-product table: for every pair (and every triple) of logical-operator classes, their cup product; on the toric code this is the 4 × 4 table of 1.3 (plus a triples column, all zero on the torus, since the torus is only two-dimensional and has no place where “three loops meet at once”). (2) Gate certificates read off the table: for the constant-depth physical operations the theory's conditions cover, a proof that each preserves the code space, which logical gate it performs and whether it is Clifford or non-Clifford, each with a machine-checkable certificate — “these gates exist”, not “all gates decided”. Item (2) is what could be written straight into the operations field with origin derived or verified. In plain words: what goes in is matrices and a multiplication recipe the Atlas already has; what comes out is a list of gates with certificates.

4.2difficulty：open-researchdifficulty: open-research

Wanted 板给难度只有两个词。hard-engineering = 「知道怎么做，但要做很多」；open-research = 「没人知道怎么做」。这道题是 open-research。对照 2.3 节：transversal-gate-cert 是 hard-engineering，因为三正交性检查、图自同构枚举、小 n 穷举都是已知算法，缺的是把它们做完；cup-products 是 open-research，因为下面三件事都还没有公认的答案。设计文档 4.1 节的对照表也这样写：“Recent theory (arXiv:2501.19375; Freedman–Hastings lineage); no computational library” —— 有理论，没有可运行的库。

The Wanted board grades difficulty with two words only. hard-engineering = “known how, but much to do”; open-research = “nobody knows how”. This item is open-research. Compare 2.3: transversal-gate-cert is hard-engineering because triorthogonality checks, graph-automorphism enumeration and small-n brute force are known algorithms awaiting completion; cup-products is open-research because the three things below have no agreed answer yet. The design document's comparison table in section 4.1 says the same: “Recent theory (arXiv:2501.19375; Freedman–Hastings lineage); no computational library”, theory exists, a runnable library does not.

4.3三个诚实的难点Three honest difficulties

一、Atlas 存的链复形没有几何。上积的教科书定义依赖「哪块面的哪个角是哪个顶点」这类形状信息，而 Atlas 的链复形库只有矩阵。Wanted 板上另一道 partial 的题 chain-complex-lib 的 statement 原文说得清楚：“Extend the minimal ChainComplex (length-3, no geometry) to cell complexes with boundaries, higher dimensions, and coordinate embeddings.” 它的 current_proxy 是 “qeclib.chain (length-3, matrix-only) + hand-coded lattice dicts.” 也就是说，上积计算器的输入格式本身还没有建好。

One: the chain complexes the Atlas stores have no geometry. The textbook definition of the cup product relies on shape information such as “which corner of which face is which vertex”, while the Atlas chain-complex library holds only matrices. The statement of another partial item on the board, chain-complex-lib, says so verbatim: “Extend the minimal ChainComplex (length-3, no geometry) to cell complexes with boundaries, higher dimensions, and coordinate embeddings.” Its current_proxy is “qeclib.chain (length-3, matrix-only) + hand-coded lattice dicts.” In other words, the input format for a cup-product calculator does not exist yet.

二、乘积码不一定是「形状」。环面码画得出来，因为它真的是一个甜甜圈的表面。BB 码不是：它是把 0/1 换成多项式的提升积（BB 教程 2.3 节），检查之间的连接不能画在任何二维曲面上（BB 教程 1.2 节：放弃局域是必然的）。一般的超图积（qLDPC 教程第 2 章）更不是曲面。「在不是形状的东西上定义上积」正是 statement 里 on explicit complexes（在具体的复形上）这几个字的重量，也是它引用一篇 2025 年论文而不是教科书的原因。本篇不知道那篇论文怎样解决这件事。

Two: a product code need not be a “shape”. The toric code can be drawn because it really is the surface of a doughnut. BB codes are not: they are lifted products with 0/1 replaced by polynomials (BB guide 2.3), and the connections among their checks cannot be drawn on any two-dimensional surface (BB guide 1.2: giving up locality is unavoidable). General hypergraph products (qLDPC guide, Chapter 2) are even less surfaces. “Defining a cup product on something that is not a shape” is the weight carried by the words on explicit complexes in the statement, and the reason it cites a 2025 paper rather than a textbook. This guide does not know how that paper resolves it.

三、从上积表到「门」还有一段路。即使算出了表，「这个常深度操作实现逻辑 T」还要单独证明它保持码空间、算出它对全部逻辑比特的作用（gross 有 k = 12 个逻辑比特，BB 操作教程 3.2 节列了认证一个门的五步），再写成证书。这一段与 transversal-gate-cert 重叠，是 hard-engineering 而非 open-research，但它今天也没有做。

Three: from the cup-product table to a “gate” there is still a road. Even with the table computed, “this constant-depth operation implements logical T” must separately be shown to preserve the code space, its action on all logical qubits computed (gross has k = 12, and BB operations guide 3.2 lists the five steps to certify a gate), and the result written as a certificate. This stretch overlaps with transversal-gate-cert and is hard-engineering rather than open-research, but it too is undone today.

4.4玩具尺度的对照：难的不是大小Toy-scale comparison: size is not the hard part

和 《大码的码距》那道题不同，这道题的难点不是组合爆炸。环面码 k = 2，逻辑算符类有 22k = 16 个（含平凡类），两两配对 16 × 16 = 256 格，1.3 节一张 4 × 4 的表就装下了它的核心。gross k = 12，逻辑 Pauli 有 224 ≈ 1.7 × 107 个（BB 操作教程 1.2 节），但两两上积只需对 2k = 24 个生成元算：24 × 24 = 576 格；三元组 24³ = 13 824 格。对计算机这都是瞬间的事。难的是第 4.3 节的前两条：算什么（在非几何的复形上如何定义）和拿什么算（输入格式还不存在）。人话：这不是一道「算不完」的题，是一道「还不知道该算什么」的题。

Unlike the “Distance for large codes” item, the difficulty here is not combinatorial explosion. The toric code has k = 2, so 22k = 16 logical-operator classes (including the trivial one), 16 × 16 = 256 pairs, and the 4 × 4 table of 1.3 already holds the core. gross has k = 12, so 224 ≈ 1.7 × 107 logical Paulis (BB operations guide 1.2), but pairwise cup products need only the 2k = 24 generators: 24 × 24 = 576 cells; triples 24³ = 13 824 cells. Instant work for a computer. The hard part is the first two points of 4.3: what to compute (how to define it on a non-geometric complex) and what to compute it from (the input format does not exist yet). In plain words: this is not a problem that “cannot be finished”; it is a problem where “what to compute” is not yet known.

记号：22k = 2k 个逻辑 Pauli 生成元各「取或不取」的组合数；24 × 24 与 24³ 是本篇自己算的格数，不是 Atlas 页面上的数。

Notation: 22k = the number of ways to include or exclude each of the 2k logical Pauli generators; 24 × 24 and 24³ are cell counts computed for this guide, not numbers from Atlas pages.

一个奇偶、一张表、十四个 claimedOne parity, one table, fourteen claimed

回放一遍。环面码上一条 Z 圈与一条 X 圈共用的边数，随变形而变，奇偶不变；这个奇偶就是两个逻辑算符对易与否，四个圈类之间的全部答案是一张 4 × 4 表，公式 h·v′ + v·h′。把「两个标签变成一个数」升级为「两个标签变成第三个标签」就是上积；三个标签的上积标记「三条圈同时相遇的地方」，而那正是一个码能否自带非 Clifford 门（T 一类）的关键——本篇只描述了这一点，没有推导。Atlas 今天在 surface 与 bivariate_bicycle 的「逻辑操作」侧面存了 9 + 5 = 14 条记录，全部 claimed，唯一的非 Clifford 是靠魔法态买来的 T，自带的一条也没有——「没有记录」而非「证明没有」。一台能在乘积码的链复形上算上积的机器，会让这些记录变成 derived 或 verified，让「哪个码更适合算」成为可以比较的一栏。难度 open-research：输入格式还没有（chain-complex-lib 是 partial），在非几何的复形上如何定义还在研究（arXiv:2501.19375，登记待补）；难的不是大小，24 × 24 = 576 格瞬间可算。

Replay. On the toric code the number of edges a Z loop and an X loop share changes under deformation; its parity does not. That parity is whether the two logical operators commute, and the full answer among the four loop classes is one 4 × 4 table, formula h·v′ + v·h′. Upgrading “two labels to one number” into “two labels to a third label” is the cup product; the cup product of three labels marks “where three loops meet at once”, which is the key to whether a code natively carries a non-Clifford gate (the T kind); this guide only described that, deriving nothing. Today the Atlas holds 9 + 5 = 14 records in the “Logical operations” facets of surface and bivariate_bicycle, all claimed; the only non-Clifford one is a T bought with magic states, and native ones number zero, “no record” rather than “proven absent”. A machine computing cup products on the chain complexes of product codes would turn those records into derived or verified and make “which code computes better” a comparable column. Difficulty open-research: the input format does not exist (chain-complex-lib is partial), and how to define the product on a non-geometric complex is under research (arXiv:2501.19375, registration pending); size is not the issue, 24 × 24 = 576 cells are instant.
