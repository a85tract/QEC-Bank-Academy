「导览」是 Atlas 给每个码条目配的一条固定路线：它不讲新理论，只把构造器真实导出的数据（坐标、检查、支撑、秩）一站一站指给你看。BB 码的这条路线有八站，标题叫「Bivariate bicycle: from torus shifts to the gross memory」（从环面平移到 gross 存储器；gross 是英文里「一罗」= 12 打 = 144 的旧量词，这个码有 144 个数据比特，因此得名，本篇标题里的「一罗」就是它）。本篇沿着它走一遍，用的是 Atlas 自己算出来的 gross 实例 [[144,12,12]]：l = 12、m = 6 的环面上 A = x³ + y + y²、B = y³ + x + x²（l、m 是环面两个方向的格数，A、B 是两个三项多项式），外加一个 3 × 3 环面上的锚点 [[18,2,3]]。你会看到 144 个坐标是哪条公式算出来的，X 检查 0 的六条腿 {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84} 一条一条从哪一项来，Z 检查 0 的 {3, 60, 66, 76, 77, 126} 为什么是「倒着走」的，72 行检查为什么只有 66 行独立，以及导览最后两站为什么要专门说「这不是电路」「这个平移对逻辑比特做了什么，要另算」。 A “guided tour” is the fixed route the Atlas attaches to every code entry: no new theory, only the data the constructor actually exported (coordinates, checks, supports, ranks), pointed out one stop at a time. The BB-code route has eight stops and is titled “Bivariate bicycle: from torus shifts to the gross memory” (“gross” is the old unit of 12 dozen = 144; the code has 144 data qubits, hence the name). This guide walks it using the Atlas's own computed gross instance, [[144,12,12]]: A = x³ + y + y² and B = y³ + x + x² on an l = 12, m = 6 torus (l and m are the site counts in the two torus directions, A and B the two three-term polynomials), plus an anchor [[18,2,3]] on a 3 × 3 torus. You will see which formula produces the 144 coordinates, where each of X check 0's six legs {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84} comes from, why Z check 0's {3, 60, 66, 76, 77, 126} “walks backwards”, why 72 rows of checks have only 66 independent ones, and why the last two stops insist “this is not a circuit” and “what this translation does to the logical qubits must be computed separately”.

关键想法Key idea

你在直觉教程里「想象」的两层环面，这里是「打印」出来的：坐标是 (i, j, layer) 的线性函数，144 = 2 × 72 是两层的面积之和。reproduce.py 断言导出的 coords 恰有 144 项、checks 恰有 144 条，且每条检查的支撑就是 HX 或 HZ 对应那一行里 1 的位置。后面每一章都在重复同一件事：把导览里的一次高亮，还原成关于 A、B 的一句代数话。

The two-layer torus you “imagined” in the intuition guide is “printed” here: coordinates are linear functions of (i, j, layer), and 144 = 2 × 72 is the area of the two layers. reproduce.py asserts that the exported coords has exactly 144 entries and checks exactly 144 rows, and that each check's support is exactly the positions of the 1s in the corresponding row of HX or HZ. Every later chapter repeats one move: turning a highlight in the tour into one algebraic sentence about A and B.

2.1读法：一项一条腿，A 管左层，B 管右层The reading rule: one term, one leg; A for the left layer, B for the right

回忆直觉教程的翻译词典：单项式 xayb 是「把格子 (i, j) 平移到 (i + a, j + b)」的置换矩阵（每行、每列恰有一个 1 的 0/1 矩阵：第 s 行唯一的 1 所在的列，就是格子 s 被送到的地方），坐标超出范围就绕回mod l 与 mod m：i 数到 12 绕回 0，j 数到 6 绕回 0。环面上没有「出界」，只有绕回。。所以 HX = [A | B] 的第 s 行（s 是格子 (i, j) 的编号 i·m + j）里，1 落在这些列上：A 的每一项 (a, b) 给出左层格子 (i + a, j + b) 的编号；B 的每一项 (a, b) 给出右层格子 (i + a, j + b) 的编号。项数就是腿数：gross 的 |A| = |B| = 3，所以每条 X 检查恰好六条腿，三条在左层、三条在右层。

Recall the intuition guide's translation dictionary: the monomial xayb is the permutation matrix “move site (i, j) to (i + a, j + b)” (a 0/1 matrix with exactly one 1 in every row and every column: the column of the single 1 in row s is where site s is sent), wrapping aroundmod l and mod m: i counts up to 12 and wraps to 0, j counts up to 6 and wraps to 0. On a torus there is no “out of bounds”, only wrapping. when a coordinate runs off the end. So in row s of HX = [A | B] (s is the index i·m + j of site (i, j)) the 1s sit in these columns: each term (a, b) of A gives the index of the left-layer site (i + a, j + b); each term (a, b) of B gives the index of the right-layer site (i + a, j + b). Term count equals leg count: gross has |A| = |B| = 3, so every X check has exactly six legs, three in the left layer and three in the right.

(arXiv:2308.07915) says “an error threshold of 0.8% for the standard circuit-based noise model”, for their family of BB codes, under standard circuit noise (every gate, idle step, preparation and readout may fail), with their depth-7 circuit and detector construction and the BP-OSD decoder (belief propagation plus ordered-statistics post-processing), obtained from simulations — the five qualifiers of the intuition guide's §6.4, read with the same threshold-ledger rules as “Decoders · High-School Edition”. Not one pixel of the player concerns any of these five things, which is why stop ⑦ is labelled curated: literature narrative, not machine recomputation.

6.2平移是自同构：Z 检查 0 挪一格就是 Z 检查 1Translations are automorphisms: Z check 0 moved one step is Z check 1

第 ⑧ 站高亮播放器编号 73，即 Z 检查 1（格子 (0, 1)）。按第 3 章的读法算它的支撑：{4, 61, 67, 72, 77, 127}。把它和 Z 检查 0 的 {3, 60, 66, 76, 77, 126} 并排放：3 → 4，60 → 61，66 → 67，76 → 77，126 → 127——每个编号加 1，就是每条腿往上挪一格（j + 1）；剩下那条 77 → 72：右层 (0, 5) 往上一格绕回 (0, 0)，编号从 77 跳到 72。六条腿一起挪，形状不变。这就是原话「Torus translations preserve these supports as a set」的意思：对所有 144 个比特同时做平移 y（两层各自的 (i, j) → (i, j + 1)），Z 检查 0 的支撑恰好变成 Z 检查 1 的支撑，Z 检查 1 变成 Z 检查 2……72 条检查只是被重新编号，作为一个集合一条也没多、一条也没少。X 检查同理。

Stop ⑧ highlights player index 73, i.e. Z check 1 (site (0, 1)). Chapter 3's rule gives its support: {4, 61, 67, 72, 77, 127}. Set it beside Z check 0's {3, 60, 66, 76, 77, 126}: 3 → 4, 60 → 61, 66 → 67, 76 → 77, 126 → 127 — each index plus 1, i.e. each leg moved one step up (j + 1); the remaining 77 → 72: right (0, 5) moved one up wraps to (0, 0), and the index jumps from 77 to 72. All six legs move together; the shape is unchanged. That is what “Torus translations preserve these supports as a set” means: translate all 144 qubits at once by y ((i, j) → (i, j + 1) in each layer) and the support of Z check 0 becomes exactly the support of Z check 1, Z check 1 becomes Z check 2, and so on. The 72 checks are merely renumbered; as a set, not one is added or lost. Likewise for the X checks.

于是环面平移是码的自同构automorphism：把稳定子群映到自身的物理操作（这里是比特的一个置换）。它保证码空间不变，但对逻辑比特做了什么需要另算。。第 ⑧ 站的后半句才是重点：「That alone does not name a logical gate: the induced action on the twelve logical Pauli pairs and the routing cost must still be computed for the chosen block.」说人话：把 144 个比特整体挪一格，码空间确实回到自己。一个把稳定子群映回自身的比特置换，必然在 12 个逻辑比特上做某个 Clifford 操作（把每个逻辑 Pauli 变成另一个逻辑 Pauli 的操作，例如 H、S、CNOT；恒等也算一种）。问题是哪一个：上面的矩阵恒等式只证明码空间不变，算不出它对 24 个逻辑 Pauli（12 对 X̄、Z̄）的作用；导览和 Atlas 的证书都没有算这一步，所以在 Atlas 里，它的具体逻辑作用和实现代价都还是「未计算」。文献里算过：Bravyi 等人（arXiv:2308.07915 v2，9.2 节「Logical Gates based on Automorphisms」）选了一组以单项式编号的逻辑算符，平移 s 把编号为 n 的 X̄ 换成编号为 s·n 的 X̄（Z̄ 同理），于是每个平移都是一个逻辑 CNOT 电路（最多差一个逻辑 Pauli 修正）；作者还写道除 s = 1 外没有哪个平移作用成逻辑恒等，但对 gross 这一句不成立：逐一检验 72 个平移对全部逻辑 X̄、Z̄ 的作用，x⁶（沿长为 12 的方向平移半圈）作用成逻辑恒等，其余 70 个非恒等平移都作用非平凡（检验方法见直觉教程 6.5 节）。作者也说，还不清楚怎样把这些 CNOT 用于有用的计算。而且「挪一格」在真机上是把每个比特搬到邻居的位置，或者等价地把 144 根连线全部重接——布线代价要另算。Atlas 因此只把它记为物理自同构（verified），不记为逻辑操作；直觉教程6.5 节说的正是这件事，逻辑操作侧面（逻辑操作）记录的五条操作全部是 claimed。

So torus translations are automorphismsA physical operation (here a permutation of qubits) that maps the stabilizer group to itself. It guarantees the code space is preserved, but what it does to the logical qubits must be computed separately. of the code. The second half of stop ⑧ is the point: “That alone does not name a logical gate: the induced action on the twelve logical Pauli pairs and the routing cost must still be computed for the chosen block.” In plain words: shift all 144 qubits one step and the code space does return to itself. A qubit permutation that maps the stabilizer group to itself necessarily performs some Clifford operation on the 12 logical qubits (an operation that turns every logical Pauli into another logical Pauli, such as H, S, CNOT; the identity counts too). The question is which one: the matrix identity above proves only that the code space is preserved and cannot give the action on the 24 logical Paulis (12 pairs X̄, Z̄). Neither the tour nor the Atlas certificates compute that step, so in the Atlas its specific logical action and implementation cost remain uncomputed. The literature has computed it: Bravyi et al. (arXiv:2308.07915 v2, section 9.2 “Logical Gates based on Automorphisms”) choose logical operators labelled by monomials; a translation s turns the X̄ labelled n into the X̄ labelled s·n (likewise for Z̄), so every translation is a logical CNOT circuit (up to a logical Pauli correction); the authors also write that no translation other than s = 1 acts as the logical identity, but for gross that does not hold: checking all 72 translations on every logical X̄ and Z̄ shows that x⁶ (a half-turn along the direction of length 12) acts as the logical identity, while the other 70 nonidentity translations have a nontrivial logical action (the check is described in §6.5 of the intuition guide). The authors add that it is not yet clear how to use these CNOTs for useful computation. And “one step” on real hardware means moving every qubit to its neighbour's place, or equivalently rewiring all 144 connections — a routing cost to be accounted separately. The Atlas therefore records it only as a physical automorphism (verified), not as a logical operation; the intuition guide's §6.5 says exactly this, and all five operations recorded in the operations facet (logical operations) are claimed.

关键想法Key idea

The tour's last two stops are two lines of defence.
