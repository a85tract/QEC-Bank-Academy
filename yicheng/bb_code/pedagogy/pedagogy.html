第 1 章Chapter 1

从环面多项式到 CSS 链复形From torus polynomials to a CSS chain complex

这一章固定 constructor.py 使用的约定，把对易的论证一分不欠地付清。目标不只是记住 HX = [A | B]、HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]，而是看清为什么恰恰是这个公式：转置有转置的任务，交换有交换的任务，缺一个都不成立。最后用 l = m = 3、A = 1 + x、B = 1 + y 把整套约定钉在一个能穷举的小例子上。 This chapter fixes the convention used by constructor.py and pays the commutation argument in full. The goal is not merely to remember HX = [A | B], HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ], but to see why exactly this formula: the transpose has a job, the swap has a job, and neither alone suffices. At the end, l = m = 3, A = 1 + x, B = 1 + y pins the whole convention to a small example that can be enumerated exhaustively.

1.1正则环面表示：一个约定，三条恒等式The regular torus representation: one convention, three identities

令 ei,j 是环面格点 (i, j) 上的标准基向量，i 取 0 到 l−1，j 取 0 到 m−1（数学写法：(i, j) ∈ ℤl × ℤm，即两个坐标各自绕圈）。构造器的循环移位 Sl 在第 i 行、第 i+1 列（下标模 l）放一个 1——这正是《BB 码 · 高中数学版》2.1 节的那个矩阵，方向约定「第 i 格送到第 i+1 格」。把格点拉平成一维下标 im + j（先数 i、再数 j），定义

Let ei,j be the standard basis vector at torus site (i, j), with i from 0 to l−1 and j from 0 to m−1 (in symbols (i, j) ∈ ℤl × ℤm: each coordinate wraps around). The constructor's cyclic shift Sl puts a 1 in row i, column i+1 (indices mod l), exactly the matrix of §2.1 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition”, with the orientation “cell i goes to cell i+1”. Flatten the sites into the one-dimensional index im + j (count i first, then j) and define

x ≔ Sl ⊗ Im　·　y ≔ Il ⊗ Sm　⇒　xl = I　·　ym = I　·　xy = yx

Sl = l × l 的循环移位置换矩阵；Im = m × m 单位矩阵；⊗ = 克罗内克积（直觉教程 2.2 节：把左边矩阵的每个元素放大成一整块右边矩阵）；x、y = 只动第一个坐标 / 只动第二个坐标的平移，都是 lm × lm 的置换矩阵；I = lm × lm 单位矩阵。Sl = the l × l cyclic-shift permutation matrix; Im = the m × m identity; ⊗ = the Kronecker product (intuition guide, §2.2: every entry of the left matrix is blown up into a whole copy of the right one); x, y = the translations that move only the first / only the second coordinate, both lm × lm permutation matrices; I = the lm × lm identity.

人话：x 让格点 (i, j) 变成 (i+1, j)，y 让它变成 (i, j+1)。第一条恒等式 xl = I 是「往 x 方向走 l 步回到原地」；第二条同理；第三条 xy = yx 是「先右后上和先上后右到的是同一个格子」——它成立的深层原因是 x 和 y 作用在克罗内克积的不同因子上。reproduce.py 对 gross 逐条断言了 x¹² = I、y⁶ = I 与 xy = yx。这些矩阵合起来给出了环 R 的正则表示regular representation：把环里每个元素 r 变成「乘以 r」这个线性映射的矩阵。加法变成矩阵加法，乘法变成矩阵乘法，所以多项式里的等式与矩阵里的等式一一对应。：

In plain words: x sends site (i, j) to (i+1, j), y sends it to (i, j+1). The first identity xl = I says “l steps in the x direction bring you home”; the second likewise; the third, xy = yx, says “right-then-up and up-then-right reach the same cell”, and the deep reason it holds is that x and y act on different factors of the Kronecker product. reproduce.py asserts x¹² = I, y⁶ = I and xy = yx for gross one by one. Together these matrices give the regular representationThe regular representation turns every ring element r into the matrix of the linear map “multiply by r”. Addition becomes matrix addition and multiplication matrix multiplication, so identities among polynomials and identities among matrices correspond one to one. of the ring R:

R = GF(2)[x, y] / (xl − 1, ym − 1)　(特征 2 里 xl − 1 = xl + 1，有的论文写加号，同一个商环in characteristic two xl − 1 = xl + 1; papers using the plus sign describe the same quotient)

GF(2) = 只有 0、1、按 1 + 1 = 0 运算的数域；GF(2)[x, y] = 系数为 0/1 的 x、y 多项式全体；/ ( … ) = 「模掉括号里的东西」，即把 xl − 1 与 ym − 1 都当作 0，也就是 xl = 1、ym = 1 两条规则（直觉教程 2.3 节「学名一瞥」）。GF(2) = the field with only 0 and 1, where 1 + 1 = 0; GF(2)[x, y] = all polynomials in x, y with 0/1 coefficients; / ( … ) = “modulo what is in the brackets”, i.e. treat xl − 1 and ym − 1 as 0, which is the pair of rules xl = 1, ym = 1 (intuition guide, §2.3, “a glimpse of the ring”).

1.2转置 = 反向平移Transpose = inverse translation

每个单项式 xayb 是一个置换矩阵（每行每列恰一个 1）。置换矩阵的转置等于它的逆，所以

Every monomial xayb is a permutation matrix (exactly one 1 per row and per column). A permutation matrix's transpose is its inverse, hence

(xayb)ᵀ = x−ay−b　(负指数按模 l、模 m 约化：gross 里 y−3 = y3，x−1 = x11negative exponents reduced mod l, mod m: in gross y−3 = y3, x−1 = x11)

a、b = 单项式的两个指数，即往 x 方向平移 a 格、往 y 方向平移 b 格；ᵀ = 转置，把矩阵的行列互换。a, b = the two exponents of the monomial, i.e. a cells of translation along x and b along y; ᵀ = transpose, swapping rows and columns.

这就是转置在这里有几何意义的原因：它把一次环面平移倒过来。为什么会这样？置换矩阵 P 在第 i 行第 π(i) 列放 1，「把 i 送到 π(i)」；转置后 1 跑到第 π(i) 行第 i 列，「把 π(i) 送回 i」。人话：往右挪三格的矩阵，转置就是往左挪三格。这一条在 1.4 节要用两次。

That is why the transpose has a geometric meaning here: it reverses a torus translation. Why? A permutation matrix P has its 1 in row i, column π(i), “send i to π(i)”; after transposing, the 1 sits in row π(i), column i, “send π(i) back to i”. In plain words: the matrix that moves everything three cells right has, as its transpose, the matrix that moves everything three cells left. §1.4 will use this twice.

1.4为什么必须「交换再转置」：一行证明，两个任务Why the swapped transpose is forced: one line of proof, two jobs

在环面每个格点放两个数据比特：前 lm 列是左块，后 lm 列是右块。定义 HX = [A | B]。CSS 码要求 HXHZᵀ = 0（碰撞定理的矩阵形式：每对 X、Z 检查重叠偶数个比特）。选 HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]，分块相乘：

Place two data qubits at every torus site: the first lm columns are the left block, the second lm the right block. Define HX = [A | B]. A CSS code needs HXHZᵀ = 0 (the matrix form of the collision theorem: every X check overlaps every Z check on an even number of qubits). Choose HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ] and multiply blockwise:

HXHZᵀ = [A | B] · [B ; A] = AB + BA = AB + AB = 0　(第三个等号用 AB = BA，第四个用 1 + 1 = 0the third equality uses AB = BA, the fourth uses 1 + 1 = 0)

[A | B] = 把 A、B 左右并排的 lm × 2lm 矩阵；[B ; A] = 把 B、A 上下堆叠的 2lm × lm 矩阵，它就是 HZᵀ（因为 (Bᵀ)ᵀ = B、(Aᵀ)ᵀ = A）；AB = 矩阵乘积，也是多项式乘积。[A | B] = the lm × 2lm matrix with A and B side by side; [B ; A] = the 2lm × lm matrix with B stacked over A, which is HZᵀ (since (Bᵀ)ᵀ = B, (Aᵀ)ᵀ = A); AB = the matrix product, equally the polynomial product.

AB = BA 来自 1.1 节的 xy = yx：A、B 都是 x、y 的多项式，而两个变量对易，所以它们的任何两个多项式对易——这是「把检查算在阿贝尔（可交换）环上」的全部回报。1 + 1 = 0 是 GF(2)。于是任何 l、m、A、B 都给出一个合法的 CSS 码。

AB = BA comes from xy = yx in §1.1: A and B are polynomials in x, y, and since the two variables commute, so do any two polynomials in them; this is the whole payoff of computing checks over an abelian (commutative) ring. 1 + 1 = 0 is GF(2). Hence any l, m, A, B give a legal CSS code.

转置和交换各有任务。转置把 Z 一侧的关联倒过来，让 Z 检查成为 X 检查的对偶映射——X 检查沿某方向伸出去的腿，Z 检查沿反方向收回来（1.2 节）。交换把重叠项路由成 AB + BA。若只转置不交换，取候选 HZ' = [Aᵀ | Bᵀ]，乘积变成 A² + B²，没有任何理由为零。对 gross 可以具体算：A² = x⁶ + y² + y⁴，B² = y⁶ + x² + x⁴ = 1 + x² + x⁴（因为 y⁶ = 1），两者相加是一个六项多项式，矩阵每行 6 个 1，秩 48——离零远得很。人话：不交换的话，gross 的每条 X 检查会与 6 条 Z 检查各撞奇数次，码根本不合法。所以「交换 + 转置」不是审美，是让定理成立的唯一便宜路线。

Transpose and swap each have a job. The transpose reverses the Z-side incidence so that the Z check is the dual map of the X check: where an X check reaches out along some direction, the Z check reaches back along the opposite one (§1.2). The swap routes the overlap into AB + BA. Transpose without swapping, with the candidate HZ' = [Aᵀ | Bᵀ], gives A² + B², which has no reason to vanish. For gross one can compute it: A² = x⁶ + y² + y⁴, B² = y⁶ + x² + x⁴ = 1 + x² + x⁴ (since y⁶ = 1), and their sum is a six-term polynomial whose matrix has 6 ones per row and rank 48, nowhere near zero. In plain words: without the swap, every gross X check would collide an odd number of times with 6 different Z checks, and the code would simply be illegal. So “swap + transpose” is not taste; it is the only cheap route that makes the theorem true.

Commutation rests on geometry in the toric code (a corner joins two edges) and on algebra in a BB code (AB = BA and 1 + 1 = 0). In the one-line proof the transpose supplies duality and the swap pairs the product into two identical terms. reproduce.py asserts, for gross, that HZ equals [Bᵀ | Aᵀ] entry by entry and that HXHZᵀ vanishes mod 2.

最后一条常被压缩成「度 6」。保留 X/Z 的拆分对电路很要紧：3 条 X 型交互和 3 条 Z 型交互用不同的辅助比特制备方式，故障传播的样子也不同。还有一个方法学细节：reproduce.py 不是「看到三项就推断重量 3」，而是把 A、B、HX、HZ 的每一行每一列都数了一遍（脚本第 103–110 行八条断言）——因为如果两个指数对约化后重合，项会抵消，重量会掉到 1。

The last statement is often compressed to “degree 6”. Keeping the X/Z split visible matters for a circuit: three X-type and three Z-type interactions use different ancilla preparations and propagate faults differently. One methodological detail: reproduce.py does not “see three terms and infer weight 3”; it counts every row and every column of A, B, HX, HZ (eight assertions, script lines 103–110), because if two exponent pairs coincide after reduction the terms cancel and the weight drops to 1.

wang2026lowoverhead (Ke Wang et al., “Demonstration of Low-Overhead Quantum Error Correction Codes”, arXiv 2505.09684, registration pending) is the only literal BB experiment identified by the six-lens sweep. Its block and hardware count must stay attached to the result: the code is [[18,4,4]], not gross; the device is 32 transmons with long-range couplings (18 data qubits; the card assigns no role to the other 14, intuition guide, §6.6); the operation is all nonlocal weight-six checks with repeated syndrome extraction; the metric is logical error per logical qubit per cycle (8.91 ± 0.17)%; the interpretation is “feasibility of a low-overhead, nonlocal-check experiment”. 5.3提案为什么各不相同Why the proposals differ

七个提案攻的是不同的瓶颈。Rydberg 版图：利用可重构或长程的原子相互作用快速实现 Tanner 图。分层局域：付出移动、通信或对昂贵检查的更慢测量，以适应局域基底。Morphing：在提取中途换码，让瞬时图的度更低。互素子类：约束多项式搜索，让码率与冷原子版图一起设计。模块化架构：把块变成带辅助、通信、编译与资源记账的指令集。光子融合：把奇偶检查翻译成发射器产生的资源态和一个考虑损耗的融合网络。分布式模块：把一个 gross 块拆开，暴露出有噪声的非局域 Bell 链路带来的逻辑代价。它们不是同一个探测器模型的可互换实现：移动会增加空闲故障；morphing 改变了哪个稳定子码在起作用；光子损耗信道不同于 transmon 的退极化模型；联网分割引入链路噪声。每一个都需要自己的电路与译码器主张。

The seven proposals attack different bottlenecks. Rydberg layout: exploit reconfigurable or long-range atom interactions to realize the Tanner graph quickly. Layered locality: pay motion, communication, or slower measurement of costly checks to fit a local substrate. Morphing: change the code during extraction so the instantaneous graph has lower degree. Coprime subclass: constrain the polynomial search so rate and cold-atom layout are co-designed. Modular architecture: turn blocks into an instruction set with ancillas, communication, compilation and resource accounting. Photonic fusion: translate parity checks into emitter-generated resource states and a loss-aware fusion network. Distributed modules: split one gross block and expose the logical penalty from noisy nonlocal Bell links. These are not interchangeable implementations of one detector model: movement can add idle faults; morphing changes which stabilizer code is active; a photonic loss channel differs from a transmon depolarizing model; a networked partition introduces link noise. Each needs its own circuit and decoder claim.

6.2多项式 → 群代数：为什么对易，父家族是谁Polynomial → group algebra: why they commute, and who the parent is

单项式 xayb 标记群元素 (a, b) ∈ ℤl × ℤm——就是「往 x 方向走 a 步、往 y 方向走 b 步」这个平移。用1.1 节的正则表示，群的乘法（两个平移先后做）变成矩阵乘法。因为这个群是阿贝尔的（可交换的：先走 a 再走 c，等于先走 c 再走 a），A 和 B 的正则矩阵就对易。人话：BB 码对易定理的全部力量，来自「环面上的平移可以换顺序」这一件事；这在 gross 里是 72 个平移两两可交换。

The monomial xayb labels the group element (a, b) ∈ ℤl × ℤm, i.e. the translation “a steps along x, b steps along y”. Under the regular representation of §1.1, group multiplication (doing two translations in turn) becomes matrix multiplication. Because the group is abelian (commutative: a steps then c steps equals c steps then a steps), the regular matrices of A and B commute. In plain words: the whole force of the BB commutation theorem comes from the single fact that torus translations can be reordered; in gross that is 72 translations, pairwise interchangeable.

BB ⊂ 阿贝尔 2BGAabelian 2BGA ⊂ 两块码two-block codes　(三个包含都是真包含，不是等号all three containments are strict, not equalities)

⊂ = 「是……的子集」；2BGA（two-block group-algebra code）= 用某个有限群 G 的群代数里两个元素 a、b 造 HX = [a | b]、HZ = [bᵀ | aᵀ] 的码；G 换成 ℤl × ℤm 就是 BB。两块码 = 更一般地，任何两个对易的方阵 A、B 拼成的 [A | B]、[Bᵀ | Aᵀ]。⊂ = “is a subset of”; 2BGA (two-block group-algebra code) = a code with HX = [a | b], HZ = [bᵀ | aᵀ] built from two elements a, b of the group algebra of some finite group G; with G = ℤl × ℤm it is BB. Two-block code = more generally, [A | B], [Bᵀ | Aᵀ] for any two commuting square matrices A, B.

这条链就是 Atlas 关系侧面唯一一条类型化关系的来历：special_case_of → two_bga，原文「BB 码是 ℤl × ℤm 的阿贝尔两块群代数码；一般的 2BGA 码可以用别的群，不必有二元多项式表示」，引 wang2023abelian（Renyu Wang、Hsiang-Ku Lin、Leonid P. Pryadko，"Abelian and Non-Abelian Quantum Two-Block Codes"，2023，arXiv 2305.06890）与 lin2023twoblock（Hsiang-Ku Lin、Leonid P. Pryadko，"Quantum Two-Block Group Algebra Codes"，2023，arXiv 2306.16400）——两个键据文献扫描报告，登记待补；目标条目 two_bga 本身在 Atlas 里还是 pending（《BB 码 · 关系》讲了这一点）。三个包含为什么都不是等号：① 一个 2BGA 码可以用另一个群（比如非阿贝尔群）；② 非阿贝尔的构造可以让左、右作用对易，而完全没有两个变量的阿贝尔多项式环；③ 一般的提升积可以有比两块 BB 复形大得多的底矩阵（《qLDPC 码 · 高中数学版》4.2 节：BB 是底矩阵 1 × 1 的提升积，底矩阵一大就不是 BB 了）。人话：BB 是一个很特殊的角落；「两块」「群代数」「提升积」每往外走一步都放开一个限制，放开的东西就不再享有本页证过的性质。

This chain is the origin of the single typed relation on the Atlas relations facet, special_case_of → two_bga, whose text reads “Bivariate bicycle codes are the abelian two-block group-algebra codes for ℤl × ℤm; general 2BGA codes may use other groups and need not have a two-variable polynomial presentation”, citing wang2023abelian (Renyu Wang, Hsiang-Ku Lin, Leonid P. Pryadko, “Abelian and Non-Abelian Quantum Two-Block Codes”, 2023, arXiv 2305.06890) and lin2023twoblock (Hsiang-Ku Lin, Leonid P. Pryadko, “Quantum Two-Block Group Algebra Codes”, 2023, arXiv 2306.16400); both keys are, per the scout report, registration pending, and the target entry two_bga is itself still pending in the Atlas (“BB Codes · Relations” covers this). Why none of the three containments is an equality: (1) a 2BGA code may use another group, for instance a nonabelian one; (2) a nonabelian construction can make left and right actions commute without any two-variable abelian polynomial ring; (3) a general lifted product can have base matrices far larger than the two-block BB complex (“qLDPC Codes · High-School Edition”, §4.2: BB is the lifted product with a 1 × 1 base matrix; enlarge the base and it is no longer BB). In plain words: BB is a very special corner; each step outward to “two-block”, “group algebra” or “lifted product” releases one restriction, and whatever is released no longer enjoys the properties proved on this page.

6.3群代数 → 链复形：R 线性与「精确矩阵」的区别Group algebra → chain complex: R-linearity versus “the exact matrix”

映射 [B ; A] 与 [A | B] 是 R 线性R-linear：不只对 0/1 系数线性，对「乘以环 R 里任何元素」也线性。比如把输入平移一格（乘 x），输出也恰好平移一格。这正是 4.2 节平移自同构的来源。的，二者复合为零（1.5 节），于是定义了一个长度为二的复形。忘掉 R 线性，只把它们当 GF(2) 上的 0/1 矩阵，就得到二元 CSS 矩阵；保留 R 线性，就看见了平移与商模。两种描述在固定了同一组基与同一个正则表示时，保住的是同一个精确的码。

The maps [B ; A] and [A | B] are R-linearLinear not only over 0/1 coefficients but with respect to “multiply by any ring element of R”: translate the input one cell (multiply by x) and the output translates by exactly one cell too. This is where the translation automorphisms of §4.2 come from., and their composition vanishes (§1.5), so they define a length-two complex. Forget R-linearity, treating them as mere 0/1 matrices over GF(2), and you have the binary CSS matrices; keep it, and translations and quotient modules come into view. With the same bases and the same regular representation fixed, the two descriptions preserve the same exact code.

这里藏着一个对 Atlas 复现契约至关重要的区别。换一组基（比如把比特重新编号、把左右两块对调、把行按别的顺序排）可以保住码空间——同一组量子态、同样的 n、k、d——却改变行序与比特序。而0.2 节的契约要求的是精确矩阵：验证器的匹配器叫 exact-checkmatrix-v1，比较整张 72 × 144 的数组。所以「我的构造和 Atlas 的等价」不满足契约；「我的构造和 Atlas 的逐元素相同」才满足。人话：链复形告诉你「这两个码是同一个码」；复现契约问的是「这两张表是同一张表吗」——问题不同，答案可以不同。

A distinction crucial to the Atlas replay contract hides here. Changing bases (renumbering qubits, swapping the left and right blocks, ordering rows differently) can preserve the code space, the same set of quantum states, the same n, k, d, while changing the row and qubit order. But the contract of §0.2 demands the exact matrix: the verifier's matcher is named exact-checkmatrix-v1 and compares the whole 72 × 144 array. So “my construction is equivalent to the Atlas one” does not satisfy the contract; “my construction agrees with the Atlas one entry by entry” does. In plain words: the chain complex tells you “these two codes are the same code”; the replay contract asks “are these two tables the same table?”, a different question with a possibly different answer.

6.4链复形 → 商模：秩为什么掉，以及秩不是码距Chain complex → quotient module: why the rank drops, and why rank is not distance

[A | B] 的像是理想 (A, B)——所有能写成 Au + Bv 的多项式。因此商 R/(A,B) 量的正是一个秩亏损：gross 里像的维数是 66，商的维数是 72 − 66 = 6（2.3 节）。因式分解与公共根的方法能预测这个商何时非零、它的维数怎样随周期或边界变化：postema2025existence（Jasper Johannes Postema、Servaas J. J. M. F. Kokkelmans，"Existence and Characterisation of Bivariate Bicycle Codes"，2025，arXiv 2502.17052，登记待补）用这套结构刻画 BB 码的存在条件与渐近极限；wang2026bbs（Renyu Wang、Leonid P. Pryadko，"Algebra of Bivariate-Bicycle Surface Codes"，2026，arXiv 2606.08771，登记待补）把根 / 边界分析推得更远（第 7 章会用到）。人话：A 和 B「共同碰不到」的那 6 个方向，就是逻辑信息的一半；换周期，比如把同样的 A、B 放到 9 × 6 的环面上，这个商就掉到 54 − 50 = 4，k 变成 8，那正是 bb108（直觉教程 5.5 节）。

The image of [A | B] is the ideal (A, B), every polynomial writable as Au + Bv. The quotient R/(A,B) therefore measures exactly one rank defect: in gross the image has dimension 66 and the quotient dimension 72 − 66 = 6 (§2.3). Factorization and common-root methods predict when this quotient is nonzero and how its dimension changes with periods or boundaries: postema2025existence (Jasper Johannes Postema, Servaas J. J. M. F. Kokkelmans, “Existence and Characterisation of Bivariate Bicycle Codes”, 2025, arXiv 2502.17052, registration pending) uses this structure to characterize existence conditions and asymptotic limitations, and wang2026bbs (Renyu Wang, Leonid P. Pryadko, “Algebra of Bivariate-Bicycle Surface Codes”, 2026, arXiv 2606.08771, registration pending) extends the root/boundary analysis (Chapter 7 uses it). In plain words: the 6 directions that A and B “jointly fail to reach” are half of the logical information; change the periods, say the same A, B on a 9 × 6 torus, and the quotient drops to 54 − 50 = 4, k becomes 8, and that is bb108 (intuition guide, §5.5).

秩仍然不是码距Rank is still not distance

商空间告诉你有多少逻辑类，不告诉你它们多重。一个商类可以有一个低重量的代表元，也可以每个代表元都摊在整个环面上。码距问的是「模掉全部稳定子之后，最轻的非平凡类有多重」——这是一个完全不同的极小化问题。gross 的 k = 12 由秩算出（verified），d = 12 却要两份证书（bounded，2.4 节），正是这个区别在账本上的样子。

The quotient tells you how many logical classes there are, not how heavy they are. A quotient class may have a low-weight representative, or every representative may be spread across the torus. Distance asks “after all stabilizer reductions, how heavy is the lightest nontrivial class?”, an entirely different minimization. Gross's k = 12 is computed from ranks (verified) while its d = 12 needs two certificates (bounded, §2.4); that is exactly this distinction as it appears in the ledger.

6.5多项式 → Tanner 图与电路：一条边说的只是「有交互」Polynomial → Tanner and circuit views: an edge says only “these interact”

A 或 B 的每一个非零元都是一条边，连一条检查与一个数据比特。gross 的 3 + 3 项给出重量 6 的检查，以及每个数据比特上 3 条 X 关联加 3 条 Z 关联（2.2 节）。数一数：HX 有 72 行 × 6 个 1 = 432 条 X 型边，HZ 同样 432 条，Tanner 图共 144 个数据节点、144 个检查节点、864 条边（这三个数是本页按 72 × 6 自己乘出来的，不在 Atlas 页面上）。bravyi2024bicycle 证明这张图能拆成两个平面的度 3 耦合层（直觉教程 6.1 节）——这是硬件工程师最爱的视图，因为它直接说出需要多少根线。

Every nonzero entry of A or B is an edge joining one check to one data qubit. The gross 3 + 3 terms give weight-six checks and three X plus three Z incidences per data qubit (§2.2). Count them: HX has 72 rows × 6 ones = 432 X-type edges, HZ another 432, so the Tanner graph has 144 data nodes, 144 check nodes and 864 edges (these three numbers are this page's own 72 × 6 arithmetic, not on any Atlas page). bravyi2024bicycle shows the graph splits into two planar degree-three coupling layers (intuition guide, §6.1), the hardware engineer's favourite view because it says directly how many wires are needed.

但这张图对一个故障电路是必要而不充分的。一条 Tanner 边说「这两个系统有交互」，它不规定：哪个辅助比特负责这条检查；交互发生在哪一时刻；CNOT 的控制端与目标端是谁；复位与测量的时序；哪些测量乘积是探测器。这五条正是3.2 节「Atlas 刻意不做的事」的清单。shaw2025morphing 是最好的反例教材：它在周期中途改变瞬时的码与图，把度 6 降到度 5——所以「降低连接度」不是把同一张静态矩阵重画一遍，而是换了一个码在跑。人话：Tanner 图是电路的骨架照片，不是电路。

Yet the graph is necessary but insufficient for a fault circuit. A Tanner edge says “these two systems interact”; it does not specify which ancilla owns the check; when the interaction happens; which end is CNOT control and which target; reset and measurement timing; which measurement products are detectors. Those five items are exactly the list in §3.2, “what the Atlas deliberately does not do”. shaw2025morphing is the best counter-example: it changes the instantaneous code and graph mid-cycle, lowering degree 6 to degree 5, so “reducing connectivity” is not a redrawing of the same static matrix but a different code running. In plain words: the Tanner graph is an X-ray of the circuit's skeleton, not the circuit.

Q1 · 为什么交换 Aᵀ 与 Bᵀ 的位置这么要紧？Q1 · Why does swapping Aᵀ and Bᵀ matter?

交换之后，[A | B]·[Bᵀ | Aᵀ]ᵀ = AB + BA = 0。不交换，乘积是 A² + B²，没有理由为零——gross 里它是一个每行 6 个 1、秩 48 的矩阵。交换把 CSS 对易条件变成了两个可交换代数元素的对易子commutator：AB − BA（在 GF(2) 上就是 AB + BA）；两个元素可交换 ⇔ 对易子为零。（1.4 节）。

With the swap, [A | B]·[Bᵀ | Aᵀ]ᵀ = AB + BA = 0. Without it the product is A² + B², which has no reason to vanish; in gross it is a matrix with 6 ones per row and rank 48. The swap turns the CSS commutation condition into the commutatorAB − BA (over GF(2), AB + BA); two elements commute ⇔ their commutator is zero. of two commuting algebra elements (§1.4).

Q2 · Atlas 机器验证了 gross 码完整的码距证明吗？Q2 · Does the Atlas machine-verify the gross code's complete distance proof?

没有。它重跑的是一个重量 12 的上界见证（rand-lowweight-v1，种子 5，2000 次迭代）；「不存在重量低于 12 的逻辑算符」这一排除仍引自 bravyi2024bicycle。两边碰头，12 这个值成立，出处等级是 bounded 而不是 verified（2.4 节）。只有 n = 18 的 toric_L3 被穷举，才是 verified（1.6 节）。

No. It reruns a weight-12 upper witness (rand-lowweight-v1, seed 5, 2,000 iterations); the exclusion of weights below 12 remains sourced to bravyi2024bicycle. The matching bounds justify the value 12 at provenance bounded, not verified (§2.4). Only the n = 18 toric_L3, which is enumerated exhaustively, is verified (§1.6).

Q3 · 0.8% 是每个 BB 码固有的阈值吗？Q3 · Is 0.8% an intrinsic threshold of every BB code?

不是。Bravyi 等人估计的是他们那个 BB 存储器家族在标准电路噪声模型、深度 7 的提取、他们的探测器构造与 BP-OSD 配置下的电路级阈值。它是一个带协议限定的家族阈值，不是单块码的伪阈值，也不是任意一张 BB 检查矩阵的性质。换家族、换电路、换噪声归一化或换译码器，就定义了另一个阈值实验（3.5 节）。

No. Bravyi et al. estimate a circuit-level threshold for their BB memory family under the standard circuit-noise model, depth-seven extraction, their detector construction and their BP-OSD configuration. It is a protocol-qualified family threshold, not a one-block pseudo-threshold and not a property of an arbitrary BB check matrix. Changing the family, circuit, noise normalization or decoder defines a different threshold experiment (§3.5).

Q4 · 为什么 k = 12 不能从「每个多项式三项」推出？Q4 · Why does k = 12 not follow from having three terms in each polynomial?

项数决定稀疏度，不决定秩。这里两张 CSS 检查矩阵的秩都是 66，144 − 66 − 66 = 12 个逻辑维度。不同的三项多项式可以有不同的公共商 R/(A,B)，因此有不同的 k 和码距——同样的 A、B 搬到 9 × 6 环面上，商维数从 6 掉到 4、k 从 12 掉到 8（2.3 节、6.4 节）。

Term count fixes sparsity, not rank. Here both CSS check matrices have rank 66, leaving 144 − 66 − 66 = 12 logical dimensions. Different three-term polynomials can have a different common quotient R/(A,B), hence different k and distance; the same A, B moved to a 9 × 6 torus drop the quotient dimension from 6 to 4 and k from 12 to 8 (§2.3, §6.4).

Q5 · 平移保持稳定子，它们就已经是十二个可寻址的逻辑门了吗？Q5 · If translations preserve the stabilizers, are they already twelve addressable logical gates?

不是。保持稳定子只证明码空间映到自身。还必须算出它在一组逻辑 Pauli 基上诱导的 24 × 24 辛作用：它可能是平凡的，可能混合几个逻辑比特，也可能路由代价高昂。卡片只登记带有被引证书与明确适用范围的操作，所以 gross 的五行操作里没有「平移」这一行（4.2 节）。

No. Stabilizer preservation proves only that the code space maps to itself. One must compute the induced 24 × 24 symplectic action on a logical Pauli basis; it may be trivial, may mix several logical qubits, and may be expensive to route. The card records only operations with a cited certificate and explicit applicability, which is why “translation” is not among gross's five operation rows (§4.2).
