十二个逻辑比特挤在一块，对门集提出了什么要求What twelve logical qubits in one block demand of a gate set

Atlas 的 logical-operations.md 开篇一句：「A high-rate memory is not yet a computer.」这一章先说清「计算」对门集的要求（Clifford 门加一个非 Clifford 门，且要能对指定的逻辑比特做），再说明 k = 12 带来的一个新词——可寻址；然后把 Atlas 的 5 条记录并排放好，并解释 Atlas 用来判定「这算不算一个门」的三个问题。 The Atlas file logical-operations.md opens with one sentence: “A high-rate memory is not yet a computer.” This chapter first states what “computing” demands of a gate set (Clifford gates plus one non-Clifford gate, applicable to chosen logical qubits), then introduces the new word that k = 12 brings, addressability; then it lines up the 5 Atlas records side by side and explains the three questions the Atlas uses to decide “does this count as a gate”.

1.1存住不等于计算：需要哪些门Storing is not computing: which gates are needed

《格点手术 · 高中数学版》1.1 节讲过门集：任何量子运算都能拆成单比特门加 CNOT；纠错架构里只用有限的一组——H、S、CNOT 合称 Clifford 门把泡利串映成泡利串的门（H、S、CNOT 及其组合）。在稳定子语言里可以完全追踪；单靠它们不能通用计算。，再加一个 T 门才通用。表面码那一篇的结论是：平面上局域、常深度的操作最多做到 Clifford（Bravyi–König 定理），T 门要靠魔法态。BB 码的情形要重新问一遍，因为它不是二维局域码，那条定理不直接适用——但先别高兴：第 5 章会看到，Atlas 与文献扫描的结论是「no source supplies a universal transversal gate set for the original gross block」，非 Clifford 的边界在 BB 上同样存在，只是理由不同。

Section 1.1 of “Lattice Surgery · High-School Edition” covered gate sets: any quantum computation decomposes into single-qubit gates plus CNOT; error-corrected architectures use a finite set, H, S and CNOT together called the Clifford gatesGates that map Pauli strings to Pauli strings (H, S, CNOT and their compositions). Fully trackable in the stabilizer language; not universal on their own., plus one T gate for universality. The surface-code guide concluded that local constant-depth operations in the plane reach at most Clifford (the Bravyi–König theorem) and the T gate needs magic states. For BB the question must be asked afresh, because BB is not a 2D local code and that theorem does not apply directly; but hold the cheer: Chapter 5 shows that the Atlas and the scout report conclude “no source supplies a universal transversal gate set for the original gross block”, so the non-Clifford boundary exists on BB as well, for a different reason.

BB 还多一层要求。表面码一块贴片一个逻辑比特，「对这块贴片做 H」就是「对这个逻辑比特做 H」。gross 一块 144 比特的码里住着 12 个逻辑比特；对整块做一个物理操作，效果落在 12 个逻辑比特的某种组合上。Atlas 原文：「The packed value k = 12 makes addressability especially important. A collective operation on all twelve logical qubits is not a substitute for a compiler that selects the desired logical combination while preserving the others.」人话：一个「同时对 12 个都做点什么」的操作，不等于「只对第 3 个做 H」；后者叫可寻址addressability：能对指定的一个或几个逻辑比特做门，而不扰动同一块里的其余逻辑比特。k = 12 的块必须回答这个问题，k = 1 的贴片不必。。intuition.md 把它列为失败模式之一：「Twelve logical qubits in one block are useful only if an architecture can prepare, measure, couple, and route the desired logical combinations without erasing the memory advantage.」

BB adds one more requirement. One surface-code patch holds one logical qubit, so “apply H to this patch” is “apply H to this logical qubit”. One 144-qubit gross block houses 12 logical qubits; a physical operation on the whole block acts on some combination of the 12. The Atlas text: “The packed value k = 12 makes addressability especially important. A collective operation on all twelve logical qubits is not a substitute for a compiler that selects the desired logical combination while preserving the others.” Plainly: an operation that “does something to all 12 at once” is not “H on logical 3 only”; the latter is called addressabilityThe ability to apply a gate to one or a few chosen logical qubits without disturbing the rest in the same block. A k = 12 block must answer this; a k = 1 patch need not.. intuition.md lists it among the failure modes: “Twelve logical qubits in one block are useful only if an architecture can prepare, measure, couple, and route the desired logical combinations without erasing the memory advantage.”

1.2Atlas 判定「这算不算门」的三个问题The three questions the Atlas asks before calling something a gate

logical-operations.md 的第二句给出判定标准：「The relevant question is not ‘does the code have symmetries?’ but ‘which physical process preserves the code, what logical symplectic action does it induce, and what fault-tolerance and routing resources certify that action?’」拆成三问。① 哪个物理过程保持码：具体的一层物理门或一次置换，作用后每条稳定子仍是稳定子（可能变成另一条）。② 它诱导什么逻辑辛作用：辛作用symplectic action：一个 Clifford 操作把每个逻辑 Pauli 算符（X̄1…X̄k、Z̄1…Z̄k）送到哪个逻辑 Pauli 算符，写成一个 2k × 2k 的 0/1 矩阵。k = 12 时是 24 × 24。「辛」指它保持对易/反对易关系。是这个操作对 2k 个逻辑 Pauli 生成元各做了什么，写成一个 2k × 2k 的 0/1 矩阵——gross 是 24 × 24。人话：知道「码空间被映回自身」还不够，要知道里面的 12 个比特各被怎样搬动了。③ 什么容错与布线资源认证它：这个过程在真实噪声下是否仍是容错的（一个物理错误不会变成多个逻辑错误），以及硬件上要怎样连线才能做。三问答完，Atlas 才写一条记录。第 3 章的平移正好卡在第 ② 问。

The second sentence of logical-operations.md gives the criterion: “The relevant question is not ‘does the code have symmetries?’ but ‘which physical process preserves the code, what logical symplectic action does it induce, and what fault-tolerance and routing resources certify that action?’” Three questions. (1) Which physical process preserves the code: a concrete layer of physical gates or a permutation after which every stabilizer is still a stabilizer (possibly a different one). (2) What logical symplectic action it induces: the symplectic actionWhere a Clifford operation sends each logical Pauli operator (X̄1…X̄k, Z̄1…Z̄k), written as a 2k × 2k binary matrix; 24 × 24 for k = 12. “Symplectic” means it preserves commutation relations. is what the operation does to each of the 2k logical Pauli generators, written as a 2k × 2k binary matrix, 24 × 24 for gross. Plainly: knowing “the code space maps to itself” is not enough; you must know how each of the 12 qubits inside was moved. (3) What fault-tolerance and routing resources certify it: whether the process stays fault tolerant under real noise (one physical error must not become several logical ones) and how the hardware must be wired to perform it. Only when all three are answered does the Atlas write a record. The translations of Chapter 3 get stuck at exactly question (2).

2.1四条共轭规则，两块稳定子群Four conjugation rules, the two-block stabilizer group

Atlas 原文的设定：「Take two identical BB blocks, labelled control c and target t, and apply physical CNOT from qubit i of c to qubit i of t for every i.」CNOT 是 Clifford 门，它把 Pauli 算符变成 Pauli 算符（《格点手术 · 高中数学版》5.2 节的 Heisenberg 图景），规则只有四条：

The Atlas set-up: “Take two identical BB blocks, labelled control c and target t, and apply physical CNOT from qubit i of c to qubit i of t for every i.” CNOT is a Clifford gate and maps Pauli operators to Pauli operators (the Heisenberg picture in section 5.2 of “Lattice Surgery · High-School Edition”), by only four rules:

Xc ⇒ XcXt　·　Xt ⇒ Xt　·　Zc ⇒ Zc　·　Zt ⇒ ZcZt
人话：控制位的 X 复印到目标位；目标位的 Z 复印回控制位；另两个不动plainly: an X on the control is copied onto the target; a Z on the target is copied back onto the control; the other two stay

记号：下标 c、t = 控制（control）块与目标（target）块上编号相同的那一对物理比特；Xc = 控制比特上的 Pauli X，依此类推；⇒ 这里读作「经 CNOT 共轭后变成」。Notation: subscripts c, t = the pair of physical qubits with the same index on the control and target blocks; Xc = Pauli X on the control qubit, and so on; ⇒ here reads “becomes, after conjugation by the CNOT”.

现在把一条 gross 的 X 检查放进去。控制块的 X 检查 0 是 X 作用在比特 {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84} 上（参数导读 1.3 节转录的 reproduce.py 断言）。这六个比特各自与目标块的同号比特做了一次 CNOT，按第一条规则，每个 Xc 都变成 XcXt：结果是「控制块 {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84} 上的 X」乘「目标块 {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84} 上的 X」——也就是控制块的 X 检查 0 乘以目标块的 X 检查 0，两者都是稳定子，乘积仍是稳定子。目标块的 X 检查 0 按第二条规则不动。Z 一侧对称：控制块的 Z 检查 0（比特 {3, 60, 66, 76, 77, 126}）按第三条规则不动；目标块的 Z 检查 0 按第四条规则变成自己乘控制块的 Z 检查 0。Atlas 的原话就是这四句：「an X stabilizer on the control block acquires the matching X stabilizer on the target; an X stabilizer on the target stays fixed. Dually, a target Z stabilizer acquires the matching control Z stabilizer, while a control Z stabilizer stays fixed. Every image remains in the two-block stabilizer group.」这里用到了「两块相同」：目标块的 X 检查 0 恰好也落在 {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84} 上，复印过去的 X 才刚好是一条现成的检查。两块不同，复印过去的图形一般不是目标块的稳定子，码就被破坏了。

Now feed in one gross X check. X check 0 of the control block is X on qubits {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84} (the reproduce.py assertion transcribed in section 1.3 of the parameters guide). Each of these six qubits did one CNOT with the same-numbered target qubit, so by the first rule every Xc becomes XcXt: the result is “X on control {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84}” times “X on target {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84}”, that is control X check 0 times target X check 0, both stabilizers, so the product is a stabilizer. Target X check 0 stays fixed by the second rule. The Z side mirrors it: control Z check 0 (qubits {3, 60, 66, 76, 77, 126}) stays fixed by the third rule; target Z check 0 becomes itself times control Z check 0 by the fourth. The Atlas says exactly these four things: “an X stabilizer on the control block acquires the matching X stabilizer on the target; an X stabilizer on the target stays fixed. Dually, a target Z stabilizer acquires the matching control Z stabilizer, while a control Z stabilizer stays fixed. Every image remains in the two-block stabilizer group.” This used “two identical blocks”: target X check 0 also lies exactly on {1, 2, 18, 75, 78, 84}, so the copied X is precisely an existing check. With two different blocks the copied pattern is in general not a stabilizer of the target, and the code is broken.

逻辑算符同理。Atlas：「The same conjugation applies to corresponding logical Pauli pairs, giving the parallel logical CNOT action.」取控制块第 i 个逻辑比特的 X̄i（某个与全部 Z 检查对易、又不是 X 检查乘积的 X 串，BB 教程 4.4 节），第一条规则把它变成 X̄icX̄it；Z̄it 变成 Z̄icZ̄it。这正是「逻辑比特 i（控制）对逻辑比特 i（目标）做 CNOT」的四条特征变换（《格点手术》5.2 节列过），对 i = 1 到 12 同时成立：一层 144 个物理 CNOT = 12 个并行的逻辑 CNOT。这就是 《格点手术》1.2 节说「横向 CNOT 对任何两块相同的 CSS 码成立」的证明——CSS 保证 X 检查只含 X、Z 检查只含 Z，四条规则才能分别套用。

Logical operators follow the same way. Atlas: “The same conjugation applies to corresponding logical Pauli pairs, giving the parallel logical CNOT action.” Take the X̄i of logical qubit i on the control block (some X string commuting with every Z check yet not a product of X checks, BB guide section 4.4); the first rule turns it into X̄icX̄it, and Z̄it becomes Z̄icZ̄it. These are exactly the four characteristic transformations of “CNOT from logical i (control) to logical i (target)” (listed in section 5.2 of “Lattice Surgery”), holding for i = 1 to 12 simultaneously: one layer of 144 physical CNOTs = 12 parallel logical CNOTs. This is the proof behind the statement in section 1.2 of “Lattice Surgery” that “transversal CNOT works for any two identical CSS codes”: CSS guarantees that X checks contain only X and Z checks only Z, so the four rules can be applied separately.

2.2成本字段：对齐、搬运、相关译码The cost field: alignment, transport, correlated decoding

记录 0 的成本原文：「one aligned pairwise physical-CNOT layer; module transport, alignment, and correlated decoding are additional architecture costs」。分号前是门本身：一层对齐的、逐对的物理 CNOT——144 个门同时做，深度 1，这是「横向」的全部含义。分号后是三笔另算的账。模块搬运与对齐：144 对比特要同时两两相邻或相连。表面码那篇1.2 节说过，可移动的中性原子阵列能把两块码叠到一起，固定布线的超导芯片做不到；gross 的两块各 144 个数据比特，还各带 144 个帮手比特，对齐的工程量随之放大。相关译码：CNOT 把控制块的 X 错误复印到目标块（规则一），把目标块的 Z 错误复印回控制块（规则四）——门做完，两块的错误不再独立，译码器必须把两块 288 个数据比特的症状一起译（译码器导读 4.4 节操作合同里「whether X/Z correlations … are present」这一项在此变成「块间相关」）。Atlas 的总结：「This is a code-space statement. A hardware implementation still needs aligned pairs, a fault model for correlated two-block errors, and a decoder that handles them.」人话：2.1 节证的是「码空间层面」的事实；在真机上，它还要一份两块联动的错误模型和一个会处理这种联动的译码器。引文 yoder2025tour（据文献扫描报告：Yoder 等，“Tour de Gross: A Modular Quantum Computer Based on Bivariate Bicycle Codes”，2025，arXiv 2506.03094，登记待补）「embeds this primitive inside a gross-family modular architecture」——把这个原语嵌进一个以 gross 家族为单元的模块化架构里。

Record 0's cost, verbatim: “one aligned pairwise physical-CNOT layer; module transport, alignment, and correlated decoding are additional architecture costs”. Before the semicolon is the gate itself: one layer of aligned, pairwise physical CNOTs, 144 gates at once, depth 1, which is all that “transversal” means. After the semicolon are three separately billed items. Module transport and alignment: 144 pairs of qubits must be simultaneously adjacent or connected. Section 1.2 of the surface-code guide noted that movable neutral-atom arrays can stack two code blocks while fixed-wiring superconducting chips cannot; two gross blocks have 144 data qubits each plus 144 helper qubits each, and the alignment engineering scales with them. Correlated decoding: the CNOT copies control-block X errors onto the target (rule one) and target-block Z errors back onto the control (rule four); after the gate the errors of the two blocks are no longer independent, and the decoder must decode the syndromes of both blocks' 288 data qubits jointly (the operational-contract item “whether X/Z correlations … are present” of section 4.4 of the decoders guide becomes “inter-block correlations” here). The Atlas sums up: “This is a code-space statement. A hardware implementation still needs aligned pairs, a fault model for correlated two-block errors, and a decoder that handles them.” Plainly: section 2.1 proved a fact “at the code-space level”; on a real machine it still needs a two-block error model and a decoder that handles it. The citation yoder2025tour (per the scout report: Yoder et al., “Tour de Gross: A Modular Quantum Computer Based on Bivariate Bicycle Codes”, 2025, arXiv 2506.03094, registration pending) “embeds this primitive inside a gross-family modular architecture”.

2.3它不能做什么：12 对同时，无法挑选What it cannot do: all 12 pairs at once, no selection

横向 CNOT 的效果是「第 i 个逻辑比特（控制块）→ 第 i 个逻辑比特（目标块），i = 1…12，同时」。它做不到三件事。第一，块内的门：控制块第 3 个与第 7 个逻辑比特之间的 CNOT，它没有任何机制。第二，挑选：只对第 3 对做 CNOT、其余 11 对不做——144 个物理 CNOT 是一起做的，拆不开。第三，任何单比特逻辑门：H、S 都不在这条记录里。Atlas 的卡片承诺表（第 5 章 5.4 节）把记录 0 的边界写成：承诺「aligned pairwise CNOT preserves two BB blocks」，不可推出「zero routing or decoder cost」。所以横向 CNOT 是 BB 门集里的第一块砖，不是全部；第 3、4 章讨论的自同构与手术，正是为了补「块内」与「挑选」这两个缺口。

The transversal CNOT's effect is “logical i (control block) → logical i (target block), for i = 1…12, simultaneously”. Three things it cannot do. First, gates within a block: a CNOT between logical 3 and logical 7 of the control block has no mechanism here. Second, selection: a CNOT on pair 3 only, leaving the other 11 pairs alone; the 144 physical CNOTs are applied together and cannot be separated. Third, any single-qubit logical gate: neither H nor S is in this record. The card's promise table (section 5.4 in Chapter 5) states record 0's boundary as: promise “aligned pairwise CNOT preserves two BB blocks”, excluded inference “zero routing or decoder cost”. So the transversal CNOT is the first brick of the BB gate set, not the whole of it; the automorphisms and surgery of Chapters 3 and 4 exist precisely to fill the “within-block” and “selection” gaps.

关键想法Key idea

四条共轭规则 + 两块相同 + CSS = 横向 CNOT：控制块的 X 检查复印成「自己乘目标块的同号检查」，目标块的 Z 检查复印成「自己乘控制块的同号检查」，全都留在两块的稳定子群里；对逻辑算符同理，得到 12 个并行逻辑 CNOT。成本：一层 144 个对齐的物理 CNOT 之外，还要搬运对齐、以及处理块间相关错误的联合译码器。它不能做块内门、不能挑对、不能做单比特门。

Four conjugation rules + two identical blocks + CSS = transversal CNOT: control X checks are copied into “themselves times the same-numbered target check”, target Z checks into “themselves times the same-numbered control check”, all staying inside the two-block stabilizer group; likewise for logical operators, giving 12 parallel logical CNOTs. Cost: beyond one layer of 144 aligned physical CNOTs, transport and alignment plus a joint decoder for inter-block correlated errors. It cannot do within-block gates, cannot select pairs, cannot do single-qubit gates.

把 gross 的两层比特整体往右挪一格，每条检查变成邻格的那条检查，稳定子群原封不动——这叫码的自同构。72 个平移都是自同构，可 Atlas 一个也不叫「逻辑门」。这一章先用矩阵恒等式和一个具体编号证明平移保持码，再照录 Atlas 认证一个门的五步，说清卡在哪一步；然后读记录 1（折叠横向门）与记录 2（自同构导出的 Clifford 门），看文献是怎样把「对称」变成「门」的，以及为什么这些结果不自动属于 gross。 Shift both gross layers one site to the right and every check becomes the neighbouring site's check, leaving the stabilizer group untouched: an automorphism of the code. All 72 translations are automorphisms, yet the Atlas calls none of them a “logical gate”. This chapter first proves with a matrix identity and one concrete index that a translation preserves the code, then copies the Atlas's five steps for certifying a gate and shows where it gets stuck; then it reads record 1 (fold-transversal gates) and record 2 (automorphism-derived Cliffords) to see how the literature turns “symmetry” into “gate”, and why those results do not automatically belong to gross.

3.1平移保持稳定子：一个恒等式，一个编号Translations preserve the stabilizers: one identity, one index

BB 教程 2.2 节的两个平移 x、y 是 72 × 72 的置换矩阵；A、B、Aᵀ、Bᵀ 都是 x、y 的多项式，所以都与 x、y 对易。Atlas 原文：「Let P be either torus translation x or y, and apply P to both data blocks. Since A, B, Aᵀ, Bᵀ all commute with P, HX diag(P,P) = P HX, HZ diag(P,P) = P HZ. Left multiplication by P merely permutes check rows. Thus the physical qubit permutation maps the stabilizer group to itself. reproduce.py checks these exact matrix identities for the gross instance.」人话：右边乘 diag(P,P) 是「把左层和右层的比特都按 P 重新编号」，左边乘 P 是「把检查的行重新编号」；两者相等，说明比特挪过之后得到的检查集合，只是原来的检查集合换了个顺序。reproduce.py 第 116–120 行对 x 和 y 各断言了这两条恒等式（模 2）。

The two translations x, y of BB guide section 2.2 are 72 × 72 permutation matrices; A, B, Aᵀ, Bᵀ are polynomials in x, y and therefore commute with them. The Atlas text: “Let P be either torus translation x or y, and apply P to both data blocks. Since A, B, Aᵀ, Bᵀ all commute with P, HX diag(P,P) = P HX, HZ diag(P,P) = P HZ. Left multiplication by P merely permutes check rows. Thus the physical qubit permutation maps the stabilizer group to itself. reproduce.py checks these exact matrix identities for the gross instance.” Plainly: multiplying by diag(P,P) on the right “renumbers the qubits of both layers by P”, multiplying by P on the left “renumbers the check rows”; their equality says the set of checks obtained after moving the qubits is the original set in a different order. Lines 116–120 of reproduce.py assert both identities (mod 2) for x and for y.

到实验台上看这件事。预设 gross，点格子 (0,0) 的 X 检查（圆点），记下六条腿的位置；再点右边相邻格子 (1,0) 的 X 检查：同一个六腿图形整体右移一格，读数里的比特编号各加 6。往上点 (0,1) 的检查是 y 的效果，编号各加 1。x 的 12 个幂与 y 的 6 个幂组合出 12 × 6 = 72 个平移（这是 BB 教程说的 x12 = y6 = 1 的直接推论），每一个都把 72 条 X 检查置换成 72 条 X 检查、72 条 Z 检查置换成 72 条 Z 检查。

3.3记录 1：折叠横向门，对称是一种选码条件Record 1: fold-transversal gates, symmetry as a code-selection condition

记录 1 的门叫「selected logical Clifford generators」，机制 fold-transversal，引文 eberhardt2024fold（据文献扫描报告：Eberhardt 与 Steffan，“Logical Operators and Fold-Transversal Gates of Bivariate Bicycle Codes”，2024，arXiv 2407.03973，登记待补）。Atlas 的解释：「A fold pairs qubits related by a reflection or other involution so that constant-depth physical Cliffords implement a logical operation.」人话：找一个把环面「对折」的对称（反射之类，做两次回到原处的那种），把折线两侧对应的比特配成对，对每一对做同一个两比特 Clifford 门（或对折线上的比特做单比特门）——深度仍是常数，但门不再是「第 i 对第 i」，而是「第 i 对它的镜像」。文献为对称的 BB 例子构造了显式的逻辑基与这样的门，Atlas 点名两个：[[98,6,12]] 与 [[162,8,12]]——注意这两个都不是 Atlas 的 BB 实例，也不在 Table 3 里。

Record 1's gate is “selected logical Clifford generators”, mechanism fold-transversal, citation eberhardt2024fold (per the scout report: Eberhardt and Steffan, “Logical Operators and Fold-Transversal Gates of Bivariate Bicycle Codes”, 2024, arXiv 2407.03973, registration pending). The Atlas explanation: “A fold pairs qubits related by a reflection or other involution so that constant-depth physical Cliffords implement a logical operation.” Plainly: find a symmetry that “folds” the torus (a reflection or similar, one that returns you home when done twice), pair the qubits mirrored across the fold line, and apply the same two-qubit Clifford to every pair (or a single-qubit gate on the qubits on the line); the depth is still constant, but the gate is no longer “i with i” but “i with its mirror image”. The paper constructs explicit logical bases and such gates for symmetric BB examples, of which the Atlas names two: [[98,6,12]] and [[162,8,12]]; note that neither is an Atlas BB instance, nor in Table 3.

Record 2's gate is “logical Clifford operations through automorphisms”, its mechanism likewise labelled fold-transversal on the card, citation sayginel2025automorphisms (Sayginel, Koutsioumpas, Webster, Rajput, Browne, “Fault-Tolerant Logical Clifford Gates from Code Automorphisms”, 2025, arXiv 2409.18175, full metadata registered). This turns “symmetry → gate” into a pipeline that can be run on any code. The same group also uses automorphisms for decoding (koutsioumpas2025autdec in section 3.5 of the decoders guide): the same symmetries, used once to move logical qubits and once to change decoding frames.

成本原文：「a certified Tanner-graph permutation and its physical routing; the induced symplectic action must be computed for the chosen block」。「for the chosen block」四个字是重点：流程是一般的，结果是逐块的——对 gross 跑一遍才有 gross 的门表；Atlas 没有替 gross 认证这一步，卡片的用词是「未在此认证」，不是「无人算过」（sayginel2025automorphisms 就报告了 gross 的逻辑作用群）。卡片承诺表把记录 2 的边界写成：承诺「a resolved logical action can implement Cliffords」，不可推出「every graph symmetry is useful」（第 4 步可能算出单位矩阵）。

The cost, verbatim: “a certified Tanner-graph permutation and its physical routing; the induced symplectic action must be computed for the chosen block”. The words “for the chosen block” are the point: the workflow is general, the results are per block; only running it on gross yields gross's gate table; the Atlas has not certified that step for gross — the card says “not certified there”, not “never computed” (sayginel2025automorphisms does report gross's logical-action group). The card's promise table states record 2's boundary as: promise “a resolved logical action can implement Cliffords”, excluded inference “every graph symmetry is useful” (step 4 may return the identity matrix).

关键想法Key idea

自同构是「保持码」的物理置换：gross 的 72 个平移都是（HX diag(P,P) = P HX，检查 0 → 检查 6）。门还差两问：算出 24 × 24 的辛作用（可能是单位、置换或缠绕的 Clifford），认证容错布线。记录 1 与 2 是文献对选定实例走完这两问的结果——对称是选码条件，结论逐块成立，gross 的门表在 Atlas 里尚未认证（文献里有计算，见上）。

An automorphism is a physical permutation that “preserves the code”: all 72 gross translations are (HX diag(P,P) = P HX, check 0 → check 6). A gate needs two more answers: compute the 24 × 24 symplectic action (which may be the identity, a permutation, or an entangling Clifford), and certify fault-tolerant routing. The surface code's boundary is a theorem: Bravyi–König proves that local constant-depth operations on 2D topological codes reach at most Clifford. The boundary the Atlas records is empirical: across the six-lens literature scan, no paper supplies a universal transversal gate set for gross. Plainly: for the surface code it is “proved impossible”, for gross a different theorem applies: Eastin–Knill holds for any code that detects single-qubit errors (d ≥ 2), needs no two-dimensional locality, and likewise rules out a universal transversal gate set for gross. The boundary the Atlas records is the empirical version of the same fact: no scanned paper supplies a universal transversal gate set for gross — the theorem will not change, the scan result may as the literature grows, but they point the same way. When you read “BB codes have transversal Clifford gates”, ask first: which subfamily, which block, which certificate.

5.3魔法态：非 Clifford 门的来源仍在码外Magic states: the non-Clifford gate still comes from outside the code

五条记录的 level 全是 Clifford。要通用，还差一个 T 门（或等价的非 Clifford 门）。Atlas 列出四条路：「magic-state injection/cultivation, switching, measurement-based resources, or another certified mechanism」。第一条是表面码架构的主流：先制备带噪的魔法态magic state：一个非稳定子态（例如 T 门作用在 |+⟩ 上得到的态）。有了它，用 Clifford 操作加测量就能实现 T 门；制备与提纯（蒸馏或培育）是逻辑计算开销的主项。，用 Clifford 操作提纯（蒸馏，或近年更省的「培育」cultivation），再通过门传送消耗它实现 T——《格点手术》1.3 节介绍过蒸馏。第二条是码切换（在有 T 门的码与 BB 码之间来回切）。第三条是基于测量的资源。文献扫描里 xu2025batched 的方案正是把「parallel magic-state cultivation」配给自对偶 BB 块——仍是魔法态路线，只是并行化了。intuition.md 的总结：「Non-Clifford completion still needs magic states, switching, surgery, or another certified resource.」人话：BB 把存储变便宜了 12 倍，没有把 T 门变便宜；在任何 BB 架构的总账里，T 门那一项仍要单列。

All five records have level Clifford. Universality still needs a T gate (or an equivalent non-Clifford gate). The Atlas lists four roads: “magic-state injection/cultivation, switching, measurement-based resources, or another certified mechanism”. In the literature scan, the proposal of xu2025batched pairs “parallel magic-state cultivation” with self-dual BB blocks, still the magic-state road, only parallelised. Replay. Turning the warehouse into a computer means answering three questions: which physical process preserves the code, what 24 × 24 symplectic action it induces on the 12 logicals, what fault-tolerance and routing resources certify it; k = 12 adds a fourth, addressability; the 5 Atlas records are all Clifford, all claimed, all about the family rather than gross (Chapter 1). Transversal CNOT: four conjugation rules copy a control X check into “itself times the same-numbered target check”, everything stays in the stabilizer group when the blocks are identical and CSS, 144 physical CNOTs = 12 parallel logical CNOTs; transport, alignment and inter-block correlated decoding are billed separately; it cannot do within-block gates or select pairs (Chapter 2). Translations are automorphisms: HX diag(P,P) = P HX, check 0 → check 6, 72 translations, verified by reproduce.py; but a gate needs five more steps, gross's symplectic action is not certified in the Atlas (the literature reports it) and could in general be the identity, a permutation or an entangling Clifford; records 1 and 2 are the literature's completion of those steps for selected instances, symmetry being a code-selection condition (Chapter 3). Surgery: measure inter-block product operators through a long-range ancilla system, five resources, the label surgery keeping them from hiding under “Clifford gate”; k = 12 makes “which operator to measure” a compiler question (Chapter 4).
