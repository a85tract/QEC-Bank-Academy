一条边：special_case_of → two_bga，另一端待建One edge: special_case_of → two_bga, far end not yet built

「BB 码和别的 qLDPC 码有关系」谁都会说。Atlas 要的是一句能被程序存档、能被人核对的话：关系的类型是什么、指向哪个条目、凭哪两篇文献这么说。这一章先看这句话的格式，再逐字读 BB 条目仅有的一条，最后解释导出页上那个灰色的 pending 标签是怎么来的、说的是什么、不说什么。 Anyone can say “BB codes are related to other qLDPC codes”. The Atlas wants a sentence a program can archive and a person can check: what type of relation, pointing at which entry, on the authority of which two papers. This chapter first shows the format of that sentence, then reads the BB entry's only one word by word, and finally explains where the grey pending chip on the exported page comes from, what it says, and what it does not.

1.1一条关系的四个字段，和它外面那层元数据The four fields of a relation, and the metadata around them

在源文件 atlas/bivariate_bicycle/card.yaml 里，「关系」是 relations 列表下的一条小记录，内容只有四个字段：type（关系的类型，取自一份受控词表——表面码条目用过 product_of 和 same_bulk_phase_as，本条目用的是 special_case_of，「是……的特例」）、target（指向的另一个 Atlas 条目的 id）、how（一句话说「怎么个特例」）、refs（一组文献键，每个键都应在文献注册表里登记——参考文献导读专讲这件事）。记录外面还包着机器用的一层：applies_to = eczoo-entity:qcga#collection，即这句话的主语是整个 BB 码家族——Atlas 把本条目映射到 Error Correction Zoo 的 qcga 实体——不是 gross 这一个实例；source_locator = bivariate_bicycle/card.yaml#/relations/0，它在源文件里的地址；source_sha256 = 91f961d0…，内容的指纹，改一个字母就对不上。在 card.yaml 里搜 "field": "relations" 会命中 2 处，但记录只有 1 条：一次在 /assertions 里，一次原样出现在 /scoped_facets/relations 里（后者是按侧面分好类的同一批断言，和表示阶梯导读 1.1 节数 16 处 8 条记录是同一个道理）。说人话：一条关系不是一句「有点像」，而是一句有主语、有类型、有出处、有指纹、能被程序核对的话。「有类型的关系」这个格式本身，《Surface Code · 关系》第 1 章有更从头的介绍，这里不重复。

In the source file atlas/bivariate_bicycle/card.yaml, a relation is a small record under the relations list with just four content fields: type (drawn from a controlled vocabulary; the surface-code entry uses product_of and same_bulk_phase_as, this entry uses special_case_of), target (the id of another Atlas entry), how (one sentence saying in what way it is a special case), and refs (bibkeys, each of which should be registered in the reference registry; the references guide is about exactly that). Around the record sits a layer of machine metadata: applies_to = eczoo-entity:qcga#collection, meaning the subject is the whole BB family, the Atlas mapping this entry to the Error Correction Zoo entity qcga, and not the single gross instance; source_locator = bivariate_bicycle/card.yaml#/relations/0, its address in the source; and source_sha256 = 91f961d0…, a fingerprint of the content that one changed letter would break. Searching card.yaml for "field": "relations" hits 2 places, yet there is only 1 record: once under /assertions and once more, verbatim, under /scoped_facets/relations (the same assertions sorted by facet, the same reason the representation-ladder guide, §1.1 counts 16 hits for 8 records). In plain words: a relation is not “sort of similar”; it is a sentence with a subject, a type, sources, a fingerprint, and a program that can check it. For the format of a typed relation from scratch, see Chapter 1 of “Surface Code · Relations”; this page does not repeat it.

关系relation = ( type , target , how , refs[ ] )
主语subject = eczoo-entity:qcga#collection
位置locator = bivariate_bicycle/card.yaml#/relations/0
sha256 = 91f961d0…

记号：type、target、how、refs = 记录的四个字段名；refs[ ] 的方括号表示它是一个列表（可以有多个键）；qcga = Error Correction Zoo 给 BB 码家族的条目代号；#collection = 「这一整族」，与 #object（单个码）、#subject（页面里被构造器实际实现的那个对象）相区分；sha256 = 一种内容哈希算法，输出 64 个十六进制字符，这里只写前 8 个。

Notation: type, target, how, refs = the record's four field names; the brackets in refs[ ] mean it is a list (several keys allowed); qcga = the Error Correction Zoo id of the BB family; #collection = “the whole family”, as opposed to #object (one code) or #subject (the object the constructor actually builds on a page); sha256 = a content-hash algorithm whose output is 64 hexadecimal characters, of which only the first 8 are shown.

先回想 BB 的配方（《BB 码 · 高中数学版》3.1 节）：在环 R = GF(2)[x, y]/(xl − 1, ym − 1) 里取两个稀疏多项式 A、B，每个多项式变成一张 lm × lm 的 0/1 矩阵，拼成 HX = [A | B]、HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]，对易靠 AB = BA。两块群代数码（two-block group-algebra code，简称 2BGA）把这条配方里唯一有「BB 味」的东西——那个环 R——换成任意有限群 G 的群代数group algebra GF(2)[G]：形如「若干个群元素的 0/1 组合」的表达式全体，加法是逐项 XOR，乘法按群的乘法展开。BB 的环 R 就是 G = Z_l × Z_m 的群代数：群元素 (a, b) 写成单项式 x^a y^b。 GF(2)[G]：元素是「若干个群元素的 0/1 组合」，取两个这样的元素 a、b，仍然拼成两块 [A | B] 与 [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]。当 G = Zl × Zm——环面上的平移群，元素 (a, b) 表示「右移 a 格、上移 b 格」，两个平移的先后顺序无关——群代数正是 R，群元素 (a, b) 正是单项式 xayb，于是得到的就是 BB 码。这就是 how 的前半句。后半句在划边界：一般 2BGA 可以用别的群，包括不可交换的群（那时 AB = BA 不再自动成立，要靠「一块左乘、一块右乘」来保证对易，《qLDPC 码 · 高中数学版》4.4 节讲了这个技巧），因此也就没有「x、y 两个变量的多项式」这种写法。说人话：BB 是一个大家族里、群恰好是「环面平移」的那一支；家族里别的成员长得不像多项式，但两块的骨架是一样的。

First recall the BB recipe (§3.1 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition”): in the ring R = GF(2)[x, y]/(xl − 1, ym − 1) take two sparse polynomials A and B, turn each into an lm × lm binary matrix, assemble HX = [A | B] and HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ], and let AB = BA do the commuting. A two-block group-algebra code (2BGA) replaces the one ingredient of that recipe that tastes of BB, the ring R, by the group algebraGF(2)[G]: all expressions of the form “a 0/1 combination of group elements”, added by termwise XOR and multiplied by expanding with the group's product. The BB ring R is the group algebra of G = Z_l × Z_m: the group element (a, b) is written as the monomial x^a y^b. GF(2)[G] of an arbitrary finite group G: its elements are 0/1 combinations of group elements; pick two of them, a and b, and assemble the same two blocks [A | B] and [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]. When G = Zl × Zm, the translation group of the torus, whose element (a, b) means “move a cells right and b cells up” in either order, the group algebra is exactly R and the element (a, b) is exactly the monomial xayb, so what comes out is a BB code. That is the first half of the how. The second half draws the boundary: general 2BGA codes may use other groups, including non-commutative ones (then AB = BA no longer comes for free and commutation is secured by “one block multiplies from the left, the other from the right”, the trick of §4.4 of “qLDPC Codes · High-School Edition”), so there is no “polynomial in two variables x, y” to write down. In plain words: BB is the branch of a large family whose group happens to be “torus translations”; other members do not look like polynomials, but the two-block skeleton is the same.

Notation: G = a finite group (a set of objects that can be multiplied, with an identity and inverses, e.g. all torus translations); |G| = the number of its elements; GF(2)[G] = its group algebra (above); a, b = the two chosen elements of the group algebra, A, B = their |G| × |G| matrices (in BB, the matrices of the two polynomials, §2.3); Zl × Zm = pairs of an integer mod l and an integer mod m, i.e. all translations of the l × m torus; l, m = cell counts along the two torus directions; R = the BB polynomial ring (§2.3 of the same guide, “a glance at the proper name”); n = number of data qubits = 2|G|, two qubits per group element (per cell); ∈ reads “belongs to”.

1.3为什么另一端是 pending：边画好了，点还没建Why the far end is pending: the edge is drawn, the node is not built

导出页上，two_bga 不是一个链接，而是一段等宽的代码文字，旁边一个灰色小标签 pending。规则写在站点构建脚本 tools/site/build.py 里，只有一句：目标 id 在「已存在的条目」集合里，就渲染成链接，状态记 resolved；不在，就渲染成代码文字加 pending 标签。Atlas 目前没有 atlas/two_bga/card.yaml 这个条目。实体本体的一次评审记录（2026-08-31）把它列在「冻结的十二条指向未来条目的关系」清单里，同一张清单上还有 bacon_shor → compass_code_2d、color_666 → color_code_2d、haah_cubic → generalized_haah 等——也就是说，「先画边、后建点」在 Atlas 里是一种正常状态，不是 BB 条目的疏漏。说人话：这条边已经画好，how 写了、两篇文献挂了、指纹算了；只是另一端的点还没建。将来有人写出 two_bga 的卡片，BB 这张卡一个字都不用改，重新导出，代码文字自动变成链接。反过来也要读对：pending 说的是目标条目的状态，不是这条断言的可信度——断言本身是完整的。

On the exported page, two_bga is not a link but a piece of monospace text with a grey chip, pending. The rule lives in the site builder tools/site/build.py and is one sentence long: if the target id is in the set of existing entries, render a link and record the state resolved; otherwise render code text plus the pending chip. The Atlas currently has no atlas/two_bga/card.yaml. An entity-ontology review record (2026-08-31) lists it among “the frozen twelve future-entry relations”, alongside bacon_shor → compass_code_2d, color_666 → color_code_2d, haah_cubic → generalized_haah and others, so “edge first, node later” is a normal state in the Atlas, not an oversight of the BB entry. In plain words: the edge is drawn, the how is written, the two papers are attached, the fingerprint is computed; only the node at the far end is missing. When someone writes the two_bga card, not one character of the BB card changes; re-export, and the code text becomes a link. Read it the right way round, too: pending describes the state of the target entry, not the trustworthiness of the assertion, which is complete.

为什么只有一条？bicycle、超图积、提升积都不是 typed 边Why only one? Neither bicycle, HGP nor lifted product is a typed edge

第 2 章会讲 BB 的家谱：bicycle → 广义 bicycle → BB，以及 BB ⊂ 2BGA ⊂ 提升积。这些都是文献里明明白白的话，为什么卡片只登记了「2BGA 的特例」这一条？Level-2 页「变体与边界地图」给了理由：「广义 bicycle、2BGA、提升积、双变量 bicycle 在历史上和代数上互相重叠，但它们不是可以互换的类型名」；「精确的父类是可交换两块群代数码」。也就是说，Atlas 只把精确的那一层父子关系写成 typed 边，把历史沿革和更宽的包含关系留在散文里。另一个细节可以佐证：Atlas 有一个 bicycle 条目（第 3 章），但 BB 卡片没有到它的边——「祖先」不是受控词表里的一种关系类型。还有一段来历：2026-07-05 的项目计划稿里，这条关系最初写的 how 是「bivariate bicycle codes are 2-block group-algebra codes over Z_l x Z_m」，refs 只有 bravyi2024high；发布的卡片把 how 改得更精确（加上「general 2BGA codes may use other groups…」的后半句），refs 换成了真正定义 2BGA 的两篇。

Chapter 2 tells the family tree: bicycle → generalized bicycle → BB, and BB ⊂ 2BGA ⊂ lifted product. All of that is plainly in the literature, so why does the card record only “a special case of 2BGA”? The Level-2 page “variant and boundary map” gives the reason: “‘generalized bicycle’, ‘2BGA’, ‘lifted product’ and ‘bivariate bicycle’ overlap historically and algebraically, but they are not interchangeable type names”; “the exact parent is abelian two-block group-algebra code”. The Atlas, in other words, writes only the exact parent as a typed edge and keeps the history and the wider inclusions as prose. A second detail supports this: the Atlas has a bicycle entry (Chapter 3), yet the BB card has no edge to it; “ancestor” is not a relation type in the controlled vocabulary. And a piece of history: in the project plan of 2026-07-05 this relation's how first read “bivariate bicycle codes are 2-block group-algebra codes over Z_l x Z_m” with bravyi2024high as its only ref; the shipped card sharpened the how (adding the second half, “general 2BGA codes may use other groups…”) and replaced the refs by the two papers that actually define 2BGA.

关键想法Key idea

BB 条目在 Atlas 地图上只有一条边：它是两块群代数码在 G = Zl × Zm 上的特例。边的四个字段都在、指纹也在，只是目标条目还没建，所以标 pending。这条边的方向、主语（整个家族）和边界（一般 2BGA 未必是多项式）都写在 how 里，一个字都不多。

The BB entry has one edge on the Atlas map: it is the special case of two-block group-algebra codes at G = Zl × Zm. All four fields and the fingerprint are present; only the target entry is unbuilt, hence pending. The edge's direction, subject (the whole family) and boundary (general 2BGA need not be polynomial) are all in the how, with not one word to spare.

家谱：从一只轮子到任意有限群The family tree: from one wheel to an arbitrary finite group

一条 typed 边只说了「父类是谁」。要理解为什么父类是它，得把家谱摊开：2004 年一只轮子的 bicycle 码，2019 年两只不同轮子的广义 bicycle，2024 年两个变量的 BB，以及把「环」换成「群代数」之后的 2BGA 与更一般的提升积。这一章每一代都用 Atlas 里真有的实例算一遍——[[6,2,2]]、[[18,2,3]]、[[144,12,12]]——并在每一步标明：这一步在卡片上是 typed 边、是散文，还是什么都没有。 A typed edge only says who the parent is. To see why the parent is this one, unfold the family tree: the one-wheel bicycle code of 2004, the two-different-wheels generalized bicycle of 2019, the two-variable BB of 2024, and, once the ring is replaced by a group algebra, 2BGA and the still more general lifted product. Each generation is computed here on an instance the Atlas actually ships, [[6,2,2]], [[18,2,3]], [[144,12,12]], and at every step we say whether, on the card, it is a typed edge, prose, or nothing at all.

2.1一只轮子：bicycle 码（2004）One wheel: the bicycle code (2004)

MacKay、Mitchison 与 McFadden 的「Sparse Graph Codes for Quantum Error-Correction」（mackay2004sparse，2004，已登记，arXiv quant-ph/0304161）只用一个变量造码：在环 GF(2)[x]/(xl − 1) 里取一个稀疏多项式 C——也就是一张 l × l 的循环矩阵circulant matrix：每一行都是上一行右移一格的方阵；等价于一个变量的多项式。任意两个循环矩阵对易。——令 H = [C | Cᵀ]，并让 HX = HZ = H。对易条件自动成立：H Hᵀ = C Cᵀ + Cᵀ C = 2 C Cᵀ = 0，因为同一个变量的两个多项式一定对易（C 与 Cᵀ 都是 x 的多项式），而 GF(2) 里 1 + 1 = 0。「自行车」指的就是 C 与 Cᵀ 这两只轮子。Atlas 的 bicycle 条目（T1）用 C = 1 + x、l = 3 给出见证实例 [[6,2,2]]：n = 6 个数据比特、k = 2 个逻辑比特、码距 d = 2，三个数全部 verified。两只轮子一模一样，所以它只能探测、不能纠正一个错误——但它把「同一个环上的多项式自动对易」这个想法压缩到了六个比特里，《BB 码 · 高中数学版》2.4 节正是从它讲起。在本条目的 card.yaml 里，mackay2004sparse 只出现在条目级的 refs 列表里，不绑定任何侧面记录：家谱这件事，Atlas 写在 Level-2 页「变体与边界地图」的散文里，不是一条 typed 边。

MacKay, Mitchison and McFadden, “Sparse Graph Codes for Quantum Error-Correction” (mackay2004sparse, 2004, registered, arXiv quant-ph/0304161), build codes with one variable: in the ring GF(2)[x]/(xl − 1) take a sparse polynomial C, i.e. an l × l circulant matrixA square matrix each of whose rows is the previous row shifted one step right; equivalent to a polynomial in one variable. Any two circulants commute., set H = [C | Cᵀ], and let HX = HZ = H. Commutation is automatic: H Hᵀ = C Cᵀ + Cᵀ C = 2 C Cᵀ = 0, because two polynomials in the same variable always commute (C and Cᵀ are both polynomials in x) and 1 + 1 = 0 in GF(2). The “bicycle” is the pair of wheels C and Cᵀ. The Atlas entry bicycle (T1) uses C = 1 + x with l = 3 for its witness [[6,2,2]]: n = 6 data qubits, k = 2 logical qubits, distance d = 2, all three verified. The two wheels are identical, so it can only detect, not correct, a single error, yet it compresses the idea “polynomials over one ring commute for free” into six qubits, which is where §2.4 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition” starts. In this entry's card.yaml, mackay2004sparse appears only in the entry-level refs list and is bound to no facet record: the Atlas writes the ancestry as prose on the Level-2 page “variant and boundary map”, not as a typed edge.

Notation: C = a sparse polynomial in one variable x, i.e. an l × l circulant; Cᵀ = its transpose, every translation reversed (x ↦ x−1, §2.3); l = length of the cycle (number of cells); H = a parity-check matrix, [C | Cᵀ] the two matrices side by side; n, k, d = data qubits, logical qubits, distance (§4.4 and §5.8 of “From High-School Math to the Toric Code”); n = 2l because each block has l columns.

2.2两只不同的轮子：广义 bicycle（2019/2021）Two different wheels: generalized bicycle (2019/2021)

Panteleev 与 Kalachev 的「Degenerate Quantum LDPC Codes With Good Finite Length Performance」（panteleev2021degenerate，2021，已登记，arXiv 1904.02703）把两只轮子换成两个不同的多项式 a、b（仍然只有一个变量 x），并第一次写下 BB 后来照抄的两块格式：HX = [A | B]，HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]——「交换再转置」。对易证明和 BB 一模一样：HXHZᵀ = AB + BA = 2AB = 0，只用「一个变量的多项式互相对易」。这篇论文同时做了三件事：定义广义 bicycle（generalized bicycle，GB）码、对有限长度做搜索、提出 BP-OSD 译码器。所以在本条目的卡片上，这个键被 decoders[0]（bp-osd，status standard）引用，而不是被关系引用：它对 BB 的贡献既是祖先，也是工具。Atlas 目前没有独立的「广义 bicycle」条目，也就没有可以指向的 target。说人话：从 bicycle 到 GB，是「两只一样的轮子」变成「两只可以不同的轮子」；从 GB 到 BB，是「一个方向的圈」变成「两个方向的环面」。两步都保留了同一个证明。

Panteleev and Kalachev, “Degenerate Quantum LDPC Codes With Good Finite Length Performance” (panteleev2021degenerate, 2021, registered, arXiv 1904.02703), replace the two wheels by two different polynomials a and b (still in one variable x) and write down, for the first time, the two-block format BB later copies: HX = [A | B], HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ], “swap, then transpose”. The commutation proof is identical to BB's: HXHZᵀ = AB + BA = 2AB = 0, using only that polynomials in one variable commute. The paper does three things at once: it defines generalized bicycle (GB) codes, searches finite lengths, and introduces the BP-OSD decoder. On this entry's card the key is therefore cited by decoders[0] (bp-osd, status standard), not by the relation: its contribution to BB is both ancestor and tool. The Atlas has no separate generalized-bicycle entry, so there is no target to point at. In plain words: bicycle to GB turns “two identical wheels” into “two wheels that may differ”; GB to BB turns “a cycle in one direction” into “a torus in two”. Both steps keep the same proof.

相切不是关系Touching is not a relation

BB 家族与 HGP 家族在整个周期环面码系列上重合（A = 1 + x、B = 1 + y、l = m = L；[[18,2,3]] 是 L = 3 那一个），但 BB 卡片上没有到 hgp、也没有到 toric 的边。「两族共享一个系列」和「一族是另一族的特例」是两句强度不同的话：gross 不是任何超图积（超图积的 n = n₁n₂ + m₁m₂ 是两个因子尺寸的乘积之和，144 = 2 × 72 那种「一格两比特」的结构来自提升，不来自乘积，《qLDPC 码 · 高中数学版》4.3 节）。toric 条目的 same_bulk_phase_as → surface、product_of → hgp 是它自己的边，不会因为 toric_L3 也是 BB 实例就传给 BB。

The BB and HGP families coincide on the whole periodic toric series (A = 1 + x, B = 1 + y, l = m = L; [[18,2,3]] is the L = 3 member), yet the BB card has no edge to hgp and none to toric. “Two families share a series” and “one family is a special case of the other” are sentences of different strength: gross is not a hypergraph product of anything (an HGP has n = n₁n₂ + m₁m₂, a sum of products of factor sizes; the “two qubits per cell” structure 144 = 2 × 72 comes from lifting, not from a product, §4.3 of “qLDPC Codes · High-School Edition”). The toric entry's same_bulk_phase_as → surface and product_of → hgp are its own edges and do not transfer to BB because toric_L3 happens to be a BB instance too.

2.4更大的家族：两块群代数码与提升积The larger family: two-block group-algebra codes and lifted products

现在往上走两级。第一级，从环到群代数。1.2 节已经说过：把 R 换成任意有限群 G 的群代数 GF(2)[G]，两块骨架不动，就得到 2BGA 码（wang2023abelian）。BB 是 G = Zl × Zm 的可交换特例，这就是卡片上唯一的那条 typed 边。第二级，从 1 × 1 到任意大小的底矩阵。lin2023twoblock 把 2BGA 放进提升积lifted product（Panteleev–Kalachev）：超图积的模板里，底矩阵的每个 0/1 元素换成环 R（或群代数 GF(2)[G]）里的一个元素，「复制」时带上平移；转置附带环的反演。超图积是 R = GF(2) 的特例；BB 是底矩阵 1 × 1、R 为环面多项式环的特例。里：它们是「最小的提升积块」——底矩阵只有 1 × 1。《qLDPC 码 · 高中数学版》4.2 节从超图积的模板把这一点推了出来：两个 1 × 1 的底矩阵 [a]、[b]，超图积模板给出 1·1 + 1·1 = 2 个「格」，每个格提升成 |G| 个比特，n = 2|G|，HX = [a | b]，HZ = [bᵀ | aᵀ]——两个多项式、两层比特、交换转置，全部从模板里掉出来；|G| = 72 时 n = 144，正是 gross 的比特数。于是有一条包含链：BB ⊂ 2BGA ⊂ 提升积，每一步都是「特例」，不是「同义词」。表示扫描报告把这一点写成一条防错规则：它「防止把 BB、HGP、lifted product 当作同义词」。

Now climb two levels. Level one, from ring to group algebra. As §1.2 said: replace R by the group algebra GF(2)[G] of any finite group G, keep the two-block skeleton, and you have a 2BGA code (wang2023abelian). BB is the commutative case G = Zl × Zm, which is the card's single typed edge. Level two, from 1 × 1 to base matrices of any size. lin2023twoblock places 2BGA inside the lifted productLifted product (Panteleev–Kalachev): in the hypergraph-product template, every 0/1 entry of the base matrices is replaced by an element of a ring R (or a group algebra GF(2)[G]), so “copying” carries a translation; transposition carries the ring's inversion. HGP is the case R = GF(2); BB is the case of 1 × 1 base matrices with R the torus polynomial ring.: they are “the smallest lifted-product blocks”, with 1 × 1 base matrices. §4.2 of “qLDPC Codes · High-School Edition” derives this from the HGP template: two 1 × 1 base matrices [a], [b] give 1·1 + 1·1 = 2 “cells”, each lifted to |G| qubits, n = 2|G|, HX = [a | b], HZ = [bᵀ | aᵀ]: two polynomials, two layers, swapped transposes, all falling out of the template; at |G| = 72, n = 144, the gross qubit count. Hence a chain of inclusions, BB ⊂ 2BGA ⊂ lifted product, each step a special case, none a synonym. The representations scout report states it as a guard: it “prevents treating ‘BB’, ‘HGP’, and ‘lifted product’ as synonyms”.

关键想法Key idea

家谱有两条线：历史线（一只轮子 → 两只轮子 → 两个变量）和代数线（环面群 → 任意群 → 任意底矩阵）。BB 站在两条线的交点。Atlas 只把代数线上精确的下一级——2BGA——写成 typed 边；历史线留在散文里；与超图积的关系是共享整个周期环面码系列（A = 1 + x、B = 1 + y、l = m = L）。读文献时，凡看到「BB 就是提升积 / 就是 HGP」这类同义化的说法，都该按这张表纠正成「是……的特例」或「共享一个系列」。

The tree has two lines: the historical one (one wheel → two wheels → two variables) and the algebraic one (torus group → any group → any base matrix). BB stands at their crossing. The Atlas writes only the exact next level on the algebraic line, 2BGA, as a typed edge; the historical line stays prose; the link to hypergraph products is a shared series, the periodic toric codes (A = 1 + x, B = 1 + y, l = m = L). Whenever a paper says “BB is just a lifted product / just an HGP”, correct it by this table to “is a special case of” or “shares a series with”.

Atlas 里叫「BB」的东西不止一张卡。除了本条目（家族卡，T3），还有七张：bb72、bb90、bb108、bb288、bb5、bicycle、gross。这一章说清每张卡是什么层级、记了哪几个数字、哪个数字带哪个证据词，以及它们和家族卡是什么关系——答案有点意外：在地图上，这八张卡之间目前没有任何一条 typed 边；把它们连在一起的是同一台构造器。 More than one card in the Atlas is called “BB”. Besides this entry (the family card, T3) there are seven: bb72, bb90, bb108, bb288, bb5, bicycle, gross. This chapter states each card's tier, which numbers it records and with which evidence word, and how it relates to the family card. The answer is a little surprising: on the map there is currently no typed edge at all among the eight; what ties them together is the same constructor.

3.2成员与家族之间：不是边，是同一台构造器Between members and family: not an edge but the same constructor

七张成员卡的「关系」侧面都写着同一句：「Not yet surveyed at T1; this absence is not a negative literature conclusion.」所以「bb72 是一个 BB 码」这句话，在 Atlas 地图上今天不是一条 typed 边，而是你从两处读出来的事实：成员卡的描述（「A fixed 72-qubit polynomial block」，一个固定的 72 比特多项式块），以及它的构造器——同一条配方 HX = [A | B]、HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]。《BB 码 · 高中数学版》第 5 章对八个条目逐个重算了 n 与 k：bb72 两个秩各 30，k = 72 − 60 = 12；bb90 各 41，k = 90 − 82 = 8；bb108 各 50，k = 108 − 100 = 8；bb288 各 138，k = 288 − 276 = 12；bb5 各 13，k = 30 − 26 = 4。说人话：地图上这八个点排在一起，是因为它们长得像、算法一样，不是因为有人画了线。这和表面码那边刚好相反：表面码的亲戚们（XZZX、扭结缺陷……）各有条目、也各无边，但表面码自己至少有两条边（《Surface Code · 关系》1.2 节）；BB 家族则是一条边指向家族之外，家族之内一条也没有。

All seven member cards' Relations facets carry the same sentence: “Not yet surveyed at T1; this absence is not a negative literature conclusion.” So “bb72 is a BB code” is, on today's Atlas map, not a typed edge but a fact you read off two places: the member card's description (“A fixed 72-qubit polynomial block”) and its constructor, the same recipe HX = [A | B], HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]. Chapter 5 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition” recomputes n and k for all eight: bb72 has both ranks 30, k = 72 − 60 = 12; bb90 both 41, k = 90 − 82 = 8; bb108 both 50, k = 108 − 100 = 8; bb288 both 138, k = 288 − 276 = 12; bb5 both 13, k = 30 − 26 = 4. In plain words: the eight points sit together on the map because they look alike and compute alike, not because someone drew lines. This is the mirror image of the surface-code situation: the surface code's relatives (XZZX, twist defects, …) each have an entry and no edge, but the surface code itself has at least two edges (“Surface Code · Relations”, §1.2); the BB family has one edge pointing outside the family and none inside it.

家族内部还有一层代数上的亲缘，同样没有登记成边：bb72、bb108 与 gross 用完全相同的 A = x³ + y + y²、B = y³ + x + x²，只是环面不同——6 × 6、9 × 6、12 × 6——商空间 R/(A, B) 的维数分别是 6、4、6，所以 k 是 12、8、12（4.3 节「三项不决定 k」与5.5 节）。「同样的多项式、不同的环面」是一种真实的关系，但受控词表里没有这种类型，Atlas 就不写；它留在推导页里供人核对。

There is a further algebraic kinship inside the family, likewise not recorded as an edge: bb72, bb108 and gross use exactly the same A = x³ + y + y², B = y³ + x + x², differing only in the torus, 6 × 6, 9 × 6, 12 × 6, so the quotient R/(A, B) has dimension 6, 4, 6 and k is 12, 8, 12 (§4.3, “three terms do not fix k”, and §5.5). “Same polynomials, different torus” is a real relationship, but the controlled vocabulary has no such type, so the Atlas does not write it; it stays on the derivation pages for anyone to check.

3.3「gross」为什么有两张卡Why “gross” has two cards

同一张 144 比特的矩阵在 Atlas 里出现两次。在家族卡上，它是实例 gross：n = 144 verified、k = 12 verified、d = 12 bounded——两份证书，文献下界加种子 5 的见证上界。在成员卡 gross（T1，映射到 eczoo 的 gross 实体）上，它只有 n = 144 verified、k = 12 verified，码距一栏显示「?」：T1 卡只重算秩，不承诺码距，成员卡的描述也明说「Decoder, operation, relation, and realization facets remain unsurveyed」。说人话：同一个对象，家族卡上是「带两份证书的实例」，成员卡上是「只验证了骨架的条目」。引用 d = 12 时要指向家族卡的实例，不要指向成员卡。两张卡之间同样没有 typed 边——参数导读 4.2 节从实例表的角度把这件事讲全了，这里不重复。

The same 144-qubit matrix appears twice in the Atlas. On the family card it is the instance gross: n = 144 verified, k = 12 verified, d = 12 bounded, two certificates, the literature lower bound and the seed-5 witness upper bound. On the member card gross (T1, mapped to the eczoo entity gross) it has only n = 144 verified and k = 12 verified, with “?” in the distance column: a T1 card recomputes ranks and promises no distance, and its description says so, “Decoder, operation, relation, and realization facets remain unsurveyed”. In plain words: one object, “an instance with two certificates” on the family card and “an entry with only its skeleton verified” on the member card. When you cite d = 12, point at the family card's instance, not the member card. There is no typed edge between the two cards either; §4.2 of the parameters guide covers this from the instance table's side, so it is not repeated here.

关键想法Key idea

Atlas 里的 BB 家族是「一张 T3 的家族卡 + 七张 T1/T0 的成员卡」，成员资格写在构造器里，不写在边里。读任何一张成员卡，先看层级：成员卡上 n 与 k 都是 verified；d 则分三种——bicycle 与 bb5 穷举 verified，bb72 / bb90 / bb108 / bb288 只是 claimed，gross 成员卡上根本没有。

The BB family in the Atlas is “one T3 family card plus seven T1/T0 member cards”; membership is written in the constructor, not in edges. Reading any member card, check the tier first: on the member cards n and k are verified, while d comes in three kinds — exhaustively verified for bicycle and bb5, merely claimed for bb72 / bb90 / bb108 / bb288, and absent altogether on the gross member card.

4.1变量数与多项式限制：多变量，互素子类Number of variables and polynomial restrictions: multivariate, the coprime subclass

多变量。Voss、Xian、Haug 与 Bharti「Multivariate Bicycle Codes」（voss2025multivariate，2025，arXiv 2406.19151，登记待补）在 x、y 之外加第三个独立的循环变量 z：环变成 GF(2)[x, y, z]/(xl − 1, ym − 1, zp − 1)，群变成 Zl × Zm × Zp。它仍是可交换群代数的亲戚（所以仍在 1.2 节那条边的父类 2BGA 里），但不再是双变量。Level-2 页的判决很干脆：「一个检查重量相同的多变量候选，不是参数表更长的 BB 实例」——多出来的方向会改变局域维度（三维而不是二维环面）和硬件嵌入，即使项数不变。说人话：六腿检查不等于 BB；要看腿伸向几个方向。

Multivariate. Voss, Xian, Haug and Bharti, “Multivariate Bicycle Codes” (voss2025multivariate, 2025, arXiv 2406.19151, registration pending), add a third independent cyclic variable z to x and y: the ring becomes GF(2)[x, y, z]/(xl − 1, ym − 1, zp − 1) and the group Zl × Zm × Zp. It remains an abelian group-algebra relative (so still inside the parent 2BGA of the edge in §1.2) but is no longer bivariate. The Level-2 verdict is blunt: “a multivariate candidate with the same check weight is not a BB instance with a longer parameter list”; the extra direction changes the locality dimension (a three-dimensional rather than two-dimensional torus) and the hardware embedding even when the term count stays fixed. In plain words: six legs do not make a BB code; count how many directions the legs point in.

互素子类。Ming Wang 与 Frank Mueller「Coprime Bivariate Bicycle Codes and Their Layouts on Cold Atoms」（wang2026coprime，2026，arXiv 2408.10001，登记待补）限制生成元用互素coprime：两个多项式没有公共因子。这里指把 A、B 限制成有特定互素因子结构的多项式，使码率可以在搜索前由因子的周期算出。的多项式因子，于是码率可以在完整搜索之前就选定，并同时为冷原子设计搬运版图。这是一个子类：每个互素 BB 都满足更宽的双变量形式，反过来普通 BB 的多项式未必有互素分解。Level-2 页的警告是：「子类的码率公式不能不检查因子与周期就套到 gross 码上」。说人话：子类有漂亮的公式，但公式的前提是「互素」，gross 的 A = x³ + y + y²、B = y³ + x + x² 是搜索出来的，没人保证它满足。

Here: constraining A and B to polynomials with a specific coprime factor structure, so that the rate can be computed from the factors' periods before any search. polynomial factors, so the rate can be chosen before the full finite search, and co-design a moving-atom layout for cold atoms. 4.2边界：剪开环面之后，k 与 d 要从头算Boundary: once the torus is cut open, k and d start from scratch

周期 BB 码把环面对边粘起来（xl = 1、ym = 1）。去掉一些比特和检查、或者把重复的瓷砖在某处截断，这层粘合就破了。Level-2 页把随之出现的问题拆成三个：① 哪些内部检查原样保留？② 哪些边界检查必须缩短、增加或删除，CSS 对易条件才仍然成立？③ 哪些圈变成了边界或开放的串，从而改变 k 与 d？三篇文献从三个方向做这件事。Eberhardt、Pereira 与 Steffan「Pruning qLDPC Codes: Towards Bivariate Bicycle Codes with Open Boundary Conditions」（eberhardt2024pruning，2024，arXiv 2412.04181，登记待补）从周期 BB 码里剪掉检查和比特得到开边界的后代，并在例子上给出折叠横向操作；扫描报告特别注明：剪枝在「循环超图积子情形」有保证，在此之外只是演示，「不是对任意 BB 多项式的定理」。Liang、Eberhardt 与 Yu-An Chen「Planar Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check Codes with Open Boundaries」（liang2025planar，2025，arXiv 2504.08887，登记待补）构造与 BB 内部图案相关的平面开边界码，并区分「有限平面的局域」与「周期环面的近局域」。Steffan、Choe、Breuckmann、Pereira 与 Eberhardt「Tile Codes: High-Efficiency Quantum Codes on a Lattice with Boundary」（steffan2025tile，2025，已登记，arXiv 2504.09171）把反复出现的 BB 式稳定子瓷砖变成开边界平面码；报告的一句话是全章的钉子：「共享一块内部瓷砖不意味着与环面 BB 码相等；边界的截断改变逻辑维数与逻辑算符。」

A periodic BB code glues opposite torus edges together (xl = 1, ym = 1). Remove some qubits and checks, or terminate a repeated tile somewhere, and the gluing breaks. The Level-2 page splits what follows into three questions: ① Which bulk checks survive unchanged? ② Which boundary checks must be shortened, added or removed so that CSS commutation still holds? ③ Which cycles become boundaries or open strings, changing k and d? Three papers attack this from three sides. Eberhardt, Pereira and Steffan, “Pruning qLDPC Codes: Towards Bivariate Bicycle Codes with Open Boundary Conditions” (eberhardt2024pruning, 2024, arXiv 2412.04181, registration pending), prune checks and qubits from periodic BB codes to get open-boundary descendants and give fold-transversal operations on examples; the scout report notes that pruning is guaranteed for the “cyclic-HGP subcase” and beyond it only demonstrated, “not a theorem for arbitrary BB polynomials”. Liang, Eberhardt and Yu-An Chen, “Planar Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check Codes with Open Boundaries” (liang2025planar, 2025, arXiv 2504.08887, registration pending), build planar open-boundary codes related to BB bulk patterns and distinguish finite planar locality from periodic-torus near-locality. Steffan, Choe, Breuckmann, Pereira and Eberhardt, “Tile Codes: High-Efficiency Quantum Codes on a Lattice with Boundary” (steffan2025tile, 2025, registered, arXiv 2504.09171), turn recurring BB-like stabilizer tiles into open-boundary planar codes; one sentence of the report nails the whole chapter: “Sharing a bulk tile does not imply equality to a toric BB code; boundary termination changes logical dimension and operators.”

The safe workflow, in the Level-2 page's words: construct the finite open matrices, recompute rank, validate commutation, and find new logical representatives. Copying periodic k, d across the cut is invalid. 4.3对偶性与字母表：自对偶不是白送的，qudit 换了单位Duality and alphabet: self-duality is not free, qudits change the unit

自对偶。普通 BB 已经有一种 X/Z 之间的对称：HZ 是 HX 交换两块再转置。但 Level-2 页强调这不自动给出能实现逻辑 H 或 S 门的「物理自对偶」。Zijian Liang 与 Yu-An Chen「Self-Dual Bivariate Bicycle Codes with Transversal Clifford Gates」（liang2025selfdual，2025，arXiv 2510.05211，登记待补）在扭环面上加额外约束，得到检查重量为八的自对偶 BB 码，带横向的 CNOT、H 与 S；枚举出的例子包括 [[16,4,4]] 和更大的块。两层含义必须分开：「CSS 的转置对称帮助证明对易」——这是发布的构造都有的；「物理自对偶加上算清的逻辑作用可以认证横向 H/S」——这只属于被引用的那个子族。本条目「逻辑操作」侧面的第五条记录就是它，标 claimed，cost 一栏明写「not a gross-code claim」。说人话：自对偶 BB 的八腿检查和 gross 的六腿检查是两种不同的码；不能因为都叫 BB，就把 H、S 门搬到 gross 上。

Self-duality. Ordinary BB already has a symmetry between X and Z: HZ is HX with the blocks swapped and transposed. But the Level-2 page stresses that this does not automatically yield a “physical self-duality” implementing logical H or S. Zijian Liang and Yu-An Chen, “Self-Dual Bivariate Bicycle Codes with Transversal Clifford Gates” (liang2025selfdual, 2025, arXiv 2510.05211, registration pending), impose extra constraints on a twisted torus and obtain self-dual BB codes of check weight eight with transversal CNOT, H and S; enumerated examples include [[16,4,4]] and larger blocks. Two implications must stay apart: “CSS transpose symmetry helps prove commutation”, which every shipped construction has; and “physical self-duality plus a resolved logical action can certify transversal H/S”, which belongs only to the cited subfamily. The fifth record of this entry's Logical operations facet is exactly this, labelled claimed, its cost field saying “not a gross-code claim”. In plain words: the eight-leg checks of self-dual BB and the six-leg checks of gross are two different codes; sharing the name BB does not move the H and S gates onto gross.

Search perturbations. The Level-2 line: once X and Z are mixed within a stabilizer row, the defining object is a full symplectic check matrix (the (x | z) notation of the representation-ladder guide, §2.1) and “the CSS polynomial commutation proof no longer applies”; “Similarity of a Tanner graph or ancestry in a BB search is not enough to keep the strict family label.” In plain words: a code mutated from gross is not necessarily a BB code any more.

4.5渐近边界，与搬动一个数字之前的六问The asymptotic boundary, and six questions before moving any number

最后一根「轴」不是变体，而是一条天花板——注意它只对所研究的 BB 子类证明过，并没有覆盖每一根变体轴。Postema 与 Kokkelmans「Existence and Characterisation of Bivariate Bicycle Codes」（postema2025existence，2025，arXiv 2502.17052，登记待补）证明所研究的 BB 子类渐近是坏的：固定项数、无限放大环面，码率与相对码距不能同时保持非零。Level-2 页说这个结果和 gross 这个强有力的有限点并存：有限搜索可以找到有价值的高码率、中等码距的存储器；固定的构造约束不必给出非零的渐近码率与相对码距。于是正确的产品说法是——原文——「在特定连接模型下的强有限常数」，而不是「来自二维局域环面的渐近好 qLDPC 码」。《BB 码 · 高中数学版》6.6 节与参数导读 5.4 节都从各自角度说过这一点；真正渐近好的路在《qLDPC 码 · 高中数学版》4.4 节——换成非阿贝尔群，也就是离开 1.2 节那条边的「可交换」限定。

The last “axis” is not a variant but a ceiling — proved, note, only for the studied BB subclass, not for every variant axis. Postema and Kokkelmans, “Existence and Characterisation of Bivariate Bicycle Codes” (postema2025existence, 2025, arXiv 2502.17052, registration pending), prove the studied BB subclass asymptotically bad: with a fixed number of terms and an ever larger torus, rate and relative distance cannot both stay nonzero. The Level-2 page says this result and the strong finite gross point coexist: a finite search can find a valuable high-rate, moderate-distance memory; fixed construction constraints need not yield nonzero asymptotic rate and relative distance simultaneously. Hence the correct product claim, verbatim, is “strong finite constants under a specific connectivity model”, not “asymptotically good qLDPC from a two-dimensional local torus”. §6.6 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition” and §5.4 of the parameters guide each say this from their own angle; the genuinely asymptotically good road is in §4.4 of “qLDPC Codes · High-School Edition”: switch to a non-abelian group, i.e. leave the “commutative” qualifier of the edge in §1.2.

Level-2 页的六问，逐字：① 系数域与变量数没变吗？② 周期与边界条件一样吗？③ 稳定子在提取过程中是静态的还是变形的？④ 这个块的自对偶性真的被证明了吗？⑤ 剪枝之后重算过秩吗？⑥ 所引的译码器用的是同一个探测器模型吗？「任何一问答否，新对象就需要自己的可执行实例与来源账本。」说人话：d = 12、k = 12、「十二倍」属于 gross 这一块普通的、周期的、静态的、二元的 BB 码；0.8% 则属于那篇论文在指定协议下对整个存储器家族做的有限尺寸标度，连 gross 单块的伪阈值都不是。

The Level-2 page's six questions, verbatim: ① Are the coefficient field and number of variables unchanged? ② Are the periods and boundary conditions identical? ③ Are the stabilizers static or morphed during extraction? ④ Is self-duality actually proved for this block? ⑤ Was rank recomputed after pruning? ⑥ Does the cited decoder use the same detector model? “If any answer is no, the new object needs its own executable instance and provenance ledger.” In plain words: d = 12, k = 12 and “twelve-fold” belong to the one ordinary, periodic, static, binary BB block called gross; 0.8% belongs to the paper's finite-size scaling across its memory family under its specified protocol, and is not even a one-block pseudo-threshold.

关键想法Key idea

变体不是「BB 的花样」，而是七根各自独立的轴：变量数、多项式限制、边界、对偶性、字母表、CSS 条件、提取方式。每根轴一动，n、k、d、电路码距、译码器里至少有一个要重算。名字（gross、BB5、BB6）不是轴；渐近坏是对所研究子类证明的边界，其他轴上的变体并不被这条定理覆盖。

Variants are not “flavours of BB” but seven independent axes: number of variables, polynomial restrictions, boundary, duality, alphabet, CSS condition, extraction. Move any one and at least one of n, k, d, circuit distance or decoder must be recomputed. Names (gross, BB5, BB6) are not axes; asymptotic badness is proved for the studied subclass only; variants on the other axes are not covered by that theorem.
