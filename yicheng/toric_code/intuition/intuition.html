只需要一点点数学Just a little math

整份教程用到的数学，只有四样：负数、平方、概率、数个数的奇偶。这一章把它们一次备齐。已经会的读者快速滑过即可——但至少看一眼 1.4，那是后面一切的钥匙。 This entire tutorial uses exactly four pieces of math: negative numbers, squares, probability, and counting even-vs-odd. This chapter stocks them all. If you know them, skim — but glance at 1.4, which is the key to everything later.

1.1负数：带方向的数Negative numbers: numbers with a direction

从 1+1=2 出发，我们会数数和加法。减法是「往回走」：3 − 5 走过了 0，落在 0 左边 2 步的地方，我们把那里记作 −2。每个数 x 都有一个镜像 −x：它们相加正好回到 0，即 x + (−x) = 0。

Starting from 1+1=2 we can count and add. Subtraction means “walking backwards”: 3 − 5 walks past 0 and lands two steps to its left, a place we call −2. Every number x has a mirror −x, and together they cancel: x + (−x) = 0.

乘法是「连加」：3 × 2 = 2+2+2。那么乘一个负数呢？3 × (−1) = (−1)+(−1)+(−1) = −3，没问题。可 (−1) × (−1) 是什么——「负一次」加法没法直接想象。别背口诀，让规律替我们决定：

Multiplication is repeated addition: 3 × 2 = 2+2+2. So 3 × (−1) = (−1)+(−1)+(−1) = −3, fine. But what is (−1) × (−1)? You cannot “add something minus-one times”. Don't memorize a slogan — let the pattern decide:

3×(−1) = −3　→　2×(−1) = −2　→　1×(−1) = −1　→　0×(−1) = 0　→　(−1)×(−1) = +1

左边每次少乘一个 1，右边每次增加 1。要让规律不断裂，(−1)×(−1) 只能等于 +1。由此立刻得到一个我们要用几十次的推论：

Each line drops one “1” on the left and climbs by one on the right. For the pattern to survive, (−1)×(−1) must be +1. This gives a corollary we will use dozens of times:

一串 (−1) 连乘，结果只看个数的奇偶：偶数个 (−1) 相乘 = +1，奇数个 = −1。例如 (−1)×(−1)×(−1) = (+1)×(−1) = −1。

A chain of (−1)s multiplied together depends only on whether their count is even or odd: an even number of (−1)s gives +1, an odd number gives −1. E.g. (−1)×(−1)×(−1) = (+1)×(−1) = −1.

Note the last line: squares of negative numbers are positive — the two (−1)s cancel. “Squaring erases the sign” looks small now and becomes a pillar of the quantum world later. One more pair worth memorizing: 0.6² + 0.8² = 0.36 + 0.64 = 1.

工具箱里还要放进一件借来的古董。两千多年前的几何学家证明了：直角三角形两条直角边 a、b 与最长边（斜边）c 之间，永远有 a² + b² = c²——勾股定理。我们不重演它的证明（那是几何课的乐趣），只领走一个直接推论：到原点距离恰好为 1 的所有点 (a, b)，正是满足 a² + b² = 1 的点，它们连成的曲线就是半径为 1 的圆。记住这幅画面：「a² + b² = 1 ⇔ 点 (a, b) 躺在单位圆上」。第 3 章它将成为主角的舞台。

One borrowed antique goes into the toolbox too. Over two millennia ago geometers proved that a right triangle's two legs a, b and its longest side (hypotenuse) c always satisfy a² + b² = c² — the Pythagorean theorem. We won't replay its proof (that's geometry class's treat), only collect a direct consequence: the points (a, b) at distance exactly 1 from the origin are precisely those with a² + b² = 1, and together they trace the circle of radius 1. Keep the picture “a² + b² = 1 ⇔ the point (a, b) lies on the unit circle” — in Chapter 3 it becomes the protagonist's stage.

比特、噪声与三倍保险Bits, noise, and triple insurance

量子纠错的每一个套路，都是普通（经典）纠错套路的升级版。所以先在没有量子的世界里，把「出错—发现—修复」这套完整动作练一遍。 Every move in quantum error correction is an upgraded version of an ordinary (classical) move. So let's first rehearse the full routine — fail, detect, repair — in a world with no quantum in it.

2.1比特：一切信息的原子The bit: the atom of information

一个比特最小的信息单位：一个只能处于 0 或 1 两种状态之一的东西。bit = binary digit（二进制数字）。就是一个只有两种可能的答案：0 或 1、否或是、灭或亮。任何承载它的东西都行——开关的通断、电压的高低、硬币的正反。你此刻屏幕上的一切，本质上是几十亿个比特。

A bitThe smallest unit of information: something that is in exactly one of two states, 0 or 1. Short for “binary digit”. is an answer with only two options: 0 or 1, no or yes, off or on. Anything can carry one — a switch, a voltage, a coin lying heads or tails. Everything on your screen right now is a few billion bits.

2.2噪声：世界会拨动你的开关Noise: the world flips your switches

存储和传输都不完美：宇宙射线、发热、元件老化，都可能把 0 变成 1、1 变成 0。我们把这种意外称为翻转，并用一个数 p 表示「每个比特被翻转的概率」。体验一下 p 的威力：

Storage and transmission are imperfect: cosmic rays, heat, ageing parts can turn a 0 into a 1 and vice versa. Call this accident a flip, and let the number p be “the probability that any given bit gets flipped”. Feel what p does:

P(某个比特被翻转a given bit flips) = p　·　P(安然无恙survives) = 1 − p

2.3三倍保险：把 0 说三遍Triple insurance: say 0 three times

对策古老而直白：重复。想发 0，就发 000；想发 1，就发 111。这一步叫编码把 1 个「逻辑」比特（真正想保护的信息）写进多个「物理」比特（实际存在的载体）的动作。。接收方数一数哪个多——两票以上算数——这一步叫多数表决解码。于是，1 个错误能被纠正；只有 2 个及以上同时出错才会翻车。

The ancient, blunt fix: repetition. To send 0, send 000; to send 1, send 111. This step is called encodingWriting one “logical” bit (the information you actually care about) into several “physical” bits (the actual carriers).. The receiver counts votes — two or more wins — which is majority-vote decoding. Now one flip gets corrected; only 2 or more simultaneous flips cause disaster.

这里出现了本教程最重要的一对词：真正想保护的那个比特叫逻辑比特；实际用到的三个载体叫物理比特。用 3 个物理的，换 1 个更结实的逻辑的。

Here appears the most important word-pair of the tutorial: the bit we actually protect is the logical bit; the three carriers are physical bits. We spend 3 physical bits to buy 1 sturdier logical bit.

「三倍保险」到底好多少？把所有翻车的情形列出来就知道了。三个比特里恰好某两个被翻，有且只有 3 种选法——(1和2)、(1和3)、(2和3)——每种的概率是 p·p·(1−p)；三个全翻是 1 种，概率 p³。加起来：

How much better is triple insurance? Just list every disaster. “Exactly two of the three flip” happens in exactly 3 ways — (1&2), (1&3), (2&3) — each with probability p·p·(1−p); “all three flip” is 1 way, probability p³. Sum:

P(翻车disaster) = 3·p²·(1−p) + p³　≈ 3p²　(当 p 很小for small p)

当 p = 1% 时：裸比特出错 1%，重复码只有约 0.03%——好了三十多倍。而且 p² 的平方是「面积级的小」：p 越小，优势越悬殊。这个「用数量换质量，错误率从 p 降到 ~p²」的交易，就是一切纠错码的灵魂。

At p = 1%: a bare bit fails 1% of the time, the repetition code about 0.03% — thirty-odd times better. And p² is “area-level small”: the smaller p, the more lopsided the win. This trade — spend quantity, gain quality, error rate p → ~p² — is the soul of all error correction.

2.4不看信件的体检：奇偶检查Health-checks that never read the letter: parity checks

多数表决有个隐藏动作：它读了三个比特。经典世界里读一读无妨；但我们即将进入的量子世界，「读」这个动作本身就会毁掉信息（第 3 章见）。所以现在就练习一种不读内容的检修法。

Majority vote hides a step: it reads all three bits. Reading is harmless classically; but in the quantum world we're heading to, the act of reading destroys the information (see Chapter 3). So let's practise a repair method that never reads the content.

量子比特：会叠加的比特The qubit: a bit that superposes

量子比特只在三件事上不同于普通比特：状态是「配比」而非「取舍」、读取会破坏、错误有两种。这一章把三件事全部讲清，而且只用第 1 章的数学。 A qubit differs from a bit in exactly three ways: its state is a blend rather than a choice, reading it damages it, and it has two kinds of errors. This chapter covers all three using nothing beyond Chapter 1.

3.1状态 = 两个配比数字A state = two blend-numbers

普通比特要么 0 要么 1。量子比特一个遵循量子力学的两状态系统（一个原子的两个能级、一段超导电路……）。英文 qubit。（比如一个原子、一段超导电路）则同时携带「0 的成分」和「1 的成分」，各配一个数字——两种成分同时在场，这就是大名鼎鼎的叠加。我们把状态写成一对数 (a, b)：a 是 0-成分的配比，b 是 1-成分的配比。物理学家爱用一种带尖角的括号来记这两种纯状态：|0⟩ 与 |1⟩（读作「ket 0」「ket 1」，就是给标签加了个礼盒），于是整个状态写作：

An ordinary bit is 0 or 1. A qubitAny two-state system obeying quantum mechanics (two energy levels of an atom, a superconducting circuit…). (an atom, a superconducting circuit) carries a “0-part” and a “1-part” simultaneously, each with a number attached — both parts present at once: the famous superposition. Write the state as a pair (a, b): a is the blend-number of the 0-part, b of the 1-part. 但环面上存在第二种圈。横着走一整排、从右边绕回左边接上自己——它闭合（无端点、无警报），却不围住任何区域：它绕的是整个环面，像套在甜甜圈上的橡皮筋，收缩不成一个点。上面实验台的第二个示范按钮画的正是它。这种圈无法写成面检查的乘积，所以 5.5 的「等于零」论证对它失效——它通过所有体检，却对编码状态做了某件真实的事。

But a torus hosts a second kind of loop. March straight across a whole row, wrap from the right edge back to the left, and close on yourself — closed (no endpoints, no alarms), yet enclosing no region: it loops around the torus itself, like a rubber band around a donut, unshrinkable to a point. The second demo button above draws exactly this. Such a loop cannot be written as a product of face checks, so §5.5's “equals zero” argument fails — it passes every exam yet does something real to the encoded state.

「通过所有检查、又不等于零的操作」——这正是逻辑算符的定义：它改写的不是某个物理比特，而是被全体比特共同保管的逻辑信息。环面上有两个本质不同的绕法（水平圈、竖直圈），给出两个独立的逻辑 Z：

“Passes every check, yet is not zero” — that is the definition of a logical operator: it rewrites not any physical qubit but the logical information kept jointly by all of them. The torus has two essentially different windings (horizontal, vertical), giving two independent logical Z's:

Z̄₁ = Z-圈，水平缠绕-loop winding horizontally　·　Z̄₂ = Z-圈，竖直缠绕-loop winding vertically

X 这边同样有两条：沿「横跨边」方向的 X-圈（在面心间穿行的虚线路径），水平、竖直各一。一对 (X̄, Z̄) 要配成一个真正的逻辑量子比特，还差最后一项验收：逻辑 X̄ 与逻辑 Z̄ 必须反对易（真量子比特的 X 和 Z 就该如此，3.5 节）。数碰撞：一条竖直的 X̄-圈与一条水平的 Z̄₁-圈，在环面上恰好交叉一次——1 是奇数，反对易 ✓。而它与竖直的 Z̄₂ 走的是平行线，交叉 0 次——对易 ✓。两对搭档各就各位：

The X side has two as well: X-loops running in the “across-edges” direction (the dashed paths through face centres), one horizontal, one vertical. For a pair (X̄, Z̄) to form a genuine logical qubit, one final inspection: logical X̄ must anticommute with logical Z̄ (that's what X and Z of any true qubit do, §3.5). Count collisions: a vertical X̄-loop and a horizontal Z̄₁-loop cross on a torus exactly once — odd, anticommute ✓. With the vertical Z̄₂ it runs parallel, crossing 0 times — commute ✓. Two matched pairs, each in place:

X̄₁Z̄₁ = −Z̄₁X̄₁　·　X̄₁Z̄₂ = +Z̄₂X̄₁　(交叉数 1 与 0crossing numbers 1 and 0)　⇒　恰好 2 个逻辑量子比特，与 5.3 的账目吻合exactly 2 logical qubits — matching §5.3's ledger

「可收缩 / 缠绕」这类性质叫做拓扑性质：随便揉捏变形都不改变。逻辑信息存在「圈绕不绕」里，而噪声只会制造局部的小链——够不着「绕一整圈」这种全局属性。这就是「拓扑保护」的全部含义，也是本教程标题里那个词的兑现。顺带一提：那些成对出现、沿链端移动的警报点，物理学家叫它们任意子（anyon）——在我们的故事里，你已经与它们玩了一整章。

Properties like “shrinkable vs winding” are topological: arbitrary kneading and stretching never change them. The logical data lives in “does the loop wind?”, while noise only builds short local chains — it cannot reach a global property like winding. That is the entire meaning of “topological protection”, the cash value of this tutorial's subtitle. By the way: those paired alarm dots gliding at chain-ends are what physicists call anyons — you have been playing with them all chapter.

5.7纠错 = 给警报配对Correcting = pairing up the alarms

现在可以设计修理程序（专名：译码器读取全部警报、推断该施加什么修复操作的算法。英文 decoder。）了。测量所有检查后，我们看到的只是一撮亮着的警报点。策略：把警报两两配对，沿任意路径把每对连起来，沿路径施加 Z 作为修复。修复链与真实错误链首尾相接，合成闭合圈。此刻 5.5 与 5.6 的二分法接管一切：

Now we can design the repair routine (proper name: the decoderThe algorithm that reads all alarms and infers what repair to apply.). After measuring every check we see only a scatter of lit alarms. Strategy: pair them up, connect each pair along any path, and apply Z along it as the repair. Repair chain plus true error chain join end-to-end into closed loops. From here the §5.5/§5.6 dichotomy takes over:

错误 ⊕ 修复 = 可收缩圈 ⇒ 完美复原　·　错误 ⊕ 修复 = 缠绕圈 ⇒ 无声的逻辑错误error ⊕ repair = contractible ⇒ perfect recovery · error ⊕ repair = winding ⇒ silent logical error

妙处：修复不必猜中真实错误——差之毫厘也无妨，只要合成的圈可收缩就全胜。这正是 5.4 节那份「模糊」的回报。风险也清楚了：把该配的两对配串了，修复链可能与错误链合成绕环面一周的缠绕圈——满盘皆输还毫无警报。亲自当一回译码器（失败几回更有收获）：

5.8尺寸红利：越大越安全The size dividend: bigger is safer

要酿成一次无声的逻辑错误，错误串必须缠绕整个环面，最短也要 L 步；译码器还会纠正一半以内的部分，所以粗略地说，需要约 L/2 个错误恰好连成一线地合谋。第 4.6 节的语言：

To brew a silent logical error, the error string must wind the whole torus — at least L steps; and the decoder cancels anything up to about half of that, so roughly L/2 errors must conspire in a line. In §4.6's language:

码距distance d = L　·　对两种错误对称成立symmetrically for both error types　·　扩大网格 ⇒ d 任意增长grow the grid ⇒ d grows without bound

任务书四条指标——两族检查 ✓（星与面）、全体对易 ✓（角连两边）、两种错误对称且码距可放大 ✓（d = L）、每条检查只碰 4 个近邻 ✓——全部兑现。至于「L/2 个错误连成一线的概率随 L 缩得多快」「p 小到多少之后越大越好」，这些定量问题的答案是一条震撼的曲线，叫阈值——它属于姊妹篇《从 Toric Code 到 Surface Code》第 6 章的压轴实验。

All four requirements of the brief — two check families ✓ (stars & faces), all commuting ✓ (corners touch two sides), symmetric and growable distance ✓ (d = L), each check touching only 4 neighbours ✓ — delivered. As for “how fast does the chance of L/2 aligned errors shrink with L” and “below what p does bigger become better”, the quantitative answer is one stunning curve called the threshold — the finale experiment of “From Toric Code to Surface Code”, Chapter 6.

本教程做过的简化，在此一次性结清：① 我们全程只用实数振幅。完整的量子力学使用复数，多一种操作 Y（= 同时来一下 X 和 Z）；好消息是 Y 错误会同时触发两族检查，toric code 顺手就把它捉了，所以主线结论一条不改。② 我们假设测量本身不出错。真实设备的检查读数也会错，解法是反复测多轮、在时间方向上也做配对——《从 Toric Code 到 Surface Code》4.3 节会正面讨论。③ 我们的译码器用「就近配对」，最优算法（如最小重量完美匹配）更聪明，但思想相同。

All simplifications, settled in one bill: ① We used real amplitudes throughout. Full quantum mechanics uses complex numbers and one more operation, Y (= an X and a Z at once); happily a Y error trips both check families and the toric code catches it in stride, so no headline conclusion changes. ② We assumed measurements themselves are perfect. Real readouts also err; the cure is repeating rounds and pairing across time as well — faced squarely in “From Toric Code to Surface Code”, §4.3. ③ Our decoder pairs greedily; optimal algorithms (minimum-weight perfect matching) are smarter, same idea.
