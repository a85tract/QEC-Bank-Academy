0.1行李一：量子比特与「平方=概率」Item 1: the qubit and “square = probability”

一个量子比特的状态（《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》3.1 节）是一对配比数字（振幅），可正可负；测量得 0 或 1 的概率是各自振幅的平方；测量后状态跳到所得答案上（坍缩）。未知的量子状态既不能复印（不可克隆），也经不起直接阅读。

A qubit's state (“From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §3.1) is a pair of blend-numbers (amplitudes), possibly negative; measuring yields 0 or 1 with probability each amplitude squared; afterwards the state jumps onto the answer (collapse). Unknown quantum states can be neither photocopied (no-cloning) nor read without damage.

状态state = a·|0⟩ + b·|1⟩　·　P(0) = a²　·　P(1) = b²　·　a² + b² = 1

0.2行李二：两种错误，两副眼镜Item 2: two errors, two lenses

噪声有两种基本手法（《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》3.3–3.5 节）：X（交换两个振幅——0/1 眼镜里的翻转）与 Z（给 1-成分变号——它在 0/1 眼镜里隐形，却是 +/− 眼镜里的翻转，其中 |±⟩ = (|0⟩±|1⟩)/√2）。同一比特上 X 与 Z 反对易：交换先后顺序，差一个整体 −1。在复习台上戴两副眼镜各看一遍：

Noise has two elementary moves (“From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §3.3–3.5): X (swap the amplitudes — a flip in the 0/1 lens) and Z (re-sign the 1-part — invisible in the 0/1 lens, yet a genuine flip in the +/− lens, where |±⟩ = (|0⟩±|1⟩)/√2). On one qubit X and Z anticommute: swapping their order costs one overall −1. Watch through both lenses:

X(a,b) = (b,a)　·　Z(a,b) = (a,−b)　·　XZ = −ZX

0.3行李三：不偷看的检查与碰撞规则Item 3: no-peek checks and the collision rule

多比特检查如 Z₁Z₂ 只问「这两个比特一样吗」（+1/−1），绝不问「各是什么」——它不破坏合法的叠加。编码状态被要求对清单上的每条检查都「必然 +1」；清单的学名是稳定子（《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》4.4 节）。n 个比特配 k 条独立检查，剩下 n−k 个逻辑量子比特。错误 E 触发检查 S 报警，当且仅当 E 与 S 反对易；判据是数格子：

Multi-qubit checks like Z₁Z₂ ask only “are these two equal?” (+1/−1), never “what are they?” — legal superpositions survive. Encoded states must answer “+1 surely” to every check on the list (formally, the stabilizers; “From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §4.4). n qubits with k independent checks leave n−k logical qubits. An error E trips a check S exactly when E and S anticommute; the test is cell-counting:

警报 ⇔ 反对易 ⇔ X 撞 Z 的格数为奇数alarm ⇔ anticommute ⇔ odd number of X-meets-Z cells　·　k逻辑logical = n − #独立检查independent checks

0.4行李四：toric code 全图Item 4: the toric code at a glance

L×L 的绕回网格（环面；《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》5.1–5.3 节）：每条边一个量子比特（共 2L²）；每个顶点一条星形检查 Av（4 条边上的 X），每块面一条面检查 Bp（4 条边上的 Z）。星与面永远共享 0 或 2 条边（角连两边），所以全体检查对易。两条「乘积=I」的恒等式让独立检查只有 2L²−2 条——环面恰好存 2 个逻辑量子比特。

An L×L wrap-around grid (a torus; “From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §5.1–5.3): one qubit per edge (2L² total); at each vertex a star check Av (X on its 4 edges), on each face a face check Bp (Z on its 4 edges). A star and a face always share 0 or 2 edges (corners touch two sides), so all checks commute. Two “product = I” identities leave 2L²−2 independent checks — the torus stores exactly 2 logical qubits.

Av = XXXX　·　Bp = ZZZZ　·　∏Av = ∏Bp = I (∏ = 全部乘起来multiply them all)　·　k = 2L² − (2L²−2) = 2

0.5行李五：链、警报、圈Item 5: chains, alarms, loops

错误连成链，警报只亮在链的端点（《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》5.4–5.8 节；链中间偶数次相撞，抵消）。闭合且可收缩的圈 = 若干检查的乘积 = 对编码信息什么也不做；缠绕环面一整圈的圈通过所有检查却改写逻辑信息——它就是逻辑算符 Z̄ / X̄，最短长度 L 即码距 d。纠错 = 给警报配对、连线修复；「错误 ⊕ 修复」（⊕：合并两批边、成对相消）可收缩则完胜、缠绕则败。到沙盘里活动一下手脚：

Errors form chains; alarms light only at endpoints (“From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §5.4–5.8; interiors are struck an even number of times and cancel). A closed contractible loop = a product of checks = does nothing to the data; a loop winding the torus passes every check yet rewrites logical data — it is the logical operator Z̄ / X̄, of minimum length L, the distance d. Correcting = pairing alarms and joining them; “error ⊕ repair” (⊕: merge the two edge sets with pairwise cancellation) contractible = win, winding = loss. Stretch your legs in the sandbox:

第 1 章Chapter 1

为什么要剪开环面Why cut the torus open

环面码的一切优点都依赖「绕回去」的连线。可真实的量子芯片是一块平板，比特只能和身边的邻居说话。这一章直面代价：剪开会破坏什么、又必须保留什么。 Every virtue of the toric code leans on wrap-around wiring. But a real quantum chip is a flat slab whose qubits talk only to nearest neighbours. This chapter faces the price: what cutting destroys, and what must survive.

先看清「绕回去」在硬件上意味着什么：最右一列的比特要和最左一列做同一条检查——两端相隔整块芯片的导线，要么飞跨芯片（工艺噩梦），要么把整个平面卷成圆筒再接成环（更是噩梦）。超导、离子阱、中性原子……各路平台都更喜欢只连邻居的方形阵列。

First, what wrap-around means in hardware: qubits in the rightmost column must share checks with the leftmost — wiring that leaps across the whole chip (a fabrication nightmare), or a plane rolled into a tube and closed into a ring (worse). Superconducting circuits, ion traps, neutral atoms — every platform prefers square arrays wired only to neighbours.

那就拿起剪刀。但《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》5.3 节的记账立刻警告我们，这一刀不便宜。环面的账本是：

So pick up the scissors. But the ledger of “From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §5.3 warns at once: this cut is not free. On the torus,

n = 2L²　·　检查checks = 2L²，其中独立的of which independent 2L²−2　⇒　k = 2

那两条「白送的恒等式」（∏Av = I，∏Bp = I）之所以成立，全靠「每条边恰好被两颗星、两块面分享」——这是绕回世界才有的完美均匀。剪开后边缘出现残缺检查，恒等式失效，k 会掉回 0？还是能抢救出 1 个逻辑比特？下面把整个手术分三步看完：

Those two “free identities” (∏Av = I, ∏Bp = I) held because every edge is shared by exactly two stars and two faces — the perfect uniformity only a wrap-around world provides. After the cut, mutilated checks appear along the rim, the identities die — does k crash to 0, or can one logical qubit be rescued? Watch the whole surgery in three steps:

第 ③ 步不是随手收拾，而是本教程的主角登场。上下两侧让星缺一条腿（三腿星是合法检查）；左右两侧让面缺一条腿——并且额外保留悬在边缘的半截边（每条上仍住着一个量子比特）。这两种「收边方式」性质完全不同，是下一章的全部内容。

Step ③ is not casual tidying — it is this tutorial's protagonist entering. On the top/bottom sides the stars lose one leg (a three-leg star is a legal check); on the left/right the faces lose one leg — and the dangling half-edges are kept, each still housing a qubit. These two ways of hemming an edge behave utterly differently, and they are the whole of the next chapter.

两种边界：毛边与光边Two kinds of boundary: rough and smooth

剪开后的版图上，左右两侧和上下两侧长得不一样、职责也不一样。给它们起名、立规矩、再重新记账——账本将给出一个漂亮到可疑的答案：1。 On the cut-open layout, the left/right sides and the top/bottom sides look different and act differently. Name them, write their rules, redo the ledger — which returns an answer almost suspiciously pretty: 1.

看上一章第 ③ 步的成品。左右两侧参差地伸出半截边，像布料的毛边——叫它毛边界悬挂着半截边（每条半截边上仍有一个量子比特）的边界。这里的面检查缺一条腿（三腿 Z 检查）。通称「粗糙边界 rough boundary」。（术语：粗糙边界）：这里的面检查只剩三条腿，Bp = ZZZ。上下两侧剪得干净利落，没有悬边——叫它光边界没有悬挂边、被顶点收束的边界。这里的星形检查缺一条腿（三腿 X 检查）。通称「光滑边界 smooth boundary」。（术语：光滑边界）：这里的星检查只剩三条腿，Av = XXX。

Inspect step ③'s result. The left and right sides sprout ragged half-edges, like fabric's frayed hem — call it the rough boundaryA boundary with dangling half-edges (each still carrying a qubit). Face checks here lose a leg (three-leg Z checks).: here the face checks keep only three legs, Bp = ZZZ. The top and bottom are cut clean, no danglers — the smooth boundaryA boundary hemmed by vertices, no dangling edges. Star checks here lose a leg (three-leg X checks).: here the star checks keep three legs, Av = XXX.

内部bulk: Av=XXXX, Bp=ZZZZ　·　光边界smooth: Av=XXX　·　毛边界rough: Bp=ZZZ

先验收老规矩还在不在：任何星与任何面，共享边数仍然只能是 0 或 2（角连两边的几何论证只依赖局部形状，边界处照样成立——在下面的实验台上开「显示检查」自行核对）。所有检查依旧两两对易 ✓。

First re-inspect the old rule: any star and any face still share exactly 0 or 2 edges (the corner-touches-two-sides argument is purely local and survives at boundaries — verify on the bench below). All checks still commute pairwise ✓.

然后重新记账。设「距离参数」d既是版图的尺寸，也是码距：最短横跨串恰走 d 条边（第 3 章证明）。（下面可切换）：竖直方向 d 排悬边、水平方向 d 列——数出来的结果永远是：

Then redo the ledger. With the size parameter dBoth the layout size and the code distance: the shortest spanning string uses exactly d edges (proved in Chapter 3). (switchable below), the counts always come out to:

n = d² + (d−1)²　·　星stars = d(d−1)　·　面faces = d(d−1)　·　平面上没有恒等式，全体独立no identities on a plane — all independent

为什么恒等式没了？把全部星相乘：内部每条边仍被两颗星分享而抵消，但悬边只被一颗星碰到——乘积在每条悬边上都剩一个孤零零的 X，不等于 I。「白送的减 2」消失，于是：

Why did the identities die? Multiply all the stars: interior edges still cancel (two stars each), but a dangling edge is touched by only one star — the product leaves a lone X on every dangler, ≠ I. The free “−2” is gone, so:

k = n − 检查数#checks = d² + (d−1)² − 2d(d−1) = (d − (d−1))² = 1² = 1
关键想法Key idea

代数在这里眨了下眼睛：d²+(d−1)²−2d(d−1) 恰是完全平方公式 (x−y)² = x²−2xy+y² 的右边，x=d、y=d−1，答案永远是 1。平面版图对任何尺寸 d 都恰好存 1 个逻辑量子比特。这就是 surface code（表面码）。

The algebra winks at us: d²+(d−1)²−2d(d−1) is exactly the right side of (x−y)² = x²−2xy+y² with x=d, y=d−1 — always 1. The planar layout stores exactly one logical qubit at every size d. This is the surface code.

第 3 章Chapter 3

终止在边界上的链Chains that end on boundaries

环面上，警报永远成双成对。平面上不再如此：链可以把一端（甚至两端）搭在边界上，让警报凭空少一个。这既是新的麻烦，也是逻辑比特的新藏身处。 On the torus, alarms always came in pairs. Not on the plane: a chain may rest one end (or both) on a boundary, making an alarm simply vanish. A fresh nuisance — and the logical qubit's new hiding place.

3.1毛边界吃掉 Z-链的警报The rough boundary swallows Z-chain alarms

回想警报的机理（《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》5.4 节）：Z-链的端点处，有一颗星被链奇数次撞到，于是报 −1。现在让一条 Z-链一路画到左侧的悬边上。悬边朝外的那一头，本该有下一颗星——但那里根本没有星（芯片到头了）。没有检查，就没有警报。链的这一端就这样无声地「死」在了毛边界上：

Recall the alarm mechanism (“From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §5.4): at a Z-chain's endpoint some star is struck an odd number of times and reads −1. Now draw a Z-chain out onto a dangling edge at the left. Beyond the dangler's outer tip there ought to be a next star — but no star exists there (the chip has ended). No check, no alarm. That end of the chain quietly “dies” on the rough boundary:

警报数 = 不在边界上的端点数#alarms = #endpoints not resting on a boundary　(Z-链 ↔ 毛边界；X-链 ↔ 光边界Z-chains ↔ rough; X-chains ↔ smooth)

分工是严格的。Z-链若把端点搭在上下（光）边界的边上，那里的三腿星照样看得见它——照样报警（去下面验证）。同理，X-链只能死在光边界；毛边界对它绝不留情。一种边界赦免一种链，恰好和「面在毛边界缺腿、星在光边界缺腿」一一对应。

The division of labour is strict. Rest a Z-chain's end on a top/bottom (smooth) edge and the three-leg star there still sees it — still alarms (verify below). Likewise X-chains may die only on the smooth boundary; the rough one shows them no mercy. Each boundary pardons one kind of chain — matching exactly “faces truncated at rough, stars truncated at smooth”.

3.2横跨两条毛边界 = 逻辑 Z̄Spanning rough to rough = the logical Z̄

把 3.1 推到极致：一条 Z-链两端都搭在毛边界上——从最左的悬边一路画到最右的悬边。零警报！它通过了全部检查。它是无害的「检查乘积」吗？不是：把面检查怎么相乘，也只能得到「两端都缩在内部或绕不出去」的圈状/贴边组合——横穿整块芯片这件事，乘积做不到（下一个折叠盒给出干净的论证）。通过一切检查、又不是检查的乘积——按《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》5.6 节的定义，这是一条逻辑算符：

Push §3.1 to the limit: a Z-chain resting both ends on the rough boundary — drawn from a leftmost dangler clean across to a rightmost one. Zero alarms! It passes every check. Is it a harmless product of checks? No: however you multiply face checks, you can never manufacture “crossing the whole chip” (the fold-out below gives a clean argument). Passing all checks yet not a product of them — by the definition of “From 1+1 to the Toric Code”, §5.6, a logical operator:

Z̄ = Z-串：毛边界 → 毛边界（左 ↔ 右）-string: rough → rough (left ↔ right)　·　X̄ = X-串：光边界 → 光边界（上 ↔ 下）-string: smooth → smooth (top ↔ bottom)　(→：串从一条边界连到另一条；↔：这一对边界→: the string runs from one boundary to the other; ↔: which pair of boundaries)
为什么横跨的串一定不是面检查的乘积？Why can a spanning string never be a product of face checks?

用对易性替我们查账。横跨的 Z̄ 与竖跨的 X̄ 恰好交叉一次——按碰撞规则，二者反对易。可任何「面检查的乘积」都必须与 X̄ 对易：X̄ 与每条单独的面检查都对易（X̄ 竖直穿行时，与任何面共享 0 或 2 条边），与它们的乘积自然也对易。一个反对易、一个必须对易——矛盾。所以 Z̄ 不在乘积清单上。这个论证同时送我们最后一块拼图：Z̄ 与 X̄ 反对易，正是一对合格逻辑比特算符的签名（同一教程 5.6 节）。

Let commutation audit the books. The spanning Z̄ and the vertical X̄ cross exactly once — by the collision rule they anticommute. But any product of face checks must commute with X̄: X̄ commutes with each individual face check (running vertically it shares 0 or 2 edges with any face), hence with any product. Anticommuting versus must-commute — contradiction. So Z̄ is not on the product list. The same argument hands us the final puzzle piece: Z̄ and X̄ anticommute — precisely the signature of a proper logical pair (§5.6 there).

4.2打开黑盒：谁去问「四条边的奇偶」？Opening the box: who asks the four-edge parity?

「测量 Bp」说来轻巧，可没有任何仪器能一口气「读四个量子比特的联合奇偶」。真实的做法是给每条检查配一个专职的帮手量子比特（术语：辅助比特不存储数据、专职收集一条检查的奇偶并被读出的量子比特。英文 ancilla。真实芯片上数据比特与辅助比特约各占一半。，ancilla）：让它挨个「碰」一遍那 4 个数据比特——每次接触都是一个两比特小操作，把一份奇偶信息叠进帮手——最后只测量帮手。数据比特从头到尾没被直接读过，四拍问询的净效果恰好就是我们从《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》一路用到这里的那句问话：「你们四个的联合奇偶是 +1 还是 −1？」

“Measure Bp” is easy to say, but no instrument reads “the joint parity of four qubits” in one gulp. The real recipe assigns each check its own dedicated helper qubit (term: the ancillaA qubit that stores no data; its job is to collect one check's parity and be read out. On real chips data and ancilla qubits are roughly half and half.): it “touches” the 4 data qubits one at a time — each touch a small two-qubit operation folding one parity's worth of information into the helper — and then only the helper is measured. The data qubits are never read directly, and the four-touch routine asks precisely the question “From 1+1 to the Toric Code” and this tutorial have leaned on: “is your joint parity +1 or −1?”

那个「碰一下」具体是什么操作？What exactly is one “touch”?

它是一种叫「受控翻转」（CNOT）的两比特操作：若数据比特处于 1-成分，就翻转帮手；处于 0-成分则不动——于是四次接触后，帮手的 0/1 恰好记录了「四个数据比特里 1 的个数的奇偶」。对量子叠加它同样成立（每个成分各自执行规则），这正是它能不破坏叠加的原因。完整的门操作理论超出本教程，只需记住：这个黑盒可靠地问出联合奇偶，别的什么都不问。

A two-qubit operation called the controlled flip (CNOT): if the data qubit is in its 1-part, flip the helper; if in its 0-part, do nothing — so after four touches the helper's 0/1 records exactly “the parity of the number of 1s among the four”. It acts component-by-component on superpositions, which is why nothing collapses. Full gate theory is beyond this tutorial; what matters: the box reliably asks the joint parity and nothing else.

诚实说明Honesty box

本教程一直假设检查读数完美。真实芯片上，帮手比特会出错、读出仪器会看走眼——警报本身可能是假警报。解药出乎意料地熟悉：把测量反复做。连测 d 轮，把「警报在时间上的闪烁」也当作链的端点，在「空间×时间」的三维版图里做同样的配对游戏。思想零新增，维度加一。这也是为什么真实设备的一个逻辑比特周期要以微秒计地反复测量。

This tutorial has assumed perfect check readings. On real chips helpers err and readout squints — an alarm can itself be false. The cure is unexpectedly familiar: repeat the measurements. This is why real devices re-measure on a microsecond beat.

第 6 章Chapter 6

阈值：那条临界线The threshold: the critical line

「码做大就更安全」不是无条件的承诺。存在一个临界错误率：低于它，加大 d 让逻辑错误指数级消失；高于它，加大 d 反而更糟。这条线可以在你的浏览器里直接测出来。 “Bigger codes are safer” is not an unconditional promise. There is a critical error rate: below it, raising d makes logical errors vanish exponentially; above it, raising d makes things worse. And that line can be measured right in your browser.

6.1纸上先推一遍First, on paper

实验设定：每一轮里，每个量子比特独立地以概率 p 中一个 Z 错误；测量、译码、修复之后，检查「错误⊕修复」是否横跨——横跨即本轮失败。失败率随 d 怎么变？两股力量在掰手腕。力量一（保护）：要凑出横跨串，至少约 d/2 个错误得排成一线（译码器会救回一半以内），概率带着 p^(d/2) 这个「面积级的小」；d 每加 2，就多乘一个 p。力量二（暴露）：芯片更大，可供失败的路线和起点也更多，数量随 d 增长。谁赢？取决于 p 的大小——两股力量恰好打平的那个 p，就叫阈值加大码距开始「有利可图」的临界物理错误率 p_th：p < p_th 时越大越好，p > p_th 时越大越糟。 pth：

The setup: each round, every qubit independently suffers a Z error with probability p; after measure–decode–repair we ask whether error ⊕ repair spans — spanning means the round failed. How does failure depend on d? Two forces arm-wrestle. Force one (protection): a spanning string needs roughly d/2 errors in a row (the decoder rescues anything under half), so the probability carries p^(d/2), that “area-level smallness” — every +2 in d multiplies by another p. Force two (exposure): a bigger chip offers more failure routes and starting points, growing with d. Who wins? It depends on p — and the p where the two forces exactly tie is called the thresholdThe critical physical error rate p_th at which growing the distance starts paying off: below it bigger is better, above it bigger is worse. pth:

PL(d, p) ≈ A · (p / pth)⌈d/2⌉　(经验规律；PL = 逻辑错误率（一轮失败的概率），A = 与 d 无关的常数；⌈·⌉ = 向上取整empirical rule; PL = logical error rate (probability one round fails), A = a constant independent of d; ⌈·⌉ = round up)

读这条公式：p < pth 时括号里小于 1，指数 ⌈d/2⌉ 越大整体越小——d 每加 2，逻辑错误率乘一次同一个缩小因子，这就是指数压制；p > pth 时括号大于 1，同一条公式立刻反噬。于是所有尺寸的曲线都会在 pth 附近穿过同一点——这正是我们要在实验里寻找的「交叉」签名。

Reading it: for p < pth the bracket is below 1, and a larger exponent ⌈d/2⌉ shrinks the whole thing — every +2 in d multiplies the logical error rate by the same reduction factor: exponential suppression. For p > pth the bracket exceeds 1 and the same formula bites back. Hence curves of all sizes should pass near one common point at pth — the “crossing” signature our experiment will hunt for.

文献里的标准数字：同样「完美测量」的设定下，用最优配对译码，表面码阈值约 10.3%；把 4.3 节的测量错误、门错误全部算进去（「电路级噪声」），阈值降到 约 0.5%–1%。这就是为什么各硬件平台把「物理错误率压到千分之几」当作生死线，也是「一个好逻辑比特需要成百上千个物理比特」这句常见论断的出处：要把逻辑错误压到 10⁻¹⁰ 级，(p/p_th)^⌈d/2⌉ 里的 d 得取几十，配上帮手比特后总数便以千计。

The standard literature numbers: in the same perfect-measurement setting, optimal matching gives a surface-code threshold of about 10.3%; folding in the measurement and gate errors of §4.3 (“circuit-level noise”) lowers it to roughly 0.5%–1%. This is why hardware platforms treat “physical error rates in the few-per-thousand” as the line of life and death — and where the familiar claim “one good logical qubit costs hundreds to thousands of physical qubits” comes from: pushing logical errors to the 10⁻¹⁰ scale forces d into the tens in (p/p_th)^⌈d/2⌉, and with helper qubits included the total runs into the thousands.

6.3现实中走到哪了Where reality has reached

这条曲线不再只属于纸面。截至本教程编写时：2023 年，Google 首次在真实芯片上演示 d=5 的表面码略优于 d=3——「加大真的更好」第一次在硬件上成立；2024 年底，其 Willow 芯片把演示推进到 d=7，每加大一号，逻辑错误率约减半，首次清晰地站到了阈值以内；同期多个平台（中性原子、离子阱、超导）都在各自路线上复现「低于阈值」的行为，也有团队探索比表面码更省比特的新型编码。数字仍在快速演进，但方向已经清楚：你在本章亲手测出的那种曲线，正在实验室里一条条变成现实。

The curve no longer lives only on paper. As of this tutorial's writing: in 2023 Google first demonstrated on real hardware that a d=5 surface code slightly beats d=3 — “bigger is better” holding on a chip for the first time; in late 2024 their Willow chip pushed to d=7, roughly halving the logical error rate with each size step — the first clear operation below threshold; and platforms from neutral atoms to trapped ions to superconducting circuits have since reproduced below-threshold behaviour along their own routes, while other teams pursue newer codes thriftier in qubits than the surface code. The numbers keep moving fast, but the direction is set: the very curves you measured in this chapter are materializing, one by one, in laboratories.

旅程终点，与三条继续走的路Journey's end — and three roads onward

从 1+1=2 到此处，你完成了一条完整的推理链：奇偶与负号 → 不偷看的检查 → 两种量子错误 → 对易的几何化 → 环面上的链与圈 → 剪开后的两种边界 → 横跨串与配对译码 → 阈值与指数压制。今天任何一篇表面码论文的核心图景——警报的星空、配对的连线、往下俯冲的曲线——你都已亲手操作过。

From 1+1=2 to here, you completed one unbroken chain of reasoning: parity and minus signs → checks that don't peek → two quantum errors → commuting made geometric → chains and loops on a torus → two boundaries after the cut → spanning strings and matching decoders → threshold and exponential suppression. The core imagery of any surface-code paper today — the starfield of alarms, the matching lines, the diving curves — you have now operated with your own hands.

三条值得继续走的路。路一：更省的版图。把我们的方格斜转 45° 再裁边，就得到「旋转表面码」——同样的码距只用 d² 个数据比特（d=5 时 25 个而非 41 个），是实验室里实际部署的版本；思想与本教程的版图完全相同，只是裁剪更精。路二：让逻辑比特做事。存住只是第一步，还得对逻辑比特做门操作：把两块码拼在一起再分开（「格点手术」lattice surgery）、以及为某些关键操作准备特制状态（「魔法态」magic states）——这是当前工程化的主战场。路三：更省的码。表面码的千倍开销并非宿命，qLDPC 等新码型用更巧的检查图谱换取更高的编码率，是近年最活跃的方向。三条路的入口文献都在下方。

Three roads worth walking on. Road one: thriftier layouts. Tilt our grid 45° and trim — the “rotated surface code”, achieving the same distance with only d² data qubits (25 instead of 41 at d=5); it is the version actually deployed in labs, identical in ideas, tighter in tailoring. Road two: making the logical qubit work. Storage is step one; logical gates come next — merging and splitting code patches (“lattice surgery”) and preparing special resource states for the stubborn operations (“magic states”): today's main engineering front. Road three: thriftier codes. The surface code's thousandfold overhead is not destiny — qLDPC and friends trade cleverer check-graphs for higher encoding rates, the liveliest research direction of recent years. ⌈　⌉直角括号，读「向上取整」（ceiling）Square-cornered brackets, read “ceiling”⌈x⌉ = 不小于 x 的最小整数：⌈2.1⌉ = 3、⌈3⌉ = 3。⌈d/2⌉ 即「d 的一半，不足则进一」。⌈x⌉ = the smallest integer not below x: ⌈2.1⌉ = 3, ⌈3⌉ = 3. So ⌈d/2⌉ is “half of d, rounded up”.
∎实心小方块，读「证毕」（Q.E.D.）A filled square, read “Q.E.D.”数学家在证明末尾盖的小方章：到此证完，无需再言。The mathematician's end-of-proof stamp: done, nothing more to say.
⇒双线箭头，读「推出 / 则」A double arrow, read “implies”A ⇒ B：由 A 可以得出 B。A ⇒ B: from A one may conclude B.
⇔双向双线箭头，读「当且仅当」A two-way double arrow, read “if and only if”A ⇔ B：A 与 B 同真同假，互为充要条件——是一条双向的「推出」。A ⇔ B: A and B stand or fall together — an implication running both ways.
X̄　Z̄字母头上一横，读「X bar / Z bar」An overbar, read “X-bar / Z-bar”一横 = 「逻辑版」：X̄ 是作用在被保护的逻辑比特上的 X，由许多物理操作组成（这里 = 缠绕圈 / 横跨串）。The bar means “logical edition”: X̄ is the X acting on the protected logical qubit, built from many physical operations (here: winding loops / spanning strings).
±读「正负」Read “plus-minus”±1 = 「+1 或 −1」两个可能取值；这里每条检查的答案都是这两个之一。±1 = the two possible values “+1 or −1”; every check here answers with one of them.
√读「根号」Read “square root”√A = 面积为 A 的正方形的边长；(√A)² = A；常客：1/√2 ≈ 0.71。√A = the side of a square of area A; (√A)² = A; frequent guest: 1/√2 ≈ 0.71.
|·⟩竖线加右尖括号，读「刻特」（ket）A bar with a right angle bracket, read “ket”|0⟩ = 「贴着标签 0 的状态」：给量子状态套的礼盒括号，本质是基向量的名字。|0⟩ = “the state labelled 0”: gift-wrap for quantum states — really just a basis vector's name.
≈读「约等于」Read “approximately equals”两边近似相等。The two sides are nearly equal.
≠读「不等于」Read “not equal”两边不相等。The two sides differ.
d读「码距」或「距离参数」Read “distance” or “size parameter”最短逻辑算符（横跨串）所走的边数；这里同时是版图尺寸：n = d² + (d−1)²。The number of edges in the shortest logical operator (spanning string); here also the layout size: n = d² + (d−1)².
pth读「p 阈值」（p sub th）Read “p threshold” (p sub th)阈值：加大 d 开始有利的临界物理错误率；p < pth 时 d 每 +2，逻辑错误率乘一次 p/pth。The threshold: the critical physical error rate above which growing d starts paying; for p < pth each +2 in d multiplies the logical error rate by p/pth.
PL(d, p)　A读「P L」「A」Read “P-L” and “A”PL = 逻辑错误率（一轮纠错失败的概率），是 d 与 p 的函数；A 是与 d 无关的常数。PL = the logical error rate (probability one correction round fails), a function of d and p; A is a constant independent of d.
→　↔读「到」「与」Read “to” and “between”边界记法：「毛边界 → 毛边界」= 串从一条毛边界连到另一条；「（左 ↔ 右）」= 指哪一对边界。Boundary notation: “rough → rough” = the string runs from one rough boundary to the other; “(left ↔ right)” names the pair of boundaries.

诚实总账The honesty ledger

《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》的三项简化在这里仍然有效：全程实数振幅（Y 错误 = X、Z 同时来，两族检查都会看见它）；除 4.3 节的讨论外假设测量完美；译码器用「就近配对」，警报一多就退为贪心，而非始终最优（见 6.2 术语说明）。此外这里新增一项：我们展示的是「未旋转」的原始版图，实验室部署的旋转版在同样的思想下更省比特。所有这些简化都不影响任何一条定性结论。

The three simplifications of “From 1+1 to the Toric Code” still stand: real amplitudes throughout (a Y error = X and Z at once — both check families see it); perfect measurements except for §4.3's discussion; a “nearest-pair” decoder that falls back to greedy pairing when alarms are many, rather than an always-optimal one (see the §6.2 term note). One addition here: we showed the original “unrotated” layout, while deployed rotated versions realize the same ideas with fewer qubits. None of these simplifications touches any qualitative conclusion.
