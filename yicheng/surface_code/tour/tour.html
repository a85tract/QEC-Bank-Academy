「导览」是 Atlas 给每个码条目配的一条七步路线：它不讲新理论，而是把构造器真实导出的数据（坐标、检查、支撑）一站一站指给你看。本篇沿 surface code 的那条路线走一遍，用的是 Atlas 自己算出来的 L=3 实例 [[13,1,3]]（L 是每条重复链的比特数，也是码距）：13 个比特的坐标从哪来，6 个 X 检查和 6 个 Z 检查为什么长成那样、为什么两族互不干扰，把链合上为何变回环面码，以及一条 X 错误链从左边界走到右边界时，警报如何亮起、挪动、熄灭。 A “guided tour” is the seven-step route the Atlas attaches to every code entry: no new theory, just the data the constructor actually exported (coordinates, checks, supports), pointed out one stop at a time. This guide walks the surface-code route using the Atlas's own computed L=3 instance, [[13,1,3]] (L is the number of bits in each repetition chain, and also the distance): where the 13 coordinates come from, why the 6 X-checks and 6 Z-checks look the way they do and never disturb each other, why closing the chains gives back the toric code, and how the alarms light, move and go dark as an X error chain walks from the left boundary to the right.

第 1 章Chapter 1

导览在看什么：被导出的数据What the tour looks at: exported data

Atlas 的导览页是一个通用播放器：它不含任何 surface code 专属的代码，只是读取两份文件（七步脚本 tour.yaml 与构造器导出的 L=3 格点，L = 链长 = 码距），把每一步的高亮和说明摆到屏幕上。这一章先弄清「导出的数据」是什么、从哪里来。 The Atlas tour page is a generic player: it contains no surface-specific code, only reads two files (the seven-step script tour.yaml and the constructor's exported L=3 lattice, L = chain length = distance) and puts each step's highlight and caption on screen. This chapter first settles what “exported data” is and where it comes from.

1.1七步路线，与每一步的出处标签Seven stops, each with a provenance label

导览的标题是「Surface code: see the hypergraph product」——看见超图积hypergraph product（HGP）：Tillich–Zémor 提出的构造，把两个经典奇偶矩阵 H₁、H₂ 合成一个 CSS 量子码。surface code 是它最小的例子：两个因子都是重复码。。七步依次是：① 从两条开链出发（13 个坐标）；② 一个 X 检查一次只动一个因子；③ Z 检查交换因子角色；④ 合上链得到环面兄弟；⑤ 开边界是缺失的关联；⑥ 两族检查铺满同一批比特；⑦ 自己去盘问构造。每一步在 tour.yaml 里都带一个 origin 字段：这七步全部标为 verified，方法分别是 surface.build(L=3)、qeclib.constructions.hgp、toric.build(L=3) 与 CheckMatrix.validate。也就是说：导览里的每一个数字都是机器现算的，不是手抄的。

The tour is titled “Surface code: see the hypergraph product” — see the hypergraph productHGP: the Tillich–Zémor construction that fuses two classical parity matrices H₁, H₂ into one CSS quantum code. The surface code is its smallest example: both factors are repetition codes.. Its seven stops: ① start with two open chains (13 coordinates); ② an X-check acts one factor at a time; ③ Z-checks exchange the factor roles; ④ close the chains for the toric sibling; ⑤ open boundaries are missing incidences; ⑥ the two check families tile the same qubits; ⑦ interrogate the construction yourself. Every stop carries an origin field in tour.yaml: all seven are marked verified, by surface.build(L=3), qeclib.constructions.hgp, toric.build(L=3) and CheckMatrix.validate respectively. In other words: every number in the tour is recomputed by the machine, not copied by hand.

本篇把七步重新分成五章：第 1 章对应 ①，第 2 章对应 ②，第 3 章对应 ③ 和 ⑥，第 4 章对应 ④，第 5 章对应 ⑤ 和 ⑦。全篇只用一个实例：Atlas 的 [[13,1,3]][[n,k,d]]：n 个物理比特、k 个逻辑比特、码距 d 的量子码记法。双层方括号专属量子码，区别于经典码的 [n,k,d]。（n = 物理比特数 13，k = 逻辑比特数 1——二者的记账公式见 Toric Code 教程 4.4 节；d = 码距 3，见同一教程 5.8 节），即 L=3 的未旋转平面 surface code。

This guide regroups the seven stops into five chapters: Chapter 1 is ①, Chapter 2 is ②, Chapter 3 covers ③ and ⑥, Chapter 4 is ④, Chapter 5 covers ⑤ and ⑦. One instance throughout: the Atlas's [[13,1,3]][[n,k,d]]: the notation for a quantum code with n physical qubits, k logical qubits and distance d. Double brackets are reserved for quantum codes, as opposed to classical [n,k,d]. (n = 13 physical qubits, k = 1 logical qubit — the ledger formula for both is in the toric-code guide, §4.4; d = distance 3, see its §5.8), the unrotated planar surface code at L=3.

范围说明Scope

Atlas 构造器覆盖的是未旋转的平面族 [[2L²−2L+1, 1, L]]（L = 链长；n = 2L²−2L+1 个物理比特，k = 1 个逻辑比特，d = L 的码距），交付实例为 [[13,1,3]]、[[25,1,4]]、[[41,1,5]]。文献里更宽的「surface code」家族（旋转版、XZZX、twist、三维……）不在导览数据之内；本篇每一个坐标和支撑，都只属于这一个族。

The Atlas constructor covers the unrotated planar family [[2L²−2L+1, 1, L]] (L = chain length; n = 2L²−2L+1 physical qubits, k = 1 logical qubit, distance d = L), shipped as [[13,1,3]], [[25,1,4]] and [[41,1,5]]. The broader “surface code” collection in the literature (rotated, XZZX, twists, 3D…) is outside the tour data; every coordinate and support on this page belongs to this one family only.

1.2两条开链的乘积：13 个坐标是怎么算出来的The product of two open chains: how the 13 coordinates are computed

导览第 ① 步的原话：「L=3 时每个因子有三个比特和两条奇偶关系。它们的超图积放下 9 个 bit×bit 比特和 4 个 check×check 比特：这 13 个坐标由 surface 构造器计算得出。」拆开读。因子是最简单的经典码——长度 3 的重复码。它的奇偶矩阵 Hparity-check matrix：每一行是一条检查，每一列是一个比特；行 i 在列 j 处为 1 表示「检查 i 看比特 j」。重复码的检查是「相邻两个比特相等」，所以每行恰有两个相邻的 1。是 2 行 3 列（Atlas 记为 rep_parity(3)）：

Stop ① verbatim: “For L=3 each factor has three bits and two parity relations. Their hypergraph product places nine bit×bit qubits and four check×check qubits: the 13 coordinates shown here are computed by the surface constructor.” Unpack it. A factor is the simplest classical code, the length-3 repetition code. Its parity-check matrix HOne row per check, one column per bit; a 1 in row i, column j means “check i looks at bit j”. A repetition check says “two adjacent bits agree”, so each row has exactly two adjacent 1s. has 2 rows and 3 columns (the Atlas calls it rep_parity(3)):

H = [ 1 1 0 ; 0 1 1 ]　·　行 = 关系（2 条），列 = 比特（3 个）rows = relations (2), columns = bits (3)

现在取两份一样的因子，叫 A 和 B。超图积的量子比特分两块：bit×bit——A 的一个比特配 B 的一个比特，3×3 = 9 个；check×check——A 的一条关系配 B 的一条关系，2×2 = 4 个。这里的「×」就是「各取一个，配成一对」。构造器再给每一对分配平面坐标（第一坐标水平、第二坐标竖直）：

Now take two identical factors, call them A and B. The hypergraph product's qubits come in two blocks: bit×bit — one bit of A paired with one bit of B, 3×3 = 9 of them; check×check — one relation of A paired with one relation of B, 2×2 = 4 of them. The “×” simply means “pick one of each and pair them”. The constructor then assigns each pair a planar coordinate (first coordinate horizontal, second vertical):

关键想法Key idea

你在直觉教程里「画」出来的方格，这里是被「算」出来的：坐标是两个索引的线性函数，比特数 13 = 3² + 2² 是两块的面积之和。后面每一章都在重复同一件事——把一个几何事实还原成关于 H 的一句代数话。

The grid you “drew” in the intuition guide is “computed” here: coordinates are linear functions of two indices, and the qubit count 13 = 3² + 2² is the area of two blocks. Every later chapter repeats the same move — reducing a geometric fact to one algebraic sentence about H.

第 2 章Chapter 2

X 检查：A 的一条关系 × B 的一个比特X-checks: one relation of A times one bit of B

导览第 ② 步把一个内部 X 检查高亮，说它「由 A 的一条奇偶关系和 B 的一个比特索引，支撑是 H⊗I 与 I⊗Hᵀ 两块贡献的并」。这一章把这句话逐字变成图上的四条腿。 Stop ② highlights one bulk X-check and says it is “indexed by a parity relation of factor A and a bit of factor B; its support is the union of H⊗I and I⊗Hᵀ contributions”. This chapter turns that sentence, word by word, into four legs on the picture.

2.1两块矩阵，两个记号Two matrix blocks, two symbols

X 检查矩阵 HX 的每一行是一条 X 检查，每一列是一个数据比特——列也分成 bit×bit（9 列）和 check×check（4 列）两块。Atlas 写的公式是：

Each row of the X-check matrix HX is one X-check, each column one data qubit — and the columns split into the same two blocks, bit×bit (9 columns) and check×check (4 columns). The Atlas's formula:

HX = [ H ⊗ I3 | I2 ⊗ Hᵀ ]　左块 6×9，右块 6×4；H = 因子的 2×3 奇偶矩阵，In = n×n 单位矩阵left block 6×9, right block 6×4; H = the factor's 2×3 parity matrix, In = the n×n identity

两个记号。⊗ 是克罗内克积Kronecker product：A ⊗ B 把 A 的每个元素 a 换成整块 a·B。若 A 有 m 行 n 列、B 有 p 行 q 列，结果有 mp 行 nq 列。这里 H ⊗ I₃ 就是「H 的每个 1 换成一个 3×3 单位块，每个 0 换成 3×3 零块」。——BB 码教程的 2.2 节用「只动一个坐标的平移」讲过它；这里只需记住它的含义：H ⊗ I 让 H 作用在第一个索引 a 上、放过第二个索引 b，I ⊗ Hᵀ 反之。ᵀ 是转置transpose：把矩阵的行列互换。H 是 2×3（关系×比特），Hᵀ 就是 3×2（比特×关系）。Hᵀ 的第 j 行列出「比特 j 参与哪些关系」。：Hᵀ 是 3×2 的矩阵，第 j 行回答「比特 j 参与哪些关系」。I3、I2 是 3×3 与 2×2 的单位矩阵identity matrix：对角线全 1、其余全 0；乘任何东西都不改变它。。

Two symbols. ⊗ is the Kronecker productA ⊗ B replaces each entry a of A by the whole block a·B. If A has m rows and n columns and B has p rows and q columns, the result has mp rows and nq columns. Here H ⊗ I₃ is “every 1 of H becomes a 3×3 identity block, every 0 a 3×3 zero block”. — the BB-code guide's §2.2 introduced it as “a shift that moves only one coordinate”; here only its meaning matters: H ⊗ I lets H act on the first index a and leaves the second index b alone, and I ⊗ Hᵀ does the reverse. ᵀ is the transposeSwap rows and columns. H is 2×3 (relations × bits), so Hᵀ is 3×2 (bits × relations). Row j of Hᵀ lists the relations bit j takes part in.: Hᵀ is 3×2, and its row j answers “which relations does bit j belong to”. I3 and I2 are the 3×3 and 2×2 identity matricesAll 1s on the diagonal, 0 elsewhere; multiplying by it changes nothing..

于是 X 检查 (a,b)（A 的关系 a，B 的比特 b）的支撑读法是：左块——把关系 a 里的两个比特 a、a+1 各配上 b，得到 bit×bit 比特 (a,b) 与 (a+1,b)，坐标 (2b,2a) 与 (2b,2a+2)，正好在检查 (2b,2a+1) 的上下；右块——把 a 配上「比特 b 参与的每条关系 b′」，得到 check×check 比特 (a,b′)，坐标 (2b′+1,2a+1)，在检查的左右。内部的 b 参与两条关系（b−1 与 b），所以左右各一条腿，总重量 4。

So the support of X-check (a,b) (relation a of A, bit b of B) reads: left block — pair each of the two bits a, a+1 in relation a with b, giving bit×bit qubits (a,b) and (a+1,b) at (2b,2a) and (2b,2a+2), directly above and below the check at (2b,2a+1); right block — pair a with “every relation b′ that bit b takes part in”, giving check×check qubits (a,b′) at (2b′+1,2a+1), to the check's left and right. An interior b belongs to two relations (b−1 and b), hence one leg each side, weight 4 in total.

关键想法Key idea

直觉教程里的「星形检查 = 顶点周围四条边」，在这里是「一条关系 × 一个比特」：竖直的两条腿来自关系 a 本身（它就是「比特 a 与 a+1 相等」），水平的两条腿来自比特 b 所属的关系。四条腿，两块矩阵，各出两条。

The intuition guide's “star check = the four edges around a vertex” is here “one relation × one bit”: the two vertical legs come from relation a itself (it says “bits a and a+1 agree”), the two horizontal legs from the relations bit b belongs to. Four legs, two matrix blocks, two legs each.

第 3 章Chapter 3

Z 检查交换角色，两族为何对易Z-checks exchange roles, and why the two families commute

导览第 ③ 步：「高亮的 Z 检查把 A 的一个比特和 B 的一条关系配对；它的两块矩阵是 I⊗H 和 Hᵀ⊗I——这正是 X 族与 Z 族交叉却对易的原因。」第 ⑥ 步补上结论：两族重叠总是偶数，构造器在导出前验证了这一点。 Stop ③: “The highlighted Z-check pairs a bit of A with a parity relation of B. Its two matrix blocks are I⊗H and Hᵀ⊗I, which is why the X and Z families cross yet commute.” Stop ⑥ adds the conclusion: the families always overlap evenly, and the constructor validates that before exporting.

3.1把 a 和 b 的职责对调Swap the duties of a and b

The matrix version: any row of HX and any row of HZ share an even number of 1s; the table of all those shared counts is the matrix product HXHZᵀ. It computes block by block:

4.1合上一条链 = 多加一行，但它不独立Closing a chain = one more row, and it is dependent

开链 rep_parity(3) 有 3 个比特、2 条关系。把链首尾相接，就多出第三条关系「比特 2 与比特 0 相等」，矩阵变成 3×3。可是三行相加为零（每个比特恰被两条关系看到），新行没有带来新信息：秩rank：矩阵中真正独立的行数。开链 H 的两行独立，秩 2；闭链三行里只有两行独立，秩仍是 2。仍是 2。变化发生在转置那一侧：

The open chain rep_parity(3) has 3 bits and 2 relations. Join the ends and a third relation appears, “bit 2 equals bit 0”, making the matrix 3×3. But the three rows sum to zero (every bit is seen by exactly two relations), so the new row carries no new information: the rankThe number of genuinely independent rows. The open chain's two rows are independent, rank 2; of the closed chain's three rows only two are independent, rank still 2. stays 2. The change happens on the transpose side:

开链open: dim ker H = 1, dim ker Hᵀ = 0
闭链closed: dim ker H = 1, dim ker Hᵀ = 1H = 因子矩阵；dim ker = 核的维数（被送到零的独立向量个数）H = factor matrix; dim ker = dimension of the kernel (independent vectors sent to zero)

这里 kerkernel（核）：被矩阵送到零的向量全体。ker H 的一个元素是「通过所有检查的码字」；ker Hᵀ 的一个元素是「一组关系，乘起来什么也不说」——即检查之间的依赖。dim = 维数，独立元素的个数。 H 是「通过全部检查的码字」空间：开链与闭链都只有全 0 与全 1 两个码字，维数 1。而 ker Hᵀ 是「关系之间的依赖」：开链没有（维数 0，Atlas 称之为「开链的代数签名」），闭链有一条（三行之和为零，维数 1）。超图积的逻辑比特数由两个因子的这两个维数决定：

Here kerThe kernel: all vectors the matrix sends to zero. An element of ker H is “a codeword passing every check”; an element of ker Hᵀ is “a set of relations whose product says nothing”, i.e. a dependency among checks. dim = dimension, the number of independent elements. H is the space of “codewords passing every check”: open or closed, only all-0 and all-1 qualify, dimension 1. But ker Hᵀ is “dependencies among relations”: the open chain has none (dimension 0, which the Atlas calls “the algebraic signature of an open chain”), the closed chain has one (three rows summing to zero, dimension 1). The hypergraph product's logical count is fixed by these two dimensions of the two factors:

k = kA·kB + kAᵀ·kBᵀ
平面planar: 1·1 + 0·0 = 1　·　环面toric: 1·1 + 1·1 = 2k = 逻辑比特数；kA = dim ker HA（A 的码字数维数），kAᵀ = dim ker HAᵀ（A 的关系依赖数）；B 同理k = number of logical qubits; kA = dim ker HA (codeword dimension of A), kAᵀ = dim ker HAᵀ (relation dependencies of A); likewise for B

比特数也跟着变：闭链有 3 条关系，check×check 块从 2×2 变成 3×3，n = 9 + 9 = 18（n = 物理比特数）；检查数 3×3 + 3×3 = 18，但两族各有一条「全部相乘 = I」的依赖，独立的只有 16 条，k = 18 − 16 = 2（k = 逻辑比特数）。这与Toric Code 教程 5.3 节的记账 2L² − (2L² − 2) = 2（L = 环面边长）是同一件事的两种说法；那两条恒等式，就是 ker Hᵀ 里多出来的那一维。

The qubit count follows: the closed chain has 3 relations, the check×check block grows from 2×2 to 3×3, n = 9 + 9 = 18 (n = physical qubits); there are 3×3 + 3×3 = 18 checks, but each family has one “product of all = I” dependency, so only 16 are independent and k = 18 − 16 = 2 (k = logical qubits). This is the toric-code guide's §5.3 ledger 2L² − (2L² − 2) = 2 (L = side length of the torus) said a second way; those two identities are exactly the extra dimension in ker Hᵀ.

5.1三条腿：Hᵀ 的端点行只有一个 1Three legs: an endpoint row of Hᵀ has a single 1

回到 2.1 的读法。X0 = (a,b) = (0,0)：左块给上下两条腿 q0、q3；右块要找「B 的比特 0 参与的每条关系」——Hᵀ 的第 0 行是 (1 0)，只有关系 0。于是只有一条水平腿 q9，左边没有 q(−1)。缺失的那条腿，是因子矩阵里缺失的那个 1；在闭链里 Hᵀ 的每一行都有两个 1，所以环面上没有三腿检查（第 4 章的表）。同理 Z0 = (0,0) 的右块 Hᵀ⊗I 找「A 的比特 0 参与的关系」，也只有一条，所以 Z0 缺下腿。

Back to the reading rule of §2.1. X0 = (a,b) = (0,0): the left block gives the vertical legs q0, q3; the right block looks for “every relation containing bit 0 of B” — row 0 of Hᵀ is (1 0), relation 0 only. So there is a single horizontal leg, q9, and no q(−1) to the left. The missing leg is a missing 1 in the factor matrix; in the closed chain every row of Hᵀ has two 1s, which is why the torus has no three-leg checks (Chapter 4's table). Likewise Z0 = (0,0): its right block Hᵀ⊗I looks for “relations containing bit 0 of A”, finds one, so Z0 lacks its lower leg.

于是边界的分工直接从矩阵读出：三腿 X 检查在 x=0 与 x=4 两列（左右）——直觉教程「两种边界」一章里的光边界；三腿 Z 检查在 y=0 与 y=4 两行（上下）——毛边界。注意方向：Atlas 导出的几何把毛边界放在上下，比直觉教程的图转了 90°；本页所有沙盘都用 Atlas 的方向。

The boundaries' division of labour then reads straight off the matrix: three-leg X-checks fill the columns x=0 and x=4 (left/right) — the smooth boundary of the intuition guide's chapter on the two boundaries; three-leg Z-checks fill the rows y=0 and y=4 (top/bottom) — the rough boundary. ker · rank核 · 秩kernel · rankker H = 被送到零的向量（码字）；ker Hᵀ = 关系之间的依赖；rank = 独立行数；corank（余秩）= 行数 − rank = dim ker Hᵀ。ker H = vectors sent to zero (codewords); ker Hᵀ = dependencies among relations; rank = number of independent rows; corank = rows − rank = dim ker Hᵀ.
∂弯体 d，读「边界」A curly d, read “boundary”链的端点集；症状 = ∂(错误链) = HZeX 或 HXeZ。The endpoint set of a chain; syndrome = ∂(error chain) = HZeX or HXeZ.
eX错误向量error vector13 维 0/1 向量，出错比特处为 1；HZeX 的第 i 位 = Z 检查 i 是否报警。A 13-entry 0/1 vector, 1 on faulty qubits; entry i of HZeX = whether Z-check i fires.
[[n,k,d]]双层方括号Double brackets量子码的参数：n 物理比特、k 逻辑比特、码距 d。本篇：[[13,1,3]] 与 [[18,2,3]]。Quantum code parameters: n physical qubits, k logical qubits, distance d. Here: [[13,1,3]] and [[18,2,3]].
⇒双线箭头，读「推出」A double arrow, read “implies”A ⇒ B：由 A 可得 B。A ⇒ B: from A one may conclude B.

诚实总账The honesty ledger

本篇只讨论 Atlas 构造器交付的未旋转平面族，且只展开 L=3；[[25,1,4]] 与 [[41,1,5]] 的坐标同一公式给出，未逐条列出。所有支撑、重叠计数与症状漫步都是静态检查矩阵上的结论，假设测量完美；辅助比特、CNOT 分层与钩子故障只在 5.2 的折叠盒里提及。「码距 3」本页未证明——L=3、4 的码距由 Atlas 穷举验证（verified），L=5 为 bounded；证明与证书见参数与实例侧面。环面兄弟 [[18,2,3]] 的数字来自 toric.build(L=3)（verified），其闭链矩阵的秩分析是本篇按 Atlas 的 HGP 页面复述的。

This page treats only the unrotated planar family shipped by the Atlas constructor, and unfolds only L=3; [[25,1,4]] and [[41,1,5]] follow the same coordinate formula and are not listed. Every support, overlap count and syndrome walk is a statement about the static check matrices, assuming perfect measurement; ancillas, CNOT layers and hook faults are mentioned only in §5.2's fold-out. Distance 3 is not proved here — distances for L=3, 4 are exhaustively verified by the Atlas (verified), L=5 is bounded; proofs and certificates live in the parameters facet. The toric sibling's [[18,2,3]] numbers come from toric.build(L=3) (verified); its closed-chain rank analysis is restated here from the Atlas's HGP page.
