第 1 章Chapter 1

译码器在决定什么What a decoder decides

译码器不是在猜「哪些比特错了」——那件事原则上不可知，也不必知。它决定的是一件粗得多、也可判的事：错误属于哪一类。把这句话写成数学，「最优译码」的定义就自动掉出来，「最轻的链最可能」也只是一道对数题。 A decoder does not guess which qubits failed — that is unknowable in principle and unnecessary in practice. It decides something far coarser and decidable: which class the error belongs to. Write that sentence in mathematics and the definition of “optimal decoding” falls out, while “the lightest chain is the likeliest” becomes an exercise in logarithms.

1.1从症状到类From syndrome to class

设真实错误是一批出错的比特 E（本章只看 Z 错误，X 错误的故事在另一副眼镜里完全对称）。我们看到的只有症状 s = ∂E。任何一个候选修复 C，只要 ∂C = s，施加后症状就清零。此时残留的是 E ⊕ C：一条没有端点的闭合链。《Surface Code · 高中数学版》第 3 章的二分法接管：它要么是若干面检查的乘积（无事发生），要么含一条横跨贴片的串（逻辑错误 Z̄）。对一块 k = 1 的贴片，Z 错误只有这两类。

Let the true error be a set E of failed qubits (this chapter follows Z errors; the X story is identical through the other lens). All we observe is the syndrome s = ∂E. Any candidate repair C with ∂C = s clears the syndrome, leaving the residual E ⊕ C: a closed chain with no endpoints. The dichotomy of “The Surface Code · High-School Edition”, Chapter 3 takes over: it is either a product of plaquette checks (nothing happened) or contains a string spanning the patch (a logical error Z̄). For a k = 1 patch, Z errors fall into exactly two classes.

于是所有满足 ∂C = s 的修复也分成两类：与 E 同类的（修复成功）和不同类的（修复失败）。译码器在两类之间选一类，然后在那一类里随便挑一条链施加——挑哪条无所谓。译码是二选一，不是大海捞针。

So all repairs with ∂C = s split into two classes as well: those in E's class (repair succeeds) and those in the other (repair fails). The decoder picks a class, then applies any chain from it — which one is irrelevant. Decoding is a binary choice, not a search for a needle.

1.2最优译码 = 押概率最大的那一类Optimal decoding = bet on the most probable class

既然是二选一，最好的策略显而易见：算出「真实错误属于第一类」和「属于第二类」的概率，押大的。真实错误 E 是随机的——每个比特独立地以概率 p 出错——所以一条具体的、长 ℓ 的链出现的概率是 pℓ(1−p)n−ℓ（ℓ 个比特错、其余 n−ℓ 个没错）。一类的概率，就是把这一类里所有链的概率加起来。这样定义的译码器叫最大似然译码器maximum-likelihood decoder：给定症状，选总概率最大的那一类。它是任何译码器能达到的上限，代价是要对一类里所有链求和。，它是一切译码器的上限。

With a binary choice the best strategy is obvious: compute the probability that the true error lies in class one versus class two, and bet on the larger. The true error E is random — each qubit fails independently with probability p — so one specific chain of length ℓ occurs with probability pℓ(1−p)n−ℓ (ℓ failed qubits, the other n−ℓ intact). The probability of a class is the sum over every chain in it. The decoder so defined is the maximum-likelihood decoderGiven the syndrome, pick the class with the largest total probability. It is the ceiling for any decoder; the price is a sum over every chain in a class., the ceiling for all decoders.

为什么「押概率最大的类」就是最优？Why is “bet on the most probable class” optimal?

固定一个症状 s。译码器的任何策略最终都是「看到 s 就押某一类」。它在这个 s 下成功的概率，等于真实错误恰好落在被押的那一类的条件概率。两类的条件概率之和是 1，押大的那个，成功概率就不小于 1/2，而且没有任何别的选择能更高——因为你只有两个选项，而两个选项的成功概率就是这两个数。对每一个症状都这样做，总的成功概率就被逐项最大化了。这就是贝叶斯意义下的最优，没有别的魔法。

Fix a syndrome s. Any decoder strategy amounts to “on seeing s, bet on some class”. Its success probability under this s equals the conditional probability that the true error lies in the class it bet on. The two conditional probabilities sum to 1; betting on the larger gives at least 1/2, and nothing can do better — there are only two options, and their success probabilities are exactly those two numbers. Do this for every syndrome and the total success probability is maximised term by term. That is Bayesian optimality; no further magic.

关键想法Key idea

译码器有两种「最好」。最大似然：把一类里所有链的概率加起来，押总和大的类——真正的最优，但要求和。最小重量：只找一条最可能（最短）的链，押它所在的类——把求和换成了求最短，快得多，偶尔会错在「一类链多、单条不长」的情形（第 3 章）。整篇教程就是这两种「最好」之间的拉锯。

A decoder has two kinds of “best”. Maximum likelihood: sum the probabilities of every chain in a class and bet on the larger sum — truly optimal, but it sums. Minimum weight: find one most probable (shortest) chain and bet on its class — a sum replaced by a shortest-path search, far faster, and occasionally wrong when a class has many chains, none of them long (Ch. 3). This whole tutorial is the tug-of-war between the two.

第 2 章Chapter 2

最小重量完美匹配Minimum-weight perfect matching

「找最短的链」听起来像图论里的最短路，其实是另一个更巧的问题：把警报两两配对，使配对的路径总长最短。这一章给这个问题起名、数清它有多少种答案（多到吓人）、再说明为什么六十年前的一个算法能在多项式时间里找到最优解——以及本页的玩具版是怎么做的。 “Find the shortest chain” sounds like a shortest-path problem; it is really a subtler one: pair the alarms so that the total path length is minimal. This chapter names the problem, counts its candidate answers (frighteningly many), and explains why a sixty-year-old algorithm finds the optimum in polynomial time — and what this page's toy version does instead.

2.1症状图：警报是点，距离是边The syndrome graph: alarms as nodes, distances as edges

一条满足 ∂C = s 的链，就是若干段路径，每段连接两个警报（或连接一个警报与一段己方边界）。把它反过来看：先决定谁和谁配对，再让每对之间走最短路——最短路的长度只取决于两个端点，可以预先算好。于是问题只剩配对。画一张新图，叫症状图syndrome graph：顶点是全部警报（平面版再加一个「边界」虚拟顶点），任意两顶点之间连一条边，边的权重 = 两警报在格点上的最短距离乘 w₀。：顶点是警报，任意两点之间连一条边，权重是它们在格点上的最短距离（乘上 w₀，但同一个 p 下这是公因子，下面就用步数）。平面版再加一个虚拟顶点「边界」，每个警报到它的权重是到最近己方边界的步数。

A chain with ∂C = s is a bundle of paths, each joining two alarms (or one alarm to a boundary of its own kind). Flip the viewpoint: decide first who pairs with whom, then let each pair walk a shortest path — its length depends only on the two endpoints and can be precomputed. Only the pairing remains. The planar version adds a virtual “boundary” vertex, each alarm's weight to it being the steps to its nearest boundary of the right kind.

平面版里每个警报还可以单独「配到边界」。记 m 个警报的配法数为 T(m)：第一个警报要么独自去边界（剩 m−1 个，T(m−1) 种），要么与其余 m−1 个之一配对（剩 m−2 个，(m−1)·T(m−2) 种）。所以 T(m) = T(m−1) + (m−1)T(m−2)，T(0) = T(1) = 1，得 1, 1, 2, 4, 10, 26, 76, 232, 764, …。这个数列在组合学里叫「电话号码数」（一群人两两通话或不通话的方式数）。实验台的读数用的就是它。

On the plane each alarm may also go to the boundary alone. Matching is the zero-temperature approximation: energy only. Maximum likelihood works at a special temperature, the Nishimori line, where energy and entropy are balanced by the error rate p itself. The threshold, in this picture, is a phase transition: below it disorder (errors) cannot flip the system; above it, it can.

One array parent: parent[x] is x's superior, and a root is its own superior. find(x): follow superiors up to the root — two elements share a cluster iff their roots coincide. union(a, b): hang a's root under b's root. The key optimisation is path compression: after find walks a path, re-point every element on it directly to the root, so the next query takes one step. One can prove n operations need about n·α(n) steps in total, where α is the absurdly slow-growing inverse Ackermann function — α(n) ≤ 3 for any n that could occur in practice. Hence “almost linear”.

《Surface Code · 高中数学版》6.2 节的压制律 PL ≈ A(p/pth)⌈d/2⌉ 暗示：d 每加 2，逻辑错误率就除以 pth/p。实验上直接测量这个倍数，记作 Λ = PL(d)/PL(d+2)。Willow 芯片测得 Λ ≈ 2.14：d 从 3 到 5、5 到 7，逻辑错误率各缩小约一半。但 Λ 是整台机器加上它的译码器拟合出来的数，包含了非均匀的噪声、相关错误、译码器的精度与实时性；它不等于任何一行账本上的 pth 除以芯片的 p。把 Λ = 2.14 反推成「pth/p = 2.14」，是这本账最常见的误读。

The suppression law of “The Surface Code · High-School Edition”, §6.2 PL ≈ A(p/pth)⌈d/2⌉ suggests that each +2 in d divides the logical error rate by pth/p. Experiments measure that factor directly and call it Λ = PL(d)/PL(d+2). The Willow chip measured Λ ≈ 2.14: from d = 3 to 5 and 5 to 7, the logical error rate roughly halved each time. But Λ is a fit to the whole machine together with its decoder — heterogeneous noise, correlated errors, the decoder's accuracy and its real-time constraints; it equals no ledger row's pth divided by the chip's p. Reading Λ = 2.14 back as “pth/p = 2.14” is this ledger's most common misreading.

关键想法Key idea

阈值是「码 + 噪声模型 + 译码器 + 归一化」这一整套的属性，不是码单独的属性。换译码器，数字就动；换症状模型，数字动得更多。所以引用一个阈值时，带上它的五个限定项；比较两个阈值时，先确认它们在同一行。

A threshold is a property of the whole package — code + noise model + decoder + normalisation — not of the code alone. Change the decoder and the number moves; change the syndrome model and it moves more. So quote a threshold with its five qualifiers, and before comparing two, make sure they sit in the same row.

尾声Epilogue

从游戏到机器，与三条继续走的路From game to machine, and three roads onward

复盘整条推理链：译码是在同调类之间二选一（第 1 章）→ 对数把「最可能」变成「最短」，问题化为症状图上的最小重量完美匹配，配法数是双阶乘，而 Edmonds 的开花算法多项式时间可解（第 2 章）→ 贪心输给 3-2-3 陷阱，匹配输给 C(a+b, a) 条退化的链，这就是 10.9% 与 10.3% 之差（第 3 章）→ 并查集译码器用奇偶与合并换取几乎线性的速度（第 4 章）→ 探测器让测量错误变成时间方向的一对端点，同一个匹配问题多一维（第 5 章）→ 一本五项限定的账把所有阈值数字各归各位（第 6 章）。你现在读一篇译码器论文的摘要，应该能立刻找到它站在账本的哪一行。

Replaying the chain: decoding is a binary choice between homology classes (Ch. 1) → logarithms turn “most probable” into “shortest”, the problem becomes a minimum-weight perfect matching on the syndrome graph, pairings number a double factorial, and Edmonds' blossom algorithm solves it in polynomial time (Ch. 2) → greed loses to the 3-2-3 trap and matching to C(a+b, a) degenerate chains, which is the gap between 10.9% and 10.3% (Ch. 3) → the union-find decoder trades parity and merging for almost-linear speed (Ch. 4) → detectors turn measurement errors into endpoint pairs along time, the same matching one dimension up (Ch. 5) → a five-qualifier ledger files every threshold number (Ch. 6). Reading a decoder paper's abstract now, you should be able to locate its ledger row at once.

三条继续走的路。其一，相关性：一个 Y 错误同时触发 X 与 Z 两族检查，分开译码会丢掉这份线索；把两族配对问题耦合起来（如 belief-matching）能把电路级阈值再推高。其二，实时性：把匹配或并查集做进 FPGA 与专用芯片，让译码延迟稳定低于一轮症状——2024 年的低于阈值实验已经在做。其三，新码：qLDPC 码的症状图不再是「每个错误恰好点亮两个警报」的匹配结构，需要置信传播加有序统计译码（BP-OSD）之类的新工具。入口文献见下。

Three roads onward. One, correlations: a Y error triggers both the X and the Z check families at once, and decoding them separately throws that clue away; coupling the two matching problems (as in belief-matching) pushes circuit-level thresholds higher. Two, real time: matching or union-find cast into FPGAs and dedicated chips, keeping decoding latency reliably below one syndrome round — the 2024 below-threshold experiments already do this. Three, new codes: the syndrome graphs of qLDPC codes lose the “every error lights exactly two alarms” matching structure and need new tools such as belief propagation with ordered-statistics decoding (BP-OSD). 诚实总账The honesty ledger

全篇简化五项，一次结清：① 「最轻配法」用 2m 状态的精确枚举，只到 16 个警报，超过退回贪心；② 并查集译码器整条边地生长，原文按半条边并规定生长顺序；③ 时空译码的实验台用一维重复码（二维时空图）演示，表面码的对应物是三维，算法相同；④ 所有蒙特卡洛只加 Z 错误、只看星检查，X 一侧完全对称；⑤ 账本里的数字是各文献在各自模型下的报告值，本页实验台的交叉点因贴片小、轮数少而偏低。以上不影响任何定性结论。

Five simplifications, settled in one bill: ① the “lightest pairing” uses exact enumeration over 2m states up to 16 alarms, then falls back to greedy; ② the union-find decoder grows whole edges, whereas the paper grows half-edges in a prescribed order; ③ the spacetime bench demonstrates on a 1D repetition code (a 2D spacetime graph), the surface-code analogue being 3D with the same algorithm; ④ every Monte-Carlo adds Z errors only and watches star checks, the X side being fully symmetric; ⑤ the ledger's numbers are the literature's values under their own models, and this page's crossings sit low because patches are small and rounds few. None of this touches any qualitative conclusion.
