第 1 章Chapter 1

存住不等于计算Storage is not computation

一块贴片是一个仓库。要算，必须对逻辑比特做门——尤其是两个逻辑比特之间的门。这一章先说清需要哪些门，再看平面芯片上有哪三条路可走，最后交代一条天花板：为什么其中一个门注定做不到，只能靠「魔法」。 A patch is a warehouse. To compute, we must apply gates to logical qubits — above all, gates between two of them. This chapter states which gates are needed, surveys the three roads open on a planar chip, and ends with a ceiling: why one gate is out of reach and must come from “magic”.

1.1需要哪些门Which gates are needed

量子计算机的任何运算都能拆成单比特门加一种两比特门——CNOT（受控翻转：控制位为 1 时翻转目标位，《Surface Code · 高中数学版》第 4 章用它给检查收集奇偶）。为了纠错，人们只用一组有限的单比特门：H（交换 0/1 眼镜与 ± 眼镜）、S（给 |1⟩ 乘 i）与 T（给 |1⟩ 乘复数相位 eiπ/4，eiθ欧拉公式：eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ，模长为 1 的复数；θ = π/4 时是「转 45°」的相位，乘上它不改变 0/1 基下的测量概率（在 ± 基下会改变）。）。H、S、CNOT 合称 Clifford 门Clifford gates：把泡利串映成泡利串的门（H、S、CNOT 及其组合）。它们在稳定子形式化里可以完全用「算符怎么变」来追踪，但单靠它们不能通用计算。——它们把泡利串变成泡利串，因此在稳定子的语言里可以完全追踪；再加一个 T 门，就通用了。

Any quantum computation decomposes into single-qubit gates plus one two-qubit gate — CNOT (controlled flip: flip the target when the control is 1; “The Surface Code · High-School Edition”, Chapter 4 used it to collect parities). 于是问题是：芯片上的两块贴片，只能沿边界贴着，怎么做一个逻辑 CNOT？

So the question: two patches on a chip can only touch along an edge — how do we perform a logical CNOT?

1.2三条路Three roads

横向 CNOT。把两块一样的贴片叠在一起，对每一对对应的物理比特做一次 CNOT。可以证明这恰好实现了逻辑 CNOT，且深度为 1——但它要求两块贴片重叠。可移动的中性原子阵列真能把两块贴片搬到一起（2023 年已在 d = 3 到 7 的表面码上演示），固定布线的超导芯片做不到。

Transversal CNOT. Stack two identical patches and apply one CNOT to each corresponding pair of physical qubits. One can prove this implements a logical CNOT in depth one — but it requires the two patches to overlap. Movable neutral-atom arrays can literally carry one patch onto another (demonstrated in 2023 on surface codes from d = 3 to 7); a superconducting chip with fixed wiring cannot.

编织缺陷。在一块大贴片上关掉一些检查挖出「洞」，把洞当逻辑比特，让一个洞绕另一个洞走一圈——这也是 CNOT。它只需要邻居连接，但一个洞绕一圈要走的路不短。

Braiding defects. Punch “holes” in one large patch by switching off checks, treat holes as logical qubits, and walk one hole around another — that too is a CNOT. It needs only neighbour connections, but a full circuit around a hole is a long walk.

格点手术。2012 年 Horsman、Fowler、Devitt 与 Van Meter 提出的第三条路：不移动、不绕圈，只在两块贴片之间加一排检查测几轮，再撤掉。它测的是乘积算符 Z̄AZ̄B 或 X̄AX̄B，而第 5 章会证明两次这样的测量恰好凑成一个 CNOT。它是今天固定布线芯片上的主流方案，本教程的其余部分都讲它。

Lattice surgery. The third road, proposed in 2012 by Horsman, Fowler, Devitt and Van Meter: no moving, no circling — just add a row of checks between two patches, measure for a few rounds, then remove them. What gets measured is the product operator Z̄AZ̄B or X̄AX̄B, and Chapter 5 proves that two such measurements compose exactly one CNOT. It is today's mainstream scheme for fixed-wiring chips, and the rest of this tutorial is about it.

1.3一条天花板：Bravyi–KönigA ceiling: Bravyi–König

三条路都只做到 Clifford 门。这不是巧合。2012 年 Bravyi 与 König 证明：在二维拓扑稳定子码上，任何局域、常深度的物理操作，能实现的逻辑门至多到 Clifford 层级；T 门不在其中。Bravyi–König 定理Bravyi–König theorem（2012/2013）：二维拓扑稳定子码上，常深度且局域的电路只能实现 Clifford 层级（更准确地说是 Clifford 层级第二层）的逻辑门。T 属于第三层，故被排除。划出了平面上「便宜的门」的边界。T 门的解法是另一种资源：先制备一个带噪的「魔法态」，用 Clifford 操作把许多份蒸馏成一份高质量的，再用它通过门传送实现 T。蒸馏是今天逻辑计算里最贵的部分，本教程不展开，只在尾声给出入口。

All three roads reach only Clifford gates. That is no coincidence. In 2012 Bravyi and König proved that on a two-dimensional topological stabilizer code, any local, constant-depth physical operation implements at most a Clifford-level logical gate; T is not among them. The Bravyi–König theoremOn 2D topological stabilizer codes, constant-depth local circuits implement only Clifford-level logical gates (more precisely, level two of the Clifford hierarchy). T lives at level three and is excluded. draws the boundary of “cheap gates” on the plane. T is supplied by another resource: prepare a noisy “magic state”, distil many copies into one good one using Clifford operations, then consume it by gate teleportation to realise T. Distillation is the most expensive part of logical computation today; this tutorial leaves it aside and only gives entry points in the epilogue.

关键想法Key idea

平面芯片上的逻辑 CNOT 不是通过「搬动量子比特」实现的，而是通过测量实现的：测两次乘积算符、读一次辅助贴片，再记一笔账。格点手术解决的是「怎样用局域的操作测一个非局域的算符」这个技术问题，其余全是《从高考数学到 Toric Code》就有的投影测量代数。

A logical CNOT on a planar chip is realised not by moving qubits but by measuring: measure two product operators, read out one ancilla patch, book one correction. Lattice surgery solves the technical problem of measuring a nonlocal operator with local operations; everything else is the projective-measurement algebra of “From High-School Math to the Toric Code”.

2.2为什么它算一种「门」Why this counts as a gate

单看，一次奇偶测量是随机的：+1 或 −1 各有概率。但结果已知——我们读到了它。于是它不是噪声，而是一个带标签的投影：测到 −1 时，只要事先约定「这种情况下后面要多做一个 X̄」，两种结果就都能记账后继续使用。单独一次奇偶测量仍不是一个确定的酉门；但几次这样的测量加上依结果的修正，合起来可以是。这种「测量 + 依结果修正」的做法叫基于测量的门measurement-based gate：用若干次投影测量加上依读数选择的 Pauli 修正，实现一个确定的酉门。修正通常不必真的施加，记在 Pauli 帐上即可（第 4 章）。。第 5 章将证明：先测 Z̄CZ̄A、再测 X̄AX̄T、最后读掉辅助贴片 A，恰好是一个 CNOT。

Taken alone, one parity measurement is random: +1 or −1 with their probabilities. But the outcome is known — we read it. So it is not noise but a labelled projection: whenever −1 comes up, an agreed “apply an extra X̄ afterwards in that case” lets both outcomes be booked and carried forward. A single parity measurement is still not a deterministic unitary gate; several such measurements plus outcome-dependent corrections together can be. 2.3难点在哪里Where the difficulty lies

代数已经讲完了，难点是物理：Z̄AZ̄B 是一个横跨两块贴片、长 2d 的算符，而芯片上没有任何仪器能一口气读出 2d 个比特的联合奇偶。《Surface Code · 高中数学版》第 4 章测一条四腿检查时，办法是给它配一个辅助比特、做四个 CNOT——对 2d 个腿这样做，辅助比特要跑遍两块贴片，一个错误就会沿路传染。格点手术的答案是：不要直接测 Z̄AZ̄B，而是加一排新的四腿检查，让它们的乘积等于 Z̄AZ̄B。每条新检查仍是局域的四腿，各自用辅助比特照常测；乘积由经典计算得出。这就是下一章。

The algebra is done; the difficulty is physical: Z̄AZ̄B is an operator of length 2d spanning two patches, and no instrument on a chip reads the joint parity of 2d qubits in one go. “The Surface Code · High-School Edition”, Chapter 4 measured a four-leg check with one ancilla and four CNOTs — doing that for 2d legs would drag an ancilla across both patches, spreading any single fault along the way. 第①步，测 Z̄CZ̄A。它问「C 与 A 相同吗」。A 的两个成分里，|a = c⟩ 属于 +1 组，|a = 1−c⟩ 属于 −1 组，各占一半。读到 m1 后 A 坍缩到唯一的一个成分：a = c 若 m1 = +1，a = 1−c 若 m1 = −1。写成一行：a = c ⊕ [m1 = −1]。状态变为 |c⟩|a⟩|t⟩。

Step ①, measure Z̄CZ̄A. It asks “are C and A equal?”. Of A's two components, |a = c⟩ lies in the +1 group and |a = 1−c⟩ in the −1 group, half each. After reading m1, A collapses to a single component: a = c if m1 = +1, a = 1−c if m1 = −1. In one line: a = c ⊕ [m1 = −1]. The state is now |c⟩|a⟩|t⟩.

第②步，测 X̄AX̄T。X̄AX̄T 同时翻转 a 与 t。它的本征态是 (|a, t⟩ ± |1−a, 1−t⟩)/√2，本征值 ±1。把 |a, t⟩ 拆成这两个本征态之和的一半：读数 m2 各半概率，测后状态为 |c⟩(|a, t⟩ + m2|1−a, 1−t⟩)/√2。

Step ②, measure X̄AX̄T. X̄AX̄T flips a and t together. Its eigenstates are (|a, t⟩ ± |1−a, 1−t⟩)/√2 with eigenvalues ±1. Split |a, t⟩ into half the sum of these two eigenstates: m2 comes up ±1 with equal probability, and the post-state is |c⟩(|a, t⟩ + m2|1−a, 1−t⟩)/√2.

Three, other physics: movable neutral-atom arrays can physically carry one patch onto another for a transversal CNOT (demonstrated up to d = 7 in 2023), and the unifying view that “code deformation and lattice surgery are gauge fixing” puts this tutorial's surgery, defect braiding and general code deformation in one framework. Five simplifications, settled in one bill: ① Chapter 5's bench simulates at the logical level, treating merge plus split as one ideal product measurement, with the split readouts' bookkeeping absorbed into the signs of the three m's; ② real amplitudes throughout, complex phases changing no conclusion; ③ merge geometry is shown on unrotated d = 3, 5 layouts, deployments rotating 45° with identical seam rules; ④ Chapter 6's majority vote is a toy, real systems relying on spacetime decoding; ⑤ “3 patches × 2d rounds” and the microsecond figures are order-of-magnitude estimates omitting routing and magic-state distillation.
