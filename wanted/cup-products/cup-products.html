拓扑替我们算出门 Topology computes the gates

环面码上，一条竖直的 X 圈和一条水平的 Z 圈恰好交叉一次；把任何一条圈怎样揉捏变形，交叉的次数可以变成 3、5、7，却永远是奇数。这个「变形也改不了的奇偶」正是逻辑 X 与逻辑 Z 反对易的原因。数学家把这一类「从圈的形状里读出来的数」叫拓扑不变量，而其中一族更精细的运算——把两个 X 型圈的标签（上同调类）乘成第三个标签——叫上积。Wanted 板上的这道题问的是：能不能让机器在 QEC-Bank 登记的每一个「乘积码」的链复形上，把这些运算真正算出来，从而证明某个码天生带有一个非 Clifford 逻辑门。今天，表面码与 BB 码条目「逻辑操作」侧面里的全部 14 条记录都写着 claimed（从论文抄来）；机器算过的只有少数小码上 Pauli / Clifford 层面的 verified 记录（例如环面码的逻辑 X / Z 圈与两块对齐的 CNOT、[[4,2,2]] 的逻辑 CZ），没有任何一条非 Clifford 门是机器算出来的。 On the toric code, a vertical X loop and a horizontal Z loop cross exactly once; knead and deform either loop as you like and the crossing count can become 3, 5 or 7, but it is always odd. This “parity that no deformation can change” is exactly why the logical X and logical Z anticommute. Mathematicians call such numbers read off from the shape of loops topological invariants, and a finer family of operations among them, multiplying the labels of two X-type loops (cohomology classes) into a third label, is called the cup product. The question on the Wanted board is whether a machine can actually compute these operations on the chain complexes of every “product code” registered in the QEC-Bank, and thereby prove that a code natively carries a non-Clifford logical gate. Today all 14 records in the “Logical operations” facets of the surface and BB entries read claimed (copied from papers); the machine-computed ones are a few Pauli- or Clifford-level verified records on small entries (the toric code's logical X / Z loops and aligned-block CNOT, the [[4,2,2]] code's logical CZ), and no non-Clifford gate has ever been machine-computed.

前置：只需要《从高考数学到 Toric Code》——泡利串、碰撞定理、稳定子、环面码的缠绕圈。别的一切（交叉数、同调类、上积、Clifford 层级、链复形、乘积码、证据词）都从一个能在 3 × 3 环面上手数的玩具出发再命名。这道题的真正数学远超高中范围：本篇描述那个对象是什么、输入什么、输出什么、QEC-Bank 为什么想要输出，并明确标出描述停在哪里。状态：open（Wanted 板原文）。 Prerequisite: only “From Exam Maths to the Toric Code”: Pauli strings, the collision theorem, stabilizers, the winding loops of the toric code. Everything else (crossing number, homology class, cup product, Clifford hierarchy, chain complex, product code, evidence words) is built from a toy you can count by hand on a 3 × 3 torus before it gets a name. The real mathematics of this item lies far beyond high school: this guide describes what the object is, what goes in, what comes out and why the QEC-Bank wants the output, and marks explicitly where the description stops. Status: open (the word on the Wanted board).

第 1 章Chapter 1

这个问题用人话说是什么：两条圈交叉几次The problem in plain words: how many times do two loops cross

先在 3 × 3 环面码上数一个数：一条 Z 圈和一条 X 圈共用几条边。再把 Z 圈变形，重数一次。你会发现这个数会变，但它的奇偶不会。这一章从这个能手数的现象出发，一步步命名到「上积」，然后说清楚它和「门」有什么关系，最后逐字读 Wanted 板的原文。 First count one number on the 3 × 3 toric code: how many edges a Z loop and an X loop share. Then deform the Z loop and count again. The number changes; its parity does not. This chapter starts from that hand-countable phenomenon, names it step by step until we reach the “cup product”, explains what it has to do with “gates”, and finally reads the Wanted board word by word.

1.1玩具：3 × 3 环面上的两条圈The toy: two loops on a 3 × 3 torus

取 L = 3 的环面码，[[18, 2, 3]]（QEC-Bank 标签 toric_L3）。第 0 章事实 4 给了两条逻辑算符。Z̄₁：沿第二行的 3 条横边各放一个 Z（蓝色）。X̄：沿第二列的 3 条横边各放一个 X——这 3 条横边被一条竖直的对偶路径dual path：不沿着边走、而是穿过边、从一块面走到相邻面的路径。X 型逻辑算符住在这种路径穿过的边上；它与每个星检查的字母都是 X，所以自然对易，不需要穿过偶数次。（橙色虚线）依次穿过，这条路径绕环面竖直一圈。两条算符各自与所有检查对易（Toric Code 教程 5.6 节已验）。现在只问一件事：它们共用几条边？

Take the toric code with L = 3, [[18, 2, 3]] (QEC-Bank label toric_L3). Fact 4 of Chapter 0 gave two logical operators. Z̄₁: a Z on each of the 3 horizontal edges of the second row (blue). X̄: an X on each of the 3 horizontal edges of the second column; these 3 edges are crossed in turn by a vertical dual pathA path that does not run along edges but crosses them, stepping from one plaquette to a neighbouring one. X-type logical operators live on the edges such a path crosses; it shares only X letters with every star check, hence commutes without any even-crossing condition. (amber, dashed) that winds once vertically around the torus. Each operator commutes with every check (verified in section 5.6 of the toric-code guide). Now ask one thing only: how many edges do they share?

答案是 1：第二行、第二列那条横边。在那一格里 Z 遇上 X，c = 1，奇数——按碰撞定理，Z̄₁ 与 X̄ 反对易。这就是第 0 章事实 5 的「恰好交叉一次」，只不过我们把「交叉」落实成了「共用一条边」。人话：两条圈相遇的次数，决定两个逻辑算符是对易还是反对易。下面的沙盘就是这个码。先自己做一遍：切到 Z，点亮第二行的 3 条横边；切到 X，点亮第二列的 3 条横边；数一数两种颜色重叠的边。

The answer is 1: the horizontal edge in the second row and second column. In that cell a Z meets an X, c = 1, odd; by the collision theorem Z̄₁ and X̄ anticommute. This is the “cross exactly once” of fact 5 in Chapter 0, with “crossing” made concrete as “sharing one edge”. In plain words: the number of times two loops meet decides whether two logical operators commute or anticommute. The sandbox below is this code. Do it yourself first: switch to Z and light the 3 horizontal edges of the second row; switch to X and light the 3 horizontal edges of the second column; count the edges carrying both colours.

1.3给圈贴标签：四个类，一张 4 × 4 表Labelling loops: four classes, one 4 × 4 table

既然只有奇偶要紧，就给每条闭合圈贴一个两位的标签 (h, v)：h = 它绕环面水平方向的圈数的奇偶，v = 绕竖直方向的圈数的奇偶。可收缩的圈是 (0,0)，Z̄₁（水平圈）是 (1,0)，Z̄₂（竖直圈）是 (0,1)，两者相乘是 (1,1)。乘检查不改变标签（1.2 节）。Toric Code 教程 5.6 节末尾说过：把「相差检查乘积即视为相同」的圈归类，恰好四类，学名同调类homology class：把「相差一个可收缩部分」的圈视为同一个类。环面上的一阶 ℤ₂ 同调恰有 4 个类，对应 4 个 Z 型逻辑算符 I、Z̄₁、Z̄₂、Z̄₁Z̄₂（相差检查乘积视为相同）。。同样给 X̄ 圈（对偶路径）贴标签 (h′, v′)。那么两条圈的交叉数只由标签决定：

1.4把两个标签乘成第三个：上积Multiplying two labels into a third: the cup product

先分清两件事。1.3 节那张表是一种配对：左边拿一条 Z 圈的标签，上边拿一条 X 串的标签，配出一个数 0 或 1。数学里还有一种乘法，两个输入是同一类东西，输出还是这一类东西，叫上积cup product，记作 ⌣：把一个 p 次上同调类和一个 q 次上同调类乘成一个 p + q 次上同调类。公式见本节。，记作 α ⌣ β。要说清它，先说清它的输入：上同调类。

First, two things must be kept apart. The table of 1.3 is a pairing: take the label of a Z loop on the left and the label of an X string on top, and get a number, 0 or 1. 「上同调」听起来远，其实 1.3 节已经用过它最简单的一例。在环面码上，「给每条边分配一个 0 或 1」就是「在标 1 的边上各放一个 X」；「边界规则」要求每块面的边上 1 的个数为偶数，这正是这串 X 与每块面检查（Z 型）对易；两种分配若只差「把某个顶点周围的边全翻一次」，就算同一个类，这正是相差一条星检查。所以环面码上一个 1 次上同调类，就是一个 X 型逻辑算符（相差星检查视为相同），1.3 节给 X 串贴的标签 (h′, v′) 就是它的标签；Z 圈的标签 (h, v) 则是同调类。这里的「1 次」（degree 1）指数值分配在边这种 1 维的格子上；分配在顶点上的叫 0 次，分配在面（三角形）上的叫 2 次。

“Cohomology” sounds remote, but section 1.3 already used its simplest instance. On the toric code, “assign a 0 or 1 to every edge” means “put an X on every edge marked 1”; the “boundary rule” demands an even number of 1s on the edges of every face, which is exactly that this X string commutes with every face check (Z-type); two assignments that differ only by “flipping every edge around one vertex” count as the same class, which is exactly differing by a star check. So on the toric code a degree-1 cohomology class is an X-type logical operator (up to star checks), and the labels (h′, v′) that 1.3 gave to X strings are its labels, while the labels (h, v) of Z loops are homology classes. “Degree 1” means the numbers sit on 1-dimensional cells, the edges; numbers on vertices make degree 0, numbers on faces (triangles) degree 2.

Notation: α[v₀v₁] = the value (0 or 1) of α on the edge from v₀ to v₁, β[v₁v₂] likewise; “·” is ordinary multiplication; ∫α ⌣ β, read “the total of alpha cup beta”, adds the values over all triangles (the Σ) and keeps the parity. Then each CCZ shares a qubit with at most 3(s − 1) others, and placing them one by one needs at most 3s − 2 layers. In the 3 × 3 × 3 example below s = 2, and the script packs the circuit into 2 layers. Lemma 2 proves that it preserves the code space and performs a logical CCZ on α of A, β of B and γ of C exactly when ∫α ⌣ β ⌣ γ = 1. In three dimensions the dual shape of a degree-1 class is a membrane (1.4), so ∫α ⌣ β ⌣ γ counts, mod 2, the points common to three dual membranes (the paper's eq. (42)), not “three loops meeting”. Example: the three-dimensional torus (a cube with each pair of opposite faces glued) has three degree-1 classes x, y, z whose dual membranes are planes perpendicular to the three axes; three mutually perpendicular planes meet in exactly one point, so ∫x ⌣ y ⌣ z = 1; whenever two directions repeat (x, x, y, say), two parallel membranes never meet and the total is 0. This guide's script checked all 27 combinations on the 3 × 3 × 3 three-torus (27 vertices, 162 tetrahedra) and recomputed after 300 random star-check deformations, with the same results. Phase: each copy has 3 logical qubits, written (x bit, y bit, z bit). The script finds that the combinations (class of A, class of B, class of C) with ∫ = 1 are exactly the 6 orderings of x, y, z, so the circuit is a product of 6 logical CCZs and the phase exponent is the sum of the 6 terms “bit of A · bit of B · bit of C”, mod 2. Example: A = (1, 0, 0), B = (0, 1, 0), C = (0, 0, 1); only the (x, y, z) term is 1, so the state is multiplied by −1. Counterexample: A = B = (1, 1, 0), C = (0, 0, 1); both the (x, y, z) and the (y, x, z) terms are 1, the sum is 2, and the state is unchanged, even though A's x bit, B's y bit and C's z bit are all 1. So the three named bits alone do not decide the phase; the other bits count too. The script checked this formula on all 512 logical assignments (each copy in a random representative). CCZ is at level 3, a non-Clifford gate, which is exactly the level Bravyi–König allows in three dimensions.

这个构造说明了什么，没说明什么。它证明：满足这些条件的码带着这些具体的常深度电路作为逻辑门，门由上积的总值决定。它不说一个码只有这些门，也不能判定任意一个常深度操作做的是什么门；单看两两或三三重叠也不能给门分类。这就是标题那句话的范围：如果机器能在码的结构上算出上积，它就能为这样构造出来的门出具证书——而不是判定这个码所有的门，也不能取代 Bravyi–König 那样的禁止定理。QEC-Bank 设计文档 3.2 节把这一点写进了数据模型：“cohomology operations (cup products) — these are operations-facet inputs, since they certify native non-Clifford logicals on product codes (Zhu, arXiv:2501.19375).” 本篇读了这篇论文的第 II.1–II.2 节（上面两个构造的出处）；第 VI.1–VI.2 节只读了检查与比特放在哪一维的格子上（4.4 节用到），没有读它怎样把一般的乘积码变成这样的复形的构造与证明（第 III–V 节及第 VI 节其余部分）。

What the construction shows, and what it does not. It proves that codes meeting these conditions carry these particular constant-depth circuits as logical gates, the gates being fixed by the totals of cup products. It does not say that a code has only these gates, it cannot decide which gate an arbitrary constant-depth operation performs, and pairwise or triple overlaps on their own do not classify gates. That is the scope of the title: if a machine can compute cup products on a code's structure, it can certify the gates built this way, not classify every gate a code might have, and not replace obstructions such as Bravyi–König. The QEC-Bank design document, section 3.2, wrote this into the data model: “cohomology operations (cup products) — these are operations-facet inputs, since they certify native non-Clifford logicals on product codes (Zhu, arXiv:2501.19375).” This guide read sections II.1–II.2 of that paper (the source of the two constructions above); of sections VI.1–VI.2 it read only which cell dimensions hold the checks and the qubits (used in 4.4), not the constructions and proofs that turn general product codes into such complexes (sections III–V and the rest of VI).

补课 · Clifford 层级：为什么 T 不是 Clifford 门Catch-up · The Clifford hierarchy: why T is not a Clifford gate

门按「它把泡利串变成什么」分级。记 U† 为撤销 U 的门，「U 把 P 变成 U P U†」。第 1 级 C₁：泡利串本身。第 2 级 C₂（Clifford 门）：把每条泡利串变成另一条泡利串（可带 ± 或 ±i）。第 3 级 C₃：把每条泡利串变成一个第 2 级的门。低一级的门都属于高一级：C₁ ⊂ C₂ ⊂ C₃（泡利串把泡利串变成 ± 它自己，所以在 C₂ 里；Clifford 门把泡利串变成泡利串，而泡利串属于 C₂，所以 Clifford 门在 C₃ 里）。CZ 在 C₂，CCZ 和 T 在 C₃ 而不在 C₂。为什么非 Clifford 的东西不可少。Gottesman–Knill 定理说：从稳定子态出发（例如全 |0⟩）、只用 Clifford 门和泡利测量的电路，普通计算机能高效模拟。「从稳定子态出发」这个条件不能省：同样的 Clifford 电路，输入换成非稳定子的魔法态就不再好模拟——Yoganathan、Jozsa 与 Strelchuk（arXiv:1806.03200）证明，只以魔法态为输入的 Clifford 电路在标准的复杂性假设下难以经典模拟；配上魔法态注入，Clifford 操作还能做通用计算（2.1 节表面码的那条 T 就是这样得到的）。所以通用量子计算需要的是某种非 Clifford 的资源：一个非 Clifford 门（T 是最常用的一例，在第 3 级），或者非稳定子的输入态。Bravyi–König 的「一个空间维度一级」说的是：D 维拓扑码上局域常深度的操作最多到第 D 级；二维最多到第 2 级，即 Clifford。

Gates are graded by what they turn Pauli strings into. Write U† for the gate that undoes U, and “U turns P into U P U†”. Level 1, C₁: the Pauli strings themselves. Level 2, C₂ (Clifford gates): every Pauli string goes to another Pauli string (possibly with ± or ±i). Level 3, C₃: every Pauli string goes to a level-2 gate. Every gate of a lower level also belongs to the higher one: C₁ ⊂ C₂ ⊂ C₃ (a Pauli string turns Pauli strings into ± themselves, so it lies in C₂; a Clifford gate turns Pauli strings into Pauli strings, which lie in C₂, so it lies in C₃). CZ is in C₂; CCZ and T are in C₃ but not in C₂. Why something non-Clifford is indispensable. The Gottesman–Knill theorem says: circuits that start from stabilizer states (all |0⟩, say) and use only Clifford gates and Pauli measurements can be simulated efficiently on an ordinary computer. The condition “start from stabilizer states” cannot be dropped: the same Clifford circuits fed non-stabilizer magic states are no longer easy to simulate. Yoganathan, Jozsa and Strelchuk (arXiv:1806.03200) show that Clifford circuits with only magic-state inputs are hard to simulate classically under standard complexity assumptions, and with magic-state injection Clifford operations become universal (that is how the surface-code T of 2.1 is obtained). So universal quantum computation needs some non-Clifford resource: a non-Clifford gate (T is the most common example, at level 3), or non-stabilizer input states. Bravyi–König's “one level per spatial dimension” says: on a D-dimensional topological code, local constant-depth operations reach at most level D; in two dimensions at most level 2, the Clifford gates.

Triorthogonality (Bravyi and Haah, arXiv:1209.2426): a 0/1 matrix is triorthogonal when the overlap of any two rows (the number of positions where both are 1) is even, and the overlap of any three rows is even as well; they prove that the stabilizer code built from a triorthogonal matrix has a transversal T gate on all its logical qubits (possibly together with Clifford gates). A worked example: the three rows 11110000, 11001100, 10101010. Pairwise overlaps: rows 1 and 2 are both 1 in positions 1 and 2, two positions; rows 1 and 3 in positions 1 and 3, two; rows 2 and 3 in positions 1 and 5, two; all even. All three rows are 1 only in position 1, one position, odd, so this matrix is not triorthogonal. 一个合格的答案是一台程序：输入 QEC-Bank 已经存着的链复形，输出一张上积表和从表里读出的、带证书的门清单。难度写的是 open-research——不是「知道怎么做但要做很多」，而是「连该怎么定义都还在研究」。这一章先说输入输出，再说三个诚实的难点，最后用玩具尺度对照一下：难的不是大小。 A qualifying answer is a program: input the chain complexes the QEC-Bank already stores, output a cup-product table and, read off it, a certified list of gates. The difficulty reads open-research: not “known how, much to do” but “even the right definition is still being researched”. This chapter gives input and output first, then three honest difficulties, then a toy-scale comparison: size is not the hard part.

4.1输入与输出Input and output

输入。一个乘积码的链复形。QEC-Bank 基础页 foundations/css-chain-complexes/intuition.md 的一句话定义：“Two classical codes with H_X H_Z^T = 0 are one chain complex; homology counts logicals.” 对环面码，这就是「顶点—边—面」三层和两张矩阵：哪些边碰哪个顶点（星检查），哪些边围哪块面（面检查）。但只有矩阵还不够：上积的公式（1.4 节）要用到复形的形状——哪些格子是三角形、每个三角形的顶点怎样排序。乘积码的矩阵本身不带这些，要从它是怎样乘出来的（两个零件各是什么、怎样配对）去造出这样的复形，这正是 arXiv:2501.19375 所做的事（4.3 节）。

Input. The chain complex of a product code. The one-line definition on the QEC-Bank foundations page foundations/css-chain-complexes/intuition.md: “Two classical codes with H_X H_Z^T = 0 are one chain complex; homology counts logicals.” For the toric code this is the three layers vertices, edges, faces and two matrices: which edges touch which vertex (star checks), which edges bound which face (plaquette checks). Matrices alone are not enough, though: the cup-product formula (1.4) needs the shape of the complex, which cells are triangles and how each triangle orders its corners. The matrices of a product code do not carry this, so such a complex must be built from how the code was multiplied together (what the two parts are and how they pair), which is what arXiv:2501.19375 does (4.3).

HX HZT = 0

记号：HX = X 型检查的 0/1 矩阵（每行一条星检查，每列一个比特），HZ = Z 型检查的矩阵；上标 T = 转置（行列互换）；乘积为 0 是「每条星检查与每条面检查共用偶数个比特」的矩阵写法，即第 0 章事实 1 的对易条件（qLDPC 教程 1.2 节）。

Notation: HX = the 0/1 matrix of X-type checks (one row per star check, one column per qubit), HZ = the matrix of Z-type checks; superscript T = transpose (swap rows and columns); the product being 0 is the matrix form of “every star and every plaquette share an even number of qubits”, the commutation condition of fact 1 in Chapter 0 (qLDPC guide 1.2).

一、QEC-Bank 存的链复形没有几何。上积的教科书定义依赖「哪块面的哪个角是哪个顶点」这类形状信息，而 QEC-Bank 的链复形库只有矩阵。Wanted 板上另一道 partial 的题 chain-complex-lib 的 statement 原文说得清楚：“Extend the minimal ChainComplex (length-3, no geometry) to cell complexes with boundaries, higher dimensions, and coordinate embeddings.” 它的 current_proxy 是 “qeclib.chain (length-3, matrix-only) + hand-coded lattice dicts.” 也就是说，上积计算器的输入格式本身还没有建好：只有 3 项（顶点、边、面这种三层）的矩阵，没有形状，也没有更高维的层。

One: the chain complexes the QEC-Bank stores have no geometry. The textbook definition of the cup product relies on shape information such as “which corner of which face is which vertex”, while the QEC-Bank chain-complex library holds only matrices. The statement of another partial item on the board, chain-complex-lib, says so verbatim: “Extend the minimal ChainComplex (length-3, no geometry) to cell complexes with boundaries, higher dimensions, and coordinate embeddings.” Its current_proxy is “qeclib.chain (length-3, matrix-only) + hand-coded lattice dicts.” In other words, the input format for a cup-product calculator does not exist yet: 3-term matrices only (three layers, like vertices, edges, faces), no shape and no higher layers.

二、乘积码不一定是「形状」，要先给它造一个。环面码画得出来，因为它真的是一个甜甜圈的表面：比特正好在曲面剖分的边上，检查正好是剖分的星和面。BB 码不是这样的码。它的检查要连到隔几格、绕过环面的比特（BB 教程 1.2 节），所以它不是「比特平铺在平面上、每条检查只碰距离有限的比特、单位面积比特数有界」这种二维局域布局上的码；对这种布局，Bravyi–Poulin–Terhal 定理限定 k·d² ≤ c·n（BB 教程 1.1 节）。注意这和「连线画不画得上曲面」是两回事：任何有限的图都能画在洞足够多（亏格足够高）的曲面上而不交叉；而表面码意义下的「曲面上的码」要求检查恰好是一个剖分的星和面，这比「图能画上去」严格得多。一般的超图积（qLDPC 教程第 2 章）同样不是现成的曲面。arXiv:2501.19375 正是为这件事而写：它的摘要说，从乘积构造得到的 qLDPC 码与 CSS 码都带着「隐藏的」单纯复形或 CW 复形结构——把零件码的 Tanner 图「加厚」成流形，再三角剖分（或收缩回一个 CW 复形），上积就定义在这个结构上，并能诱导非 Clifford 门；论文对两类三维乘积码给出了常深度的逻辑 CCZ。所以缺的不是「没人知道怎样定义」，而是两段具体的空白。其一是适用范围：论文引言写明，要得到第 3 级的非 Clifford 门，码要定义在三维（4 项）链复形上，而 gross 这样的 BB 码是 3 项的；论文的例子也是它自己构造的三维乘积码，不是 QEC-Bank 里现成的 surface 或 gross。能不能、怎样用在 QEC-Bank 的条目上，要有人去做。其二是实现：把论文的构造写成程序、在具体复形上算出上积、再把结果写成证书，今天没有这样的库（4.2 节）。

Two: a product code need not be a “shape”; one has to be built for it. The toric code can be drawn because it really is the surface of a doughnut: its qubits sit exactly on the edges of a cellulation of the surface and its checks are exactly the stars and faces. BB codes are not codes of that kind. Their checks reach qubits a few sites away and around the torus (BB guide 1.2), so they are not codes on a two-dimensional local layout, “qubits tiled in the plane, every check touching only qubits within a bounded distance, a bounded number of qubits per unit area”; for such layouts the Bravyi–Poulin–Terhal theorem caps k·d² ≤ c·n (BB guide 1.1). Note that this is a different matter from whether the connections can be drawn on a surface: every finite graph can be drawn without crossings on a surface with enough holes (high enough genus), while a “code on a surface” in the surface-code sense requires the checks to be exactly the stars and faces of a cellulation, far stricter than “the graph can be drawn”. General hypergraph products (qLDPC guide, Chapter 2) are not ready-made surfaces either. arXiv:2501.19375 is written for exactly this: its abstract states that qLDPC and CSS codes obtained from product constructions carry “hidden” simplicial or CW complex structures, obtained by thickening the Tanner graphs of the component codes into manifolds and triangulating them (or retracting to a CW complex); cup products are defined on that structure and can induce non-Clifford gates, and the paper gives constant-depth logical CCZ gates for two kinds of three-dimensional product codes. So what is missing is not “nobody knows how to define it” but two concrete gaps. One is applicability: the paper's introduction says that a gate at level 3 needs a code defined on a three-dimensional (4-term) chain complex, while BB codes such as gross are 3-term; the paper's examples are three-dimensional product codes it constructs itself, not the QEC-Bank's existing surface or gross. Whether and how it applies to QEC-Bank entries remains to be worked out. The other is implementation: turning the construction into a program, computing cup products on concrete complexes and writing the results as certificates; no such library exists today (4.2).

三、从上积表到「门」还有一段路。即使算出了表，「这个常深度电路在 gross 上实现某个逻辑 CCZ」还要在这个具体的复形上核对论文引理的条件（这样才能保证它保持码空间）、算出它对全部逻辑比特的作用（gross 有 k = 12 个逻辑比特，BB 操作教程 3.2 节列了认证一个门的五步），再写成证书。这一段与 transversal-gate-cert 重叠，是 hard-engineering 而非 open-research，但它今天也没有做。

Three: from the cup-product table to a “gate” there is still a road. Even with the table computed, “this constant-depth circuit implements some logical CCZ on gross” still needs the conditions of the paper's lemmas checked on that concrete complex (which is what guarantees it preserves the code space), its action on all logical qubits computed (gross has k = 12, and BB operations guide 3.2 lists the five steps to certify a gate), and the result written as a certificate. This stretch overlaps with transversal-gate-cert and is hard-engineering rather than open-research, but it too is undone today.

4.4玩具尺度的对照：难的不是大小Toy-scale comparison: size is not the hard part

和 《大码的码距》那道题不同，这道题的难点不是组合爆炸。先说清要数的是什么。上积的输入是比特所在那一层的上同调类：比特放在 q 维的格子上，输入就是 q 次上同调类，对 CSS 码就是 X 型逻辑算符的类（相差 X 检查视为相同）；Z 型逻辑算符是同调类，配对时才用，不是这里的输入。两两上积落在 2q 次，三三落在 3q 次。环面码与 1.5 节的例子里比特在边上，q = 1，所以前面一直说「1 次类」。q 不一定是 1：Zhu 把一般的量子码搬到 CW 复形上时（第 VI.1–VI.2 节，沿用 Freedman–Hastings 的映射），X 检查、比特、Z 检查分别放在 3 维、4 维、5 维的格子上，码空间的一组基由第 4 层的同调群 H₄ 标记（dim H₄ = k 时，量子码空间是 2k 维；同调群本身是 0/1 系数的空间，不是量子态的希尔伯特空间），输入就是 4 次类，两两乘积在 8 次、三三在 12 次；它的加厚三维超图积码把比特放在 12 维流形三角剖分的 4 维单形上，三个 4 次类的上积正好是 12 次，在整个流形上求和（引言与定理 4）。一份码的 q 次上同调类组成一个 k 维的空间：每个类由 k 个 0/1 的「位」决定，所以取 k 个基类就够。又因为上积对每个输入都是线性的（(α + α′) ⌣ β = α ⌣ β + α′ ⌣ β，论文式 (13) 的「双线性」），只要算基类之间的乘积，其余的都由加法得到。环面码 k = 2，基类就是图 1.4 的 α、β：两两上积 2 × 2 = 4 个，每个输出是 2 次类；环面上 2 次类只有一个维度（「整张曲面」），所以每个输出就是一个数，即图 1.4 下算出的 ∫ 值。gross k = 12：如果先给它造出一个带上积的复形，并在其中认出一个 12 维的逻辑输入空间（q 次上同调里对应 gross 那 12 个逻辑比特的部分），两两才有 12 × 12 = 144 个输出，三三 12³ = 1 728 个；造出的复形若让输入空间更大或更小，这两个数随之改变。每个输出写成多少个数，取决于那个复形的 2q 次、3q 次上同调各有多少维，这同样要等复形造出来才知道（4.3 节）。QEC-Bank 存的 3 项复形本身只有三层（q = 1，没有 3 次的格子），即使在它上面定义了上积，三三乘积也只能全为零。这些计数都不大，但表的格数说明不了计算要花多久：那取决于复形有多大、用什么算法，本篇没有估算。难的是第 4.3 节的前两条：在什么复形上算（要先造出来，而且要有足够的维数）和拿什么算（输入格式和程序都还不存在）。人话：这不是一道「表太大算不完」的题，而是一道「要算的那个复形和程序都还没有」的题。

Unlike the “Distance for large codes” item, the difficulty here is not combinatorial explosion. First, what is to be counted. The inputs of the cup product are cohomology classes of the layer that holds the qubits: with qubits on q-dimensional cells, the inputs are degree-q cohomology classes, which for a CSS code are the classes of X-type logical operators (up to X checks); Z-type logicals are homology classes, used in the pairing, and are not inputs here. Pairwise products land in degree 2q, triple products in degree 3q. On the toric code and in the examples of 1.5 the qubits sit on edges, q = 1, which is why the text so far said “degree-1 classes”. q need not be 1: when Zhu carries a general quantum code over to a CW complex (sections VI.1–VI.2, following the Freedman–Hastings mapping), X checks, qubits and Z checks sit on 3-, 4- and 5-dimensional cells, a basis of the code space is indexed by the layer-4 homology H₄ (when dim H₄ = k the quantum code space has dimension 2k; the homology itself is a space with 0/1 coefficients, not the Hilbert space of quantum states), and the inputs are degree-4 classes, with pairwise products in degree 8 and triple ones in degree 12; its thickened 3D hypergraph product codes put the qubits on the 4-simplices of a triangulated 12-manifold, where the product of three degree-4 classes has degree exactly 12 and is summed over the whole manifold (introduction and Theorem 4). The degree-q cohomology classes of one code form a k-dimensional space: each class is fixed by k bits of 0/1, so a basis of k classes suffices. And since the cup product is linear in each input ((α + α′) ⌣ β = α ⌣ β + α′ ⌣ β, the “bilinear” of the paper's eq. (13)), only products of basis classes need computing; the rest follow by addition. The toric code has k = 2, with basis classes the α and β of Fig. 1.4: 2 × 2 = 4 pairwise products, each output a degree-2 class; on the torus the degree-2 classes form a single dimension (“the whole surface”), so each output is one number, the ∫ values computed with Fig. 1.4. gross has k = 12: if one first builds a complex with cup products for it and identifies in it a 12-dimensional logical input space (the part of degree-q cohomology that corresponds to the 12 logical qubits of gross), there are 12 × 12 = 144 pairwise outputs and 12³ = 1 728 triple ones; a complex whose input space is larger or smaller changes both numbers. How many numbers each output takes depends on how many dimensions the degree-2q and degree-3q cohomology of that complex has, which is likewise known only once the complex is built (4.3). The QEC-Bank's stored 3-term complex has only three layers (q = 1, no degree-3 cells), so even if a cup product were defined on it, every triple product would be zero. These counts are small, but a table's cell count says nothing about how long the computation takes: that depends on the size of the complex and on the algorithm, and this guide makes no estimate. The hard part is the first two points of 4.3: on which complex to compute (it must first be built, with enough dimensions) and with what (neither the input format nor the program exists yet). In plain words: this is not a problem whose table is too big to finish; it is one where the complex to compute on and the program to compute with do not exist yet.

记号：q = 比特所在格子的维数，也就是上积输入的次数；k = 逻辑比特数，也就是 q 次上同调类空间的维数；12 × 12 与 12³ 是本篇自己算的个数，前提是认出了 12 维的逻辑输入空间，不是 QEC-Bank 页面上的数。

一个奇偶、一张表、十四个 claimedOne parity, one table, fourteen claimed

回放一遍。环面码上一条 Z 圈与一条 X 圈共用的边数，随变形而变，奇偶不变；这个奇偶就是两个逻辑算符对易与否，四个圈类之间的全部答案是一张 4 × 4 表，公式 h·v′ + v·h′。上积把两个上同调类乘成一个更高次的类，次数相加；在切成三角形的环面上，它的总值就是两条对偶圈交叉的奇偶，所以那张表也能读成上积表。它能再乘第三个类：在三维的复形上，三个 1 次类的总值数的是三张对偶膜共同交点的奇偶。Zhu 的构造把这些总值变成门：二维上两份码之间的一张 CZ 电路（Clifford），三维上三份码之间的一张 CCZ 电路（第 3 级，非 Clifford）——它证出这些门，不判定一个码的全部门。QEC-Bank 今天在 surface 与 bivariate_bicycle 的「逻辑操作」侧面存了 9 + 5 = 14 条记录，全部 claimed，唯一的非 Clifford 是靠魔法态买来的 T，自带的一条也没有——「没有记录」而非「证明没有」。一台能在乘积码的链复形上算上积的机器，能为上积构造证出的那些门（1.5 节）写出 derived 或 verified 的记录，让「哪个码更适合算」多出一栏可以比较的内容；这 14 条里靠魔法态的 T 以及手术、编织等其余机制的记录不在此列，仍要有人登记并注明出处（3.3 节）。难度 open-research：论文已经给乘积码造出了带上积的复形（arXiv:2501.19375，登记待补），但它要 4 项的复形、例子是论文自己构造的码，用到 QEC-Bank 的条目上还没人做；输入格式（chain-complex-lib 是 partial）和程序也都还没有。难的不是表的大小：即使在造出的复形里认出了 gross 的 12 维逻辑输入空间，两两上积也只有 12 × 12 = 144 个输出。

Replay. On the toric code the number of edges a Z loop and an X loop share changes under deformation; its parity does not. That parity is whether the two logical operators commute, and the full answer among the four loop classes is one 4 × 4 table, formula h·v′ + v·h′. The cup product multiplies two cohomology classes into a class of higher degree, the degrees adding; on the torus cut into triangles its total is the crossing parity of two dual loops, so the table can also be read as a cup-product table. It can multiply a third class: on a three-dimensional complex the total of three degree-1 classes counts, mod 2, the points common to three dual membranes. Zhu's construction turns these totals into gates: a CZ circuit between two copies in two dimensions (Clifford), a CCZ circuit between three copies in three dimensions (level 3, non-Clifford); it certifies these gates and does not decide all the gates of a code. Today the QEC-Bank holds 9 + 5 = 14 records in the “Logical operations” facets of surface and bivariate_bicycle, all claimed; the only non-Clifford one is a T bought with magic states, and native ones number zero, “no record” rather than “proven absent”. A machine computing cup products on the chain complexes of product codes could write derived or verified records for the gates the cup-product construction certifies (1.5), giving “which code computes better” some content that can be compared; the T by magic states and the other records among the 14 (surgery, braiding and other mechanisms) are outside its reach and still have to be entered by people with their sources (3.3). Difficulty open-research: the paper has built complexes with cup products for product codes (arXiv:2501.19375, registration pending), but it needs 4-term complexes and its examples are codes it constructs itself; nobody has yet carried it over to QEC-Bank entries, and neither the input format (chain-complex-lib is partial) nor a program exists. Table size is not the issue: even once a 12-dimensional logical input space for gross is identified in a built complex, its pairwise cup products have only 12 × 12 = 144 outputs.
