环面码和表面码的译码是一场配对游戏。在没有边界的环面码内部，一个单比特 Z 错误恰好点亮两盏警报灯（两颗星）；X 错误同样点亮两盏，只是换成面；Y 错误同时是 X 和 Z，两种各亮两盏；而在表面码的边界上，一个错误可以只点亮一盏。本篇只看环面上的 Z 错误和星警报。灯两两连线，沿线修复。这个游戏能玩，靠的是两件事——警报成对出现，而且一盏警报可以单独移动。有一类码里这两件事都不成立。第 1 章的计数板玩具里，警报四个四个地出现，一盏灯想挪一步，必须拖着另一盏一起走；真正的 Haah 立方码更彻底：它内部那些不能靠局部操作凭空造出的警报，无论单个还是成团，都挪不动。这类码叫分形子码（fracton code），配对游戏在它们身上失效，而替代的游戏至今没有一个被公认为「标准」。QEC-Bank 于是在三张卡片的译码器一栏里诚实地写下 none-known，代价是这些码在电路级性能对比表里是空行（理想症状下的通用 CSSCSS 码：每条检查要么全是 X、要么全是 Z 的稳定子码。X 错误只被 Z 检查看见，Z 错误只被 X 检查看见，两半可以分开译码。 译码与蒙特卡洛跑法仍然可用，haah_cubic 也有研究级的 RG 与 BP-OSD 记录）。这一篇讲这道开放问题是什么、QEC-Bank 现在怎么凑合、为什么我们想要答案、一个合格的答案长什么样。 Decoding the toric and surface codes is a pairing game. Inside a toric code, which has no boundary, a single-qubit Z error lights exactly two alarm lamps (two stars); an X error also lights two, on faces instead; a Y error is X and Z at once and lights two of each; and at a surface-code boundary one error can light a single lamp. This guide looks only at Z errors and star alarms on the torus. Lamps are joined in pairs, and the repair runs along the join. The game works because of two facts: alarms come in pairs, and one alarm can be moved on its own. There is a class of codes where neither holds. On the counter-board toy of Chapter 1, alarms appear four at a time, and to move one lamp a step you must drag another with it; Haah's real cubic code goes further: the alarms inside it that no local operation can create from nothing cannot move at all, singly or as a cluster. These are fracton codes; the pairing game breaks on them, and no replacement game has yet been accepted as “standard”. So the QEC-Bank honestly writes none-known in the decoder field of three cards, at the price that those codes leave empty rows in the circuit-level comparison tables (generic CSSCSS code: a stabilizer code in which every check is either all X or all Z. X errors are seen only by Z checks and Z errors only by X checks, so the two halves can be decoded separately.-pivot decoding and code-capacity Monte Carlo still run, and haah_cubic has research-grade RG and BP-OSD records). This guide explains what the open problem is, how the QEC-Bank copes today, why we want the answer, and what a qualifying answer looks like.

这个问题用人话说是什么：配对游戏为什么能玩，以及它在哪里玩不下去The problem in plain words: why the pairing game works, and where it stops working

先在 18 个比特的环面码上亲手玩一遍配对游戏，看清它靠哪两件事成立。再换到一块 4 × 4 的计数板上，亲手验证这两件事都不成立。最后给「译码器」「标准」「基准行」三个词下定义，再念板上的原文。 First play the pairing game by hand on the 18-qubit toric code and see which two facts it rests on. Then move to a 4 × 4 counter board and verify by hand that both facts fail there. Finally define the three words “decoder”, “standard” and “benchmark row”, and read the board's wording.

1.1配对游戏为什么能玩：成对，且能动Why the pairing game works: in pairs, and mobile

下面是 L = 3 的环面码沙盘（[[18, 2, 3]]双方括号是量子码的记法：n = 18 个物理比特，k = 2 个逻辑比特，码距 d = 3。）。做两件事。第一件：点一条边，放一个 Z 错误。恰好两盏灯亮——那条边的两个端点。第二件：再点一条与它相邻的边。亮灯仍是两盏，但其中一盏挪了一格：链变长了，端点跟着走，中间那个顶点碰到两个错误，2 是偶数，它安静下来。

Below is the sandbox of the toric code with L = 3 ([[18, 2, 3]]Double brackets denote a quantum code: n = 18 physical qubits, k = 2 logical qubits, distance d = 3.). Do two things. First: click one edge to place a Z error. Exactly two lamps light: the two endpoints of that edge. Second: click an adjacent edge. Still two lamps, but one of them has moved one step: the chain grew, the endpoint followed, and the vertex in the middle touches two errors; 2 is even, so it falls quiet.

这就是配对游戏成立的两条根据。成对：任何一条链恰有两个端点，所以警报总是偶数个，而且天然分成「一对一对」。能动：把链延长一条边，只有一盏灯挪位，代价 1。于是译码器面对一堆亮灯时，只需回答「谁和谁是一对」，再让每对之间走最短路——《译码器 · 高中数学版》2.1 节把它写成一张「症状图」：警报是点，最短距离是边；第 2 章的最小重量完美匹配算法在这张图上找最省的配法。人话：警报成对出现、能单独移动，「配对 + 最短路」才是一个有意义的问题。

These are the two grounds on which the pairing game stands. In pairs: any chain has exactly two endpoints, so alarms are always even in number and naturally fall into couples. Mobile: extending the chain by one edge moves exactly one lamp, at cost 1. So a decoder facing a set of lit lamps only has to answer “who is paired with whom” and let each pair take the shortest path; section 2.1 of “Decoders · High-School Edition” writes this as a “syndrome graph” with alarms as vertices and shortest distances as edges, and its Chapter 2 runs minimum-weight perfect matching on that graph to find the cheapest pairing. In plain words: because alarms come in pairs and move alone, “pair up, then shortest path” is a meaningful problem.

1.2玩具：一块警报不能单独走的计数板The toy: a counter board where an alarm cannot move alone

现在换一块板。4 × 4 = 16 个格子，每格里是一个 0 或 1（把它当作一个可以翻转的比特）。格子的角上放计数器：5 × 5 = 25 个。每个计数器只做一件事：数它周围（最多 4 个）格子里 1 的个数是奇还是偶，奇数就亮。这仍然是一条奇偶检查（第 0 章事实 1），只是每条检查碰的是「围着一个角的四格」，而不是「围着一个顶点的四条边」。全 0 时没有灯亮。翻转一个格子会怎样？

Now change boards. 4 × 4 = 16 cells, each holding a 0 or 1 (think of it as a bit you can flip). Counters sit on the corners of the cells: 5 × 5 = 25 of them. Each counter does one thing: it counts whether the 1s in the (at most 4) cells around it are odd or even, and lights up if odd. This is still a parity check (fact 1 of Chapter 0), only each check touches “the four cells around a corner” instead of “the four edges around a vertex”. With all cells 0 nothing lights. What happens when you flip one cell?

两件可以亲手核对的事。第一：翻一个格子，恰好它的 4 个角各多数到一个 1，于是 4 盏灯亮——不是 2 盏。翻右板那一排 3 格：中间的两条竖线上共 4 个角各被两个格子碰到，2 是偶数，它们安静；只有最左和最右的 4 个角亮。第二，也是关键：数一数任何一行计数器里亮着的个数。每个格子碰到它上方那一行计数器的恰好 2 个（左上角、右上角），也碰到下方那一行的恰好 2 个。所以不管翻了哪些格子，任何一行计数器里「被碰到的次数」总和是偶数，亮着的灯（被碰到奇数次的）也就总是偶数个。同理任何一列。

Two things you can check by hand. First: flipping one cell adds one 1 to each of its 4 corners, so 4 lamps light, not 2. Flip the row of 3 on the right board: the 4 corners on the two interior vertical lines are each touched by two cells, 2 is even, and they stay quiet; only the 4 corners at the far left and far right light. Second, and this is the key: count the lit counters in any one row of counters. Every cell touches exactly 2 counters in the counter row above it (its top-left and top-right corners) and exactly 2 in the row below. So whatever cells are flipped, the total number of “touches” in any counter row is even, hence the number of lit lamps in that row (those touched an odd number of times) is even too. Likewise for any column.

任何一行、任何一列计数器：亮灯数 ≡ 0 (mod 2)Any row and any column of counters: number of lit lamps ≡ 0 (mod 2)

记号：≡ 0 (mod 2) 读「除以 2 余 0」，即偶数。这条规则是本篇自己证的（上一段两句话），不出自任何 QEC-Bank 页面。

Notation: ≡ 0 (mod 2) reads “leaves remainder 0 when divided by 2”, i.e. even. This rule is proved in this guide (the two sentences above) and comes from no QEC-Bank page.

现在问 1.1 节的那两个问题。成对吗？不。假如整块板恰好只有两盏灯亮，它们必须在同一行（那一行要偶数），也必须在同一列（那一列要偶数）——同行同列的两个点是同一个点。所以「恰好两盏灯」不可能出现；最少是像图中那样的 4 盏，在一个矩形的四个角上。能单独动吗？不。要把一盏灯向右挪一格，得让旧位置灭、新位置亮而别处不变，那是一次「改变恰好两盏灯」的操作：两盏在同一行，那一行的奇偶不变，但它们在不同的两列，两列各变了一盏，变成奇数，违反列偶规则（向上挪一格则违反行偶规则）。允许的动法都是成对的，右板演示了其中一种：把左板那一格向右延长成一排，右边的一对一起向右挪了 2 格，左边的一对留在原地（图中绿色竖线）。人话：这块板上的警报没有「端点」的概念，一盏灯不是任何链的头；配对与最短路都失去了意义。下面的计数板可以亲手试：点格子翻转，右边一列和底下一行的数字是每一行、每一列计数器里亮着的盏数。

Now ask the two questions of section 1.1. In pairs? No. Suppose exactly two lamps were lit on the whole board: they must share a row (that row needs an even count) and share a column (so must that column), and two points in the same row and column are the same point. So “exactly two lamps” can never occur; the minimum is 4, at the corners of a rectangle, as in the figure. Mobile on its own? No. Moving one lamp a step to the right means turning the old position off and the new one on with nothing else changed: an operation that “changes exactly two lamps”: both are in the same row, so that row keeps its parity, but they are in two different columns, each of which changes by one lamp and turns odd, breaking the even-column rule (a step upward would break the even-row rule instead). Every allowed move goes in pairs, and the right board shows one of them: extend the left board's cell to the right into a row, and the pair on the right moves 2 steps right together while the pair on the left stays put (green bar in the figure). In plain words: on this board alarms have no “endpoints”; a lamp is not the head of any chain; pairing and shortest paths have lost their meaning. An excitation (a lit lamp) that cannot move on its own is called a fractonAn excitation, in a class of 3D quantum codes, that no local operation can transport on its own. In Haah's cubic code every excitation that cannot be created locally from nothing is immobile, alone or as a cluster of several (Aitchison et al. 2023, arXiv:2308.00138, sections II.2.2–II.2.3).; codes with this feature are fracton codes. The counter board is a classical toy meant to show one thing only, “cannot move alone”; real fracton codes are three-dimensional quantum codes. One feature of the toy does not carry over: on the counter board a pair of lamps can still move together (right board above), but not in Haah's cubic code, where the alarms that no local operation can create from nothing cannot move, alone or as a cluster; forcing one farther away lights more and more extra alarms along the way, in proportion to the distance (Aitchison, Bulmash, Dua, Doherty, Williamson, “No Strings Attached: Boundaries and Defects in the Cubic Code”, 2023, arXiv:2308.00138, sections II.2.2–II.2.3). The QEC-Bank representative is haah_cubic (Haah's cubic code), whose card description reads: “a pair of data qubits occupies every vertex of a periodic cubic lattice, and one X-type plus one inversion-related Z-type corner pattern is translated over every cube.” In its views field (origin: curated, ref haah2011local) the card states this section's conclusion in one sentence: “Local Pauli faults create bounded clusters of cube defects, but isolated fracton charge cannot be transported by an ordinary Pauli string. Error patterns and neutral clusters therefore organize recursively rather than as pairs joined by shortest paths, motivating the family-specific renormalization decoder.” In plain words: local errors only create small clusters of alarms, a single alarm cannot be carried away, error patterns are no longer “pairs plus shortest paths”, so a special-purpose decoder is needed.

The card's description, word by word (figure below). Every vertex of the cubic lattice holds two qubits, qubit 1 and qubit 2; an operator on one vertex is written as two letters, e.g. ZI = Z on qubit 1, nothing (I) on qubit 2. Every unit cube carries two checks, each touching qubits at the cube's 8 corners; which qubit each corner touches is fixed by one “corner pattern”, and the same pattern is translated onto every cube. Panel (a) is the Z check's corner pattern, panel (b) the X check's. The two patterns are “inversion-related”: move each corner of the Z pattern to the corner opposite it through the cube's centre, swap qubit 1 and qubit 2 at every vertex, change Z to X, and you get the X pattern. 「逐级归并、局部消掉」在计数板上走一遍。下面是按 RG 思路写的一个玩具版本，只为让「团」「中性」「尺度」三个词有具体的样子，不是 Bravyi 与 Haah 在 Haah 立方码上用的具体算法。规则：在尺度 r 上，把彼此距离不超过 r 的亮灯连成团（距离取行差与列差中较大的那个）；对每个团，只准翻四个角都落在这团灯外接矩形里的格子，若有一种翻法恰好把这团灯全部熄灭、别处不变，这团就叫中性，照此翻掉；不中性的团留到下一个更大的尺度。例子（下图）：真实错误是 3 个格子，译码器只看见 8 盏灯（①）。尺度 1：左上 4 盏连成一团，翻 1 格就灭，是中性的；底下 4 盏在尺度 1 上分成两团，每团是竖着的两盏，每一行里只有 1 盏，而任何翻法都让每行的亮灯数改变偶数，所以灭不掉，不中性，留下（②）。尺度 2：这两团相距 2，合成一团，翻框里的 2 格即全部熄灭（③）。三次翻格合起来恰好是真实错误。Haah 立方码上的 RG 译码器也是这样逐级放大尺度、消掉中性的团，只是团里是立方体上的警报，「中性」要按 1.2 节那张角图案来判断，而错误可能长成跨很多尺度的分形形状；这一部分超出本篇，只做描述。

“Cluster over growing scales, neutralize locally”, walked through on the counter board. What follows is a toy version written in the RG spirit, only to give the words “cluster”, “neutral” and “scale” a concrete shape; it is not the actual algorithm Bravyi and Haah use on Haah's cubic code. Rule: at scale r, join lit lamps that are at most r apart into clusters (distance = the larger of the row difference and the column difference); for each cluster, only cells whose four corners all lie in the cluster's bounding rectangle may be flipped, and if some choice of flips turns off exactly that cluster and nothing else, the cluster is neutral and is flipped away; clusters that are not neutral wait for the next, larger scale. Example (figure below): the true error is 3 cells, and the decoder sees only 8 lamps (①). Scale 1: the top-left 4 lamps form one cluster that a single flip clears, so it is neutral; the bottom 4 lamps split at scale 1 into two clusters, each a vertical pair with 1 lamp per row, and any set of flips changes each row by an even number of lamps, so they cannot be cleared: not neutral, kept (②). Scale 2: the two clusters are 2 apart, merge into one, and flipping the 2 cells inside the frame clears them all (③). The three flips together are exactly the true error. The RG decoder on Haah's cubic code likewise enlarges the scale step by step and removes neutral clusters, except that the clusters are alarms on cubes, “neutral” is judged by the corner pattern of section 1.2, and errors can grow into fractal shapes spanning many scales; that part is beyond this guide and is only described.

Section 4.3 of the BB decoders guide lists “static distance, circuit distance, decoder-found failure weight” as three quantities never to be conflated; the same discipline applies here.

Three: decide “is a 3D code worth the extra hardware dimension?”; that needs at least one circuit-level number, and row 2 of 2.2 says none existed in the search of 2026-08-09. In plain words: today the QEC-Bank can tell you what this family “is” (check matrices, n, k, bounds on d) but not a single number for “how it performs in a real circuit, where measurements also fail”.

合格的答案长什么样，为什么难What a qualifying answer looks like, and why it is hard

先从 statement 里读出「什么算解决」，再解释难度词 open-research，最后回到计数板，看清哪一部分难处它能演示、哪一部分它演示不了。 First read “what counts as solved” out of the statement, then explain the difficulty word open-research, and finally return to the counter board to see which part of the difficulty it can show and which it cannot.

4.1什么算解决What counts as solved

按 statement 的字面，一个完整的答案对一族码交付两样东西。一、一个 production-quality 的译码器：在 registry/decoders.yaml 里有条目、有 benchmark_adapters，能对这族的任何实例运行。二、基准行：至少一张，最好每个实例、每个层级一张，每张的七元组写全，数字附带能重跑的命令。层级是关键：haah_cubic 那行 none-known 的噪声设定是电路级（重复症状提取 + 数据、测量、泄漏、关联故障），所以严格地说，只有一张 circuit-level 的行才能把那行删掉；一张 code-capacity 的行是有价值的第一步（它至少让框架「看见」这个码），但它对应的是第 1 行 BP-OSD 那种设定，不是第 2 行的缺口。人话：最低要求是「框架能跑、跑出一行、别人能重跑」；完整要求是这一行在真实电路的层级上。

Taken literally, a complete answer delivers two things for one family. One: a production-quality decoder, with an entry in registry/decoders.yaml carrying benchmark_adapters, runnable on any instance of the family. Two: benchmark rows, at least one and preferably one per instance and level, each with its full seven-tuple and a replay command beside the number. The level is the crux: the noise setting of haah_cubic's none-known row is circuit-level (repeated syndrome extraction plus data, measurement, leakage and correlated faults), so strictly only a circuit-level row can delete that row; a code-capacity row is a valuable first step (it at least lets the harness “see” the code), but it matches the setting of row 1's BP-OSD, not the gap of row 2. In plain words: the minimum is “the harness runs, produces a row, and others can rerun it”; the full requirement is that this row sits at the level of real circuits.

4.2难度词：open-research，「没人知道怎么做」The difficulty word: open-research, “nobody knows how”

Wanted 板只用两个难度词。hard-engineering：知道怎么做，但要做很多——比如Floquet 码距篇那一条，论文已经定义了对象、也有人对具体电路算过，缺的是把它做成通用引擎。open-research：连怎么做都没有公认的路线。本条是后者，理由可以从卡片上读出来，正好三层。

The board uses only two difficulty words. hard-engineering: the method is known but much must be built; for instance the Floquet fault-distance item, where a paper has defined the object and explicit circuits have been analysed, and what is missing is a general engine. open-research: not even the route is agreed. This item is the latter, and the reasons can be read off the cards, in exactly three layers.

4.3计数板能演示什么，不能演示什么What the counter board can and cannot show

诚实地说清玩具的边界。计数板能演示第 ① 层的前半：警报四个一组、单个不能动，「配对」这个词失效。它不能演示译码本身为什么难——原因很具体。在这块有边界的 4 × 4 板上，计数器的读数唯一地决定翻了哪些格子：左上角那个计数器只碰到一个格子，所以它亮不亮就告诉你那个格子翻没翻；沿第一行往右，每个计数器碰到的「新」格子只有一个，逐个确定；再往下一行同理（第 5 章的练习 2 让你亲手走一遍）。也就是说，这块板上没有「同一组警报对应多种不同修复」的情形，译码只是解一遍方程。而在真正的 Haah 立方码里，检查是量子稳定子，同一组警报对应大量不同的错误图案。两个错误若给出同一组警报，它们的乘积就不点亮任何检查，于是这个乘积要么是若干检查的乘积（一个稳定子），要么是一个逻辑算符（可能再乘上若干检查）。前一种情形两个错误属于同一类，对存储的信息作用完全相同，修哪一个都行，这叫退化；后一种情形它们属于不同的类，修错了类，错误加修复就是一个逻辑算符，信息被改写。下图在 1.1 节那个 L = 3 的环面码上各举一例。所以译码器必须挑出「最可能的一类」，而不只是「最可能的一个错误」——《译码器 · 高中数学版》1.1 节「从症状到类」讲的正是这件事，只是在分形子码里「类」的结构是分形式的、跨尺度的，这超出了本篇的层级，只做描述。人话：玩具告诉你老游戏为什么玩不了，没有告诉你新游戏有多难。

Let us be honest about the toy's limits. The counter board can show the first half of layer ①: alarms come in fours, a single one cannot move, the word “pairing” fails. It cannot show why decoding itself is hard, for a concrete reason. On this bounded 4 × 4 board the counter readings determine uniquely which cells were flipped: the top-left counter touches only one cell, so whether it is lit tells you whether that cell was flipped; moving right along the first row, each counter touches only one “new” cell, fixed in turn; the next row likewise (exercise 2 of Chapter 5 walks you through it). In other words, this board has no situation of “one set of alarms, several different repairs”; decoding is merely solving equations. In Haah's real cubic code the checks are quantum stabilizers, one set of alarms corresponds to many different error patterns. If two errors give the same alarms, their product lights no check, so that product is either a product of checks (a stabilizer) or a logical operator (possibly times some checks). In the first case the two errors are in the same class and act identically on the stored information, so repairing either one is fine; this is called degeneracy. In the second case they are in different classes: repair the wrong class, and error plus repair is a logical operator, so the information is rewritten. The figure below gives one example of each on the L = 3 toric code of section 1.1. So the decoder must pick “the most likely class”, not merely “the most likely error”; section 1.1 of “Decoders · High-School Edition”, “from syndrome to class”, is about exactly this, except that in fracton codes the classes are structured fractally across scales, which is beyond this guide's level and is only described. In plain words: the toy tells you why the old game cannot be played, not how hard the new one is.

5.2练习 2：这块板上的译码是唯一的Exercise 2: decoding on this board is unique

给你 25 个计数器的读数（亮 / 灭），要还原 16 个格子。按下面的顺序做：左上角的计数器只碰到格子 (1,1)，所以格子 (1,1) = 那个计数器的读数。第一行第二个计数器碰到 (1,1) 和 (1,2)，(1,1) 已知，于是 (1,2) 被确定；一路向右，确定第一行全部 4 个格子。第二行第一个计数器碰到 (1,1) 和 (2,1)，确定 (2,1)；第二行第二个碰到 (1,1)、(1,2)、(2,1)、(2,2)，前三个已知，确定 (2,2)……16 步之后全部格子确定。结论：读数与翻转图案一一对应，这块板上不存在「同一组警报、两种修复」。这就是 4.3 节说的：玩具上同一组警报只对应一种翻法；量子码上同一组警报对应许多错误，译码器要在它们所属的类之间挑。（顺带一问：如果把板卷成环面——左右相接、上下相接——第一步的论证还成立吗？不成立：没有「只碰一个格子」的计数器了。环面上会出现「整行翻转、所有灯不亮」的图案，同一组警报于是对应不止一种翻法（在这块经典板上，这些翻法是真正不同的改动，不是 4.3 节那种无害的退化）。这一问只需想到这里，不必再往下算。）

You are given the readings (lit / unlit) of the 25 counters and must recover the 16 cells. Proceed in this order: the top-left counter touches only cell (1,1), so cell (1,1) = that counter's reading. The second counter of the first row touches (1,1) and (1,2); (1,1) is known, so (1,2) is fixed; continue right and fix all 4 cells of the first row. The first counter of the second row touches (1,1) and (2,1), fixing (2,1); the second touches (1,1), (1,2), (2,1), (2,2), three of which are known, fixing (2,2)… after 16 steps every cell is fixed. Conclusion: readings and flip patterns correspond one to one; on this board there is no “one set of alarms, two repairs”. That is what 4.3 said: on the toy one set of alarms matches exactly one set of flips; on a quantum code one set of alarms matches many errors, and the decoder must choose among their classes. (One aside: if the board were rolled into a torus, left joined to right and top to bottom, would the first step still work? No: there would be no counter touching only one cell. On the torus, patterns such as “flip a whole row, no lamp lights” appear, so one set of alarms matches more than one set of flips (on this classical board those flips are genuinely different changes, not the harmless degeneracy of 4.3). Think this far and no further; no more computation is needed.)

两条根据、三个词、一个空格Two grounds, three words, one blank

回放一遍。环面码的译码是配对游戏，根据是警报成对出现、且能单独移动。计数板上这两条都不成立：任何一行一列的亮灯数总是偶数，所以灯最少四盏、单盏挪不动——这是分形子的经典影子。QEC-Bank 用三个成熟度词记录译码器：standard、research-grade、none-known；写着 none-known 的卡片恰有三张，其中 haah_cubic 把缺口指向本条，它的三行记录里有两种研究级算法，都停在完美症状层级，电路级的记录在 2026-08-09 的检索里为零。没有框架能调用的电路级译码器，就没有电路级的基准行；没有这一行，电路级对比表里就是一个诚实的空格（码容量层级的通用 CSS 基线仍可跑）。合格的答案是一个能被框架调用、能生成七元组写全的基准行的译码器；难度是 open-research，因为没有「配对」骨架可套，也因为它排在「造码、写电路、定探测器」这串任务的末端。

Replay. Decoding the toric code is a pairing game, grounded in alarms that come in pairs and move alone. On the counter board both grounds fail: every row and column of counters has an even number of lit lamps, so lamps come at least four at a time and a single one cannot move, the classical shadow of a fracton. The QEC-Bank records decoders with three maturity words, standard, research-grade, none-known; exactly three cards carry none-known, and haah_cubic routes its gap to this item: its three records hold two research-grade algorithms, both at the perfect-syndrome level, with zero circuit-level records in the search of 2026-08-09. No circuit-level decoder the harness can call, no circuit-level benchmark row; without that row, the circuit-level comparison table shows an honest blank (the generic code-capacity CSS baseline still runs). A qualifying answer is a decoder the harness can call and that yields fully identified seven-tuple rows; the difficulty is open-research, because there is no “pairing” skeleton to reuse and because it sits at the end of the chain “build the code, write the circuit, define the detectors”.
