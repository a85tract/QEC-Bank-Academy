从理想症状
到真实电路 From ideal syndromes
to real circuits

Toric Code 教程里的译码游戏有一个隐含假设：每条检查的读数都是对的。真实机器里，读数是由一个「帮手比特」跑一段小电路收集来的，帮手自己会错、会读反、会把自己的某些错误传回数据比特（它是 CNOT 的目标端，传回去的是相位型错误）、会在路上耽误、甚至会跑出 0/1 的字母表。把这些都算进去之后，一个码「存得好不好」就不再是检查矩阵自己的属性，而是「提取电路 + 探测器模型 + 译码器 + 噪声归一化」这四件东西捆在一起的属性。Atlas 里的量子扩展码条目在理想症状下有三条译码记录，在真实电路下的那一栏却写着 none-known。这一篇讲这道开放问题是什么、Atlas 现在怎么凑合、为什么我们想要答案、一个合格的答案长什么样。 The decoding game of the toric-code guide has a hidden assumption: every check reading is correct. In a real machine a reading is collected by a “helper qubit” running a small circuit, and the helper itself can fail, be misread, pass some of its own faults back to the data qubits (it is the CNOT target, so what travels back is a phase-type error), be delayed on the way, or even leave the 0/1 alphabet. Once all of that is counted, how well a code “stores” is no longer a property of the check matrix alone but of four things pinned together: extraction circuit, detector model, decoder and noise normalisation. The Atlas entry for quantum expander codes has three decoder records under ideal syndromes; the column for real circuits reads none-known. This guide explains what the open problem is, how the Atlas copes today, why we want the answer, and what a qualifying answer looks like.

The three levels are not three difficulties of one problem but three different problems, and their answers (thresholds) cannot be compared across rows. The ledger of “Decoders · High-School Edition” gives the surface code's numbers at the three levels; first be clear what p means in each. The first two levels use the same data errors: in every round each qubit independently suffers an X flip with probability p and, independently, a Z flip with probability p; X and Z are decoded separately, so p is the probability of a flip of the type being decoded, not the total probability of some Pauli error. (For comparison: depolarizing noise of total probability p applies X, Y, Z with p/3 each; an X-type flip comes from X or Y, with probability 2p/3.) With this convention, perfect measurement and minimum-weight matching give about 10.3%; letting every check's reading also flip with probability q = p per round gives about 2.9%. Both come from the numerical simulations of Wang, Harrington and Preskill (2003) (section 5.3). Circuit level gives about 0.5%–1%, depending on the circuit and on how “one step fails” is defined (section 5.4). One concrete case is the model of Fowler et al. (2012): an extraction round has 8 steps; after an idle step or a Hadamard, X, Y or Z is inserted with probability p/3 each; preparation and readout each fail with probability p; after every CNOT one of the 15 nontrivial two-qubit Paulis is inserted with probability p/15 each; with minimum-weight matching this gives 0.57%, with p counted per step. The upper end of the range comes from other circuits and noise conventions that this guide did not check one by one. All three are estimates from numerical simulations in the literature, all with minimum-weight matching as the decoder, not computed by this guide or the Atlas. They differ by more than tenfold; the code did not change, what counts as “one error” did. Section 6.1 says any threshold number needs at least five qualifiers: error channel, syndrome model, circuit convention, decoder, normalisation. Every time a number appears below, this guide says which row it belongs to.

记号：p = 码容量与现象学两层里，每个数据比特每轮发生 X 翻转（另算 Z 翻转）的概率；电路级里，按所用模型定义的每个位置每一步出错的概率；q = 每条检查读数每轮被读反的概率；阈值 = 低于它时把码做大能让逻辑错误率变小的那个 p。

Notation: p = in the code-capacity and phenomenological levels, the probability that a data qubit suffers an X flip (Z flips counted separately) per round; at circuit level, the per-step failure probability of one location as the chosen model defines it; q = the probability that a check's reading is flipped per round; threshold = the p below which making the code larger makes the logical error rate smaller.

2.3为什么这一族躲不开 routing：卡片上的一句话Why this family cannot avoid routing: one sentence on the card

量子扩展码的检查矩阵是「非几何」的，卡片的一条 view（类型 nongeometric-syndrome-circuits，来源 curated，引文 delfosse2021measurement）原文：「Bounded check weight does not imply bounded-depth nearest-neighbor extraction in a low-dimensional layout. The check matrix of a quantum expander code is “nongeometric”; one view on the card (type nongeometric-syndrome-circuits, origin curated, citing delfosse2021measurement) reads: “Bounded check weight does not imply bounded-depth nearest-neighbor extraction in a low-dimensional layout. Expansion distributes Tanner neighbors non-geometrically, so a device needs long-range couplers, routing, or growing local depth.” In plain words: a check touching only 6 qubits (sparse) does not make those 6 qubits neighbours on the chip; expansion demands precisely that they be spread out. (What “expansion” means, and on which graph, is the next paragraph.) A circuit with neighbour-only connections in a plane cannot spread things out like this: the wires leaving a region grow only with its perimeter and cannot keep up with its area (the Delfosse–Beverland–Tremblay bound of the qLDPC guide, section 1.4). So the machine needs long-range wiring, or a travelling helper (routing), or many more steps per round. The card's realizations field adds a dated sentence: “No repeated-syndrome laboratory realization of the exact family was found as of 2026-08-09.” That is why the board's statement names routing explicitly: for the surface code it is a non-issue, for this family it is the core of the circuit design.

「扩展」说的是哪张图。量子扩展码是一条经典码 H 与自己做超图积得到的（这里 H 是 K5 的 5 × 10 关联矩阵，2.1 节）。small-set-flip 定理（Leverrier、Tillich、Zémor 2015 的定理 2）的假设落在经典因子码的 Tanner 图上：比特各画一个圆，检查各画一个方块，检查读某个比特就连一条线。设每个比特连 Δ 条检查，集合 S 的邻居 Γ(S) 是至少连着 S 中一个比特的那些检查。S 最多能有 Δ·|S| 个邻居（各比特的线全落在不同的检查上）；「左扩展」要求：凡是不超过全体固定比例的比特集合 S，都有 |Γ(S)| ≥ (1 − δ)·Δ·|S|，即几乎没有两条线撞在同一条检查上；检查一侧也要有同样的性质（「右扩展」），定理要求两侧的 δ 都小于 1/6。乘出来的量子码自己的 Tanner 图严格说并不是扩展图，论文的说法是它「保留了足够的扩展结构」，译码算法才能工作。

Which graph “expansion” is about. A quantum expander code is the hypergraph product of a classical code H with itself (here H is the 5 × 10 incidence matrix of K5, section 2.1). The hypothesis of the small-set-flip theorem (Theorem 2 of Leverrier, Tillich and Zémor 2015) is about the Tanner graph of the classical factor code: one circle per bit, one square per check, a line whenever a check reads a bit. Let every bit touch Δ checks; the neighbours Γ(S) of a set S of bits are the checks joined to at least one bit of S. S can have at most Δ·|S| neighbours (all its lines landing on different checks); “left expansion” demands that every set S of bits up to a fixed fraction of all bits has |Γ(S)| ≥ (1 − δ)·Δ·|S|, so almost no two lines land on the same check; the check side must have the same property (“right expansion”), and the theorem needs δ below 1/6 on both sides. The quantum code's own Tanner graph is, strictly speaking, not an expander; in the paper's words it retains enough expanding structure for the decoding algorithm to work.

Count it. In the K5 code the bits are edges (e01 joins vertices 0 and 1, and so on), the checks are vertices, and Δ = 2. Left in the figure: e01 and e23 share no vertex, so their neighbours are v0, v1, v2, v3, four, exactly the maximum 2 × 2. Right: e01 and e02 both touch v0, so their neighbours are only v0, v1, v2, three. δ < 1/6 requires two bits to have at least (5/6) × 4 ≈ 3.33 neighbours, i.e. all four, so as soon as such two-bit sets fall within the “fixed fraction”, the K5 graph fails the theorem's hypothesis; the triangle {e01, e02, e12} is worse, its 6 lines landing on only 3 checks. So a small instance such as [[125,37,3]] lies outside the theorem's reach: the theorem is about a family whose number of bits goes to infinity, with graphs that expand well enough (randomly sampled graphs qualify with high probability, but no efficient deterministic construction is known, nor an efficient way to certify that a given graph qualifies, a limitation that Fawzi, Grospellier and Leverrier (2018) state themselves).

Two clarifications. First, the statement does not require the decoder to be small-set-flip. The card cites the perfect-syndrome theorem (“the perfect-syndrome small-set-flip theorem”, Leverrier, Tillich and Zémor 2015), but the theory goes further. Fawzi, Grospellier and Leverrier (2018) proved that for quantum expander codes with good enough expansion, small-set-flip still works when readings can be wrong, and that it is single-shotEach decoding uses one round of noisy readings; no repeated rounds are needed for one decoding. The leftover error is not zero but stays small, and the next decoding keeps handling it.: after one round of noisy readings, except with probability e−Ω(√n), the residual error is equivalent, up to stabilizers, to local stochastic noise with parameter qΩ(1), so it stays small but is not guaranteed to vanish (Theorem 2 of the paper). The premise is a “local stochastic” noise: the probability that any chosen |S| qubits are all in error and any chosen |T| readings are all flipped is at most p|S|q|T|, with p and q below two constants that exist but are not given numerically. They also put it into a complete fault-tolerant scheme (Theorem 1 of the paper), valid for the same kind of local stochastic circuit noise below a threshold whose value is likewise not given. So what is missing is not the theory of “can one decode when readings fail”, but Atlas infrastructure: extraction circuits for concrete instances of this family (such as [[125,37,3]]), detector and noise files, an actual number, and a contract others can rerun. Besides, Fig. 2.1 already showed that an instance this small does not meet the theorem's expansion hypothesis. A BP-OSD running on the detector graph qualifies just as well, as long as all five deliverables are present.

第二，statement 也没有要求一个「好看」的数字：一份合同下跑出来的逻辑错误率，哪怕很差，只要可重放、可比较，就填上了 none-known 那一格。人话：要的是一台能让别人重跑的机器，不是一个漂亮的百分数。

Second, the statement does not ask for a “nice” number either: a logical error rate obtained under one contract, however poor, fills the none-known cell as long as it is replayable and comparable. In plain words: what is wanted is a machine others can rerun, not a pretty percentage.

The board grades difficulty with two words. open-research: nobody knows how; for example, proving a lower bound for a 144-qubit code in the distance guide, where the Atlas has no replayable method yet (the literature computed gross's exact distance 12 by mixed-integer programming, but that is not a certificate the Atlas can rerun). hard-engineering: known how, but much to do. This problem is the latter, for the reasons of Chapter 2: the surface code has walked this road (detector error model plus spacetime matching), and the BB literature has walked it once (depth-7 circuit plus detectors plus BP-OSD, giving 0.8%). 「做很多」有多多？用 1.1 节的算法数一数。一条碰 w 个比特的检查，一轮提取电路有 1 次制备 + w 个 CNOT + 1 次读出 = w + 2 个可能出错的位置。对 toric_L3 [[18,2,3]]：18 条检查，每条 4 条腿，一轮有 18 × 6 = 108 个位置（不算等待）。对 [[125,37,3]]：qLDPC 教程 3.4 节说它的因子是 K5 的 5 × 10 关联矩阵，每行 4 个 1（每个顶点连 4 条边）、每列 2 个 1（每条边两个端点）；超图积的每条检查重量 = 行重 + 列重 = 6，检查数 = 5 × 10 + 10 × 5 = 100；一轮约 100 × 8 = 800 个位置，仍不算等待。存储实验跑多少轮 T 是协议的选择，常见取法是 T = d，这里取 T = 3。BB 教程 6.4 节的电路噪声模型里一个 CNOT 有 15 种不同的出错方式（两个比特上各放 I、X、Y、Z 之一，共 4 × 4 = 16 种组合，去掉「两边都是 I」即什么都没发生的那一种）；每次制备、每次读出各算 1 种。于是三轮里的基本故障有：toric_L3 3 × 18 × (1 + 4 × 15 + 1) = 3,348 种，[[125,37,3]] 3 × 100 × (1 + 6 × 15 + 1) = 27,600 种。「哪种故障点亮哪些探测器、翻不翻逻辑」这张表——文献里叫探测器错误模型detector error model：把每一种基本故障用模拟跑一遍，记下它点亮的探测器集合与是否翻转逻辑；译码器在这张表定义的加权（超）图上工作。——就从这些基本故障逐一模拟得来。点亮同一组探测器、翻转同一组逻辑的故障会合并成一行（两个独立故障 p₁、p₂ 合并后的概率是「恰好一个发生」的 p₁ + p₂ − 2p₁p₂，因为两个都发生就互相抵消），所以表的行数比基本故障少；合并后剩多少行，本篇没有算。「上万」只说 [[125,37,3]] 的基本故障数。这些数字是本篇自己数的（脚本核对），Atlas 页面上没有；它们不吓人，机器一秒就能列完，真正花时间的是把每一行都做对、做成能重放的文件、并在换一个顺序时全部重来。这就是 hard-engineering 的字面意思。

How much is “much”? Count with the procedure of 1.1. A check touching w qubits has, per round of extraction, 1 preparation + w CNOTs + 1 readout = w + 2 locations that can fail. For toric_L3 [[18,2,3]]: 18 checks of 4 legs each, 18 × 6 = 108 locations per round (idling not counted). For [[125,37,3]]: qLDPC guide section 3.4 says its factor is the 5 × 10 incidence matrix of K5, with 4 ones per row (each vertex meets 4 edges) and 2 per column (each edge has two endpoints); every hypergraph-product check has weight = row weight + column weight = 6, and the number of checks is 5 × 10 + 10 × 5 = 100; about 100 × 8 = 800 locations per round, still without idling. How many rounds T a memory experiment runs is a choice of the protocol; T = d is common, and here T = 3. In the circuit-noise model of BB guide section 6.4 one CNOT has 15 distinct ways to fail (one of I, X, Y, Z on each of its two qubits, 4 × 4 = 16 combinations, minus “I on both”, which is no fault at all); each preparation and each readout counts as 1. So over three rounds the elementary faults number 3 × 18 × (1 + 4 × 15 + 1) = 3,348 for toric_L3 and 3 × 100 × (1 + 6 × 15 + 1) = 27,600 for [[125,37,3]]. The table of “which fault lights which detectors and whether it flips the logical”, called a detector error modelSimulate every elementary fault once and record the set of detectors it lights and whether it flips the logical; the decoder works on the weighted (hyper)graph this table defines. in the literature, is built by simulating these elementary faults one by one. Faults that light the same detectors and flip the same logicals are merged into one row (two independent faults p₁, p₂ merge to p₁ + p₂ − 2p₁p₂, the probability that exactly one happens, since both together cancel), so the table has fewer rows than there are elementary faults; how many remain after merging, this guide did not compute. “Tens of thousands” refers only to the elementary faults of [[125,37,3]]. These counts are this guide's own (checked by script) and appear on no Atlas page; they are not frightening, a machine lists them in a second. What takes the time is getting every row right, making it a replayable file, and redoing all of it when one order changes. That is the literal meaning of hard-engineering.

关键想法Key idea

这道题没有一个「聪明的一步」能省掉工作。表面码和 BB 码的先例说明每一步都做得出来；非几何的连接说明每一步都得为这一族重做一次，而且每个设计选择都要被钉进版本号里。所以它的难度词是 hard-engineering：没有未知，只有体量。

This problem has no “clever step” that saves the work. The surface-code and BB precedents show every step can be done; the nongeometric connections mean every step must be redone for this family, and every design choice must be pinned into a version number. Hence the difficulty word hard-engineering: no unknowns, only volume.

柱子是什么。每根柱子是一个比特的对数似然比 L = ln[P(0)/P(1)]：P(0) 是 BP 当前估计的「这个比特没被翻」的概率，P(1) 是「被翻了」的概率。L > 0 表示更可能没错，L < 0 表示更可能错了，|L| 越大越有把握。例：BP 开始前每个比特只有先验，滑块 p = 0.06 时 P(0) = 0.94、P(1) = 0.06，L = ln(0.94/0.06) ≈ 2.75；L = −8 表示 P(1) 是 P(0) 的 e⁸ ≈ 3000 倍，几乎肯定错了。纵轴只画到 ±8：超出的柱子截在 ±8，柱端画一个小三角标出。柱子下方的数字是比特编号（比特多时每 5 个或 10 个标一次）。这里的 BP 用归一化最小和规则（消息乘 α = 0.8），是近似算法，L 不是精确概率。滑块上的 p 既是撒错误的概率，也是 BP 的先验；拖动滑块会清掉已经算好的 BP 结果，当前这组错误保留（它是按旧的 p 撒的），下一次运行 BP 按新的 p 重算。

What the bars are. Each bar is one qubit's log-likelihood ratio L = ln[P(0)/P(1)]: P(0) is BP's current estimate of the probability that the qubit was not flipped, P(1) that it was. L > 0 means more likely fine, L < 0 more likely wrong, and the larger |L|, the surer. Example: before BP starts every qubit has only its prior; at slider value p = 0.06, P(0) = 0.94 and P(1) = 0.06, so L = ln(0.94/0.06) ≈ 2.75; L = −8 means P(1) is e⁸ ≈ 3000 times P(0), almost certainly wrong. 5.3手算题二：把一个 hook 写成表里的一行Exercise two: write one hook as a row of the table

Back to c1's extraction circuit: set h to 0, xor in b1, xor in b2, read h. Suppose h flips once between the two xors. The 125 data qubits of the hypergraph product come in two kinds: 100 labelled (edge, edge) and 25 labelled (vertex, vertex). An X check is labelled X(vertex a, edge j) and acts on the 4 qubits (e, j), e running over the 4 edges at a, and on the 2 qubits (a, v), v running over the two endpoints of j; a Z check is labelled Z(edge i, vertex b) and acts on the 4 qubits (i, e′), e′ running over the 4 edges at b, and on the 2 qubits (u, b), u running over the two endpoints of i. Here we look at the X check X(v0, e01); its 6 qubits are called q1 to q6 (left column of the figure).

测量顺序（这是合同的一部分，这里定一个）：每一轮先测完全部 X 检查，再测全部 Z 检查。一条 X 检查的做法：帮手 h 制备成 |+⟩，以 h 为控制端依次对 q1、…、q6 做 CNOT，最后测 h 的 X。故障：第 2 个与第 3 个 CNOT 之间，h 上出现一个 X。CNOT 把控制端上的 X 复制到目标端，所以 CNOT 3–6 把它复制到 q3、q4、q5、q6：一个故障，数据上是 4 比特的 X 错误。h 自己的读数不变（|+⟩ 上的 X 不改变 X 测量的结果），X 探测器一个都不亮。

Schedule (part of the contract; here we fix one): every round, all X checks are measured first, then all Z checks. One X check is measured by preparing the helper h in |+⟩, applying CNOTs with h as control onto q1, …, q6 in turn, and measuring h in the X basis. Fault: an X appears on h between the 2nd and the 3rd CNOT. A CNOT copies an X on its control onto its target, so CNOTs 3–6 copy it onto q3, q4, q5, q6: one fault, a 4-qubit X error on the data. The helper's own reading is unchanged (an X on |+⟩ does not change an X measurement), so no X detector lights.

算探测器。这 4 个比特上的 X 在同一轮随后的 Z 检查里被看见：一条 Z 检查亮，当且仅当它与 {q3, q4, q5, q6} 重叠奇数次。逐个数：q3 = (e03, e01) 在 Z(e03,v0)、Z(e03,v1) 里；q4 = (e04, e01) 在 Z(e04,v0)、Z(e04,v1) 里；q5 = (v0, v0) 在 Z(e01,v0)、Z(e02,v0)、Z(e03,v0)、Z(e04,v0) 里；q6 = (v0, v1) 在 Z(e01,v1)、Z(e02,v1)、Z(e03,v1)、Z(e04,v1) 里。Z(e03,·) 与 Z(e04,·) 各被碰两次，抵消；剩下 Z(e01,v0)、Z(e01,v1)、Z(e02,v0)、Z(e02,v1)。探测器 = 本轮读数 ⊕ 上一轮读数，错误留在数据上以后读数不再变，所以表里的这一行是：本轮这 4 个 Z 探测器亮，X 探测器不亮，下一轮什么都不亮。它和 q1、q2 上各一个 X 点亮的完全一样：4 比特的错误乘上这条 X 检查本身（一个稳定子）就是 q1、q2 上的 2 比特错误。所以这一个故障相当于两个数据错误，这就是 hook 可能压低电路码距的原因。

Compute the detectors. The X on these 4 qubits is seen by the Z checks measured next in the same round: a Z check lights exactly when it overlaps {q3, q4, q5, q6} an odd number of times. One by one: q3 = (e03, e01) is in Z(e03,v0) and Z(e03,v1); q4 = (e04, e01) in Z(e04,v0) and Z(e04,v1); q5 = (v0, v0) in Z(e01,v0), Z(e02,v0), Z(e03,v0), Z(e04,v0); q6 = (v0, v1) in Z(e01,v1), Z(e02,v1), Z(e03,v1), Z(e04,v1). Z(e03,·) and Z(e04,·) are each hit twice and cancel; left are Z(e01,v0), Z(e01,v1), Z(e02,v0), Z(e02,v1). A detector is this round's reading ⊕ the previous round's, and once the error sits on the data the readings stop changing, so this fault's row of the table reads: these 4 Z detectors light this round, no X detector lights, nothing lights next round. That is exactly what one X on each of q1 and q2 would light: the 4-qubit error times this X check itself (a stabilizer) is the 2-qubit error on q1, q2. So this single fault acts like two data errors, which is why a hook can lower the circuit distance.

尾声Epilogue

三行、四个零件、一格 none-knownThree rows, four parts, one cell reading none-known

回放一遍。真实机器里，检查的读数由帮手比特跑一段提取电路收集，帮手自己会错；单看一轮，「数据错了」和「帮手报错了」无法区分，多测一轮、只报变化的探测器把它们分成横向一对与纵向一对。允许哪些东西出错，分出三层噪声模型：码容量、现象学、电路级；三层是三道不同的题，表面码在三层下的阈值 10.3%、2.9%、0.5%–1% 不能互比。Atlas 的 quantum_expander 条目有两个实例 [[125,37,3]]（d verified）与 [[656,170,5]]（d claimed），译码器一栏三条记录：css-pivot 与 bp-osd 都写着完美症状，第三条在完整电路那一格写着 none-known（2026-08-09），small-set-flip 只有讲解没有插头。隔壁 BB 码有一个引文数字 0.8%，带五个限定项，且条目不导出电路。我们想要答案，是因为「存储性能」不是矩阵的属性，而是提取电路、探测器模型、译码器、噪声归一化四个零件捆成一份带版本、可执行的合同之后的属性；有了它，none-known 那格换成一条有 id 的记录，两个码才能在同一份合同下并排比较。合格的答案是五样可交付的东西；难度是 hard-engineering——每一步都有先例，但非几何的连接让每一步都要为这一族重做，而且每个设计选择都要钉进版本号。

Replay. In a real machine a check's reading is collected by a helper qubit running an extraction circuit, and the helper itself can fail; within one round “the data is wrong” and “the helper reported wrongly” cannot be told apart, and one more round plus detectors reporting only change separate them into a horizontal and a vertical pair. What is allowed to fail defines three noise models: code capacity, phenomenological, circuit level; they are three different problems, and the surface code's thresholds of 10.3%, 2.9% and 0.5%–1% cannot be compared across them. The Atlas quantum_expander entry has two instances, [[125,37,3]] (d verified) and [[656,170,5]] (d claimed), and three decoder records: css-pivot and bp-osd both say perfect syndrome, the third says none-known (2026-08-09) in the full-circuit cell, and small-set-flip has an explanation but no plug. Next door the BB code has one cited number, 0.8%, with five qualifiers, and its entry exports no circuit. We want the answer because “memory performance” is not a property of the matrix but of four parts, extraction circuit, detector model, decoder and noise normalisation, bound into one versioned executable contract; with it the none-known cell becomes a record with an id, and two codes can sit side by side under the same contract. A qualifying answer is five deliverables; the difficulty is hard-engineering: every step has a precedent, but the nongeometric connections make every step a redo for this family, and every design choice must be pinned into a version number.
