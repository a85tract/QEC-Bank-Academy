第 1 章Chapter 1

怎样读 BB 条目的文献表：键、登记表、三种住处How to read the BB entry's bibliography: keys, the registry, three homes

普通论文的 [12] 只告诉你「去文末找第 12 条」。QEC-Bank 的文献键是一个全局唯一、可被程序检查的名字：条目里每一句「文献说……」都挂在某个键上，每个键都应在一份受控登记表里有完整元数据。BB 条目把这套机制的两面都展示得很清楚——它有一半以上的键还没有完整元数据。这一章先解剖一个键，看登记表里两种不同的行，再数一数 32 个键在 card.yaml 里住在哪三种地方，最后列出本条目用到的全部来源词。 An ordinary paper's “[12]” only says “look up item 12 at the end”. A QEC-Bank bibkey is a globally unique, machine-checkable name: every “the literature says …” in the entry hangs on a key, and every key should have complete metadata in one controlled registry. The BB entry shows both sides of this mechanism, because more than half of its keys do not yet have complete metadata. This chapter dissects one key, looks at the two kinds of row in the registry, counts the three places where the 32 keys live in card.yaml, and lists every provenance word the entry uses.

1.1一个键的解剖，与登记表里的两种行Anatomy of a key, and the two kinds of registry row

看 bravyi2024bicycle：第一作者的姓（bravyi）+ 年份（2024）+ 一个选来帮助记忆的词（bicycle）。这个词常常取自标题，但不一定：这篇论文的标题是「High-Threshold and Low-Overhead Fault-Tolerant Quantum Memory」，里面没有 bicycle，这里取的是它研究的码族的名字（bivariate bicycle）。再看 wang2026lowoverhead：wang + 2026 + lowoverhead，这回取自标题（「Demonstration of low-overhead quantum error correction codes」里的 low-overhead 去掉连字符）。这个约定让键既好念又几乎不撞车——BB 条目里有三个姓 Wang 的键（wang2023abelian、wang2026coprime、wang2026lowoverhead），是三篇不同作者、不同年份或不同助记词的论文，靠后两段区分。所有键登记在 registry/refs.yaml。这份登记表里有两种行。完整的行有五个字段：id、title、authors、year、url（arXiv 链接），例如 bravyi2024bicycle 那一行。裸的行只有一个字段，写成一行 - {id: lin2023twoblock}：键已经被「占住」了名字，但没有任何元数据。站点构建脚本 tools/site/build.py 渲染参考文献页时只问一件事：title、authors、year、url 四个字段是否都在。都在，就显示带链接的标题、年份标签、作者和键；缺任何一个，就只显示 <code>键</code> 加一个灰色标签「metadata pending」。所以「metadata pending」的精确含义是：这个键在登记表里只有一行裸 id。它不是说论文不存在，也不是说引用它的那句话没有依据。第三种行是别名：- {id: bravyi2024high, alias_of: bravyi2024bicycle}，把一次文献调研用过的另一个键指向同一篇论文，而不算第二篇。BB 条目有两处用到别名：奠基扫描报告的备注写着「QEC-Bank 目前用 bravyi2024high，抓到的记录是 arXiv 2308.07915，另有任务把它改名为 bravyi2024bicycle」——现在改名已完成，旧键留作别名；译码扫描报告用 roffe2020bposd，卡片用 roffe2020decoding，登记表里 roffe2020bposd 是后者的别名。

Take bravyi2024bicycle: first author's surname (bravyi) + year (2024) + a word chosen as a mnemonic (bicycle). The word is often taken from the title, but not always: this paper is titled “High-Threshold and Low-Overhead Fault-Tolerant Quantum Memory”, which has no “bicycle” in it; here the mnemonic is the name of the code family it studies (bivariate bicycle). Or wang2026lowoverhead: wang + 2026 + lowoverhead, this time from the title (“low-overhead” in “Demonstration of low-overhead quantum error correction codes”, hyphen dropped). The convention keeps keys pronounceable and nearly collision-free; the BB entry has three keys surnamed Wang (wang2023abelian, wang2026coprime, wang2026lowoverhead), three papers by different authors, years or mnemonic words, told apart by the last two segments. All keys are registered in registry/refs.yaml, and that registry has two kinds of row. A complete row has five fields, id, title, authors, year, url (an arXiv link), like the row for bravyi2024bicycle. A bare row has one field, written on one line as - {id: lin2023twoblock}: the key's name is reserved, but no metadata is attached. When the site builder tools/site/build.py renders the references page it asks one question: are title, authors, year and url all present? If so it shows the linked title, a year chip, the authors and the key; if any is missing it shows only <code>key</code> plus a grey chip, “metadata pending”. So the precise meaning of “metadata pending” is: this key exists in the registry as a bare id only. It does not say the paper does not exist, nor that the sentence citing it lacks a source. A third kind of row is the alias: - {id: bravyi2024high, alias_of: bravyi2024bicycle} points a key used by some literature sweep at the same paper without counting a second paper. The BB entry uses aliases twice: the foundations scout report notes “QEC-Bank currently uses bravyi2024high; the fetched record is arXiv 2308.07915 and there is a separate task to rename it bravyi2024bicycle”, a rename since completed with the old key kept as an alias; and the decoders report uses roffe2020bposd where the card uses roffe2020decoding, the former being registered as an alias of the latter.

1.2键在 card.yaml 里的三种住处，与两组数目Three homes for a key in card.yaml, and two counts

在条目源文件 codes/bivariate_bicycle/card.yaml 里，键以三种方式出现。其一，条目级的 refs 列表：32 个键按顺序排好，从 mackay2004sparse 到 chandra2026distributed——这就是 QEC-Bank 参考文献页显示的那张表，一条不多一条不少，顺序也一样。其二，侧面记录里的 refs 列表：8 条表示阶梯记录、3 条译码器记录、5 条实现记录、1 条关系各带自己的几个键。例如译码器记录 decoders[2]（mwpm，「BB 对称性约化到环面状圆柱」）的 refs 只有 [sahay2026matching]；关系 relations[0] 的 refs 是 [wang2023abelian, lin2023twoblock]。其三，origin.ref：一个记录的「出处」字段可以指向一个键。五条逻辑操作全部写成 origin: {kind: claimed, ref: KEY}——每个门只挂一个键，并诚实地写着 claimed；表示阶梯上五条 curated 记录也各挂一个 origin.ref（wang2023abelian、bravyi2024bicycle、postema2025existence、chen2025anyon、sahay2026matching）；分类标签 qldpc 的 origin 是 curated, ref: bravyi2024bicycle。把三种住处数一遍，得到两组数目。16 个键至少被一条侧面记录或一个 origin.ref 绑定；16 个键只在条目级列表里，不绑定任何记录——本页叫它们「仅条目级」（这是本页的叫法，不是 QEC-Bank 的官方词）。仅条目级的 16 个是：mackay2004sparse、voss2025multivariate、wang2026coprime、eberhardt2024pruning、liang2025planar、steffan2025tile、koutsioumpas2025autdec、blue2026machine、pakhunov2026convergence、pakhunov2026peeling、cohen2022longrange、xu2023constant、breuckmann2026cups、xu2025batched、chen2025fusion、chandra2026distributed。它们支撑的是六份文献扫描报告和 Level-2 页里的论证（例如「变体与边界地图」的七根轴），而不是某一条可执行的断言。被引最多的键是 bravyi2024bicycle：7 处——4 条表示阶梯记录的 refs、1 条的 origin.ref、1 条译码器记录、1 个分类标签。

In the entry source codes/bivariate_bicycle/card.yaml, keys appear in three ways. First, the entry-level refs list: 32 keys in order, from mackay2004sparse to chandra2026distributed; this is exactly the table the QEC-Bank references page shows, no more, no less, in the same order. Second, refs lists inside facet records: the 8 representation-ladder records, 3 decoder records, 5 realization records and 1 relation each carry their own few keys. The decoder record decoders[2] (mwpm, “BB symmetry reduction to toric-code-like cylinders”), for instance, has refs [sahay2026matching] only; the relation relations[0] has [wang2023abelian, lin2023twoblock]. Third, origin.ref: a record's provenance field can point at a key. All five logical operations are written origin: {kind: claimed, ref: KEY}, one key per gate, honestly labelled claimed; the five curated records on the representation ladder each carry an origin.ref too (wang2023abelian, bravyi2024bicycle, postema2025existence, chen2025anyon, sahay2026matching); and the category tag qldpc has origin curated, ref: bravyi2024bicycle. Counting the three homes gives two numbers. 16 keys are bound to at least one facet record or origin.ref; 16 keys sit only in the entry-level list, bound to no record, which this page calls “entry-level only” (this page's term, not an official QEC-Bank one). The 16 entry-level-only keys are mackay2004sparse, voss2025multivariate, wang2026coprime, eberhardt2024pruning, liang2025planar, steffan2025tile, koutsioumpas2025autdec, blue2026machine, pakhunov2026convergence, pakhunov2026peeling, cohen2022longrange, xu2023constant, breuckmann2026cups, xu2025batched, chen2025fusion, chandra2026distributed. They support arguments in the six scout reports and on the Level-2 pages (for instance the seven axes of the “variant and boundary map”), not any executable assertion. The most-cited key is bravyi2024bicycle, in 7 places: the refs of 4 representation-ladder records, 1 origin.ref, 1 decoder record and 1 category tag.

1.3本条目用到的全部来源词Every provenance word the entry uses

为什么每条不能重算的论断都要指向一个键？因为 QEC-Bank 把「机器算出来的」和「文献说的」严格分开。BB 条目的分界写在直觉文稿「Why these parameters」一节：n = 144、k = 12、检查重量 6、比特度 6 是构造器对显示出来的矩阵重算的结果；gross 的 d = 12 分成两半——文献下界（bravyi2024bicycle）排除重量小于 12 的逻辑算符，QEC-Bank 用 rand-lowweight-v1（种子 5、2000 次）重跑出一个重量 12 的上界见证，「两个界相遇，所以记录的码距是 12，来源词是 bounded。QEC-Bank 没有复现文献的下界证书；这一处剩余的信任分工是明写的。」阈值、硬件结果、逻辑门则永远是文献论断。于是每个不能重算的数字都需要一个键，而每个键旁边都有一个来源词告诉你这句话有多硬。下表是 BB 条目里出现的全部来源词，每个都给出它在卡片上的确切位置：

Why must every claim that cannot be recomputed point at a key? Because the QEC-Bank keeps “what the machine computed” strictly apart from “what the literature says”. For the BB entry the split is written in the intuition essay's section “Why these parameters”: n = 144, k = 12, check weight 6 and qubit degree 6 are the constructor's recomputation of the displayed matrices; gross's d = 12 splits in two, a literature lower bound (bravyi2024bicycle) excluding logicals below weight 12 and a QEC-Bank rerun of rand-lowweight-v1 (seed 5, 2,000 iterations) finding a weight-12 upper witness: “The bounds meet, so the recorded distance is 12 with bounded provenance. The QEC-Bank has not reproduced the literature's lower-bound certificate; that remaining division of trust is explicit.” Thresholds, hardware results and logical gates are literature claims, always. 关键想法Key idea

一个文献键是一份契约：「这个数字不是我算的，是这篇论文说的，你可以去核对。」BB 条目的 32 份契约都签了名（键都在），但有 25 份还没填地址（没有标题、作者、年份、链接）。接下来两章分别读有地址的 7 份和没地址的 25 份，每一份都回答同一个问题：它在 QEC-Bank 里替谁作证。

A bibkey is a contract: “I did not compute this number; this paper states it, and you may check.” All 32 of the BB entry's contracts are signed (the keys exist), but 25 still lack an address (no title, authors, year, link). The next two chapters read the 7 with an address and the 25 without, each answering the same question: whose witness is it in the QEC-Bank?

The tools of these papers all answer the same kind of question: given two polynomials a and b, how many logical qubits does the code have, and what do its logical operators look like? ① 群代数 R① The group algebra R

R = 变量 x、y 的多项式，系数只取 0 或 1（1 + 1 = 0），并规定 xl = 1、ym = 1（向右走 l 步、向上走 m 步都回到原处）。一个单项式 xayb 就是环面上的格子 (a, b)，一条多项式就是一组格子；乘一个单项式就是把整组格子平移。例（l = 2, m = 3）：(1 + x)·xy = xy + x²y = xy + y，因为 x² = 1：格子 (1,1) 与 (0,1)。它换来的是：检查的腿、平移、相乘抵消，都变成多项式乘法。

R = polynomials in x and y with coefficients 0 or 1 only (1 + 1 = 0), with the rules xl = 1 and ym = 1 (l steps right or m steps up bring you back). A monomial xayb is the cell (a, b) of the torus, a polynomial is a set of cells, and multiplying by a monomial shifts the whole set. Example (l = 2, m = 3): (1 + x)·xy = xy + x²y = xy + y, because x² = 1: the cells (1,1) and (0,1). What it buys: legs of checks, shifts and cancellations all become polynomial multiplication.

② 提升积② Lifted product

先写一个元素是 R 中多项式的小矩阵，再把每个多项式「提升」成它的 lm × lm 平移矩阵（x 换成「向右走一步」的置换矩阵，y 换成「向上走一步」的），拿两个这样的矩阵拼出 HX、HZ（一般大小的矩阵按「超图积」的格式拼，本页用不到）。最小的情形是两个 1 × 1 矩阵 [a] 与 [b]，拼出来正是 HX = [A | B]、HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ]，也就是两块码；Lin 与 Pryadko 说 2BGA 是「最小的提升积码」，指的就是这一点。例：a = x + y、l = m = 4 提升成 16 × 16 的 A，每行两个 1；图 3.1 的码就是 [a] 与 [a] 的提升积。它换来的是：更大的矩阵能造出更多的码，而两块码是其中最小的一格。

Write a small matrix whose entries are polynomials in R, then “lift” each polynomial to its lm × lm shift matrix (x becomes the permutation matrix “one step right”, y “one step up”), and assemble HX, HZ from two such matrices (matrices of general size are assembled in the “hypergraph product” layout, which this page does not need). The smallest case is two 1 × 1 matrices [a] and [b], which give exactly HX = [A | B], HZ = [Bᵀ | Aᵀ], the two-block code; that is what Lin and Pryadko mean by 2BGA codes being the “smallest lifted-product codes”. Example: a = x + y with l = m = 4 lifts to the 16 × 16 matrix A with two 1s per row; the code of Fig. 3.1 is the lifted product of [a] and [a]. What it buys: larger matrices give many more codes, and the two-block codes are the smallest cell of that family.

③ R → R² → R：链复形与同调③ R → R² → R: chain complex and homology

三层对象：X 检查（每格一条，一份 R）、数据比特（左右两层，两份 R，写作 R²）、Z 检查（一份 R）。箭头表示「碰到」。第一支箭头把一组 X 检查 f 送到它们碰到的比特：(a·f, b·f)（左层、右层），矩阵是 HXᵀ；第二支把一组比特 (u, v) 送到碰它们奇数次的 Z 检查：b·u + a·v，矩阵是 HZ。连走两步：b·a·f + a·b·f = 2ab·f = 0，因为多项式乘法可以交换次序，而 1 + 1 = 0。这句话的意思是每条 X 检查与每条 Z 检查共用偶数个比特，也就是检查两两对易。逻辑比特是剩下的部分：第二支箭头送到 0 的比特组（没有 Z 检查发现它），去掉第一支箭头能得到的（X 检查的乘积），剩下的就叫这个复形的同调，k 是它的维数（X 与 Z 两种各一半）。例：图 3.1 的 X 串是 (u, v) = (1, 1)（多项式 1 = 格子 (0,0)，即 L(0,0) 与 R(0,0)），第二支箭头给出 b + a = 2(x + y) = 0，没有警报；它又不在第一支箭头的像里（左层奇偶性不对），所以它代表一个非零的同调类。它换来的是：「几个逻辑比特」变成拓扑里算同调的问题，现成的定理可以直接用。

Three layers: X checks (one per cell, one copy of R), data qubits (left and right layers, two copies, written R²), Z checks (one copy of R). Arrows mean “touches”. The first arrow sends a set of X checks f to the qubits they touch, (a·f, b·f) (left layer, right layer); its matrix is HXᵀ. The second sends a set of qubits (u, v) to the Z checks that touch it an odd number of times, b·u + a·v; its matrix is HZ. Two steps in a row: b·a·f + a·b·f = 2ab·f = 0, because polynomial multiplication can be done in either order and 1 + 1 = 0. In words: every X check shares an even number of qubits with every Z check, i.e. the checks commute. The logical qubits are what is left: the qubit sets the second arrow sends to 0 (no Z check detects them), minus those the first arrow produces (products of X checks); what remains is the homology of the complex, and k is its dimension (half from X, half from Z). Example: the X string of Fig. 3.1 is (u, v) = (1, 1) (the polynomial 1 = the cell (0,0), i.e. L(0,0) and R(0,0)); the second arrow gives b + a = 2(x + y) = 0, no alarm; and it is not produced by the first arrow (wrong left-layer parity), so it represents a nonzero homology class. What it buys: “how many logical qubits” becomes a homology computation from topology, where ready-made theorems apply.

④ 商 R/(a, b)④ The quotient R/(a, b)

(a, b) 是所有 f·a + g·b 组成的集合（f、g 取遍 R），叫 a、b 生成的理想；R/(a, b) 是把这个集合里的多项式都当作 0 之后剩下的东西。它的维数（还剩几个互相独立的多项式）直接给出 k：k = 2 · dim R/(a, b)。理由：③ 里第二支箭头 (u, v) ↦ b·u + a·v 的全部输出恰好就是理想 (a, b)，所以 (a, b) 的维数是 rank HZ，dim R/(a, b) = lm − rank HZ；BB 码的 rank HX 与 rank HZ 相等，所以 k = 2lm − rank HX − rank HZ = 2(lm − rank HZ) = 2 · dim R/(a, b)。例：gross 的 rank HX = rank HZ = 66，dim R/(a, b) = 72 − 66 = 6，k = 12（本页脚本）。它换来的是：k 变成一个只含 a、b 的代数问题，可以在不写出 144 列矩阵的情况下讨论。

(a, b) is the set of all f·a + g·b (f, g ranging over R), called the ideal generated by a and b; R/(a, b) is what is left once every polynomial in that set is treated as 0. Its dimension (how many independent polynomials remain) gives k directly: k = 2 · dim R/(a, b). Reason: the outputs of the second arrow of ③, (u, v) ↦ b·u + a·v, are exactly the ideal (a, b), so the dimension of (a, b) is rank HZ and dim R/(a, b) = lm − rank HZ; for BB codes rank HX equals rank HZ, so k = 2lm − rank HX − rank HZ = 2(lm − rank HZ) = 2 · dim R/(a, b). Example: gross has rank HX = rank HZ = 66, so dim R/(a, b) = 72 − 66 = 6 and k = 12 (this page’s script). What it buys: k becomes an algebra question about a and b alone, which can be discussed without writing out a 144-column matrix.

⑤ Gröbner 基⑤ Gröbner basis

理想的一组特别挑过的生成元，使「商有几维」可以机械地数出来：每个生成元有一个最高项，不能被任何一个最高项整除的单项式的个数就是 dim R/(a, b)。例（l = 2, m = 3，a = 1 + xy + y²，b = 1 + x）：b = 0 意味着 x = 1（模 2 里 −1 = 1）；代进 a 得 1 + y + y² = 0；而 y³ = 1 自动成立，因为 1 + y³ = (1 + y)(1 + y + y²)（展开：1 + y + y² + y + y² + y³，中间两对抵消）。所以这个理想由 x + 1 与 y² + y + 1 生成，这就是一组 Gröbner 基。最高项是 x 与 y²，不被它们整除的单项式只剩 1 和 y，所以 dim R/(a, b) = 2，k = 2 × 2 = 4。本页脚本按矩阵秩直接算，也是 k = 4（n = 12）。它换来的是：大的 l、m 上也能不列矩阵就数出 k。

A specially chosen set of generators of the ideal that makes “how many dimensions does the quotient have” a mechanical count: each generator has a leading term, and the number of monomials divisible by no leading term is dim R/(a, b). Example (l = 2, m = 3, a = 1 + xy + y², b = 1 + x): b = 0 means x = 1 (mod 2, −1 = 1); substituting into a gives 1 + y + y² = 0; and y³ = 1 then holds automatically, since 1 + y³ = (1 + y)(1 + y + y²) (expand: 1 + y + y² + y + y² + y³, the middle pairs cancel). So the ideal is generated by x + 1 and y² + y + 1, and that is a Gröbner basis. The leading terms are x and y²; the only monomials divisible by neither are 1 and y, so dim R/(a, b) = 2 and k = 2 × 2 = 4. This page’s script computes the matrix ranks directly and also gets k = 4 (n = 12). What it buys: k can be counted for large l, m without writing a matrix.

⑥ Koszul 复形⑥ Koszul complex

③ 里的三层链有一个代数里现成的名字：给两条多项式，第一支箭头把 f 同时乘上这两条（f ↦ (a·f, b·f)），第二支再交叉乘回去相加（(u, v) ↦ b·u + a·v），连走两步必为 0。这种链叫这两条多项式的 Koszul 复形，所以 BB 码的检查结构就是 (a, b) 的 Koszul 复形。例：图 3.1 的码 a = b = x + y，f = 1（(0,0) 格的一条 X 检查）经第一支箭头到 (x + y, x + y)，即 L(1,0)、L(0,1)、R(1,0)、R(0,1)，和图下正文列的腿一致；再经第二支箭头到 (x + y)² + (x + y)² = 0。它换来的是：代数里关于 Koszul 复形的定理可以直接拿来用；chen2025anyon 的摘要说他们用它把任意子与逻辑算符对应起来（「extend the connection between anyons and logical operators using Koszul complex theory」）。

The three-layer chain of ③ has a ready-made name in algebra: given two polynomials, the first arrow multiplies f by both (f ↦ (a·f, b·f)), the second multiplies crosswise and adds ((u, v) ↦ b·u + a·v), and two steps always give 0. Such a chain is the Koszul complex of the two polynomials, so the check structure of a BB code is the Koszul complex of (a, b). Example: for the code of Fig. 3.1, a = b = x + y, f = 1 (the X check of cell (0,0)) goes by the first arrow to (x + y, x + y), i.e. L(1,0), L(0,1), R(1,0), R(0,1), the legs listed in the text under the figure; the second arrow then gives (x + y)² + (x + y)² = 0. What it buys: theorems about Koszul complexes apply directly; the chen2025anyon abstract says they use it to relate anyons to logical operators (“extend the connection between anyons and logical operators using Koszul complex theory”).

⑦ 上同调不变量与杯积⑦ Cohomology invariants and cup products

把 ③ 的箭头反过来读，得到同一套计数的对偶版本，叫上同调；它数的是另一种字母的逻辑算符。一个不变量是一条规则：输入逻辑算符，输出 0 或 1，而且把输入乘上任何检查，输出不变，所以它只看逻辑信息。最简单的例子是「一个 X 型逻辑与一个 Z 型逻辑共用比特数的奇偶」。图 3.1 的码里，X 串 v = {L(0,0), R(0,0)} 与 Z 型逻辑 w = {L(0,0), L(1,3), L(2,2), L(3,1)} 共用 1 个比特，是奇数；w 与每条 X 检查共用偶数个比特，所以把 v 乘上任何一条 X 检查，奇偶不变（脚本：乘上一条 X 检查后 v 变成重量 6，共用数仍是 1）。杯积是把两个或更多上同调量相乘、得到新的上同调量的规则，由它能造出更高次的不变量。Breuckmann 等人给一般 CSS 码定义了杯积；按这种不变量的值给态乘上 ±1 的门是对角的逻辑门，可以用常数深度的电路实现；Λ 个量的杯积作用在同一个码的 Λ 份拷贝上，给出 Clifford 层级第 Λ 层的门（第 1 层是 Pauli 门，第 2 层是 Clifford 门，第 3 层含 CCZ 这类门），论文叫它 copy-cup 门。杯积在 CSS 码上具体怎样定义，超出本页，见原论文（arXiv 2410.16250）。

Reading the arrows of ③ backwards gives the dual version of the same count, called cohomology; it counts the logical operators of the other letter. An invariant is a rule that takes logical operators and returns 0 or 1, and whose output does not change when an input is multiplied by any check, so it sees only logical information. The simplest example is “the parity of the number of qubits shared by an X-type and a Z-type logical”. In the code of Fig. 3.1 the X string v = {L(0,0), R(0,0)} and the Z-type logical w = {L(0,0), L(1,3), L(2,2), L(3,1)} share 1 qubit, an odd number; w shares an even number of qubits with every X check, so multiplying v by any X check keeps the parity (script: after one X check v has weight 6 and still shares 1 qubit with w). A cup product is a rule that multiplies two or more cohomology quantities into a new one, and it yields higher-degree invariants. Breuckmann et al. define cup products for general CSS codes; the gate that multiplies a state by ±1 according to such an invariant is a diagonal logical gate that a constant-depth circuit can implement; a cup product of Λ quantities acting on Λ copies of the same code gives a gate in level Λ of the Clifford hierarchy (level 1 = Pauli gates, level 2 = Clifford gates, level 3 includes gates such as CCZ), which the paper calls the copy-cup gate. How the cup product is defined on a CSS code is beyond this page; see the paper (arXiv 2410.16250).

它又为什么不是 X 检查的乘积（乘积是检查本身，不改变逻辑信息）？X 检查从自己的格子往前走一步取腿：左层 (a+1, b)、(a, b+1)，右层同样两格，所以每条 X 检查在左层恰好碰 2 个比特。把几条 X 检查乘起来，同一个比特上的两个 X 相乘等于什么都不做（X·X = 1），也就是按模 2 相加。例：(0,0) 格的 X 检查碰左层 L(1,0)、L(0,1)；(1,3) 格的碰 L(2,3)、L(1,0)。两者相乘，L(1,0) 被碰两次消掉，左层剩 L(0,1)、L(2,3)，仍是 2 个。一般地，一个比特留下与否只看它被碰的次数是奇是偶；所有左层比特被碰次数的总和 = 2 × 检查条数，是偶数，所以被碰奇数次的比特也有偶数个。任何一组 X 检查的乘积在左层都占偶数个比特。图中的 X 串在左层只占 L(0,0) 一个，是奇数，所以它不是 X 检查的乘积。它既不被任何 Z 检查发现，又不是检查本身，那它就是一个会改变逻辑信息的逻辑算符，而重量只有 2：这个码的码距至多 2。n = 32、k = 8 与上面每一步都由本页脚本（notes/references/verify2.py）逐项算过。

And why is it not a product of X checks (such a product is itself a check and leaves the logical information alone)? An X check takes its legs one step forwards: left-layer (a+1, b) and (a, b+1), and the same two cells in the right layer, so every X check touches exactly 2 left-layer qubits. When X checks are multiplied, two X’s on the same qubit multiply to nothing (X·X = 1), which is addition mod 2. Example: the X check of cell (0,0) touches L(1,0) and L(0,1); that of cell (1,3) touches L(2,3) and L(1,0). In their product L(1,0) is hit twice and drops out, leaving L(0,1) and L(2,3) on the left, still 2. In general a qubit survives exactly when it is hit an odd number of times; the hits on left-layer qubits add up to 2 × (number of checks), an even number, so the qubits hit an odd number of times are also even in number. Every product of X checks occupies an even number of left-layer qubits. The X string in the figure occupies one, L(0,0), an odd number, so it is not a product of X checks. It escapes every Z check and is not itself a check, so it is a logical operator that changes the logical information, of weight only 2: the code’s distance is at most 2. n = 32, k = 8 and every step above were computed by this page’s script (notes/references/verify2.py).

模 2 系数、deg、gcdCoefficients mod 2, deg, gcd

这里的多项式系数只取 0 或 1，并且 1 + 1 = 0，所以两项相同就抵消。例：(1 + π)(1 + π + π²) = 1 + π + π² + π + π² + π³ = 1 + π³，中间的 π 与 π、π² 与 π² 各自抵消。deg（degree，次数）是多项式里最高的幂：deg(1 + π + π²) = 2。gcd（greatest common divisor，最大公因式）是能同时整除几条多项式的、次数最高的那条公因式，和整数的最大公约数一样可以用辗转相除求出来。

The polynomials here have coefficients 0 or 1 only, and 1 + 1 = 0, so equal terms cancel. Example: (1 + π)(1 + π + π²) = 1 + π + π² + π + π² + π³ = 1 + π³; the two π’s and the two π²’s cancel. deg (degree) is the highest power in a polynomial: deg(1 + π + π²) = 2. gcd (greatest common divisor) is the common factor of highest degree that divides all the given polynomials; as for integers, it can be found by repeated division with remainder (Euclid’s algorithm).

为什么斜着走 π = xy 能走遍环面Why diagonal steps π = xy visit the whole torus

π = xy 是「向右一步再向上一步」。走 t 步后停在格子 (t mod l, t mod m)。第一次回到 (0,0)，要 t 同时是 l 和 m 的倍数；l、m 互素时，最小的这种 t 就是 lm。于是 π⁰, π¹, …, πlm−1 这 lm 步停在互不相同的格子上：若第 s 步与第 t 步（s < t）停在同一格，那么 t − s 同时是 l 和 m 的倍数，至少是 lm，与 t − s < lm 矛盾。环面一共只有 lm 格，所以每格恰好走到一次，每个单项式 xayb 都等于 π 的某个幂，x、y 的多项式都能改写成只含 π 的多项式，而且 πlm = 1。l、m 不互素时就不行：l = m = 2 时第 2 步已回到原点，只到 2 格（下图右）。gross 的 l = 12、m = 6 最大公约数是 6，π 只能到 72 格中的 12 格。

π = xy is “one step right, then one step up”. After t steps you stand on the cell (t mod l, t mod m). To get back to (0,0) for the first time, t must be a multiple of both l and m; when l and m are coprime the smallest such t is lm. So the lm steps π⁰, π¹, …, πlm−1 land on different cells: if steps s and t (s < t) landed on the same cell, t − s would be a multiple of both l and m, hence at least lm, contradicting t − s < lm. The torus has only lm cells, so each is visited exactly once; every monomial xayb equals some power of π, every polynomial in x and y can be rewritten in π alone, and πlm = 1. Without coprimality this fails: for l = m = 2 step 2 is already back at the start and only 2 cells are reached (right of the figure). For gross, l = 12 and m = 6 have greatest common divisor 6, and π reaches only 12 of the 72 cells.

公式，与一个算到 k 的例子The formula, and an example worked through to k

4.3三个被防住的误引Three misattributions that got caught

防误引的手段不是要读者小心，而是把否定句写进记录本身。误引一：「BB 码的阈值是 0.8%」。先看这个数在论文里怎么来。Bravyi 等人（arXiv 2308.07915）用的是标准电路级噪声症状提取电路里的每个操作（每个 CNOT、每次制备、每次测量、每个空闲步）各自以同一个概率 p 出错，彼此独立。：提取电路里每个操作各自以同一个概率 p 出错、互相独立；译码器是他们扩展到这种噪声下的 BP-OSD（min-sum 置信传播，最多 10,000 轮迭代，OSD 用「组合扫描」版本，程序是 Roffe 等人公开的 BP-OSD 软件）。对每一个码，先模拟出 pL(p)：整块码每轮提取后失败的概率，k 个逻辑比特里任何一个出错都算失败；再解盈亏平衡方程 pL(p) = kp，右边的 kp 近似「k 个不编码的裸比特里至少一个出错」的概率。解出的 p 叫这个码的伪阈值pseudo-threshold：一个固定的码上，编码后的失败率恰好等于不编码时的失败率的那个物理错误率 p₀。它属于单个码，不是几种尺寸的曲线的交叉点。 p0：物理错误率低于它，这块编码的码就比 k 个裸比特更可靠。手算一例：gross 的 k = 12，v2 给它的 p0 = 0.0065，于是在这一点上 pL = 12 × 0.0065 ≈ 0.078，即每轮约 7.8%。pL 只在 p ≥ 10−3 模拟，更低处用拟合公式外推。所以它既不是几种尺寸的码的曲线交叉点，也不是 gross 一个码的性质。各版本的数也不同：v1（2023）表 1 列五个码，p0 从 0.007(3) 到 0.008(3)（括号里那位数论文没有解释），正文说「接近 0.008」；v2 表 1 改成 0.0048 到 0.0069（gross 为 0.0065），正文说 144 与 288 比特的两个码「接近 0.007」；v1 和 v2 的摘要都写 0.8%，Nature 发表版（2024）的摘要写 0.7%。QEC-Bank 卡片用的是摘要里的 0.8%，并在 decoders[1] 的 notes 里写明：「It is not a decoder-independent threshold of every BB construction: changing the family, CNOT order, detector model, noise normalization, or postprocessing defines a different threshold experiment.」这个数字挂在 bravyi2024bicycle 上（换了就算另一个实验的五项见《BB 码 · 高中数学版》6.4 节）。把它挂到 roffe2020decoding（BP-OSD 的基线论文）或 sahay2026matching（匹配约化）上都是错的：前者没有测 BB 的电路级阈值，后者是另一种译码器。

The defence against misattribution is not reader vigilance but negative clauses written into the records. Misattribution one: “the BB threshold is 0.8%”. First, how the paper gets this number. Bravyi et al. (arXiv 2308.07915) use standard circuit-level noiseEvery operation of the syndrome-extraction circuit (each CNOT, preparation, measurement and idle step) fails independently with the same probability p.: every operation of the extraction circuit fails independently with the same probability p. The decoder is BP-OSD extended to this noise (min-sum belief propagation, at most 10,000 iterations, the “combination sweep” version of OSD, run with the public BP-OSD software of Roffe et al.). For each code they first simulate pL(p), the probability that the whole block fails in one extraction round, where a failure of any of its k logical qubits counts. Then they solve the break-even equation pL(p) = kp, whose right side kp approximates the chance that at least one of k bare, unencoded qubits fails. The solution is that code’s pseudo-thresholdFor one fixed code, the physical error rate p₀ at which the encoded failure rate equals the unencoded one. It belongs to a single code; it is not where the curves of several sizes cross. p0: below it, the encoded block is more reliable than k bare qubits. A check by hand: gross has k = 12 and v2 gives it p0 = 0.0065, so at that point pL = 12 × 0.0065 ≈ 0.078, about 7.8% per round. pL was simulated only for p ≥ 10−3 and extrapolated below with a fitting formula. So the number is neither a crossing of curves for several code sizes nor a property of gross alone. 5.2补起来是机械的，但有两处要人来决定Closing it is mechanical, with two places that need a human decision

Every item in the six scout reports is a YAML block with bibkey, title, authors, year, arxiv. The registry needs five fields, id, title, authors, year, url, and the url is just https://arxiv.org/abs/ followed by the arxiv id. So completing the 25 is the clerical job of replacing each bare row - {id: KEY} by a five-line record; the builder will swap “metadata pending” for a linked bibliography at the next export, and, as with the pending target entry in §1.3 of the relations guide, the card itself changes by not one character. Two places need a human. First, key mismatches. The decoders report uses roffe2020bposd where the card uses roffe2020decoding; the QEC-Bank resolves it by an alias, as with the foundations report's bravyi2024high. If a pending key turns out to have a different name in another report, someone decides which is primary and which the alias. Second, spelling choices. Registry and scout reports already differ on the registered keys: the title of mackay2004sparse is “Error-Correction” in one and “Error Correction” in the other; the second author of steffan2025tile is “Shin Ho Choe” in one and “Seung-Hoon Choe” in the other; the decoders report abbreviates the authors of bravyi2024bicycle to “Sergey Bravyi et al.” while the registry lists all six. 陷阱：两个 pending，三级缺口Pitfall: two pendings, three levels of gap

「metadata pending」（键有、名字没有）与关系页上的「pending」（边有、目标条目没有）是两个不同的机制，都是构建时算出来的，都不是卡片字段。缺口分三级：25 个键没有元数据（本章）；3 个键不在 refs 列表里（5.3 节）；two_bga 目标条目不存在（关系导读）。三者都不改变任何一条记录的可信度，但都让读者少了一次「点过去看看」的机会。

“metadata pending” (key present, name absent) and the relations page's “pending” (edge present, target entry absent) are two different mechanisms, both computed at build time, neither a card field. The gap has three levels: 25 keys without metadata (this chapter); 3 keys not on the refs list (§5.3); the two_bga target entry not existing (relations guide). None changes the trustworthiness of any record, but each takes away one chance for the reader to “click through and look”.
