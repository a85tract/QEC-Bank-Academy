This guide answers the two questions of the QEC-Bank facet “Use cases & realizations”: what that matrix demands of a chip (which hardware can host six-leg checks that reach three cells away), and what real machines have actually done so far. 第 1 章Chapter 1

用例与契合：这张矩阵向芯片要什么Use case & fit: what this matrix asks of a chip

一个码不是「装到任何芯片上都一样」。它向硬件递出一份契约：比特怎么排、谁能和谁连线、一轮测量要走几步。表面码的契约是「二维、只连邻居、四腿检查」；BB 码把其中两条改掉了。这一章先写清 BB 的契约，再看 QEC-Bank 卡片上三个平台标签、两项任务各由什么记录支撑，最后把「144 个数据比特」这本账算全。 A code does not “work the same on any chip”. It hands the hardware a contract: how qubits are arranged, who may be wired to whom, how many steps one measurement round takes. The surface code's contract reads “two-dimensional, neighbours only, four-leg checks”; the BB code rewrites two of those clauses. This chapter spells out the BB contract, then traces the three platform tags and two tasks on the QEC-Bank card to the records that back them, and finally completes the “144 data qubits” ledger.

1.1契约的四条款The four clauses of the contract

先回忆 gross 码长什么样：l = 12、m = 6 的环面（l、m 是环面两个方向的格数），每格两个数据比特，n = 2lm = 144；两个多项式 A = x³ + y + y²、B = y³ + x + x² 各三项，所以每条检查六腿（|A| + |B| = 3 + 3，|A| 记 A 的项数），每个数据比特被 3 条 X 检查和 3 条 Z 检查碰到（《BB 码 · 高中数学版》3.3 节）。这些数字直接翻译成硬件要求。第一条：每个数据比特要连 6 根线，而且「往右三格」「往上三格」这种腿不是邻居——Bravyi 等人证明这张Tanner 图检查矩阵的图形版：一侧是比特、一侧是检查，H 的每个 1 是一条边。它决定芯片需要哪些耦合器，不决定 CNOT 的顺序。可以拆成两层平面图、每层每个比特最多三根线，但一层最近邻的方格芯片装不下它（6.1 节）。说白了：要么有长程耦合器，要么原子能搬，要么把码切成几块用链路连起来——第 1.2 节的三个平台标签正是这三种答案。第二条：检查重量 6，一条检查要 6 个 CNOT 而不是 4 个。第三条（这条和下一条是 Bravyi 等人论文的提取方案，不是静态矩阵推出的硬件必然）：每条检查一个辅助比特——gross 有 144 条检查，就要 144 个辅助比特，总数 288。第四条：144 条检查的 864 个 CNOT 要排进 7 层（6.2 节）；顺序排错，电路的实际码距会低于静态的 12。QEC-Bank 直觉文稿把这条说得最硬：「症状电路、CNOT 顺序、探测器模型与译码器是存储器的一部分；一张静态的 [[n,k,d]] 矩阵本身不认证电路码距。」

Recall what the gross code looks like: an l = 12 by m = 6 torus (l, m are the cell counts along the two directions), two data qubits per cell, n = 2lm = 144; two three-term polynomials A = x³ + y + y² and B = y³ + x + x², so every check has six legs (|A| + |B| = 3 + 3, where |A| is the number of terms of A) and every data qubit is touched by 3 X checks and 3 Z checks (§3.3 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition”). Those numbers translate directly into hardware demands. Clause one: every data qubit needs 6 wires, and legs such as “three cells to the right” or “three cells up” are not neighbours. Bravyi et al. show the Tanner graphThe picture of a check matrix: qubits on one side, checks on the other, one edge per 1 in H. It decides which couplers a chip needs, not the CNOT order. splits into two planar layers with at most three wires per qubit per layer, but a single-layer nearest-neighbour grid cannot hold it (§6.1). In plain words: either you have long-range couplers, or the atoms can move, or you cut the code into modules joined by links; the three platform tags of §1.2 are exactly those three answers. Clause two: check weight 6, so a check costs 6 CNOTs, not 4. Clause three (this clause and the next are the extraction protocol of Bravyi et al., not consequences of the static matrix): one ancilla per check; gross has 144 checks, hence 144 ancillas and 288 qubits in all. Clause four: the 864 CNOTs of the 144 checks must fit into 7 layers (§6.2); a wrong order gives the circuit a smaller distance than the static 12. The QEC-Bank intuition essay puts it bluntly: “The syndrome circuit, CNOT ordering, detector model, and decoder are part of the memory. A static [[n,k,d]] check matrix alone does not certify circuit distance.”

这句话里的探测器要先说清。一条检查每轮测一次，第 t 轮的读数记 st（0 或 1）。探测器不直接看 st，而是把同一条检查相邻两轮的读数比一比：Dt = st ⊕ st−1（⊕ 是模 2 加，两轮相同得 0，不同得 1）。例子：某个数据比特在第 3、4 轮之间被翻了一下，碰到它的检查从第 4 轮起一直读 1，读数串是 0, 0, 0, 1, 1, 1……，探测器只在第 4 轮亮一次；如果数据没错、只是第 4 轮的测量读错了，读数串是 0, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0……，探测器在第 4、5 轮各亮一次。两种故障留下的「亮灯图案」不同，译码器靠这个区分它们。探测器模型就是一张完整的表：电路里每一种可能的故障，会点亮哪几个探测器。换电路、换 CNOT 顺序、换测量节拍，这张表都会变，所以它和译码器一样是存储器的一部分。

The detector in that sentence needs a definition first. A check is measured once per round; call its outcome in round t st (0 or 1). A detector does not look at st itself; it compares the same check in two consecutive rounds: Dt = st ⊕ st−1 (⊕ is addition mod 2: 0 if the two rounds agree, 1 if they differ). Example: a data qubit is flipped between rounds 3 and 4, so a check touching it reads 1 from round 4 on; the outcome string is 0, 0, 0, 1, 1, 1, …, and the detector lights once, in round 4. If the data are fine and only the round-4 measurement misreads, the string is 0, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0, … and the detector lights in rounds 4 and 5. The two faults leave different light patterns, and that is how a decoder tells them apart. The detector model is the complete table: for every possible fault in the circuit, which detectors it lights. Change the circuit, the CNOT order or the measurement cadence and the table changes, which is why it belongs to the memory just as the decoder does.

1.2三个平台标签，两项任务，只有一条真机记录Three platform tags, two tasks, one real-machine record

卡片的 fit 字段写着三个平台：superconducting-long-range（有长程耦合器的超导芯片）、neutral-atom-reconfigurable（能用光镊搬动原子的中性原子阵列）、modular-networked（几块处理器用量子链路连成网）；两项任务：memory（把逻辑比特存住）与 modular-computation（在模块之间做逻辑运算）。三个标签对应 1.1 节第一条款的三种解法：长线、搬动、切块。但请对照下表最后一列——只有第一个平台有 demonstrated 记录（昆仑处理器，第 3 章）；中性原子和模块化两个标签目前各由 proposed 记录支撑（第 4 章）。这与表面码实现页不同：那里两个平台标签都有真机实验。换成人话：BB 卡片的「契合」是「设计上契合、有人认真算过」，不全是「有人在实验室里做出来了」。

The card's fit field lists three platforms: superconducting-long-range (superconducting chips with long-range couplers), neutral-atom-reconfigurable (atom arrays whose atoms are moved by optical tweezers) and modular-networked (several processors joined by quantum links); and two tasks: memory (keep logical qubits alive) and modular-computation (logical operations across modules). The three tags are the three answers to clause one of §1.1: long wires, motion, modules. But look at the last column of the table: only the first platform has a demonstrated record (the Kunlun processor, Chapter 3); the neutral-atom and modular tags are each backed, at present, by proposed records (Chapter 4). That differs from the surface-code realizations page, where both platform tags carry real experiments. In plain words: “fit” on the BB card means “fits by design and someone has costed it carefully”, not always “someone built it in a lab”.

1.3把「144」这本账算全Completing the “144” ledger

卡片和论文最常被引用的一句是「十二倍」：144 个数据比特，旋转表面码存 1 个 d = 12 的逻辑比特，gross 存 12 个。这句话只比数据比特。按 1.1 节第三条款，gross 还要 144 个辅助比特，全账是 288 个物理比特；旋转表面码 d = 12 的辅助比特是 143 个（每条检查一个，d² − 1 条检查），全账 287。所以「十二倍」在全账上仍然近似成立——但这是码距相同时静态比特数的比较，没有算译码器、路由、逻辑门。论文的标题数字是另一种比法，来自电路级噪声下的数值模拟（arXiv 2308.07915 第 3、8 节）：每个操作以概率 p = 0.1% 独立出错的去极化电路噪声（定义见 4.2 节末尾）；gross 一侧用 MIN-SUM 置信传播加 combination-sweep 版本的 OSD 译码，288 个物理比特，估计每轮逻辑错误率约 2 × 10⁻⁷，论文由此估计能把 12 个逻辑比特保存近一百万轮；对照是 12 块独立的旋转表面码贴片（d = 9 到 15，标准症状电路，同样的噪声，数据来自作者之一与 Vargo 的模拟软件，论文此处没有写明表面码一侧的译码器），要把每轮错误率从 10⁻³ 压到 10⁻⁶ 需要近 3000 个物理比特。论文据此说节省超过 10 倍；两边的目标错误率并不相同（约 2 × 10⁻⁷ 对 10⁻⁶），所以这是近似的比较。反过来，第 3 章的昆仑实验用了 32 个 transmon（目前最常用的一种超导量子比特）跑一个 n = 18 的码：32 是整台设备的数目（含它选择的辅助资源），18 是静态数据比特的参数。实现账本特别叮嘱：32 不能和 gross 的 144 直接比——一个是设备实现，一个是矩阵参数。下面的图是数据比特账：横轴数据比特数，纵轴逻辑比特数，把鼠标放到点上看 k·d²/n。

The most quoted sentence about the card and the paper is “twelve-fold”: with 144 data qubits a rotated surface code stores 1 logical qubit at d = 12, the gross code stores 12. That sentence compares data qubits only. By clause three of §1.1 gross also needs 144 ancillas, so the full ledger is 288 physical qubits; a d = 12 rotated surface code needs 143 ancillas (one per check, d² − 1 checks), full ledger 287. So “twelve-fold” still roughly holds on the full ledger, but it is a comparison of static qubit counts at equal distance: no decoder, routing or logical gates included. The paper's headline number compares differently and comes from numerical simulation under circuit-level noise (arXiv 2308.07915, sections 3 and 8): depolarizing circuit noise in which every operation fails independently with probability p = 0.1% (defined at the end of §4.2); on the gross side, MIN-SUM belief propagation with the combination-sweep version of OSD as decoder, 288 physical qubits, an estimated logical error rate of about 2 × 10⁻⁷ per cycle, from which the paper estimates that 12 logical qubits survive nearly one million cycles; the comparator is 12 separate rotated surface-code patches (d = 9 to 15, the standard syndrome circuit, the same noise, data from simulation software by one of the authors and Vargo; the paper does not name the surface-code decoder at this point), which need nearly 3000 physical qubits to push the per-cycle error rate from 10⁻³ to 10⁻⁶. On this basis the paper claims a saving of more than 10 times; the two target error rates differ (about 2 × 10⁻⁷ versus 10⁻⁶), so the comparison is approximate. Conversely, the Kunlun run of Chapter 3 used 32 transmons (the most widely used kind of superconducting qubit) for an n = 18 code: 32 is the whole device (including its chosen ancilla resources), 18 is a static data-qubit parameter. The realization ledger insists: 32 must not be compared directly with gross's 144; one is a device implementation, the other a matrix parameter. 记号：k·d²/n = 逻辑比特数乘码距平方再除以数据比特数，是比较有限码的一个指标：存了几个、保护多强、花了几个，合成一个数。它和 BPT 界的关系是：对比特排在二维格子上、单位面积比特数有上限、每条检查只碰固定距离以内的比特的码族，BPT 界（《BB 码 · 高中数学版》1.1 节）说 k·d² ≤ c·n，即这个比值不超过常数 c（c 只取决于检查的作用范围和每个格点的维数）；某一块码算出的 k·d²/n 只是一个具体数，不是 c 的「实测值」；143 = 12² − 1，287 = 144 + 143，均为本页算的，旋转表面码的比特账见表面码实现页第 4 章。

Notation: k·d²/n = logical qubits times distance squared over data qubits, a metric for comparing finite codes: how many stored, how well protected, at what cost, folded into one number. Its relation to the BPT bound: for code families whose qubits sit on a 2D lattice with a bounded number per unit area and whose checks touch only qubits within a fixed distance, the BPT bound (§1.1 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition”) says k·d² ≤ c·n, i.e. this ratio stays below a constant c (c depends only on the check range and the dimension per site); the k·d²/n of one particular code is just a number, not a “measured value” of c; 143 = 12² − 1 and 287 = 144 + 143 are this page's arithmetic, the rotated-surface qubit ledger is in Chapter 4 of the surface realizations page.

关键想法Key idea

BB 码的契约比表面码多要一层耦合、每条检查多两条腿、每轮多三层门。卡片的三个平台标签是三种付法：长线、搬动、切块。三种里目前只有「长线」被真机兑现过一次，而且兑现的是 n = 18 的小块，不是 144。

The BB contract asks for one more coupling layer than the surface code, two more legs per check and three more gate layers per round. The card's three platform tags are three ways to pay: long wires, motion, modules. Of the three, only “long wires” has been honoured by a real machine so far, once, and for an n = 18 block rather than 144.

第 2 章Chapter 2

怎样读一条 BB 实验记录：五个字段，三句话How to read a BB experiment record: five fields, three sentences

表面码那边的读法是「每轮逻辑错误率、码距缩放、Λ」（《Surface Code · 用例与实现》第 2 章）。BB 码多了一个麻烦：一块码里有好几个逻辑比特，而且「BB」是一族码、几十种变体。QEC-Bank 的实现账本因此规定：一条 BB 记录要交代五个字段，否则「BB 做出来了」这句话粗到没法比较。 On the surface-code side the reading rule is “logical error per cycle, distance scaling, Λ” (Chapter 2 of “Surface Code · Use Cases & Realizations”). BB codes add a complication: one block holds several logical qubits, and “BB” names a family with dozens of variants. The QEC-Bank realization ledger therefore rules that a BB record must state five fields; without them, “BB has been demonstrated” is too coarse to compare.

2.1五个字段The five fields

2.2「每个逻辑比特每轮」是什么意思What “per logical qubit per cycle” means

表面码实验报告的 εL每轮逻辑错误率（logical error per cycle）：跑一轮纠错循环后逻辑比特被翻转的概率，由多轮衰减曲线拟合得到；定义见《Surface Code · 用例与实现》2.1 节。 是「这一个逻辑比特每轮翻转的概率」（2.1 节）。BB 块里有 k 个逻辑比特——昆仑的 [[18,4,4]] 有 4 个——所以卡片写的是「每个逻辑比特每轮的逻辑错误率 (8.91 ± 0.17)%」：把整块的失败按逻辑比特归一化后的数字。人话：按逻辑比特平均后，量级是「每个逻辑比特每轮约 8.9%」（卡片没给归一化公式，所以不能直接当成某一个比特的翻转概率）；「±0.17%」是卡片登记的不确定度，卡片没有说明它是哪种误差棒。卡片没有给出整块层面的原始数字，也没有给出归一化的公式，本页不替论文重建它。比较时还要问一轮多长——超导芯片一轮约一微秒（表面码实现页用的量级），中性原子慢得多——卡片同样没有登记这个时长。

A surface-code experiment reports εLLogical error per cycle: the probability that the logical qubit flips over one correction cycle, fitted from a multi-cycle decay curve; defined in §2.1 of “Surface Code · Use Cases & Realizations”., “the probability that this one logical qubit flips per cycle” (§2.1). A BB block holds k logical qubits, four for Kunlun's [[18,4,4]], so the card writes “logical error per logical qubit per cycle (8.91 ± 0.17)%”: the block's failure normalised per logical qubit. In plain words: averaged per logical qubit it is of the order “about 8.9% per logical qubit per round” (the card gives no normalisation formula, so it is not literally one qubit's flip probability); “±0.17%” is the uncertainty the card records, with no statement of which error-bar convention it follows. The card gives neither the raw block-level number nor the normalisation formula, and this page does not reconstruct it for the paper. When comparing, also ask how long a cycle is (about a microsecond on superconducting chips, the order of magnitude used on the surface-code realizations page; far longer for neutral atoms); the card does not register that either.

2.3三句强度不同的话Three sentences of different strength

表面码那页把「越大越好」「低于阈值」「超过最好的物理比特」分成三句（2.3 节）。BB 这里对应的三句是：「可行」——契约的前两条款在真机上兑现过，症状电路也重复跑了：非局域六腿检查全部测了；辅助比特怎么分配、电路多深，卡片没有登记。「随码距压制」——实现账本的原话：「需要至少两个或更多码距，在可比的电路、噪声与译码器下，逻辑错误随码距增长而下降；一个固定的 [[18,4,4]] 点提供不了这个推断。」「低于阈值」——比第二句更强，要在多个码距上稳定地看到压制因子 Λ 大于 1（2.2 节）。此外 BB 还有第四句「跑了 gross」和第五句「做了容错逻辑门」。昆仑记录只说了第一句。下一章逐字核对。

The surface page separates “bigger is better”, “below threshold” and “beyond the best physical qubit” (§2.3). One fixed [[18,4,4]] point cannot supply that inference.” “Below threshold”: stronger still, a suppression factor Λ stably above 1 across several distances (§2.2). BB adds a fourth sentence, “ran gross”, and a fifth, “performed a fault-tolerant logical gate”. The Kunlun record says only the first. The next chapter checks it word by word.

The four proposals attack different bottlenecks in the pipeline, so they are not four interchangeable implementations of one detector model. Card: “Gate routing and Rydberg interactions were co-designed for BB connectivity. The result is a numerical architecture proposal rather than a laboratory logical-memory demonstration.” Ledger: established “gate/layout optimization for BB connectivity”; did not establish “laboratory logical memory”. Plain words: neutral atoms do two-qubit gates through Rydberg-state interactions (a Rydberg state is a highly excited state with a very large electron orbit, reached by laser; two such atoms close together interact strongly); this paper computes where to put the atoms and which gates go first, optimised for gross's degree-6 Tanner graph. It attacks the layout layer: realise the Tanner graph fast. No atom was moved. Reference: from the scout report, registration pending: C. Poole, T. M. Graham, M. A. Perlin, M. Otten and M. 分层的二维硬件 · berthusen2025localLayered two-dimensional hardware · berthusen2025local

卡片原文：「用搬动或通信把长程的 BB 检查嵌进一台局域连接的设备，包括让某些代价高的检查测得少一些的调度。改变了的节拍与电路噪声必须作为提案的一部分被译码。」账本：确立了「量化的搬动 / 通信代价与改变的检查节拍」；未确立「探测器模型不变，或免费的局域化」。人话：如果芯片只有最近邻连线，就把远处的腿用搬动或量子通信补上，代价是时间；再省一点，就让最贵的几条检查隔几轮才测一次。但「隔几轮测一次」改变了探测器（1.1 节的定义）。若一条检查每 2 轮才测一次，它的探测器只能比较 st ⊕ st−2：两次测量之间发生的故障要到下一次测量才显现，分不清是在哪一轮；两次测量之间若有两个故障各翻了这条检查的一个比特，这条检查的读数不变，它的探测器根本不亮（在每轮都测的电路里，两故障落在不同轮时它会亮两次）。探测器变少、每个探测器看的时间窗变长，「哪种故障点亮哪些探测器」这张表就变了，译码器看到的不再是深度 7 那张图——所以这篇的阈值数字属于它自己的电路与译码器，不能拿 0.8% 来套。它攻的是「版图」与「电路节拍」两层。文献：据文献扫描报告，登记待补：N. Berthusen 等，“Toward a 2D Local Implementation of Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check Codes”，arXiv 2404.17676。

Card: “Motion or communication embeds long-range BB checks in a locally connected device, including schedules that measure some costly checks less often. The altered cadence and circuit noise must be decoded as part of the proposal.” Ledger: established “quantified motion/communication and altered check cadence”; did not establish “unchanged detector model or free localization”. Plain words: if the chip has only nearest-neighbour wiring, supply the far legs by motion or quantum communication and pay in time; to save more, measure the most expensive checks only every few rounds. But “every few rounds” changes the detectors (defined in §1.1). If a check is measured only every 2 rounds, its detector can only compare st ⊕ st−2: a fault between the two measurements shows up only at the next one, with no way to tell which round it happened in; and if two faults between the measurements each flip one qubit of that check, its outcome does not change and its detector never lights (in a circuit that measures every round, the two faults would light it twice when they fall in different rounds). Fewer detectors, each watching a longer time window: the table of which fault lights which detectors changes, and the decoder no longer sees the depth-7 graph, so this paper's threshold numbers belong to its own circuit and decoder and 0.8% cannot be pasted onto it. It attacks the layout and circuit-cadence layers. Reference: from the scout report, registration pending: N. Berthusen et al., “Toward a 2D Local Implementation of Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check Codes”, arXiv 2404.17676.

H. Shaw, B. M. Terhal，“Lowering Connectivity Requirements for Bivariate Bicycle Codes Using Morphing Circuits”，arXiv 2407.16336。它同时是表示阶梯页 R4 那一格的第二个文献键。

Card: “A related BB family lowers connectivity from degree six to five and uses six CNOT rounds by morphing the code during extraction. It changes the family and detector model; it is not a cheaper schedule for every BB block.” Ledger: established “six CNOT rounds, lower connectivity, surface-code input/output route”; did not establish “a universal reschedule of every BB block”. Plain words: “morphing” swaps the code for another one midway through a measurement round and back; halfway through, the stabilizer group alive on the chip is not gross's. So each qubit needs only 5 wires and each round only 6 gate layers, but the price is that this is a different family with different detectors; it cannot be used to save gross a layer. It is pinned to both the Tanner-graph and circuit layers. Reference: from the scout report, registration pending: M. H. Shaw and B. M. 陷阱：提案不可互换Pitfall: proposals are not interchangeable

实现账本的原话：「这些不是同一个探测器模型的可互换实现。搬动会增加空闲错误；变形改变了哪一个稳定子码在活动；光子的损耗信道不同于 transmon 的退极化模型；网络化的切分引入链路噪声。每一个都需要自己的电路与译码器声明。」换成人话：四篇论文里的任何一个阈值或错误率数字，都只属于那篇论文自己的电路加译码器，不能挪到另一篇上，更不能挪到 0.8% 那一行（译码器页）。引文里的「退极化模型」是模拟用的噪声模型，定义要写全：一个单比特位置（空等、单比特门）以 1 − p 的概率不出错，以 p/3 的概率各出 X、Y、Z 之一；一个出错的两比特门（例如 CNOT）在门后以 p/15 的概率各出 15 种非恒等的两比特 Pauli 乘积之一（每个比特 I、X、Y、Z 四选一，去掉 I⊗I，共 4 × 4 − 1 = 15 种）。Bravyi 等（arXiv 2308.07915 第 6 节）的电路噪声就是这样定义的，另加：制备出错 = 制成正交的态，测量出错 = 读数翻转。它是为了便于模拟而设的理想化模型，不是 transmon 真实噪声的描述：真实器件的错误种类与比例要从实验中测，不一定对称，也不一定彼此独立。

The ledger's words: “These are not interchangeable implementations of an identical detector model. Movement can add idle faults; morphing changes which stabilizer code is active; a photonic loss channel differs from a transmon depolarizing model; a networked partition introduces link noise. Each needs its own circuit and decoder claim.” In plain words: any threshold or error-rate number in one of the four papers belongs to that paper's own circuit plus decoder; it cannot be moved to another paper, still less to the 0.8% row (decoders page). The “depolarizing model” in the quote is a simulation noise model, and its definition should be complete: a single-qubit location (idling, a single-qubit gate) is error-free with probability 1 − p and suffers X, Y or Z with probability p/3 each; a faulty two-qubit gate (a CNOT, say) is followed by one of the 15 non-identity two-qubit Pauli products with probability p/15 each (I, X, Y or Z on each qubit, minus I⊗I: 4 × 4 − 1 = 15). The circuit noise of Bravyi et al. (arXiv 2308.07915, section 6) is defined exactly so, plus: a faulty preparation makes the orthogonal state, a faulty measurement flips the outcome. It is an idealised model chosen to make simulation tractable, not a description of real transmon noise: the error types and rates of a real device have to be measured, and need not be symmetric or independent.

尚未演示的：只抄出处写下的Not yet demonstrated: only what the sources wrote down

诚实的实现页要把空白说得和成绩一样具体。这一章不预测，只转抄实现账本与直觉文稿明确写下的东西，并把「还没做」和「靠放大做不到」分开。 An honest realizations page states the gaps as concretely as the achievements. This chapter forecasts nothing; it transcribes what the realization ledger and the intuition essay wrote down, and separates “not yet done” from “cannot be reached by scaling up”.

5.1账本写下的四条空白The four gaps the ledger wrote down

实现账本「Negative results from the sweep」一节，截至检索日期 2026-07-26，没有任何被抓取的实验做过以下四件事：① 运行 [[144,12,12]] 的 gross 存储器——每一个「十二倍」的数字都还只在纸上和模拟里。② 在一个 BB 家族内演示随码距增大的逻辑错误压制——2.3 节的第二句话，需要至少两个码距。③ 实现一个容错的 BB 逻辑门——逻辑操作页的五条记录全是 claimed，没有一条 demonstrated。④ 在硬件上验证 yoder2025tour 的完整模块化指令集。账本接着说：「这些缺席是条目知识状态的一部分。它们防止一份提案被渲染成一次演示，并为未来的更新指出具体目标。」人话：这四条不是遗憾，是标尺——下一次有 BB 实验登上卡片时，看它划掉了哪一条。

The ledger's section “Negative results from the sweep” states that, as of the sweep date 2026-07-26, no fetched experiment had done any of four things: ① run the [[144,12,12]] gross memory: every “twelve-fold” number still lives on paper and in simulation. ② demonstrated increasing-distance logical suppression within a BB family: the second sentence of §2.3, which needs at least two distances. ③ implemented a fault-tolerant BB logical gate: all five records on the logical-operations page are claimed, none demonstrated. ④ validated the full yoder2025tour modular instruction set in hardware. The ledger continues: “These absences are part of the entry's knowledge state. They prevent a proposal from being rendered as a demonstration and identify concrete targets for future updates.” In plain words: the four are not regrets but a yardstick; when the next BB experiment reaches the card, see which line it strikes out.

5.2直觉文稿指出的结构性边界Structural boundaries named by the intuition essay

下面四条不是「还没做」，而是「做了也不会自动变好」，全部来自 QEC-Bank 直觉文稿的「Failure modes」。连接性是资源：「两层平面耦合层仍然需要长程或可重构的耦合器、层间的交叉、或搬动。一层最近邻的芯片不会靠给比特改名就得到 gross 码的开销。」静态码距可能高估电路：一个辅助比特的错误会沿后续 CNOT 传播成关联的数据错误；深度 7 的调度是一个具体电路，别的顺序可能有更小的电路码距——所以「d = 12」不是实验能直接兑现的承诺，实验兑现的是它那个电路的码距。挤在一起的逻辑比特需要可寻址：「一块里的 12 个逻辑比特只在架构能制备、测量、耦合、路由所需的逻辑组合而不抹掉存储优势时才有用。」有限的常数不意味着渐近的好：Postema 与 Kokkelmans 证明所研究的 BB 子类渐近是坏的（postema2025existence，登记待补；《BB 码 · 高中数学版》6.6 节）——把 gross 的两个多项式固定、只放大环面，不会得到码率与相对码距同时不为零的家族。人话：BB 的价值在「有限尺寸的优秀常数」，把这句说成「二维局域的好 qLDPC 码」是错的。

The next four are not “not yet” but “would not automatically improve even if done”, all from the “Failure modes” of the QEC-Bank intuition essay. Connectivity is a resource: “The two planar coupling layers still require long-range or reconfigurable couplers, crossings between layers, or motion. A one-layer nearest-neighbor chip does not obtain gross-code overhead by relabeling qubits.” Static distance can overstate a circuit: one ancilla fault may propagate through later CNOTs into a correlated data error; the depth-7 schedule is one specific circuit and other orderings can have a smaller circuit distance, so “d = 12” is not a promise an experiment redeems directly; what it redeems is the distance of its own circuit. Packed logicals need addressability: “Twelve logical qubits in one block are useful only if an architecture can prepare, measure, couple, and route the desired logical combinations without erasing the memory advantage.” Finite constants do not imply asymptotic goodness: Postema and Kokkelmans prove the studied BB subclass asymptotically bad (postema2025existence, registration pending; §6.6 of “BB Codes · High-School Edition”): fixing gross's two polynomials and only enlarging the torus does not yield a family whose rate and relative distance both stay nonzero. In plain words: BB's value is “strong finite constants”; rewording that as “asymptotically good qLDPC from a two-dimensional local torus” is wrong.

陷阱：三级可信度Pitfall: three tiers of trust
