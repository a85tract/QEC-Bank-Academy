用例与契合：这套码是为哪种硬件写的Use case & fit: which hardware the code was written for

一个纠错码落到任何芯片上都一样好用？不是。它对硬件递出一份「契约」：比特怎样排、谁能和谁说话、多久测一次。这一章写清表面码的契约，再看 QEC-Bank 卡片上的两个平台标签和两项任务从哪里来。（QEC-Bank 是本系列所属的量子纠错码数据库；卡片是它为每个码建的档案，本页 3.1 节时间轴和 3.2 节主表里的硬件数字都抄自表面码卡片的 realizations 字段，即「实验实现」一栏；正文和折叠框里另外补充的数字（循环时长、所用译码器、各变体的 Λ 等）取自原论文，旁边注明「论文」或「摘要」。） Does an error-correcting code work equally well on any chip? No. It hands the hardware a “contract”: how qubits are arranged, who may talk to whom, how often to measure. This chapter spells out the surface code's contract, then traces the two platform tags and two tasks on the QEC-Bank card. (The QEC-Bank is the database of quantum error-correcting codes this series belongs to; a card is its file on one code, and the hardware numbers in the §3.1 timeline and the §3.2 main table are copied from the realizations field, the “experimental realizations” section, of the surface-code card; the extra numbers added in the text and fold-outs (cycle time, which decoder was used, the Λ of each variant, and so on) come from the original papers and are marked “paper” or “abstract” where they appear.)

1.1契约的四条款The four clauses of the contract

直觉教程第 1 章剪开环面，正是因为真实芯片是平板、比特只能和邻居说话——契约第一条：二维、只连最近邻。第二条来自检查的形状：每颗星、每块面最多碰四条边，每条检查是重量 ≤ 4 的小操作。第三条来自4.2 节打开的黑盒：每条检查配一个帮手（辅助）比特。第四条来自4.3 节：测量本身会错，所以必须反复、快速地测量。QEC-Bank 直觉文稿把第四条说得很硬。设想一个偷懒的办法：把所有检查当成能量项写进一个哈密顿量（稳定子哈密顿量），码空间就是能量最低的状态，再把芯片冷却，指望它像磁铁记住南北极那样自己待在码空间里，不用测量。这种「自己纠错」的存储器叫自纠错存储器。Bravyi 与 Terhal 证明：检查只连近邻的二维稳定子码做不到这一点，热涨落造成的错误不会被能量自动压住。所以「持续的主动测量是存储器的一部分，不是可选的实现细节」。

Chapter 1 of the intuition guide cut the torus open precisely because a real chip is flat and its qubits talk only to neighbours — clause one: two-dimensional, nearest-neighbour only. Clause two follows from the checks' shape: each star or face touches at most four edges, so every check is a small weight ≤ 4 operation. Clause three comes from the box opened in §4.2: one helper (ancilla) qubit per check. Clause four comes from §4.3: measurements fail, so the machine must measure repeatedly and fast. The QEC-Bank intuition essay states clause four bluntly. Picture a lazy alternative: write every check into a Hamiltonian as an energy term (a stabilizer Hamiltonian), so that the code space is the lowest-energy state, cool the chip, and hope it stays in the code space by itself, the way a magnet remembers its north pole, with no measurements at all. A memory that corrects itself this way is called self-correcting. Bravyi and Terhal proved that two-dimensional stabilizer codes with nearest-neighbour checks cannot do this: errors from thermal fluctuations are not held back by the energy. Hence “continuous active measurement is part of the memory, not an optional implementation detail”.

契约contract = 2D 最近邻2D nearest-neighbour + 重量 ≤ 4 的检查weight ≤ 4 checks + 每检查一个帮手one helper per check + 微秒级反复测量repeated microsecond measurement

四条里没有一条写着「必须是超导」或「必须是原子」。契约只谈几何与节拍；平台若在某条上违约（比如芯片上每个比特只和三个邻居有耦合，排成六边形），要么改造测量电路的排程，要么多做 SWAP 门把比特的状态挪到能相互作用的位置，付出额外的门和错误。第 3 章会看到 Willow 上恰好做了这种改造实验。

None of the clauses says “must be superconducting” or “must be atoms”. The contract is about geometry and tempo; when a platform breaks a clause (say, each qubit is coupled to only three neighbours in a hexagonal pattern) one either reschedules the measurement circuit or adds SWAP gates to move qubit states where they can interact, paying in extra gates and errors. Chapter 3 shows exactly such an experiment on Willow.

1.2两个平台标签，两项任务Two platform tags, two tasks

卡片的 fit 字段只登记两个平台和两项任务——不是「所有可能的平台」，而是有 demonstrated 记录支撑的那些。两个标签的差别：固定超导方格上比特不能动，两块码之间的门靠贴边缝合（《格点手术 · 高中数学版》第 5 章用两次合并做一个 CNOT）；可重构中性原子阵列里原子能被光镊（聚焦的激光束）搬动，把两块码对齐叠放、每一对对应比特同时做一个物理 CNOT，得到横向 CNOTtransversal CNOT：两块同样的码块逐对比特做物理 CNOT。它把第一块的逻辑 X̄ 复制到两块上（X̄⊗I → X̄⊗X̄），把第二块的逻辑 Z̄ 复制到两块上（I⊗Z̄ → Z̄⊗Z̄），这正是逻辑 CNOT 的作用；条件是对应比特能相互作用。（图 1.1）。所有物理 CNOT 同时进行，只占一层时间（深度一），而且每个物理比特只和另一块里的一个比特作用，一个物理错误不会在块内扩散。

The card's fit field registers two platforms and two tasks — not “every conceivable platform” but those backed by a demonstrated record. The difference between the tags: on a fixed superconducting grid qubits cannot move, so gates between patches go through seam merging (Chapter 5 of “Lattice Surgery · High-School Edition” builds a CNOT from two merges); in a reconfigurable neutral-atom array, optical tweezers (focused laser beams) can stack two patches in alignment and apply one physical CNOT to every pair of corresponding qubits at once, giving a transversal CNOTPairwise physical CNOTs between two identical blocks. It copies the first block's logical X̄ onto both blocks (X̄⊗I → X̄⊗X̄) and the second block's logical Z̄ onto both (I⊗Z̄ → Z̄⊗Z̄), which is exactly what a logical CNOT does; it requires corresponding qubits to be able to interact. (Figure 1.1). All the physical CNOTs run simultaneously and take one time layer (depth one), and each physical qubit interacts with a single qubit of the other block, so one physical error cannot spread inside a block.

同一份文稿提醒：「每一个固定版图上的两比特门都是手术」这句话是错的，缺陷编织同样能在固定硬件上工作。做法见 Fowler 等 2012 年综述第 XIV 节：在贴片里关掉几条检查，留下一个「洞」；一个逻辑比特用一对洞来存。洞分两种：关掉 Z 型检查（面）得到 Z 切口洞，关掉 X 型检查（星）得到 X 切口洞。把 Z 切口比特的一个洞沿闭合路线绕 X 切口比特的一个洞走一圈，两个比特的逻辑算符按 X̄₁ → X̄₁X̄₂、Z̄₂ → Z̄₁Z̄₂ 变化（图 1.2），这正是以 Z 切口比特为控制、X 切口比特为目标的逻辑 CNOT。两个同类的洞互相绕，得不到 CNOT：两个 Z 切口比特之间的 CNOT 要借一个 X 切口比特做中介，做三次编织再加两次逻辑测量（同一综述第 XIV 节 D 小节）。

The same essay warns that “every fixed-layout two-qubit gate is surgery” would be false: defect braiding also works on fixed hardware. The recipe is in Section XIV of the 2012 review by Fowler et al.: switch off a few checks in the patch to leave a “hole”, and store one logical qubit in a pair of holes. Holes come in two kinds: switching off Z-type checks (faces) gives a Z-cut hole, switching off X-type checks (stars) an X-cut hole. Move one hole of a Z-cut qubit along a closed route around one hole of an X-cut qubit, and the logical operators change as X̄₁ → X̄₁X̄₂ and Z̄₂ → Z̄₁Z̄₂ (Figure 1.2): exactly a logical CNOT with the Z-cut qubit as control and the X-cut qubit as target. Braiding two holes of the same kind gives no CNOT: a CNOT between two Z-cut qubits borrows an X-cut qubit as an intermediary, with three braids and two logical measurements (Section XIV D of the same review).

关键想法Key idea

「契合」是双向的：码向硬件要几何与节拍，硬件向码要可改造的排程。卡片的平台标签只登记已用实验兑现契约的那一边。

“Fit” runs both ways: the code asks for geometry and tempo, the hardware asks for adaptable schedules. The card's platform tags record only the side where experiments have honoured the contract.

第 2 章Chapter 2

怎样诚实地读一篇实验How to read an experiment honestly

实验论文报告的不是「阈值」，而是三样东西：每轮逻辑错误率、它随码距怎么变、一个叫 Λ（压制因子）的倍数。三样各有确切定义，也各有一种流行的误读。 An experiment does not report “a threshold”. It reports three things: logical error per cycle, how it changes with distance, and a factor called Λ (the suppression factor). Each has a precise definition and a popular misreading.

2.1每轮逻辑错误率Logical error per cycle

存储实验的原始数据是一条衰减曲线：跑 N 轮后，读出的逻辑比特与准备的不一致的概率。把它拟合成「每轮独立地以概率 εL每轮逻辑错误率（logical error per cycle）：一轮纠错循环后逻辑比特被翻转的概率，由多轮衰减曲线拟合得到。 翻转一次」，εL 就是论文报告的每轮逻辑错误率。实验对很多个不同的 N 各重复成千上万次，得到一串失败频率，再找一个 εL 让模型曲线最贴近这串点；所以 εL 是由整条曲线拟合出来的数，总带一个 ± 的拟合误差。它还依赖一轮多长：同样的 εL，一轮 1 微秒和一轮 1 毫秒的机器每秒丢失的信息差一千倍，跨平台比较前先问循环时长。

A memory experiment's raw data is a decay curve: after N cycles, the probability that the logical bit read out disagrees with the one prepared. Fit it as “each cycle independently flips the logical bit with probability εLLogical error per cycle: the probability that the logical bit is flipped over one correction cycle, fitted from a multi-cycle decay curve.”, and εL is the logical error per cycle the paper reports. The experiment repeats the run thousands of times for each of many values of N, obtains a series of failure frequencies, and picks the εL whose model curve fits those points best; εL is therefore fitted from a whole curve and always carries a ± fitting error. It also depends on how long a cycle is: at the same εL, a machine with 1-microsecond cycles loses information a thousand times faster per second than one with 1-millisecond cycles, so ask for the cycle time before comparing platforms.

P失败fail(N) ≈ N · εL　(小 εL、少轮数的近似；论文拟合完整衰减曲线small-εL, few-cycle approximation; papers fit the full curve)

例：εL = 0.143%（Willow d = 7），N = 10 轮，P失败 ≈ 10 × 0.143% ≈ 1.4%。N·εL 变大以后这个近似会高估，因为同一个逻辑比特被翻两次等于没翻。字母表：N = 循环轮数；εL = 每轮逻辑错误率（上文定义）；P失败(N) = 跑完 N 轮后逻辑比特被翻转的概率。Example: εL = 0.143% (Willow, d = 7) and N = 10 cycles give Pfail ≈ 10 × 0.143% ≈ 1.4%. Once N·εL grows the approximation overestimates, because a logical bit flipped twice is not flipped at all. 2.2码距缩放与 ΛDistance scaling and Λ

直觉教程 6.1 节的经验律 PL ≈ A·(p/pth)⌈d/2⌉（PL = 逻辑错误率；A = 常数；p = 每个物理比特的错误概率，见《从 1+1 到 Toric Code》2.2 节；pth = 阈值；d = 码距，见同一教程 5.8 节；⌈d/2⌉ = d/2 向上取整）说：码距 d 每加 2，指数 ⌈d/2⌉ 加 1，逻辑错误率就多乘一次 p/pth。例：若 p/pth = 1/2，d = 3 时 PL ≈ A·(1/2)²，d = 5 时 ≈ A·(1/2)³，两者之比是 2 = pth/p。实验测不到 pth，但能直接测那个因子：同一芯片上做 d=3、5、7，相邻两个 εL 相除，得到Λ压制因子（suppression factor）：Λ = ε_L(d)/ε_L(d+2)。Λ > 1 表示加大码距确实有利；它是从实测数据拟合出的倍数。。Λ > 1 就是「加大真的更好」在这台机器上成立。

The empirical law of §6.1 of the intuition guide, PL ≈ A·(p/pth)⌈d/2⌉ (PL = logical error rate; A = a constant; p = the error probability per physical qubit, see §2.2 of “From 1+1 to the Toric Code”; pth = the threshold; d = the code distance, see §5.8 of the same guide; ⌈d/2⌉ = d/2 rounded up), says that each +2 in d raises the exponent ⌈d/2⌉ by one, multiplying the logical error rate by one more factor p/pth. Example: if p/pth = 1/2, then PL ≈ A·(1/2)² at d = 3 and A·(1/2)³ at d = 5, a ratio of 2 = pth/p. An experiment cannot measure pth, but it can measure that factor: build d = 3, 5, 7 on one chip, divide neighbouring εL values, and you get ΛSuppression factor: Λ = ε_L(d)/ε_L(d+2). Λ > 1 means growing the distance really helps; it is a factor fitted from measured data.. Λ > 1 is “bigger really is better” holding on this machine.

Λ = εL(d) / εL(d+2)　·　经验律暗示 Λ ~ pth/p，但 Λ 是整机加译码器的拟合值the law suggests Λ ~ pth/p, but Λ is a fit to device plus decoder

字母表：Λ = 压制因子，码距每加 2 时每轮逻辑错误率缩小的倍数；εL(d) = 码距为 d 时的每轮逻辑错误率；d = 码距；p = 每比特物理错误率；pth = 阈值。Legend: Λ = the suppression factor, how many times the logical error per cycle shrinks when the distance grows by 2; εL(d) = logical error per cycle at distance d; d = code distance; p = physical error rate per qubit; pth = threshold.

这里最容易滑倒：括号里是 p/pth，于是想把 Λ 读成 pth/p。QEC-Bank 直觉文稿明确否决：「Λ 是一个设备加译码器的拟合，不是 pth/p 的直接测量。」真实芯片上各个比特的错误率不一样（非均匀噪声），有时一个事件同时弄坏好几个比特（相关错误），有时比特跑出 0/1 两个能级、落到更高的能级上（泄漏），译码器也不是最优的；这些全揉进了 Λ，而经验律一条也没考虑。《译码器 · 高中数学版》6.4 节整节讲这一点；同一教程的阈值账本把 Λ=2.14 与 10.9%、10.3%、2.9%、0.57% 各放一行，并注明 Λ 那一行不是阈值——不同实验，不能互相换算。

This is the slippery spot: the bracket holds p/pth, so one is tempted to read Λ as pth/p. The QEC-Bank intuition essay vetoes it: “Λ is a device-and-decoder fit, not a direct measurement of pth/p.” On a real chip the qubits have different error rates (heterogeneous noise), one event sometimes damages several qubits at once (correlated errors), a qubit sometimes escapes its two levels 0 and 1 into a higher level (leakage), and the decoder is not optimal; all of this is folded into Λ, and the empirical law accounts for none of it. §6.4 of “Decoders · High-School Edition” spends a section on this; the same guide's threshold ledger gives Λ = 2.14 its own row beside 10.9%, 10.3%, 2.9% and 0.57%, noting that the Λ row is not a threshold — different experiments, no conversion.

为了把「Λ 是拟合值」看具体，下面再跑一次直觉教程里的阈值实验。它的模型是完美测量、只有 Z 错误——码容量模型code-capacity model：只有数据比特出错、检查读数完美的噪声模型。阈值账本的前三行（10.9%、10.3%、≈9.9%）属于它。，不是任何真实芯片的模型。它的译码器是混合的：一轮里警报不超过 14 个时，做精确的最小权配对（把警报两两连起来或连到边界，使修复链的总边数最少）；超过 14 个时改用贪心配对（每次先连最近的一对），这时译码变差，所以测出的阈值偏低。取 p 很小处两条相邻曲线相除得到的「浏览器 Λ」，与 Willow 的 2.14 没有任何可比性；它只是「Λ 依赖整套设定」的一个能动手的例子。

To make “Λ is a fit” concrete, the bench below reruns the intuition guide's threshold experiment. Its model is perfect measurements, Z errors only — the code-capacity modelA noise model with data errors only and perfect check readings. The first three ledger rows (10.9%, 10.3%, ≈9.9%) belong to it., not the model of any real chip. Its decoder is a hybrid: when a round has at most 14 alarms it computes an exact minimum-weight matching (pair the alarms with each other or with the boundary so that the repair chains use the fewest edges in total); with more than 14 alarms it falls back to greedy pairing (always join the closest pair first), which decodes worse and pulls the measured threshold down. The “browser Λ” you get by dividing two neighbouring curves at small p is in no way comparable with Willow's 2.14; it is only a hands-on example of “Λ depends on the whole setup”.

2.3三句不同的话Three different sentences

新闻常把三句合成一句，实验里它们是三个独立判定。「越大越好」只要求相邻码距的 εL 下降，哪怕一点点：2023 年 Google 的记录从 d=3 的 3.028% 到 d=5 的 2.914%（d=3 的数是同一芯片上一组 d=3 贴片的平均，摘要原话「an ensemble of distance-3 logical qubits」），两数之比约 1.04（论文原话「相对错误降低约 4%」），所以卡片用词是「降低」。这个 1.04 不是 Willow 的 Λ = 2.14；论文另外用模拟算了一个 Λ3/5 = 1.10（见 3.3 节），卡片没有登记任何 Λ。「低于阈值」更强：多个码距（3→5→7）上稳定的、明显大于 1 的 Λ——2024 年 Willow 的内容。「逻辑寿命超过最好的物理比特」（英文叫 beyond break-even）是另一条坐标：编码比特与同芯片最好的单个裸比特比谁存得久，与码距缩放无关；Willow 的记录同时含有这句，摘要给出的倍数是 2.4 ± 0.3。

Headlines fuse three sentences into one; experiments keep three verdicts. “Bigger is better” asks only that εL fall between neighbouring distances, however slightly: Google's 2023 record went from 3.028% at d = 3 to 2.914% at d = 5 (the d = 3 figure is an average over a set of d = 3 patches on the same chip, in the abstract's words “an ensemble of distance-3 logical qubits”), a ratio of about 1.04 (the paper: “a relative error reduction of about 4%”), so the card says “reduced”. This 1.04 is not Willow's Λ = 2.14; the paper separately computes a simulated Λ3/5 = 1.10 (see §3.3), and the card registers no Λ at all. “Below threshold” is stronger: a stable Λ well above 1 across several distances (3→5→7) — the content of Willow 2024. “Logical lifetime beyond the best physical qubit” (“beyond break-even”) is a different axis: the encoded qubit against the chip's best single bare qubit, which one keeps its state longer, independent of distance scaling; Willow's record contains this sentence too, with a factor of 2.4 ± 0.3 in the abstract.

2.4存储实验与计算实验Memory versus computation experiments

存储实验的被测对象是一轮循环，报告 εL；计算实验的被测对象是一个逻辑门，报告门后逻辑状态的质量。计算实验可以只让门这一步容错，而用非容错方式准备输入。卡片上唯一的计算类记录（bluvstein2024logical）正是如此：横向门容错，但 d=5、7（d = 码距）表面码块的逻辑输入态准备不容错。所以卡片写道：这是逻辑操作演示，不是低于阈值的表面码存储声明。

A memory experiment measures a cycle and reports εL; a computation experiment measures a logical gate and reports the quality of the logical state after it. A computation experiment may make only the gate fault-tolerant while preparing inputs non-fault-tolerantly. The card's only computation record (bluvstein2024logical) is exactly that: the transversal gate was fault-tolerant, the preparation of the d = 5 and d = 7 (d = code distance) surface-code inputs was not. Hence the card's wording: a logical-operations demonstration, not a below-threshold surface-memory claim.

Eickbusch 等，“Demonstrating dynamic surface codes”（2024），eickbusch2024dynamic。三种改造排程各解一条契约违约：六边形连接把每比特需要的耦合器从 4 个降到 3 个；行走版每轮交换数据比特与测量比特的角色，贴片像在芯片上「走动」，每个比特每两轮被测量并复位一次（图 3.2），积累的泄漏因此被清掉；iSWAP 版改用 iSWAP 门代替 CNOT，论文说不增加额外开销。iSWAP 是芯片原生的两比特门：|00⟩ 和 |11⟩ 不变，|01⟩ → i|10⟩，|10⟩ → i|01⟩。这里 i 是虚数单位（i² = −1），乘上 i 等于在复平面上转 90°。只有 |01⟩、|10⟩ 两个分量乘上 i，|00⟩、|11⟩ 不乘，所以这是相对相位：它会改变叠加态后续的干涉结果。若四个分量都乘上同一个数，那是整体相位，任何测量都看不出来。卡片只登记定性结论：三者都随码距压制错误，证明码「可以适应连接、泄漏与原生门的约束」。论文摘要给出从 d=3 到 d=5 的压制因子 Λ：六边形 2.15(2)、行走 1.69(6)、iSWAP 1.56(2)（括号里是末位数字的误差，2.15(2) 即 2.15 ± 0.02）；这些数不在卡片里。它们都用 Harmony 译码器（把多个匹配译码器的结果合成一个判决）加上一张用强化学习（一种按反馈反复试错调参的机器学习方法）训练出的噪声先验（译码器假定的各类错误概率表）得到；每个 ε 都是 X 基与 Z 基两种实验的平均，d=3 的数还是同一芯片上几块 d=3 贴片的平均。论文附录 F 强调 Λ「强烈依赖所用译码器」：例如六边形版改用稀疏 blossom 匹配译码器加通用噪声先验，得到 2.09(2)。

A. Eickbusch et al., “Demonstrating dynamic surface codes” (2024), eickbusch2024dynamic. Three re-engineered schedules, each fixing one contract breach: hexagonal connectivity cuts the couplers each qubit needs from 4 to 3; the walking code swaps the roles of data and measure qubits every round, so the patch “walks” across the chip and every qubit is measured and reset once every two rounds (Figure 3.2), which removes accumulated leakage; the iSWAP version uses iSWAP gates instead of CNOTs, according to the paper without extra overhead. iSWAP is a native two-qubit gate of the chip: |00⟩ and |11⟩ are unchanged, |01⟩ → i|10⟩ and |10⟩ → i|01⟩. Here i is the imaginary unit (i² = −1), and multiplying by i is a 90° turn in the complex plane. Only the |01⟩ and |10⟩ components pick up the i, while |00⟩ and |11⟩ do not, so this is a relative phase: it changes how a superposition interferes later. Multiplying all four components by the same number would be a global phase, which no measurement can see. The card records only the qualitative conclusion: all three suppress error with distance, showing the code “can be adapted to connectivity, leakage, and native-gate constraints”. The paper's abstract gives the suppression factor Λ from d = 3 to d = 5: hexagonal 2.15(2), walking 1.69(6), iSWAP 1.56(2) (the bracket is the error in the last digit, 2.15(2) meaning 2.15 ± 0.02); these values are not on the card. All three use the Harmony decoder (which merges the verdicts of several matching decoders into one) with a noise prior (the table of error probabilities the decoder assumes) trained by reinforcement learning (a machine-learning method that tunes parameters by repeated trial and feedback); each ε is the average of an X-basis and a Z-basis experiment, and the d = 3 values are further averaged over several d = 3 patches on the same chip. Appendix F of the paper stresses that Λ “is strongly dependant on the decoder used”: the hexagonal code decoded with a sparse-blossom matching decoder and a generic noise prior gives 2.09(2), for example.

为什么清掉泄漏的是复位，不是「换个名字」：泄漏的比特落在 |2⟩ 这样的高能级上。X、Z 这类 Pauli 纠正只在 |0⟩ 与 |1⟩ 之间翻转或加符号，作用在 |2⟩ 上不会把它带回 0/1，所以译码器算出的任何 Pauli 修复都救不回它。复位是另一种操作：论文用的多能级复位不管比特处在 |0⟩、|1⟩ 还是 |2⟩，都把它拉回 |0⟩。标准排程只复位测量比特，数据比特上的泄漏会一直留着（论文的对照实验里，不加泄漏清除的标准排程出现了持续近 50 轮的警报相关尾巴，要另加一步叫 DQLR 的数据比特泄漏清除操作才能消掉）；行走排程让每个比特每两轮轮到一次测量加复位，泄漏的比特最多等两轮就会被复位。论文实测：每次复位后，那一组比特的泄漏比例下降约一个数量级，行走版里没有再加任何专门的泄漏清除步骤。

Why it is the reset that removes leakage, not the relabelling: a leaked qubit sits in a higher level such as |2⟩. Pauli corrections such as X and Z only flip or sign-change between |0⟩ and |1⟩; applied to |2⟩ they do not bring it back into 0/1, so no Pauli repair the decoder computes can rescue it. A reset is a different operation: the multi-level reset used in the paper returns the qubit to |0⟩ whether it was in |0⟩, |1⟩ or |2⟩. The standard schedule resets only the measure qubits, so leakage on data qubits stays (in the paper's comparison, the standard schedule without leakage removal showed a tail of correlated alarms lasting nearly 50 rounds, which a separate data-qubit leakage-removal step, DQLR, was needed to remove); the walking schedule gives every qubit a measurement and reset every two rounds, so a leaked qubit waits at most two rounds for a reset. Measured in the paper: after each reset, the leaked fraction of that group drops by about an order of magnitude, and the walking code used no dedicated leakage-removal step at all.

5.2直觉文稿指出的结构性限制Structural limits named by the intuition essay

这一节的三条性质不同：前两条是带前提的定理，第三条是常用协议的代价，不是定理。

The three items here differ in kind: the first two are theorems with stated assumptions, the third is a cost of the usual protocols, not a theorem.

① 没有被动的热存储。Bravyi–Terhal 证明：检查只连近邻的二维稳定子哈密顿量不能自纠错（1.1 节）。

① No passive thermal memory. Bravyi–Terhal prove that a two-dimensional stabilizer Hamiltonian with nearest-neighbour checks cannot be self-correcting (§1.1).

② 没有「浅电路」做的 T 门。先说门（下方补课 5a 从零讲 H、S、T）：把任意 Pauli 算符「夹」一遍后仍得到 Pauli 算符的门叫 Clifford 门，H、S、CNOT 及它们的组合都是；它们全体（Clifford 群）不够做通用计算，还缺一个 T 门这样的非 Clifford 门。Bravyi 与 König 的定理有三个前提：码是几何局域的稳定子码（每条检查只碰彼此相邻的比特），码距足够大，逻辑门由深度固定（不随码距增长）、每个门只作用于相邻比特的电路实现。这样的电路里一个错误只能扩散到固定大小的邻域，这正是它吸引人的地方。结论：在二维里，这样实现的逻辑门都在 Clifford 群里（见《格点手术 · 高中数学版》1.3 节）。所以在这些前提下，二维表面码上没有这样实现的逻辑 T 门，通用计算要另找资源：用魔术态（补课 5a 的 ①→④），或者临时换到另一种码上做这一步（码切换）；卡片上没有记录演示这一步。

② No T gate from a shallow circuit. First the gates (catch-up 5a below teaches H, S and T from zero): a gate that turns every Pauli operator, when sandwiched by it, into a Pauli operator again is a Clifford gate; H, S, CNOT and all their combinations are Clifford. All of them together (the Clifford group) are not enough for universal computation, which also needs a non-Clifford gate such as T. The Bravyi–König theorem has three assumptions: the code is a geometrically local stabilizer code (each check touches only qubits near each other), its distance is large enough, and the logical gate is implemented by a circuit of fixed depth (not growing with the distance) whose gates act only on nearby qubits. In such a circuit an error can spread only over a neighbourhood of fixed size, which is what makes it attractive. The conclusion: in two dimensions every logical gate implemented this way lies in the Clifford group (see §1.3 of “Lattice Surgery · High-School Edition”). So under these assumptions the 2D surface code has no logical T gate implemented this way, and universal computation needs another resource: magic states (steps ① to ④ of catch-up 5a), or switching temporarily to a different code for that step (code switching); no card record demonstrates that step.

③ 操作消耗时空（协议的代价，不是定理）。格点手术与码变形类的协议（合并、分裂、辫织）每一步要维持 d 轮测量（d = 码距；见同一教程第 6 章）；这是这些协议的设计决定的，换一种协议代价就不同。卡片上另有两类不付这笔钱的逻辑操作。一类是可移动原子上的横向 CNOT：只需一层物理 CNOT，剩下的代价是搬运原子，以及译码时要把两块的症状放在一起解（关联译码）。另一类是 Pauli 框架更新：需要施加的 Pauli 纠正不真去做，只在经典电脑里记下来，之后解读测量结果时把它算进去，不需要任何物理操作。用的哪种协议、多少轮，代价必须单独报告。

③ Operations consume spacetime (a protocol cost, not a theorem). Lattice-surgery and code-deformation protocols (merge, split, braiding) sustain d rounds of measurement per step (d = code distance; see Chapter 6 of the same guide); this follows from how those protocols are designed, and another protocol has another cost. The card also lists two kinds of logical operation that do not pay this price. One is the transversal CNOT on movable atoms: a single layer of physical CNOTs, the remaining costs being atom transport and decoding the syndromes of both blocks together (correlated decoding). The other is the Pauli-frame update: a Pauli correction that should be applied is not performed but recorded in the classical computer and taken into account when later measurement results are interpreted, with no physical operation at all. Which protocol and how many rounds is a cost to report separately.

Clifford 门是什么。纠错时我们追踪的错误是 Pauli 算符（X、Y、Z 及其乘积）。门 U 若把每个 Pauli 算符 P「夹」成另一个 Pauli 算符，即 U P U† 仍是（带 ± 号的）Pauli 算符，就叫 Clifford 门（U† 是 U 的逆）。可以手算验证：H X H = Z，H Z H = X；S X S† = Y；CNOT 把 X⊗I 变成 X⊗X（这就是图 1.1 里 X̄ 被复制的原因）。好处是，Clifford 门之前的一个 Pauli 错误，到门之后仍是一个 Pauli 错误，记账方式不变。T 不行：T X T† = (X + Y)/√2，是两个 Pauli 的和，不再是单个 Pauli 算符。

What a Clifford gate is. The errors error correction tracks are Pauli operators (X, Y, Z and their products). A gate U is Clifford if it “sandwiches” every Pauli operator P into another one, that is, U P U† is again a Pauli operator up to a ± sign (U† is the inverse of U). By hand: H X H = Z and H Z H = X; S X S† = Y; CNOT turns X⊗I into X⊗X (which is why X̄ gets copied in Figure 1.1). The benefit: a Pauli error before a Clifford gate is still a Pauli error after it, so the bookkeeping stays the same. T fails the test: T X T† = (X + Y)/√2, a sum of two Paulis rather than a single Pauli operator.
