Logical operations

Available gates, mechanisms, costs, and provenance.

  • CNOT surgery claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: O(d) rounds per merge/split plus an ancilla routing patch

    References: horsman2012surface

  • CNOT transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: constant-depth physical CNOT layer; transport and correlated-decoding costs remain

    References: bluvstein2024logical

  • CNOT braiding claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: O(d) or more rounds and a distance-sized reserved hole trajectory

    References: fowler2012surface

  • single-qubit Clifford braiding claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: twist/corner code deformation over distance-preserving paths

    References: brown2017poking

  • H / S fold-transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: constant-depth gates plus a fold or equivalent qubit permutation

    References: moussa2016transversal

  • logical |0> / |+> initialization measurement-based claimed

    Level: stabilizer preparation · Cost: prepare a physical product state, measure the complementary stabilizers for d rounds, and decode the spacetime record

    References: fowler2012surface

  • logical Z / X readout measurement-based claimed

    Level: stabilizer measurement · Cost: destructively measure all data qubits in the selected basis and decode the final data/syndrome history

    References: fowler2012surface

  • Pauli-frame update measurement-based claimed

    Level: Clifford bookkeeping · Cost: a classical frame update after decoded logical measurements; no immediate physical Pauli layer is required

    References: fowler2012surface

  • T measurement-based claimed

    Level: non-Clifford · Cost: inject and distill a magic state, then consume it by gate teleportation; factory spacetime dominates

    References: bravyi2005magic
