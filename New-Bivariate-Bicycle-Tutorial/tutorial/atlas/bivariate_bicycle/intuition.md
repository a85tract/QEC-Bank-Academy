# Bivariate bicycle code

## Essence

A bivariate bicycle (BB) code starts with two sparse binary polynomials on a
finite torus. Replacing each monomial by a cyclic translation gives two
commuting matrices \(A\) and \(B\). The CSS checks

\[
H_X=[A\mid B],\qquad H_Z=[B^T\mid A^T]
\]

then commute for an algebraic reason: \(H_XH_Z^T=AB+BA=0\) over
\(\mathbb F_2\). Instead of searching over stabilizer matrices and separately
repairing anticommutation, the construction chooses an abelian algebra where
commutation is automatic and spends the search budget on rate, distance, and
hardware layout.

The reference gross block uses a \(12\times6\) torus and two three-term
polynomials. The constructor recomputes 144 data qubits, 12 logical qubits,
weight-six checks, and qubit degree six. Its static distance is 12 with split
provenance: Bravyi et al. supply the lower bound, while the Atlas reruns a
fixed-seed weight-12 upper witness. The full calculation is in the
[gross parameter ledger](pedagogy/gross-parameter-ledger.md).

## Design problem

The target is not an asymptotically good two-dimensional phase. It is a useful
finite memory block: pack several logical qubits behind one syndrome circuit
without giving up bounded-weight checks. A distance-12 rotated surface-code
patch uses 144 data qubits for one logical qubit; the gross block uses the same
data-qubit count for twelve. That twelve-fold data-only comparison is the
finite-block attraction reported by `bravyi2024bicycle`, not a claim that
ancillas, routing, decoding, or logical operations are free.

The price is connectivity. Each check touches three qubits in each of two data
layers, and the Tanner graph needs two planar degree-three coupling layers
rather than one nearest-neighbor square grid. The syndrome circuit, CNOT
ordering, detector model, and decoder are part of the memory. A static
`[[n,k,d]]` check matrix alone does not certify circuit distance.

There is also an asymptotic boundary. The quotient-ring analysis of
`postema2025existence` finds the studied BB subclass asymptotically bad.
Moderate blocks may still have excellent constants, but increasing \(l,m\)
does not silently turn the gross construction into a constant-rate,
growing-relative-distance qLDPC family.

## Derivation chain

1. **Make two translations commute.** Let \(S_l\) and \(S_m\) be cyclic
   shifts and set \(x=S_l\otimes I_m\), \(y=I_l\otimes S_m\). They commute
   because they act on different tensor factors.
2. **Turn sparse polynomials into sparse matrices.** A monomial \(x^a y^b\)
   is a permutation matrix, so a polynomial with \(w\) distinct monomials has
   row and column weight \(w\), unless repeated monomials cancel over
   \(\mathbb F_2\).
3. **Use the swapped dual.** Put \(A,B\) in \(H_X=[A|B]\), then put their
   swapped transposes in \(H_Z=[B^T|A^T]\). The swap makes the overlap
   \(AB+BA\), rather than the generally nonzero \(A^2+B^2\).
4. **Read it as a chain complex.** The maps
   \(R\xrightarrow{[B;A]}R^2\xrightarrow{[A|B]}R\) compose to zero.
   Logical Paulis are middle homology classes. The complete convention and
   the toric-code anchor are derived in the
   [polynomial chain-complex page](pedagogy/polynomial-chain-complex.md).
5. **Search the quotient, not arbitrary checks.** The rank deficit of the
   ideal generated by \(A,B\) determines how many logical qubits remain.
   Sparse terms constrain check weight and connectivity; common algebraic
   structure raises \(k\), but too much common structure can create
   low-weight logicals.

The `card.yaml` replay recipe binds each instance's `l`, `m`, `a_terms`, and
`b_terms` into two `bivariate_polynomial@1` factors, then applies
`bivariate_bicycle@1`. The verifier requires the resulting stabilizer array to
match the ordinary constructor exactly, including polynomial, check, and
qubit order, for both shipped instances.

## Multiple pictures

- **Polynomial picture.** Monomials are translations and transpose is
  translation reversal. This is the shortest route from four input parameters
  to the exact matrices.
- **Group-algebra picture.** The ring is the regular representation of
  \(\mathbb Z_l\times\mathbb Z_m\). It explains why BB is an abelian
  two-block group-algebra specialization, not a synonym for every 2BGA or
  lifted-product code.
- **Homological picture.** Checks are consecutive boundary maps and logicals
  are the middle quotient. This separates “commutes with all checks” from
  “is itself a product of checks.”
- **Tanner/circuit picture.** Every polynomial term is an incidence edge.
  This exposes degree, routing layers, hook propagation, and why a syndrome
  schedule cannot be inferred from a static matrix.
- **Quotient-module picture.** Common factors and roots predict rank defects,
  finite rate, and boundary sensitivity.
- **Anyon/Koszul picture.** Polynomial ideals become excitation types and
  mobility constraints. The topological-frustration result prevents the
  overstatement that all finite BB codes are merely decoupled toric copies.
- **Toric-cylinder decoder picture.** Selected symmetries expose matching
  subproblems on cylinders. That is a decoder reduction, not a global
  equivalence of phases.

The [representation dictionary](pedagogy/representation-dictionary.md) states
what each picture preserves and where it stops.

## Why these parameters

For the gross instance, \(lm=72\). There are two qubits per torus site, so
\(n=2lm=144\). Each of \(A=x^3+y+y^2\) and
\(B=y^3+x+x^2\) is a sum of three permutation matrices. Every X or Z check
therefore has weight \(3+3=6\), and every data qubit participates in three
checks of each Pauli type.

The constructor finds

\[
\operatorname{rank}H_X=\operatorname{rank}H_Z=66,
\qquad k=144-66-66=12.
\]

Equivalently, the image ideal has codimension six and the CSS middle homology
contains twice that many logical dimensions. This is a machine result for the
displayed matrices, not a promise that all three-term BB polynomials encode
twelve qubits.

Distance has two different obligations. The literature lower bound excludes
all nontrivial logicals below weight 12. Independently,
`rand-lowweight-v1` with seed 5 and 2,000 iterations finds a weight-12
centralizer class outside the stabilizer row space. The bounds meet, so the
recorded distance is 12 with `bounded` provenance. The Atlas has not
reproduced the literature's lower-bound certificate; that remaining division
of trust is explicit.

The second shipped instance uses \(A=1+x\), \(B=1+y\) on a \(3\times3\)
torus. Exact enumeration gives `[[18,2,3]]`, providing a small toric anchor
that checks the conventions without claiming every BB block decomposes into
toric codes.

## Failure modes

- **Static distance can overstate a circuit.** One ancilla fault may propagate
  through later CNOTs into a correlated data error. The seven-layer schedule
  of `bravyi2024bicycle` is a specific circuit; other orderings can have a
  smaller circuit distance. See
  [syndrome circuits and decoding](pedagogy/syndrome-circuits-and-decoding.md).
- **Connectivity is a resource.** The two planar coupling layers still
  require long-range or reconfigurable couplers, crossings between layers, or
  motion. A one-layer nearest-neighbor chip does not obtain gross-code
  overhead by relabeling qubits.
- **BP is not a magic black box.** Short cycles and degeneracy can stall
  belief propagation; OSD improves robustness but adds global, data-dependent
  latency. A reported threshold belongs to the full family-circuit-decoder
  experiment.
- **Finite constants do not imply asymptotic goodness.** The gross point is a
  strong finite block. It is not evidence that fixed sparse polynomials evade
  two-dimensional tradeoffs as the torus grows.
- **Packed logicals need addressability.** Twelve logical qubits in one block
  are useful only if an architecture can prepare, measure, couple, and route
  the desired logical combinations without erasing the memory advantage.
- **Variants change the object.** Pruning, open boundaries, morphing,
  self-duality, extra variables, and qudits change rank, distance, circuits,
  or alphabet. The [variant and boundary map](pedagogy/variant-boundary-map.md)
  keeps those claims separate.

## Operations story

An aligned physical CNOT between two identical CSS blocks is transversal:
control X checks acquire the matching target X check, while target Z checks
acquire the matching control Z check. This preserves the two-block stabilizer
group and induces the corresponding logical CNOT layer. Physical alignment,
module transport, and correlated decoding remain architecture costs.

Torus translations also preserve the static gross code. If \(P\) is an
\(x\)- or \(y\)-shift, then \(A\) and \(B\) commute with \(P\), giving
\(H_X\operatorname{diag}(P,P)=PH_X\), and likewise for \(H_Z\). This proves a
physical permutation is a code automorphism. It does **not** identify its
action on the twelve logical qubits. An automorphism becomes a useful gate
only after its logical symplectic action and physical routing are resolved.

The literature supplies several non-universal certificates: fold-transversal
Cliffords on selected symmetric examples, automorphism-derived Cliffords,
surface-ancilla surgery in modular proposals, cup-product operations on
qualified blocks, and transversal Cliffords in an extra self-dual subfamily.
None is a universal transversal gate set for the original gross block.
Non-Clifford completion still needs magic states, switching, surgery, or
another certified resource. The applicability boundaries are derived in
[logical operations](pedagogy/logical-operations.md).

## Litmus questions

**Q1:** Why does swapping \(A^T\) and \(B^T\) matter? **A:** With the swap,
\([A|B][B^T|A^T]^T=AB+BA=0\). Without it, the product is
\(A^2+B^2\), which need not vanish. The swap turns CSS commutation into the
commutator of two commuting algebra elements.

**Q2:** Does the Atlas machine-verify the gross code's complete distance
proof? **A:** No. It reruns a weight-12 upper witness, while the exclusion of
weights below 12 remains sourced to `bravyi2024bicycle`. The matching bounds
justify value 12 with `bounded`, not `verified`, provenance.

**Q3:** Is 0.8% an intrinsic threshold of every BB code? **A:** No. Bravyi
et al. estimate a circuit-level threshold for their BB memory family under
the standard circuit-noise model, depth-seven extraction, detector
construction, and BP-OSD configuration. It is a protocol-qualified family
threshold, not a one-block pseudo-threshold or a property of an arbitrary BB
check matrix. Changing the family, circuit, noise normalization, or decoder
defines a different threshold experiment.

**Q4:** Why does \(k=12\) not follow from having three terms in each
polynomial? **A:** Term count fixes sparsity, not rank. Here both CSS check
matrices have rank 66, leaving twelve logical dimensions. Different
three-term polynomials can have a different common quotient, hence different
\(k\) and distance.

**Q5:** If translations preserve the stabilizers, are they already twelve
addressable logical gates? **A:** No. Stabilizer preservation proves only that
the code space maps to itself. One must compute the induced action on a
logical Pauli basis; it may be trivial, may mix several logical qubits, and
may be expensive to route. The card records only operations with a cited
certificate and explicit applicability.
